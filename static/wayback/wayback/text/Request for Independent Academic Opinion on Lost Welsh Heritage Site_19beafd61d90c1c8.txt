Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to request an *independent academic opinion* regarding a Welsh
historic site that was *demolished following a refusal to list in the late
1980s*, and which was later shown to have archaeological and historical
significance.

The site in question was *Great House Farm*, formerly occupied by my family
(Buckler / Williams). At the time, Cadw declined to list the building,
stating it was not of sufficient interest. However, subsequent
archaeological excavation in the early 1990s produced artefacts now held in
museum collections, and there is credible historical evidence suggesting
the site may have had *associative significance connected to early
wireless/radio experimentation*.

At this moment I am *not pursuing litigation* and am not seeking advocacy.
I am simply asking whether a member of your department might be willing, on
a *pro bono basis*, to:

   - Review a small bundle of documentary material (press coverage, basic
   timeline, and publicly available records), and
   - Offer a brief academic view on whether, *under modern heritage
   criteria*, the site would likely merit recognition for historic or
   associative significance, or whether the original assessment appears
   incomplete by current standards.

This request is made in good faith and for the purposes of *historical
accuracy and record correction*, not dispute.

If this is something your department or an individual academic might be
willing to consider, I would be grateful if you could advise on the
appropriate next step.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
