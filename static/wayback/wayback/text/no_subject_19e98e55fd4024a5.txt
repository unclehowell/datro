

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am conducting historical research into Great House Farm (Ty Mawr), 
Llandough, near Penarth.

During this research I have encountered a reference in Stephenson & 
Alexander records (reference DSA/6/1158) indicating that Great House Farm, 
Llandough may have been sold in 1938 to a Mr. L.D. Thomas of Porthcawl.

I am attempting to establish:

   - The full identity of L.D. Thomas of Porthcawl.
   - Whether he purchased Great House Farm, Llandough in 1938.
   - Whether any sale particulars, conveyances, correspondence, solicitor's 
   papers, estate records, family papers, receipts, abstracts of title, 
   mortgages or related documents survive.
   - Whether the records identify the vendor of the property.
   - Whether the records show whether the farm was sold with vacant 
   possession or subject to an existing occupier.
   - Whether any members of the Williams or Buckler families are mentioned 
   in connection with the transaction.

I would be extremely grateful for copies, scans, catalogue references, or 
guidance towards any surviving records relating to this transaction.

Thank you for any assistance you can provide.

Yours faithfully,

Sion Buckler
