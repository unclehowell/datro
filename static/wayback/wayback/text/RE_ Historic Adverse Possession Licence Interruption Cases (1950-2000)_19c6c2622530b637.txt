The BSB is the regulator for barristers in England and Wales. We have the ability to take action where matters impact the regulatory objectives set out by the Legal Services Act. Action we can take includes matters like supervisory action to prevent future breaches of the handbook and providing guidance where necessary. It also can include enforcement action, through an investigation and tribunal process, when a barrister breaches the BSB Handbook.

If you have concerns about the conduct of a barrister, or about a business or organisation we regulate, or if you have concerns about any other aspect of our regulation, you can let us know by making a report to us.
