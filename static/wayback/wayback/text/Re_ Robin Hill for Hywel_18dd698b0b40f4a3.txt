Hi,

I'm sure we both have tales of life well lived to exchange. I'm perfectly
open to making a new friend.

I'm not exactly sure what I've done to deserve the universe conspiring in
my favour so much lately. I'm already blessed with 4 incredible best
friends (2 men/2 woman) of decades. And a wife and two children.

I'm the grandson of a best actor/ 1993 Wesh Bafta award winner: Ray Smith,
from the TV series Dempsey and Makepeace. His son (my uncle) started the
band Tigertailz. And all the success originated at our house here on Robin
Hill as it happens. My father was William Buckler from Great House Farm in
Llandough. (See BP Vs Buckler 1987). Our farm was next to the church in
Llandough. I believe the kitchen table I grew up eating breakfast on, was
the same table Marconi invented the radio while he stayed with our family.

The whole BP Vs Buckler story was local headlines at the time. But take
anything you read with a pinch of salt. What I understand to have actually
happened, with the war chest of information I inherited from my dad's
death, was that my parents and grandparents before them, leased and worked
the farm for hundreds of years. We acquired the title deeds in exchange for
allowing some quarrying to be done. BP and Cotswold archeologists teamed up
to try to get the treasure under our farm by trying to unlawfully evict and
remove us from the equation. And after 34 years battling BP we lost our
farm in court to them. And after my family and I stood there with the
community, watching them bulldose our home down, BP exccevated all the
Roman burials under our farm and sold the artifacts and land. My father's
heart literally stopped, he fought to defend our home and family to the
death.

I grew up. Moved on. I won an assisted place in Llandovery College. And
went onto work in military intelligence/ cyber security and warfare.

Until in 2013 I stumbled across some things nobody was meant to see. My 36
day old son got abducted and used to try to ransom me. My options were
either be silent or be party to corruption and crimes. I chose exile and
refused to return until my son and I got justice.

It was a long road to justice and getting out of exile and returning home
to Britain after almost a decade overseas. I immediately got a puppy,
caught up with old family and friends and got some work in elderly adult
healthcare with the Vale Council.

I refrained from going back up to Yorkshire and kicking that old bees nest
last year. I can only hope and pray my son's ok. Instead I stayed out of
trouble at my families horse farm (Newmill Farm) in Monknash.

Then unexpectedly a woman I spent my time in exile with (and loved very
much), reached out to reciprocate my feelings and announced we were
pregnant - And we both couldn't have been happier. She is the love of my
life and woman of my dreams.

So last October I flew right back to the Caribbean island which took me a
decade to escape, married my now wife (November 27th), then got us a car
and villa near her families home. Then on the 27th December our daughter
Delyth was born - happy and healthy. After just 3 short hours of labor too.
And within a matter of hours we were all home together in our spare room/
nursery, surrounded by our family and friends.

I'm just back in Britain now to get my dog and take him back to the
Caribbean with me. I'm right now arranging a ferry to Europe and I'll fly
back from there. And I'm trying to arrange obtaining my medals and getting
tailored for my army 1's dress/ uniform, ahead of our wedding later this
year.

But a fisherman always sees another fisherman from afar. I've worked with
intelligence agencies who would love your methods of communication. Feels
very revolutionary to hand deliver a letter. I could take some pigeons to
the other side of the pond if you want and we can expand that modus
operandi.

I look forward to hearing anything you might have to say. My new friend.

Speak soon,

Hywel
