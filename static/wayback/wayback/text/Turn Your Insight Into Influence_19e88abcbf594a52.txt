People remember content that changes how they think about something. Often, the strongest ideas come from moments in your work that taught you something worth sharing. This month is about turning those insights into content that builds credibility and expands your influence. When you share ideas in a clear and memorable way, people begin to associate you with a perspective they want to return to. Over time, that consistency strengthens recognition and helps your expertise reach further. How to Turn Insight into Influence Strong insights become more valuable when other people can clearly understand and apply them. The way you frame an idea often matters just as much as the idea itself. Here are some ways to turn your experience into content people remember: Pull lessons from moments in your work that changed how you approach something Focus on explaining the thinking behind your perspective so people can follow your process Revisit ideas that matter to you so your audience begins to associate you with them Turn strong insights into repeatable formats you can use across written posts and video Share observations that help people see familiar challenges in a different way You don’t need every post to sound like formal thought leadership. Often, the most effective insights come from sharing something specific in a way that feels clear, useful, and grounded in real experience. Over time, the ideas you return to help shape how people understand your expertise and what they come to you for. Content Challenge The way you approach your work is often shaped by lessons you’ve learned through experience. Sometimes one takeaway stays with you and influences how you make decisions, communicate, or lead. What’s one takeaway from your work that shapes how you operate? Create a post responding to the question, use the hashtag #OneThingToKnow, and drop the link to your post in the comments so the community can learn from your perspective.
