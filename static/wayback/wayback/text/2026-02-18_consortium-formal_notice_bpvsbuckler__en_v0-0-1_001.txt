Dear Mr Gale,

Thank you for your reply of 12 February. I would like to clarify and narrow
my request to ensure there is no misunderstanding regarding the records I
am trying to locate.

My correspondence is *not* seeking an adjudication of ownership or title —
I fully accept the stated remit boundaries of the Manorial Documents
Register (MDR). Rather, my enquiry concerns the existence of *any archival
reference, index card, catalogue entry, or pointer to a document
historically known as the “Williams Title”*, which was, according to
contemporaneous family testimony, at one point:

   1.

   Held in the *local public library* in the first half of the twentieth
   century; and
   2.

   Indexed in some manner (e.g., an index card or catalogue reference) that
   persisted even after the original document could not be located.

My understanding of the MDR — as described on your own website — is that it
exists to provide brief descriptions of manorial records and details of
their locations:

“An official index of English and Welsh manorial records providing brief
descriptions of documents and details of their locations… It does *not*
include title deeds or conveyances, and is *not* a register of ownership of
manorial lordships or rights.”
https://discovery.nationalarchives.gov.uk/manor-search
<https://discovery.nationalarchives.gov.uk/manor-search?utm_source=chatgpt.com>

Based on that description, I would be grateful if you could confirm whether:

   1.

   *Any catalogue entries, index cards, reference pointers, or archival
   descriptions* relating to the *Manor of Llandough* or the *Williams
   family title* exist in the MDR or related archival catalogues.
   2.

   If so, the *reference identifiers* and information on how these can be
   consulted in person or via digitised records.

To be precise, I am looking for historical references to the *index card or
listing* for the Williams Title — whether held in a library, local record
office, or other institutional archive — rather than a claim for ownership
judgment itself.

If such a reference does not currently appear in the MDR, I would greatly
appreciate guidance on where any known catalogue cards or entry lists for
manorial documents from *Glamorgan / Llandough* prior to 1950 might now
reside (e.g., regional archives, National Archives holdings, or affiliated
local repository catalogues).

Anyway I anticipate your forgiveness, if I have erred in writing to you
earlier.

Thank you again and in advance for your assistance.

Yours sincerely,


Sion Buckler
