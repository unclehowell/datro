Dear Ms Diggins,

I would be grateful for clarification on a specific point.

It has long been understood within my family that an index card existed
referring to the title deeds of Great House Farm, Llandough. While the
deeds themselves reportedly went missing, the index reference was said to
remain at one time.

My question is simply this:

If an index card or catalogue entry once existed within Glamorgan Archives
(or its predecessor systems), would a record of that entry survive
following digitisation, even if the underlying document was withdrawn,
transferred, or missing?

In other words, are superseded or removed catalogue references retained
within archival metadata systems?

I am not alleging wrongdoing. I am trying to establish whether any trace of
such an index entry would ordinarily remain within your records management
framework.

Yours sincerely,


Sion Buckler
