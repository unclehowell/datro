Project Mandate

Purpose

The purpose of this project is to investigate, document, and rectify the historical dispossession of the Buckler family from Great House Farm, Llandough, and to resolve the fraudulent claims associated with the 1987 “BP vs Buckler” case.

Background

In 1987, legal proceedings (BP vs Buckler) were initiated based on what is alleged to be identity fraud concerning Mary Williams. This resulted in the Buckler family losing their rightful occupancy and ownership of Great House Farm. Recent research into the National Archives and Land Registry records has revealed systemic failures and potential administrative mistakes in the register.

Objectives

  • Establish the legal “identity fraud” baseline for Mary Williams.

  • Secure a “Phase 2” resolution from HM Land Registry regarding Title WA231076.

  • Obtain a clear position from the Welsh Government and MoJ on the historical fraud.

  • Restore the rightful title and/or compensation for the heirs of Mary Williams.

Scope

  • In-Scope: Investigation of Land Registry Title WA231076; Correspondence with Senedd, MoJ, MoD, and Vale Council; Historical records analysis.

  • Out-of-Scope: Current unrelated land disputes in the Llandough area not involving the original Williams/Buckler family assets.

Constraints

  • Transition/Dissolution of the Senedd (May 2026).

  • Institutional inertia from CADW and the Vale of Glamorgan Council.

  • Procedural complexity of “Stage Two” complaints at HM Land Registry.

Stakeholders

  • Project Board: Sion Buckler and heirs of Mary Williams.

  • Interested Parties: HM Land Registry, Ministry of Defence, Welsh Government, Vale of Glamorgan Council.
