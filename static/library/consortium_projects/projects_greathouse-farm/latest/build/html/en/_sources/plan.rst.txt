Project Plan
============

Description
-----------

The project is structured into three distinct stages of resolution.

Stage 1: Documentation & Initial Filing (Complete)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Retrieval of National Archives records.
- Initial filing of "Mistake in the Register" with Land Registry.
- Identification of key stakeholders (BP, MoD, CADW).

Stage 2: Formal Escalation (Current)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Filing of Stage Two complaints with Land Registry.
- Challenging the "Investigation" shield used by the MoD.
- Overcoming the "Nothing to do with us" stance from CADW.
- Forcing a Full Response from Joe Martin MS and others after the "setting up office" delay.

Stage 3: Legal/Ombudsman Resolution (Scheduled for Q3 2026)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Referral to the Public Services Ombudsman for Wales (if Stage 1/2 fail).
- Application to the Land Registry Tribunal.

Planning Assumptions
--------------------

- That the National Archives records provide sufficient evidence of the 1987 discrepancy.
- That the MoD's internal investigation will eventually be subject to Freedom of Information or Parliamentary oversight.

Monitoring and Control
----------------------

Progress is monitored via weekly email reviews and the maintenance of a Highlight Report documenting institutional positions.
