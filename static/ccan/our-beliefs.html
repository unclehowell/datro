Our Core Beliefs

Discover the heart of the CCAN Peoples Movement. We believe in a fair and equal society, free from corruption and tyranny, striving to create heaven on earth. Join us in our mission to uphold Christian values and constitutional principles.

The Foundation of Our Movement

At the heart of the CCAN Peoples Movement are three core beliefs that guide our actions and shape our vision for a better future. These are the pillars upon which we build our community and strive for positive change.

  • Christ: We are rooted in Christian values, seeking guidance and inspiration from the teachings of Jesus Christ.
  • Truth: We are committed to honesty, integrity, and transparency in all our dealings.
  • Transparency: We promote openness and accountability, ensuring that our actions are always in the best interests of the people.

For the People, By the People

The CCAN Peoples Movement is driven by the people, for the people. We aim to liberate ourselves from what we see as tyrannical British rule. We understand the concerns and frustrations of those who feel disenfranchised and unheard. Our movement is open to all who seek liberation, justice, and a return to Christian values.

Join Our Cause

After learning about our beliefs, we hope you will consider joining us in our mission. By keeping our core values in mind, and supporting the cause where you can, you're joining a like-minded community that will make a difference.
