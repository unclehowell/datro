Welcome to #ccan

 

Brothers and sisters, friends across every shore and continent, ccan stands before you today at a moment of historic opportunity—a financial revolution that beckons us to reclaim not only our economies but our souls, our heritage, and our destiny. We are the Christian Constitutional Alliance of Nations, united in conviction and purpose. 

 

This is a movement by the Indigenous primordial native Britons dedicated to upholding Christian values and our constitution. Our goal is to gain freedom from the elite British one world order rule by reigniting Christianity and upholding the English Constitution. We look forward to offering community engagement programs and educational initiatives that reflect Christian values and the Constitution of England and Wales.

 

There is no cost to joining the movement - we are all ccan by virtue of our indigenous birthright to the lands of England Ireland Scotland Wales.

Our mission

Our mission is to offer community engagement initiatives rooted in our Christian faith, alongside educational initiatives that mirror both Christian values and the Constitution of England and Wales. We aim to foster a community where individuals can grow spiritually and intellectually, grounded in strong moral principles and a deep understanding of constitutional rights.

A healthy body

We believe our bodies are sacred gifts and should be looked after. Our approach includes exercise routines through classes and activities, as well as promoting healthy eating. This holistic approach aims for the physical and spiritual uplifting of all men and women, fostering a community of well-being and mutual support.
