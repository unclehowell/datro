## Evidence Sources — Foundational
- **The National Archives (TNA):** Discovery catalogue at https://discovery.nationalarchives.gov.uk
  - Records of the Land Registry, Court of Chancery, Court of Appeal
  - Manorial documents registered with the MAF series
  - Tithe maps and apportionments (IR 29, IR 30)
- **Glamorgan Archives:** https://glamarchives.gov.uk
  - Local authority records, planning applications, building control
  - Bute Estate papers, Romilly Estate papers
  - Parish records for Llandough
- **National Library of Wales (NLW):** https://www.library.wales
  - Welsh newspapers, estate records, maps
  - Manorial records for Glamorgan
