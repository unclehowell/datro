# CONTEXT (high) — cnei

## Project Context
- cnei is one of several flywheel branches in the Datro project
- The wayback library is the central evidence repository
- Evidence is indexed via `_treeview.json` files in wayback/ subdirectories
- Actual media files live in R2 bucket `wayback-media`
- The flywheel worker (`flywheel-cf/src/index.js`) reads wing files and executes agent cycles

## Great House Farm Context
- Located at Llandough, nr. Penarth, Vale of Glamorgan, CF64 postcode area
- Also known as Ty Mawr
- Subject of long-running legal dispute BP v Buckler
- Manorial history tied to Manor of Llandough
- Adjacent to Church View Close development
- The B4267 runs north-south past the farm; the A4055 to the east
- Nearest railway station: Dingle Road / Cogan (Penarth branch line)
- Historical county: Glamorgan (before 1974), then South Glamorgan, now Vale of Glamorgan
