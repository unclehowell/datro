Project Brief

Project Definition

The project aims to challenge the 1987 dispossession via administrative and legal channels, specifically targeting the Land Registry’s “mistake in the register” provisions.

Business Case

The restoration of Great House Farm is not merely a matter of family property but a test of the integrity of the Welsh Land Registry system and its ability to correct historical fraud. The “BP vs Buckler” case stands as a documented instance of contested legal identity that must be resolved to ensure public confidence in land titles.

Project Quality Management

Quality will be measured by the successful escalation of complaints to Stage Two/Three and the receipt of legible, substantive responses from government ministers that address the fraud allegations directly rather than pivoting to administrative excuses.

Project Team Structure

  • Project Manager: Sion Buckler

  • Senior User: Heirs of Mary Williams

  • Senior Supplier: Legal and Historical Researchers

Project Approach

The approach follows an escalation ladder:

  1. Administrative: Exhausting internal complaint procedures (Land Registry, Ombudsman).

  2. Political: Leveraging Senedd and Parliamentary oversight (MS/MP engagement).

  3. Legal: Direct challenge to the 1987 proceedings if administrative remedies fail.
