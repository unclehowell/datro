Your Rights

Data Deletion Instructions

DATRO Consortium Ltd  ·  UK GDPR — Right to Erasure  ·  Updated 25 February 2025

Your right to erasure is protected by law. Under Article 17 of the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to request that DATRO Consortium Ltd deletes the personal data we hold about you. This page explains exactly how to do that, what happens next, and what data we are legally required to retain.

You can request deletion of your personal data by any of the methods below. All requests are treated equally regardless of how they are submitted.

What Happens Step by Step

1

Submit your request

Use any of the methods above. Please include your full name, the email address associated with your account, and a brief description of what you would like deleted (or simply state "all data").

2

Identity verification

To protect you from unauthorised deletion, we will verify your identity before processing. We may ask you to confirm details from your account or provide a copy of ID. This step is required by law.

3

Acknowledgement within 72 hours

We will acknowledge receipt of your request within 72 hours and confirm whether we are able to action it or whether any exemptions apply.

4

Processing & deletion

We will complete deletion of all eligible data within 30 calendar days of verifying your identity. In complex cases this may be extended by up to a further two months; we will notify you if this applies.

5

Confirmation

We will send you a written confirmation once deletion is complete, specifying what was deleted and, where applicable, what was retained and why.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

The table below shows the categories of data we hold and the action taken upon a valid deletion request.

Data Category Examples Action on Request Reason if Retained
Account & profile data Name, email, username, password, profile photo Deleted —
Social login tokens Facebook / Google OAuth tokens and linked IDs Deleted —
Activity & usage logs Login history, feature usage, session data Deleted —
Marketing preferences & comms Email opt-in records, newsletter history Deleted —
Pull request / commit attribution records Account ID embedded in commit history linked to your identity Anonymised Commit history integrity; your identity is removed but the contribution record is retained in anonymised form
Revenue share & payment records Payment amounts, bank details, payout history Retained 7 years Legal obligation — HMRC financial record-keeping requirements
KYC / identity verification records ID documents submitted for payment verification Retained 5 years Anti-money laundering (AML) and legal compliance obligations
Contractual & legal correspondence Dispute records, legal notices, formal complaints Retained up to 7 years Limitation Act 1980 — potential legal claims
Aggregated / anonymised analytics Attribution statistics with no personally identifying information Retained (anonymised) No longer personal data — cannot be linked back to you
Anonymised data — once your personal identifiers are removed, the remaining aggregated or anonymised data is no longer personal data under UK GDPR and is therefore outside the scope of the right to erasure.

When We May Decline a Deletion Request

Under UK GDPR Article 17(3), we may decline to delete certain data where retention is necessary for:

  • Compliance with a legal obligation (e.g. tax records, AML requirements);
  • The establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims;
  • Performance of a task carried out in the public interest;
  • Archiving purposes in the public interest, scientific or historical research, or statistical purposes, where deletion would seriously impair the purpose.

Where we decline all or part of your request, we will explain the specific legal basis in our response and inform you of your right to complain to the ICO.

Facebook / Meta App — Data Deletion

If you used DATRO's services via a Facebook application, you may also request deletion of your Facebook-connected data directly through Facebook:

1

Via Facebook Settings

Go to Facebook → Settings & Privacy → Settings → Security and Login → Apps and Websites. Find the DATRO app, click Remove, and select the option to delete all activity.

2

Via DATRO's Deletion Callback

Meta may automatically trigger our data deletion callback URL when you remove the app. This will automatically queue deletion of your Facebook-sourced data from our systems within 30 days.

3

Direct confirmation

For certainty, you can also email privacy@datroconsortium.com referencing "Facebook Data Deletion" and your Facebook user ID or linked email address.

Other Connected Platforms (Google, Apple, etc.)

If you connected your account via Google, Apple, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, or another OAuth provider, you can revoke DATRO's access via that platform's app permissions settings. This will prevent future data sharing. To delete data already received from these platforms, submit a deletion request to us using any method above.

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Apple: Settings → [Your Name] → Password & Security → Apps Using Apple ID
  • Twitter/X: Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions
  • LinkedIn: Settings → Data Privacy → Permitted Services

Revoking access on the third-party platform does not automatically delete data already held by DATRO. Please submit a separate deletion request to us to ensure complete removal.

Your Other Rights

The right to erasure is one of several rights you have under UK GDPR. You may also:

  • Access a copy of all personal data we hold about you (Subject Access Request)
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Restrict processing of your data pending a dispute
  • Port your data to another service in a machine-readable format
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing relies on it

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.
