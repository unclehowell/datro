1. Who We Are

DATRO Consortium Ltd ("DATRO", "we", "us", "our") is a company registered in England and Wales. We are the data controller responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018 (together, "UK Data Protection Law").

2. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to all services, websites, mobile applications, and third-party platform integrations operated by DATRO Consortium Ltd, including but not limited to:

  • Our website(s) and web applications
  • Our Facebook / Meta applications
  • Services accessed via Google, Apple, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, or other OAuth sign-in providers
  • Any API integrations with third-party platforms

This policy does not cover third-party websites or services that we do not own or control, even if linked from our platforms.

3. What Personal Data We Collect

3.1 Data you provide directly

  • Name, email address, username, and password
  • Profile information (photo, bio, preferences)
  • Payment information (processed securely via a PCI-DSS compliant processor; we do not store full card numbers)
  • Communications and correspondence with us
  • Any other data you choose to submit through forms or surveys

3.2 Data collected automatically

  • IP address and approximate geolocation
  • Browser type, operating system, device identifiers
  • Pages visited, features used, time and date of access
  • Referring URL and exit pages
  • Log files and error reports

3.3 Data from third-party platforms

When you connect a third-party account (e.g. Facebook, Google), we may receive the data described in Section 7, subject to the permissions you grant.

4. How We Collect Your Data

  • Directly from you — when you register, fill in forms, or contact us
  • Automatically — via cookies and similar technologies when you use our services
  • From third parties — social login providers, analytics services, and advertising networks
  • From publicly available sources — where permitted by law

5. Legal Bases for Processing

We rely on the following legal bases under Article 6 UK GDPR:

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — to provide the services you have requested
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — to improve our services, prevent fraud, and ensure security, where your interests do not override ours
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — for marketing communications and non-essential cookies; you may withdraw consent at any time
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — to comply with applicable law

Where we process special-category data, we rely on explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)) or another applicable condition.

6. How We Use Your Data

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Process transactions and send related notices
  • Send service-related communications (e.g. security alerts, policy updates)
  • Send marketing communications where you have consented or where permitted under PECR
  • Personalise your experience
  • Analyse usage patterns to improve functionality
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

We do not sell your personal data.

7. Third-Party Platforms & Social Login

Our services may integrate with third-party platforms including Meta/Facebook, Google, Apple, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, GitHub, and others. When you choose to connect such an account, we receive the data those platforms share with us according to your privacy settings and the permissions you grant.

Typical data received includes: name, email address, profile photo, and a platform-specific user identifier. We do not request permissions beyond what is necessary for the functionality you use.

Important: Your use of third-party platforms is governed by that platform's own privacy policy. We encourage you to review those policies. DATRO is not responsible for the data practices of third-party platforms.

You may disconnect any linked account at any time through your account settings or through the third-party platform's settings.

8. Facebook & Meta Platform

DATRO operates one or more applications on the Meta platform ("Facebook Apps"). In connection with these apps, we operate under the Meta Platform Terms and our obligations as a data controller and, where applicable, as a data processor.

8.1 Data received from Meta

When you authorise our Facebook App, we may receive data including your Facebook name, email address, public profile information, friend list (where explicitly requested and consented to), and any other permissions you grant at the time of authorisation.

8.2 Restricted data use

Data obtained via the Meta platform is used solely for the purpose for which it was collected and as stated in our authorisation dialogue. We do not use Facebook data for advertising targeting without separate consent, and we do not transfer Facebook data to third-party ad networks, data brokers, or data resellers.

8.3 Data deletion

You may request deletion of your Facebook-linked data at any time by using our data deletion callback URL or by contacting us at the address in Section 18. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

8.4 Meta's own privacy policy

Your data held by Meta is subject to Meta's Privacy Policy, available at facebook.com/policy.

9. Google & Other OAuth Providers

Where our service supports sign-in via Google, we use Google's OAuth 2.0 protocol. Data we receive is limited to the scopes displayed at authorisation time (typically name, email address, and profile picture). We do not access your Google Drive, Calendar, Gmail, or other Google services unless you explicitly grant and confirm those additional permissions.

Google's Privacy Policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

The same principles apply to all other OAuth providers (Apple, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, GitHub, etc.). We request the minimum scopes required and data is used only for authentication and account management purposes.

10. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies (pixels, local storage, session storage) to operate our services and to understand how they are used. Cookies are categorised as follows:

  • Strictly necessary — essential for the service to function (no consent required)
  • Functional — remember your preferences and settings
  • Analytics — help us understand how you use our services (e.g. Google Analytics)
  • Marketing — used to serve relevant advertising; only set with your consent

You can manage or withdraw cookie consent at any time via our cookie preference centre (accessible in the footer of our website) or by adjusting your browser settings. Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect the functioning of our service.

We comply with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR) with respect to cookies.

11. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Service providers and processors — cloud hosting, payment processors, analytics providers, email platforms — who process data on our behalf under written data processing agreements
  • Professional advisers — solicitors, accountants, auditors, under confidentiality obligations
  • Competent authorities — where required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, where the receiving party agrees to honour this policy

All third-party processors are required to implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data.

12. International Transfers

Some of our service providers may be located outside the United Kingdom. Where we transfer personal data to countries not covered by UK adequacy regulations, we implement appropriate safeguards such as:

  • UK International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTAs)
  • The UK Addendum to EU Standard Contractual Clauses
  • Binding Corporate Rules (where applicable)

You may request a copy of the safeguards in place by contacting us at the address in Section 18.

13. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, and in accordance with our legal obligations. Typical retention periods are:

  • Account data — for the duration of your account plus up to 7 years after closure (to comply with financial/legal obligations)
  • Transaction records — 7 years (HMRC requirements)
  • Marketing preferences — until you withdraw consent or object
  • Log files — up to 12 months
  • Cookie data — as described in our cookie preference centre

When data is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymised.

14. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Right of access — to obtain a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — to have inaccurate data corrected or incomplete data completed
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — to request deletion of your data in certain circumstances
  • Right to restrict processing — to limit how we use your data in certain circumstances
  • Right to data portability — to receive a structured, machine-readable copy of data you provided to us
  • Right to object — to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing
  • Rights relating to automated decision-making — not to be subject to solely automated decisions that significantly affect you
  • Right to withdraw consent — at any time, where processing is based on consent
To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@datro.xyz (or the address in Section 18). We will respond within one calendar month. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

15. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or, where applicable, the age of digital consent in the user's jurisdiction, which in the UK is 13). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without verifiable parental or guardian consent.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete such data.

16. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, accidental loss, destruction, or damage. These measures include encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS), access controls, regular security assessments, and staff training.

No transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security. If you become aware of any security vulnerability, please report it to us promptly.

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) within 72 hours and will notify you without undue delay where required by law.

17. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technologies, legal requirements, or other factors. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page
  • Notify you via email (where we hold your email address) or via a prominent in-app notice
  • Where required by law, seek your renewed consent

Your continued use of our services after the effective date of any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
