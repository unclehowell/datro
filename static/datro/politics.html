Our Position

As a security group, our core mission is to safeguard people, rights, and infrastructure—never to threaten them. We believe enduring security is inseparable from legitimate, accountable governance. For that reason, we support the democratic disestablishment of the British establishment and the peaceful transition to three sovereign nations—England, Wales, and Scotland—each with its own fully empowered institutions, constitution, and public mandate.

This is not a call for disorder; it is a call for clarity. We advocate constitutional change by lawful, electoral, and civic means: referenda, representative mandates, and due process. We reject sectarianism and all forms of prejudice. We affirm international human-rights standards, the rule of law, and the primacy of non-violence. Our operational stance is simple: protect communities, protect truth, and protect the democratic pathway to self-determination.

We recognise that public safety improves when decisions are made closer to the people they affect. Independent national frameworks allow tailored security, resilient supply chains, and accountable emergency planning—while maintaining cross-border cooperation where it best serves citizens. Our commitment is to help build the capabilities, protocols, and professional standards that such a transition requires—calmly, competently, and transparently.
