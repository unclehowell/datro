Title and legal cautions

VG41 deadline and 31 December 1969 transfer

The chat reports an application deadline of 2 January 1970, two days after the reported WGR-to-BP Pension Trust transfer dated 31 December 1969. The date proximity alone proves nothing about manipulation or invalid registration. Tab's own counter-check says the registration authority could register a green without a private application. The Wiki already records the later VG41 objection period, 1 May 1970–31 July 1972, in EV-0263.

WA231076 versus WA240304 — Forms 1E and 1C

The chat reports a Form 1E / Form 1C comparison and a reference to Wontner's Guide (1975), p.174. This does not prove that BP lacked a deed or made a false application. Obtain both full first-registration applications, declarations, title plans and supporting documents; verify the practice-book passage before drawing a conclusion.

Smith v Molyneaux [2016] UKPC 35

The legal-critique site already carries the correct caveat: the Privy Council dealt with a British Virgin Islands case, the parties accepted the relevant proposition, and the Board held permission need not be acknowledged. It is persuasive, not binding, in England and Wales; it does not amount to general approval of every aspect of the 1987 Buckler judgment. Official case record.

1940–42 woodland transfer — do not conflate parcels

The chat reports an offer of approximately 109 acres of Leckwith Woods to Cardiff in 1940 and completion in April 1942. The current working land-transfer assessment treats the deed and plan as decisive: no link to the Great House Farm 1916 tenancy has been established, and any overlap with A-North remains unproved. Keep the reported 1942 Cardiff transfer separate from the later woodland-title chain unless instruments and mapped boundaries show that the same land is involved.
