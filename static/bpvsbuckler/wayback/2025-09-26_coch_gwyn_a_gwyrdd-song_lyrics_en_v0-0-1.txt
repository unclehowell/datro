Coch Gwyn a Gwyrdd Song
Welsh to English Translation
Version 1.0 - 26 SEP 2025

[Verse 1]
Coch yw'r llyw y ddraig a lynwyd (Red is the color of the dragon I drew)
Ac au wyrdd a gwyn fy rhoddwyd (and it's green and white I gave [it])
hon yw'r banner. Cymru gyfau (this is the banner. Wales gather)
codwyn nhi yn ywch ei hofran (lift it up high and wave [it, we will])

[Chorus]
Rhydd, Rhydd nawr fydda'r nod (Free Free now will be the goal)
Codwyn y baner i chwyfio (Picking up the flag to wave [it])
A'r rhyddid au'r cymru ar ddod (And rushing to Wales they'll come)
ay'r WARY hyd yn nawr, wedi githro (And even the Wary until now, were shaken)

[Verse 2]
Coch oedd llyw y gwead y gwallt wyd (Red was the color of the old hair)
A ddros Cymru "WHEN WE" collwyd (And throught Wales "WHEN WE" lost)
Ar ei derwau gwyrdd y'r "HAIR DIE" (And on the Oak, green from the hair die)
Gwaed o gyrf ei'n dadau (Blood from the bodies of our fathers)

gyda rhybudd llwyr y gymru (With a warning i.e spoon of Wales)
