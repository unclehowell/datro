BP vs Buckler 1987

Williams Family View vs Bute/BP Estate View

Title Dispute Highlights

1667
Williams View: Hereditary Ownership Acquired

Family "acquired" Great House Farm as purchase / fee farm / 999-year lease / Court Baron admission. Treated as absolute ownership for 300+ years. Gentry stewards of monastic "Court".

1667
Estate View: Manorial Lease Only

Herbert/Pembroke/Bute records: simple tenancy/lease. Superior lordship always retained. No freehold conveyance ever recorded.

1916
Williams View: Bureaucratic Tidying

Yearly agricultural tenancy is paperwork on top of Daniel Thomas equitable title (1895–1905 quarrying deal). Family still believes they own the land.

1916
Estate View: New Formal Tenancy

Bute grants fresh yearly agricultural tenancy to John Williams. Family now documented tenants only.

1938–1965
Family Betrayal Branch

Frederick Buckler + eldest son allegedly engage solicitor privately, sell title behind Mary Williams’ back. “It’s all dealt with legally.” Mary continues asserting ownership in 1974. Rift surfaces after her 1983 death.

31 Oct 1974
Williams View: Identity Fraud – Licence Rejected

Letters addressed to fictional “Mrs Buckler” (Mary never used husband’s name). Explicit rejection in press & court. Adverse possession continues.

1974
Estate View: Unilateral Lifetime Licence

BP Pension Trust & BP Properties send letters. Silence = acceptance. Possession now permissive. Clock stops (1987 Court of Appeal confirms).

31 Jul 1987
Williams View: Ancestral Title Erased

BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2. Licence to a dead woman defeats 320-year claim. “Fraud unravels all” never tested.

1987
Estate View: Licence Defeats Adverse Possession

Court of Appeal (Dillon LJ): Unilateral permission stops the clock. Title never extinguished.

6 Dec 1988
Williams View: Cultural Crime

Pregnant mother + 3 infants evicted by 25 officials. 800-year house (over Roman villa + 1,026 burials) demolished overnight.

1988
Estate View: Lawful Clearance

BP enforces Court of Appeal order. Site cleared for Church View Close housing.

1970s–1988
Public Perception Branch

1970s: Strong support – tree planting, 1,700-signature petition, open day, local press sympathy (“400-year family home”). 1987–88: Shifts to quiet acceptance after court ruling. No national scandal, but lasting local memory of injustice.
