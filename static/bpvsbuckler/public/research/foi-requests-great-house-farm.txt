REQUEST 1 — National Library of Wales (Aberystwyth)
================================================================================

To: The National Library of Wales
    Llanbadarn Fawr
    Aberystwyth
    SY23 3BU

Freedom of Information Act 2000 — Request for Information

Dear FOI Officer,

I am requesting information under the Freedom of Information Act 2000
regarding the Bute Estate Records held at NLW (fonds GB 0210 BUTE).

My name is Sion Buckler, Retired Sergeant, residing at Robin Hill,
Dinas Powys. I am writing on behalf of the late Mary Williams, formerly
of Manor of Llandough, Vale of Glamorgan, and on behalf of all other
heirs and successors of the Williams/Buckler Estate.

Specifically, I request:

1. Any surviving documents relating to the tenancy or conveyance of
   Great House Farm, Llandough (near Penarth), Glamorgan, between the
   Marquess of Bute (or his trustees/agents) and any member of the
   Williams family, particularly:
   - A tenancy agreement dated February 1916 granting a yearly
     agricultural tenancy to John Williams
   - Any prior conveyance, agreement, or document predating 1916 that
     might establish John Williams' claim to the farm
   - Any documents relating to an alleged agreement between Daniel
     Thomas (& Son) of Llandough and the Williams family regarding
     quarrying rights and eventual title to Great House Farm, reportedly
     dated circa 1895–1905

2. The reason why manorial records for the Manor of Llandough (series
   within GB 0210) appear to end in 1847, and whether any post-1847
   manorial records for Llandough exist in any other NLW collection or
   were transferred elsewhere.

3. Any NLW records (including but not limited to: Bute Estate
   correspondence, legal papers, family settlements, or estate
   administration files) that document the relationship between the
   Bute estate and the Williams family at Great House Farm, Llandough,
   in the period 1890–1940.

4. Whether the 1938 sale of Great House Farm by Stephenson & Alexander
   (auctioneers) for £5,500 is documented in NLW holdings, and if so,
   to whom the property was sold and what legal basis (freehold
   reversion, lease, or other) was conveyed.

Please confirm in writing what information you hold, what you are able
to disclose, and any applicable fees under Section 12 of the FOI Act.

Yours faithfully,

Sion Buckler Esq. (Ret'd Sergeant)
Robin Hill
Dinas Powys

On behalf of the late Mary Williams,
Manor of Llandough, Vale of Glamorgan

2. HOW TO SEND:
   - Most authorities accept FOI requests by email (look for "FOI" or
     "Information Rights" on their website) or by post.
   - Send all 6 simultaneously — they go to different authorities.
   - Keep copies of everything sent and note the date — the 20-working-
     day response clock starts from receipt.

3. FEES: Most individual FOI requests to archives and councils cost
   nothing or under £10. The Section 12 fee limit is £660 total across
   all requests to one authority.

4. REFERENCE NUMBERS: When you receive responses, note the reference
   numbers and file them with the research documents.

5. YOU WILL NEED TO OBTAIN: The HM Land Registry title number for Great
   Great House Farm. This can be found via:
   - https://www.gov.uk/search-property-information-land-registry
   - Or from a conveyancer, or potentially from the BP v Buckler
     judgment itself (the case reference mentions first registration
     in November 1982).
