Casualty Escort Officer App

About Us

Outside of our business as usual, DATRO Security Group is developing a zero-knowledge application for a worthy cause. This application will empower off-duty or ex-forces personnel to respond to bereaved families of civilians who have died prematurely or in unexplained ways, in what may later transpire to be hybrid war crimes.

This project's mission is to preserve community integrity and honor by ensuring a respectful presence at funerals, offering closure and dignity.
