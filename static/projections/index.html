Company Roadmap

Introduction

Platinum Luxury Transportation Ltd. is a newly established, fully foreign-owned company managed to European standards. The share capital stands at GBP 5,000,000, divided into 5,000,000 outstanding shares valued at GBP 1 each.

The company is awaiting European Central Bank certification to issue bonds valued at USD 1,000,000 to raise capital from investors.

Operating at a much higher standard than typical businesses in the Dominican Republic, we are experiencing growing demand for luxury services. After successfully running smaller-scale car rental and transportation businesses for three years, we now aim to expand to meet the demand for luxury-oriented services.

We will provide luxury VIP transfers for 5-star hotels in Punta Cana, partnering with establishments such as the Hard Rock Hotel and Live Aqua Hotel. Fixed-term contracts will cover the entire vehicle fleet, guaranteeing a minimum 2-year period, renewable for an additional 2 years. This strategy will fuel accelerated growth.

Our Vision & Business Plan

Based on market demand, we prepared this business plan to enable dynamic growth and penetrate the lucrative VIP transportation market. The plan is divided by time horizon.

Short-Term Plan

6-12 months (Start: May 2024)

  • Vehicle Purchase: Acquire 4 high-end SUVs (Chevrolet Suburban/Tahoe, Lincoln Navigator, Cadillac Escalade).
  • Partnerships: Sign initial contracts with Hard Rock Hotel Punta Cana and Live Aqua Hotel.
  • Banking: Select a primary banking partner to build credit history and secure future financing.
  • Operations Hub: Establish a back-office in the Uvero Alto area with parking, offices, and a car wash.
  • Capital Raising: Launch the first bond issue of USD 1,000,000.
  • Financial Stability: Accumulate capital to repay startup investors and transition to bond/bank loan financing.

Medium-Term Plan

1-2 years

  • Fleet Expansion: Purchase 8 additional luxury vehicles and 1 armored vehicle for VIP clients.
  • Brand Merger: Incorporate an existing car rental company under the Platinum brand.
  • Diversification: Renew rental fleet with 8 SUVs/2 convertibles and add 6 ATVs, 4 buggies, and tour buses.
  • New Venture: Establish a subsidiary for importing and selling vehicles from the USA.
  • Financing: Issue a second wave of bonds worth USD 1,000,000.
  • Corporate Structure: Unify all business segments under a single comprehensive brand.

Long-Term Plan

2-5 years

  • Headquarters: Purchase land in Punta Cana to build a company headquarters with offices, a showroom, service center, and more.
  • Asset Monetization: Lease parts of the premises to third parties to increase cash flow.
  • Major Funding: Issue 3rd, 4th, and 5th bond waves totaling USD 3,000,000 for construction.
  • Financial Transition: Fully transition from bond financing to bank financing, refinance the HQ, and sell shares to private investors for long-term stability.

Guarantees & Investment Protection

Depending on the total amount of the provided investment, we will be able to offer the investor various types of guarantees:

Vehicle Registration

Procured vehicles can be registered in the investor’s name, with title directly issued. We also offer a notarial custody option against the title of the car registered under the company's name.

Real Estate Guarantee

A real guarantee in the form of land located in a very lucrative location of Las Terrenas of equal value of the provided investment. The total value of the land allocated for this purpose is over USD 1,000,000.

Company Shares & Bonds

Company shares and bonds guarantee in the total amount of USD 1,000,000.

Upon conclusion of the investment period, the principal investment amount will be paid back in full. Interest is scheduled to be paid quarterly throughout the whole investment period.

In case of continuous collaboration after a 24-month investment period, we are open to negotiating the purchase of the company's shares or the purchase of bonds issued by the company.
