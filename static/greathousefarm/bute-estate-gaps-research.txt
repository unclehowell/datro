# BUTE ESTATE GAPS — Focused Research Document
# Great House Farm, Llandough-juxta-Penarth
# Compiled: 12 May 2026
# ======================================================================


## 1. THE DOCUMENTARY GAP: 1895–1938

The Bute Estate Records contain a critical lacuna for the Llandough/Great
House Farm area spanning approximately 1895–1938. The February 1916
yearly agricultural tenancy granted by the 4th Marquess of Bute to John
Williams falls squarely in the dead centre of this gap.

No single document in any public archive currently explains:
  - How the Williams family's occupation was regularised in 1916
  - Whether a conveyance of freehold or leasehold title preceded the tenancy
  - What role Daniel Thomas (& Son) played in the transfer of equitable title
  - Why the 1938 reversion sale to Western Ground Rents is not reflected in
    the estate's own archives at NLW


## 2. SPECIFIC GAPS BY RECORD SERIES

### 2.1 Manorial Records (NLW GB 0210 BUTE)
Coverage: [?1373]–1847
Nothing survives after 1847. The Manor of Llandough records end nearly
50 years before the 1916 tenancy. No post-1847 manorial records for
Llandough have been located in any NLW collection or transferred elsewhere.

### 2.2 Glamorgan Estate Rentals (NLW GB 0210 BUTE)
Coverage: 1728–1893
Rentals cease in 1893, three years before the 1896 event (if any) that
might have regularised Williams' position. No rental survives for the
1916 tenancy grant.

### 2.3 Cardiff Estate Rentals (NLW GB 0210 BUTE)
Coverage: 1842–1895
A slightly later end date than the main rentals, but still 21 years
before the 1916 tenancy.

### 2.4 Estate Correspondence (NLW GB 0210 BUTE)
Coverage: 1787–1910 (mainly 1817–1863)
Correspondence effectively ends in 1910, six years before the 1916
tenancy. The estate's own administrative silence on Llandough from
c.1895 onwards is itself significant — it suggests either the property
had been informally conveyed or that the estate no longer considered it
worth recording.

### 2.5 Family Settlements (NLW GB 0210 BUTE)
Coverage: [early 17th cent.]–1902
No settlements after 1902. The 3rd Marquis died in 1900; estate
administration under the 4th Marquis may not have generated formal
settlements for minor properties like Great House Farm.

### 2.6 Family Probate Records (NLW GB 0210 BUTE)
Coverage: 1612–1900
Nothing after the 3rd Marquis's death. The probate gap mirrors the
settlements gap.

### 2.7 Debenham Tewson – Bute Records (GB 0214 DBDT)
Coverage: 1849–1976
The DBDT58 sub-fonds for Llandough contains only:
  - DBDT58/1 (1883): Lease to Richard Price
  - DBDT58/2 (1883): Lease to John Hallett
Both describe "land and premises situate in Llandough Street, Cardiff" —
NOT Great House Farm itself.
There is NOTHING in the Debenham Tewson collection for 1916.

### 2.8 Glamorgan Deeds — Williams and Williams, Solicitors (GB 0210 E)
Sub-fonds of deeds covering 1698–1905. Contains deeds relating to
properties in Glamorgan. **Potentially** contains Williams family
conveyances, but requires on-site investigation at NLW Aberystwyth.

### 2.9 E. Williams of Hove Collection (GB 0210 EWIAMS)
Covers 1437–1823, including Glamorgan premises 1612–1775. May contain
early Williams family conveyances. Requires on-site investigation.

### 2.10 D. Gethin Williams Collection (GB 0210 DGETHWIL)
Covers 1687–1889, deeds relating to properties in Carmarthenshire and
Glamorgan. May contain relevant Williams family conveyances.
Requires on-site investigation.

### 2.11 National Archives GB 0214 Series
  - DA: Well-documented, including WGR's £5m acquisition of Mountjoy Ltd
    leasehold interests in 1938
  - DRA: Contains Daniel Thomas assignments (DRA 418-420, 1906-1907)
    and Williams family deeds (DRA 5/31, Thomas Williams of Glôg,
    1753–1889)
  - DSA: Stephenson & Alexander auction records. DSA/6/1158 confirmed
    as Llandough-juxta-Cowbridge, NOT Llandough-juxta-Penarth


## 3. THE "TWO LLANDOUGHS" PROBLEM

Two distinct parishes share the name "Llandough":

  A. Llandough-juxta-Penarth (near Cardiff/Penarth) — the Williams
     family's Great House Farm
  B. Llandough-juxta-Cowbridge (near Cowbridge) — contains Llandough
     Castle, the Cowbridge and District Local History Society papers,
     and the Thomas family of Great House Farm, Llandow

Confusion between these two locations pervades several archival
references and has led to at least one misidentified catalogue entry.
The Stephenson & Alexander sale DSA/6/1158 (£5,500, 1938) relates to
the Cowbridge Llandough, not the Penarth Llandough.

Cadw listings in the area:
  - LB 13420: Great House, Llanquian Road, Llandough (near Penarth) —
    Grade II. A SEPARATE property from the Williams farm.
  - LB 13271: Ty Mawr (Great House), Aberthin, Llantwit Major — Grade II*.
    Different property — do not conflate.
  - LB 13408: Llandough Castle, Llanfair — Grade II*. The nearby castle.

## 6. THE FEE SIMPLE ABSOLUTE QUESTION

The family's claim to fee simple absolute title rests on four propositions:

  1. The 1667 Williams occupation (family oral tradition, acknowledged in the
     1987 judgment as long-standing)
  2. The Daniel Thomas equitable title arrangement (c.1895-1915) — quarrying
     rights in exchange for eventual equitable title
  3. The 1916 Bute tenancy — documented ONLY in ¶36 of BP v Buckler
  4. The 1938 reversion sale to WGR — documented in the judgment and National
     Archives

The critical question: did the 1895–1916 period see either:
  (a) a conveyance of freehold title to the Williams family (possibly via
      Daniel Thomas), or
  (b) the 1916 tenancy being a regularization of an already-existing
      freehold position?

Without the missing deed documents, this remains unproven in law.
