An Open Letter to His Majesty King Charles III

On the Matter of National Transformation in the Age of Artificial Intelligence

“We shape our tools, and thereafter our tools shape us.” – Marshall McLuhan

Your Majesty,

I write not as a subject demanding favour, nor as a rebel denying your sovereignty, but as a citizen of these Isles compelled to speak plainly and constructively about the future of our governance. In this age of upheaval—technological, ecological, and moral—we face not just a crisis of leadership, but a crisis of imagination.

I propose a new model for Britain. Not to overthrow the Crown, but to revitalise its meaning in the 21st century. I call this vision The Assembly of Champions.

Imagine a Parliament repurposed. Not filled with career politicians embroiled in party whips and ideological echo chambers, but with citizen-technologists—the modern-day hunter-gatherers. These are the architects of the new digital commons, the builders of AI tools, the writers of code that feeds, heals, and defends our people.

These Champions are not elected to speak on our behalf, but to build on our behalf. Their charge is not to legislate, but to generate value—through innovation, decentralised AI systems, and new economies that return their surplus to the very communities that sent them.

In this system:

  • A constituency selects its most capable innovator—its Champion.
  • That Champion enters Parliament not to debate, but to deploy tools, forge smart contracts, and engineer wealth for their region using sovereign AI infrastructure.
  • The surplus generated—be it energy, code, intellectual property, or microeconomies—is returned directly to the community.

It is not utopia. It is not post-democracy. It is post-industrial representative agency, where merit and community trust determine who rises to serve—and where artificial intelligence is bound in service of a humanist, decentralised Crown.

Your Majesty, Britain needs a new fire. Let Parliament be its forge. I request the convening of a public forum under royal patronage to explore this model with experts, thinkers, and citizens alike.

With deep respect,
Sion Buckler
