Platform Overview & How It Works

The DATRO Platform operates as a collaborative, commission-sharing development environment. The core workflow is as follows:

📣 Campaign Launch DATRO publishes one active Campaign at a time
→
🤖 AI-Assisted Build Contributors use AI agents to vibe-code contributions
→
🔀 Pull Request Commits filed with contributor Account ID tag
→
📊 Live Tracking Client's app carries DATRO Tracking ID; attribution collected

💷 Commission & Share Client pays; DATRO distributes Revenue Share by attribution

Revenue Share & Payment

10.1 Revenue Share Pool

DATRO will publish, for each Campaign, the percentage of net Commission that will form the Revenue Share pool available to Contributors. "Net Commission" means Commission received from the Client less any applicable taxes, payment processing fees, third-party licensing costs, and DATRO's platform fee.

10.2 Distribution Methodology

Revenue Share is distributed based on Attribution Data (see Section 11). Only Merged Contributions that are demonstrably linked — via the Tracking ID — to business outcomes during the Campaign measurement window are eligible for Revenue Share.

Scenario Revenue Share Eligibility
PR submitted but not merged Not eligible
PR merged but no attributed conversions Not eligible
PR merged and attributed conversions recorded Eligible — proportional to attribution weight
Client does not pay Commission Not eligible regardless of attribution

10.3 Attribution Weighting

DATRO uses Attribution Data to assign a weight to each Merged Contribution. The precise weighting methodology (e.g. last-touch, multi-touch, time-decay, or a proprietary model) is determined by DATRO and may vary by Campaign. DATRO will publish the applicable methodology in the Campaign Brief.
