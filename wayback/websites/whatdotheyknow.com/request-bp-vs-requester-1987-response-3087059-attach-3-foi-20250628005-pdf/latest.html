Your request has been handled under the FOIA.

The MOJ does not hold any information in the scope of your request. This is
because there is no legal or business requirement for MoJ to do so.

A search was conducted regarding correspondence with Alun Michael MP to the
Lord Chancellor in 1988 which yielded no results. A search was also conducted for
internal records related to BP Properties vs. [requester], Great House Farm, or the
eviction thereof; however, the only record held is for the Court of Appeal case, which
is also publicly available-
Termination of Adverse Possession Through Unilateral Licenses: Insights from BP
Properties Ltd v. [requester] ([1987] EWCA Civ 2): England and Wales Court of Appeal
(Civil Division) | CaseMine

In line with legislation the Lord Chancellor’s Department’s 1988 policy records would
have been destroyed.

As background, records are reviewed for permanent preservation at 20 years. The
year 1988 was reviewed in 2013 which was during the transition period, where the
permanent preservation time was reduced from 30 years to 20 years.

Ministerial correspondence on its own has a shorter retention, at present the current
retention period is 1 year.

The FOIA does not oblige a public authority to create information to answer a
request if the requested information is not held. The duty is to only provide the
recorded information held.
