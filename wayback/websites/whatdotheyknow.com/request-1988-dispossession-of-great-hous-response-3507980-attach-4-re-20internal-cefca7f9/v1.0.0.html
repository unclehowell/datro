Dear Information Rights Compliance Team,

I am writing to request an internal review of FOI2026/09019, and to formally clarify that this correspondence should also be treated as a Subject Access Request under UK GDPR (Article 15) insofar as it relates to my personal data.

1. Dual legal basis (FOIA + UK GDPR)
My original request concerns an incident in which I was a child directly present in the household at the time of the 1988 eviction/dispossession. This engages not only FOIA considerations but also my right of access to personal data under UK GDPR, including any information from or about me held within MOD systems, historical records, liaison files, or operational documentation.

Accordingly, please ensure this review is handled with the correct distinction between:
FOIA exemptions relating to third-party or operational sensitivity, and UK GDPR obligations regarding personal data concerning a data subject (myself) Where information is mixed personal and third-party data, I expect proper consideration of redaction rather than blanket refusal or “no information held” responses where records exist.

2. Anticipated issue: “No information held”
Your response states that no information is held for the non–section 23 parts of my request.
Given the nature of the event described (a coordinated enforcement operation involving multiple police officers), this conclusion requires clear evidential support.
Please confirm whether the search performed included:
MACA (Military Aid to the Civil Authorities) liaison records and requests Regional military headquarters or district command diaries (Wales / Western regions, circa 1988) Civil contingency planning files involving police support or cross-agency assistance Defence–Home Office / Welsh Office liaison correspondence streams Archived operational logs later transferred to The National Archives (including DEFE/WO series material) If these were not explicitly searched, please explain why they were considered out of scope.
A conclusion of “no information held” is only reliable if searches were targeted, reasonably comprehensive, and capable of identifying transferred archival holdings, not limited to centrally indexed MOD FOI repositories.

3. Anticipated issue: “Section 23(5) neither confirm nor deny”
I acknowledge the application of section 23(5) in relation to Special Forces information.
However, I request that the internal review confirms:
Whether the “NCND” position was applied narrowly to Special Forces only, or Whether it has been used in a manner that may have inadvertently limited wider searches across non-exempt MACA or civil support records It is important that section 23 is not treated as a proxy limitation on the scope of the entire request, as my request explicitly extends beyond Special Forces involvement.

4. Adequacy of search (core issue)
The key issue for review is not exemption alone, but whether the search strategy was capable of identifying relevant historical operational liaison records at all.
Given the passage of time (1988), I would expect a reasonable approach to include:
Historical / archived command records
Decommissioned or transferred MACA documentation Cross-referencing of police support operations involving military liaison Keyword and event-based archival searches rather than only current MOD holdings If none of these methods were used, please set out the reasoning for limiting the search methodology.

5. UK GDPR – personal data scope
As I was a child present during the events described, I formally request confirmation of:
Whether any MOD-held records contain personal data relating to me Whether any such data has been withheld under FOIA exemptions without assessment under UK GDPR Whether a separate Article 15 disclosure search has been conducted or will now be initiated If necessary, I request that this part of my request be processed independently as a SAR with appropriate identity verification steps.

6. Requested outcome of internal review
For clarity, I am seeking a review that specifically determines:
Whether the MOD’s search strategy was sufficiently wide to capture historical MACA / civil support records Whether “no information held” remains a supportable conclusion after inclusion of archived and transferred record series Whether FOIA and UK GDPR obligations have been correctly separated and applied Please confirm receipt of this request for internal review and confirm whether it will also be logged as a Subject Access Request under UK GDPR.

Yours sincerely,

[requester] (Ret'd Sgt)
