Postcode breakdown

Postcodes are made up of two parts. The part before the space (CF11) is called the outward code. The part after the space (8EQ) is called the inward code. The letters C, I, K, M, O and V are not allowed in the inward code.

The postcode can also be broken into four parts:

  1. Postal area: CF which means Cardiff.
  2. Postal district: CF11.
  3. Postal sector: CF11 8.
  4. Postcode unit: EQ.

The regional mail centre for the post area CF is the South West Distribution Centre, located at Western Approach, Distribution Park, Severn Beach, BRISTOL, BS35 4GG.
