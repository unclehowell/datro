St Augustine, Penarth

Penarth Ministry Area
Share the page

Information

The main Sunday service is quite formal but also relaxed, dignified and friendly. The liturgy is carefully planned with music that is inspiring and uplifting. The clergy aim to give challenging sermons that both educate and inspire.

There is a Sunday School, please see the newsletter for more details.

Service times and events

Service times

Sunday 10.45am Sung Eucharist
Wednesday 10am Said Eucharist
