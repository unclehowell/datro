Early Medieval human remains

Skeleton of an adult female aged 17-25 years: skull, maxilla, mandible, rib,sternum, vertebrae, L and R clavicle and scapula, L and R humerus, L and R ulna and radius, pelvis, L and R femur, L and R tibia, L fibula.

Collection Area

Archaeology & Numismatics

Item Number

95.56H/1.727

Find Information

Site Name: Great House Farm, Llandough

Grid Reference: ST 1681 7331
Collection Method: excavation
Date: 1994

Notes: Plain grave. Arms crossed on pelvis. Above Sk956. Burial pebble.

Acquisition

donation, 28/7/1994
Should any artefacts be placed on display in any museum, then Ideal Homes Wales Ltd. should be acknowledged as the donor.

Material

Bone

In store
