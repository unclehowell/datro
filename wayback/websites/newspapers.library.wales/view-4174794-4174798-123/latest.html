I | INTERESTING TEST OF NEW I RESCUE APPARATUS, I A Barnsley correspondent gives some details 01 an interesting te.-A of artificial breathing apparatus for use in noxious atmospheres which took place at Normanton, near Leeds, on Saturday, March 23, at the West Riding Collieries of Messrs. Pope and Pearson (Limited). Here a gallery had been erected resembling the conditions of a roadway in a mine after an explosion, and arrang-aments were made for filling it with irreepira-ble air. In this gallery experiments were made under the direction of Mr. W. E. Gar- forth, managing director, in the presence oi four inspectors of mines and about 200 mining engineers. The apparati experi- mented with were the W-cg and the Drager, with each of which contrivances four men were equipped. Both parties remained in the gallery for over two hours, engaged in work such as would be demanded of rcecuera after an explosion. Both appliances were proved to be practical and successful, but the Drager was the less exhausting of the two.
