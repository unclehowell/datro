    WORK RESUMED AT GILFACH. GRIEYANCES TO BE DISCUSSED. A deputation from the workmen of the Gla- morgan Coal Company's Colliery at Gilfaeh Goch, who have been out since the beginning of September, waited upon Mr. Leonard Llewellyn on Tuesday afternoon and re- quested to be permitted to return to work pending the discussion of certain alleged grie- vances. The general manager consented, and at a general meeting of the men held on Wed- nesday morning it was unanimously resolved to resume work yesterday, it being intimated that Mr. Llewellyn had agreed to meet the men to discuss their grievances at a date to be arranged as soon as possible. The men gave notice to terminate contracts on the non-Unionist question on August 1st, but afterwards wished to discuss other griev- ances. The management, however, declined to entertain any proposals for a settlement until the men had returned to work, on the ground that to do so would not be in accord- ance with the procedure of the Conciliation Board. It is hoped that an amicable and permanent settlement of the matters in con- troversy will be arrived at.
