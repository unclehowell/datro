    OFF THE LINE. SINGULAR ACCIDENT AT BARRY DOCKS. An accident of an unusual character occurred at Barry Docks on Friday morning last. An engine and three empty coal trucks were passing over the crossing near No. 7 Tip, Low Level, Barry No. 1 Douk. the trucks being in front of the engine, when the foremost wagon left the metals, and ran about 30 yards along the footway, derailing the second truck in its course. After leaving the line, the front wagon also struck one of the telegraph posts, and cut it away to within a yard of the ground. The point workings were also damaged, and the ground cut up. Luckily, no one was injured, and with the aid of a couple of enginee the trucks were reinstated.

    COURT LEET AT LLANDOUGH. The Court Leet for the Manor of Llandough, Leckwith, Cogan, Cosmeston, and Walterstone was held on behalf of the Marquess of Bute at the Merry Harrier Hotel, Llandough, on Thursday, the 6th instant. Mr Wilson (from the office of Mr J. S. Corbett, solicitor to the Bute Estate) repre- sented the steward (Sir William Thomas Lewis). The Rev C. M. Barker, curate of Llandough, was foreman of the jury. The following were ap- pointed to the various offices :—Bailiff, Mr Jabez Cossletb, Bridge Farm, Leckwith constables- Leckwith, Mr F. Davies; Llandough, Mr John Thomas; Cogan, Mr D. Rees; Cosmeston, Mr Thomas Watts; and Walterstone, Mr William Hopkins pound-keeper, Mr Richard Ball; and hayward, Mr Hopkin Jenkins. At the conclusion of the business of the court, the company sat down to an excellent dinner provided by the host, Mr T. B. Clode. Mr Wilson presided, and several suitable toasts were honoured, that of the Lord of the Manor (Lord Bute) being received with enthusiasm. Songs were sung by the Chairman, Mr Harry Miller, and others.

    ASSAULT ON A BARRY DOCK POLICE SERGEANT. MARINE FIREMAN SENT TO GAOL I FOR A MONTH. Dock-police Sergeant Franks was on the plat- form of Barry Dock Railway Station at mid-night on Tuesday last, when he heard William Gow, a marine fireman, using abusive language towards one of the porters. On being spoken to the man turned on the officer, and struck him twice in the face.—Charged with assault, Gow was brought before the Penarth justices on Wednesday, and sent to prison for a month's hard labour.
