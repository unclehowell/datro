    BORN AT HONG KONG Reference was made a Mvt days ago to the rejection by the meroanWle marine authori- ties at Cardiff and Barry of alien seamen unable to cpeak and understand sufficient English to qualify for employment in British vessels. The rejections have given rise to a curious position. It is. of course, well known that persons of British nationality are exempt from the provisions of the new Act. Thus, any person if able to satisfy the autho- rities as to his place of birth or naturalisa- tion need know nothing of the English Language as far as the new Act is concerned. This provision was made in the interests of the coolie crews engaged in India and the Far East. Within the past few days quite a number of Chinese now at Cardiff and Barry have been rejected. Some, who were unable to speak much English, were allowed to pass on exhibiting previous discharges showing that their birthplace was Hong Kong. The result has been that a-s^rge number of Chinese have claimed to have been born in Hong Kong. The authorities, however, have met this by the simple test of examining prev-iouts discharges or engagement peepers. The rejection of these men has given no trouble, as plenty with a good knowledge of Engiish are available to man the vessels.
