Colonel t J. A. Hughes, C.B., occupied the chair, and during the afternoon the choruses Fillen Heroe's" and "Lead Kindly Light" were rendered by the Barry District Glee Society, under the leadership of Mr. D. Farr. The Chairman believed that the Brother- hood movement wa,s an excellent one, and wa;s doing great work throughout the coun- try. At Barry it appeared to people in a way ordinary religious services did not. It attracted people on account of its wideness. It was tending-not before it was needed- to widen their minds. The Lord Mayor, in the course of a well- I reasoned address, said they were perplexing their minds as to how to solve lifers problems, and how to make life bearable for themselves and others. What was life? he added. Some people thought that it was to make their thousands. Others considered that it was to make their way to the golf-links on Sunday— for the benent of their health. (Laughter.) Other people's ideal was to spend their week ends at the seaside, and others to while away their time in clubs. "The whole trouble," the Lord Mayor added, "is that we are indinnd to be selfish at heart, and we are not wont to change that spirit." His Lord- ship had great confidence in the Brotherhood movement, providing it was based on the teachings of the gospel of Jesus Christ. How were the workshops to be made better places for the men ? Some would say by the masters paying higher wages and sho-rter hours to work. This was wrong, the speaker added. Workshops could be made different places if the worker did more for his mate. The tendency of people at the present time was to palm off on someone else the duty that should be near to them. Commenting upon last year's drink bill of 160 millions. his Lordship added that with 26 milhonR more to this amount, Mr. Lloyd George cculd have paid all the taxes of the country. Also. that if that 160 millions had not been spent on drink, but handed over to the }'a''way companies, they would have !'een able to have conveyed people to tbedr destination for one year free of charge. Yet they ca)led themselves a Christian country. If you like your glaso, think of the man by the wayside who has fallen," the speaker added. Think of the sister who has lost her virtue when tempted by a. glass of wine by the villain who walked the streets of Piccadilly wearing kid jglovcs, and who called himself a gentleman. We want to get back to what is our duty, and if we only do that we shall soon alter the vices of society. The secret of life was for the per- son to deprive his or herself for the sake of others.
