Mr Buckler

Thank you for your email.

In relation to the "filtered schedule", as previously explained, Amgueddfa Cymru does not hold a separate recorded document identifying, item by item, which search results were excluded following review. The review was undertaken by staff using the records and search results identified during the handling of the request. No separate audit schedule, review log or equivalent record of exclusion decisions was created.

In relation to MS 3360/3, following further review we have confirmed that the page previously supplied does not relate to the property that was the subject of FOI 2026-036. While the document refers to a property known as "Great House", it relates to a holding in the Parish of Llanfair and not to Great House Farm/Tŷ Mawr, Llandough (near Penarth/Cardiff), which was the subject of your request. The document should therefore be regarded as outside the scope of FOI 2026-036.

We do not hold any further recorded information linking MS 3360/3 to Great House Farm/Tŷ Mawr, Llandough (near Penarth/Cardiff).

In relation to Ref 13.2, our position remains unchanged. The collections record describes the item as a "Waterwheel from Llandough Farm (opposite hospital)". No further location information has been identified within that record.

We have no further recorded information to provide in relation to these matters.
