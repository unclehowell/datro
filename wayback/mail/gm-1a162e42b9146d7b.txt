Dear Requester

Many thanks for your email received onto our system on 28 September 2026.

I will answer each question in turn


  1.  We do not have any occurrence books that cover that period and area. The Occurrence books<https://glamorgan.epexio.com/en/records/DSWP/1> only go as far as 1984. The Station logs and duty books<https://glamorgan.epexio.com/en/records/DSWP/3> end at 1979, and Divisional crime registers<https://glamorgan.epexio.com/en/records/DSWP/4> end in 1971.
  2.  We do have South Wales Constabulary Weekly Orders<https://glamorgan.epexio.com/en/records/DSWP/8/12>, including the one for 1988. These include a mix of regular operational and staff updates. These may include reference to evictions, however, as they include details of serving police members you will not be able to access these volumes. You may wish to commission a paid search of these items to determine if there is anything of interest.
  3.  We do not have any press office or public relations files for this period.
  4.  Publicly available information on accessions can be found on the collection level description on our catalogue. DSWP’s CLD can be found at South Wales Police Constabulary Records<https://glamorgan.epexio.com/en/records/DSWP>. This catalogue includes everything that was deposited by South Wales Police- you may wish to have a browse of the records to determine if there is  anything else of interest.

Best wishes

Katie
