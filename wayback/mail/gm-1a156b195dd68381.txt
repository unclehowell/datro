Dear Fahema,<br><br>Thank you again for your helpful reply of 10 August 2026 about the 1897 Marconi records, which I have been working through.<br><br>I have a second, separate enquiry about the same place, Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough-juxta-Penarth, Vale of Glamorgan (near Penarth). I am trying to establish from the records who held and occupied the property over time and what happened to its telephone service and the plant on the land.<br><br>Could you tell me whether BT Group Archives holds, or can point me to, any of the following for the period 1950 to 1990:<br>1. telephone directory entries for Great House Farm, Llandough (Cardiff and Penarth directories), with the years and the name in which the line was listed;<br>2. any wayleave agreement, consent or easement file for poles, overhead lines, cables or ducts on or across Great House Farm land, including the name or capacity of the person or company who granted it (for example, the owner or the occupier) and the date;<br>3. any record of the cessation or removal of the telephone service and plant at Great House Farm in 1988 or 1989, including who requested it, in what capacity, and the date;<br>4. any exchange, line plant or engineering records for Llandough, Leckwith Road or Penarth Road that show the farm;<br>5. if records of this kind are held by Openreach rather than by BT Group Archives, the right team at Openreach to ask, or I would be grateful if you could pass this part on;<br>6. if records of this kind for the period were not kept, or have been destroyed, the retention or disposal schedule that applied, if known.<br><br>I understand that BT is not subject to the Freedom of Information Act 2000, so this is a request for your voluntary help. <p>Dear BT Archives Team,<u></u><u></u></p>
<p>I am researching the General Post Office involvement in Guglielmo Marconi’s South Wales wireless telegraphy experiments during 1897, particularly the experiments between Lavernock Point and Flat Holm.<u></u><u></u></p>
<p>The Postal Museum has advised that the relevant GPO Telegraph Department and Engineering Department records are likely to be held by BT Archives. They confirmed that their own searches of Postal Museum holdings found no relevant records and that surviving
 telecommunications material from this period is likely to be within the collections transferred to BT Archives.<u></u><u></u></p>
<p>I would be grateful if you could advise on the relevant surviving records and catalogue references.<u></u><u></u></p>
<p>My research concerns:<u></u><u></u></p>
<ul type="disc">
<li class="MsoNormal">
Marconi’s 1897 South Wales wireless experiments;<u></u><u></u></li><li class="MsoNormal">
Lavernock Point and Flat Holm;<u></u><u></u></li><li class="MsoNormal">
Penarth and Llandough (near Penarth);<u></u><u></u></li><li class="MsoNormal">
the involvement of the GPO Telegraph Department and Engineering Department;<u></u><u></u></li><li class="MsoNormal">
any surviving correspondence, reports, notebooks, engineering records, plans, photographs or administrative records connected with the experiments.<u></u><u></u></li></ul>
<p>I am particularly interested in identifying any references to local residents or private individuals who assisted Marconi or GPO engineers. Family history suggests that a Mr Williams of Great House Farm, Llandough, may have assisted with the experiments

<p>Could you please advise whether your catalogue contains references to any of the following terms or variants:<u></u><u></u></p>
