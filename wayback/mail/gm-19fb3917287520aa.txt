Dear Mr Haste,

Thank you for your letter of 30 July 2026 (reference N610CGV) and for
identifying the surviving records held in relation to titles WA231076 and
WA240304.

I am not seeking to reopen HM Land Registry’s complaints procedure or to
ask for any previous decision to be reconsidered. This is a separate
request for clarification arising from the additional information in your
letter.

Your letter is the first to set out in detail which first-registration
records survive. It therefore enables a number of factual questions that
could not previously be asked with precision.

First, earlier correspondence referred to a 1971 abstract of title having
been lodged with the first registration applications. That document does
not appear as a separate item in the schedule you have provided. Could you
please confirm whether:

the 1971 abstract of title survives as a separate document;

it forms part of one of the listed items (and if so, which); or

it is no longer held.

Second, earlier correspondence referred to a certificate given with the
first registration application in which the applicant’s solicitors stated
they were “not aware of any question or doubt affecting the title”. Your
schedule identifies the surviving first-registration application as Form 1E
(item 13 for WA231076). Could you please confirm whether that certificate
forms part of the surviving Form 1E, and if not, identify the surviving
document in which it appears.

The purpose of this enquiry is solely to establish what information is
actually contained in the surviving first-registration records you have
listed.

Could you therefore confirm whether those records — including the
first-registration application, the A13/DL list of documents lodged, the
correspondence with Linklaters & Paines, any related applications, and any
surviving examiner’s notes, file notes, memoranda or internal
correspondence — contain any reference to:

Mary Williams (also known as Mrs Buckler);

Fred Buckler;

Ty Mawr;

Great House Farm;

any assertion by another person that they owned, occupied, or claimed an
interest in the land;

any ownership dispute, possession proceedings, litigation, or competing
claim affecting the land;

any issue, doubt, objection, caution, competing interest, or other matter
affecting title.

In particular, could you confirm whether any of the surviving
first-registration records contain any indication that another party
asserted ownership of, or claimed an interest in, the land before first
registration was completed for either title.

If any such reference exists, please identify the document or item number
in which it appears.

If no such references appear anywhere in the surviving records, a simple
confirmation of that fact would be sufficient.

If these questions cannot be answered without inspection of the documents
themselves, please confirm that and identify which specific documents are
capable of answering them, so that any Form OC2 application can be limited
to those documents alone.

This request concerns only the factual contents of the records identified
in your letter of 30 July 2026.

Thank you for your assistance. I look forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,


Requester 

 and Life Memeber
