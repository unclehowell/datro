>
> The Ministry of Justice (MoJ) is committed to protecting your personal
> data and keeping it secure. One of the key safeguards we use is verifying
> the identity of anyone who requests access to personal data. This ensures
> that only you can access your information through a SAR.
>
> To process your request, we ask that you provide two forms of
> identification.
>
>
>    1. *Photo ID* – This should clearly show your face, such as a passport
>    or driving licence.
>
>
>    2. *Proof of Address* – This should show your full name and current
>    address, such as a utility bill (e.g. electricity, gas, water,
>    telephone/broadband), council tax bill, or a bank/credit card statement.
>    These documents must be dated within the last six months.
>
> You can use your driving licence as proof of your current address, but
> you’ll still need to provide an additional photo ID (such as your
> passport).
>
>
>
> We do not use photo ID to confirm your likeness but because the process
> for obtaining photo ID involves additional safeguards that help us confirm
> your identity with greater confidence. This is a necessary and
> proportionate step to make sure your data remains secure.
>
> You can find more information about how we handle personal data in our Personal
> Information Charter
