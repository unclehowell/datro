LLANDOUGH HILL DRAINAGE ISSUES


**
------------------------------------------------------------
Dear Hywel

Water leaks on and around Llandough Hill continue to pose a significant problem.

As you'll recall, our community's pressure secured a subsurface drain to help prevent water emerging from the road surface.

The drain isn't visible from the road surface, so you may not have seen it. I've therefore included a photo below.
While the drain has helped reduce the problem significantly, issues remain.

For example, a leak on the Brook Green footpath has forced the Community Council to close the route.

This is problematic, as many residents of Willowmere and Llandough Hill who do not drive rely on the path to access bus stops on Penlan Road.

While the Community Council are responsible for this stretch of land, it's clear the Vale Highways Department's works may well have diverted the water leak onto Brook Green.

They must therefore play their part in resolving the situation.
https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1GH3Nzx1q3/
I raised the Llandough Hill water leaks again this week at the Council's Resources Scrutiny Committee.

An extra £17,000 has recently been allocated to flood risk management on Llandough Hill in the Council's capital budget.

The pressure our community placed on the Council resulted in this money being allocated.

But given the extent of the problem, I question if it is enough. You can view my comments in the meeting below.

https://www.facebook.com/share/v/1BwTiNDGbZ/

Preventing water leaks, and their associated impacts such as damaging road surfaces and posing an ice risk, is a key part of Our Plan for Llandough.

Our efforts have secured investment that has helped reduce the problem. It would not have happened without us.

But there is still much more to do.

We must double down our efforts and work together to resolve the problem once and for all.

Community Hero: Rachel Green

You may have seen Llandough resident Rachel Green in the news this week, following her life saving heroics.

Rachel was on her way to the gym when she saw a man, Alan Hale, collapsed on the side of the road.

She immediately stopped her car and performed CPR, saving Alan's life.

Rachel has now been honoured by the Royal Humane Society with its Resuscitation Certificate.

It goes without saying her award is fully deserved and our whole community is exceptionally proud.

You can read more about Rachel's story by clicking the link below.
Read Rachel's story (https://www.penarthtimes.co.uk/news/26535649.woman-honoured-saving-mans-life-cpr-shock/)

Penarth Road slip hazard

Earlier this week, I received another complaint from a resident about the leak on Penarth Road.

Algae growing in the discharge is causing a slip hazard, posing a danger to pedestrians.

As you'll recall, this is not a Council manhole. It's the responsibility of BT.

I raised the slip hazard with the Council, who confirmed they're continuing to push BT to resolve it.

The Council have also provided assurances they'll carry out regular sweeping to clear the algae.

This is not sustainable in the long term, and the leak must be repaired.

I'll keep pushing all responsible authorities on this.

Our Plan for Llandough!


As your councillor, I'm working with you all to secure a better future for Llandough. Together, we have a plan to deliver for the village.

Our plan is making Llandough better by:

1. Sorting out the parking problems
2. Stamping out dog fouling
3. Repairing our roads
4. Stopping littering and flytipping
5. Delivering better sports facilities
6. Keeping the whole community involved
