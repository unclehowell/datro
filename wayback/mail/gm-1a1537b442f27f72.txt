Dear Sion Buckler

ATISN 26-0010

Thank you for your request which I received on 10 October. You asked for:

   1. The retention and destruction schedules (or equivalent policy documents) that
      applied, or still apply, to former Welsh Office / Welsh Government file series
      covering agricultural tenancies, land ownership or compulsory purchase, rural
      depopulation, or related policy in the period 1970–1997.
   2. Any recorded information identifying whether files in those series that would
      have related to Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr / Llandough-juxta-Penarth, or to
      the companies Western Ground Rents Ltd or BP Properties Ltd, were
      destroyed, transferred or retained, and if destroyed the date and authority.
   3. Confirmation of the repositories to which any surviving pre-1997 paper files
      from the relevant series were transferred (e.g. National Assembly / Welsh
      Government / The National Archives / other).
Our response

   1. All Welsh Government Retention Schedules, past and present, are available
      on the external website: Welsh Government retention and disposal schedule |
      GOV.WALES. Before 1997, the Welsh Office or other agencies would have
      had responsibility for areas affecting Wales and would have had their own
      retention schedules.

   2. Searches were carried out to find files mentioning the farm name and the
      companies listed in your request. No files were found and given the date
      range of your request, any routine records would have been destroyed in line
      with the corporate retention schedule applied at the time. Given the date
      range for the search, it is equally possible that records relating to this would
      not have been transferred to Welsh Government and disposition would have
      been carried out by a predecessor body.

   3. Material with historical significance from this period would have been
      identified using the Welsh Office’s Operational Selection Policy (available on
      TNA’s website: OSP 7 - Welsh Office 1979-1997). Historical material would
      then have been transferred by us to The National Archives (or a designated
      Place of Deposit) for permanent preservation at the appropriate time (this was
      after 30 years at that time, subsequently reduced to after 20 years for WG
      material from 2023 onwards). Records relating to Welsh Government
      property/deeds/leasing etc. are not transferred to TNA as they are not classed
      as “public records” like our business records are. These would be retained
      until the property is sold or lease agreements terminated.
