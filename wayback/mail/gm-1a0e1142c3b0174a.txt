Research enquiry — Bute Estate records GB 0210 BUTE / D153 / D219: Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough-juxta-Penarth — 1877 split and 1916 tenancy
Enquiries Service
The National Library of Wales

I am researching the title history of Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough-juxta-Penarth, Glamorgan, adjoining St Dochdwy’s churchyard (approx. ST 167 733).

Glamorgan Archives (Louise Hunt, ref. 2026/4097, 24 September 2026) has confirmed that the following references are yours, not theirs, and has directed me to you:

1. D153 — Deeds relating to premises in p. Llandough, co. Glam., comprising a dwelling house adjoining the churchyard, 1811 (D153/1–2), and land (D153 onwards, including items sometimes cited as D153/4–8).
2. D219 — Llandough manorial leases and agreements.
3. The main Bute Estate deposit, GB 0210 BUTE (deeds to 1937; Glamorgan / Cardiff rentals).

I do not ask you to adjudicate title. I ask only whether the following instruments exist in your holdings, and for catalogue references, dates, parties, acreages, and whether a plan is attached.

A. The 1877 division
Does the Bute deposit contain a conveyance, agreement, or other instrument of about 1876–1877 by which the Marquess of Bute (or trustees) conveyed or contracted to convey the farmhouse, buildings and about 10 acres adjoining the churchyard at Llandough to Daniel Thomas (or Thomas and another), while retaining about 9 acres of agricultural land to the east?

B. The 1916 agricultural tenancy
Does the deposit contain a yearly agricultural tenancy, or counterpart, of 1916 from the Marquess of Bute to John Williams of Great House Farm, Llandough?
If yes:
- does the parcels clause describe the whole farm, or only the land Bute still owned after 1877?
- is there a plan?
- what acreage is stated?

C. Rentals 1877–1936 for this farm
Do Glamorgan / Cardiff / Llandough rentals in GB 0210 BUTE distinguish, after c.1877, between:
- a house/buildings holding (Thomas / later Williams), and
- a residual Bute field holding later passed to Western Ground Rents in 1938?

D. Anything that would show the two holdings were treated as one unit again before 1938.

Purpose. HM Land Registry first-registered titles WA231076 (30 November 1982) and WA240304 (23 February 1983; later amalgamated). The 1982/83 files treat the land as one farm. The family case is that Parcel A (house and about 10 acres) had already left Bute in 1877 and was not the land let in 1916, so the 1982 merged title cannot be the same unit as the 1916 tenancy. Your deeds and rentals are the estate-side evidence that can confirm or deny that.

I live in Patagonia and cannot visit Aberystwyth in person in the short term. I would be grateful for:
- catalogue references and a short description of any matching items;
- whether copies can be supplied, and the cost;
- if the 1916 tenancy is not in GB 0210 BUTE, any note of where Bute tenancy counterparts after 1910 were deposited.

This is a new enquiry. It is not a repeat of FOI/2026/11. Glamorgan Archives hold the 1938–44 Western Ground Rents plan/rental book (D696) and have already answered on their own Bute material.
