Dear Daniel,

Thank you for your response concerning mail reference 93027237/01.

I note that Welsh Water's position is that no current records relating to
Great House Farm (Ty Mawr), Llandough have been located, and that Welsh
Water is unable to establish whether historical records existed, when they
ceased to be held, or whether any records were removed during retention
processes or migration from legacy systems.

For the purposes of my ongoing investigation, please clarify the following
points.

   1.

   You state that your enquiries “indicate that Great House Farm was
   demolished during the 1980s”. Please identify the source or record on which
   that statement is based, including any internal system, historical account,
   property record, mapping record or other source used to reach that
   conclusion.
   2.

   Please confirm whether Welsh Water searched any legacy systems, archived
   customer-account systems, paper archives, scanned records, historical
   billing systems, property databases, sewerage/water connection records, or
   other records dating from the period before the present operating systems.
   3.

   Please confirm the applicable retention periods relied upon in
   concluding that records from the relevant period would no longer be
   expected to remain available.
   4.

   Please confirm whether the statement that “not all historic information
   was transferred from legacy systems” applies to records of former
   properties and customer accounts generally, and whether Welsh Water has any
   documented migration or data-transfer policy governing the omission or
   destruction of such historic information.
   5.

   Please confirm whether Welsh Water holds any records identifying
   categories of information that were not migrated from the relevant legacy
   systems, even if the individual Great House Farm records themselves are no
   longer available.

I am not asking Welsh Water to recreate records which it does not hold. I
am asking for clarification of the evidential basis for the conclusions set
out in your response, particularly where your email confirms that Welsh
Water is unable to establish whether the historic records once existed or
how they ceased to be available.

Please retain this correspondence under mail reference 93027237/01.

Kind regards,

Requester
