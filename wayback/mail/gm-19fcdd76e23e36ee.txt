Dear Ms Owen,

Thank you for your email of 4 August 2026 and for confirming that a search
of your internal case management system has been carried out with no
results identified.

My request was not limited to whether records are currently held on that
system. It also sought confirmation of what searches (if any) were made of
historical paper records, indexes and finding aids, and what became of the
court records relating to the proceedings I identified.

You state that, due to the age of the case, the court would not currently
hold records "in line with our procedures." I should be grateful if you
would clarify the basis for that statement by addressing the following
points:

   1.

   What is the precise name of the internal case management system that was
   searched, and what period of records does it cover?
   2.

   Were any surviving paper finding aids or historical records examined,
   including cause books, order books, registers of judgments or orders,
   warrant books, index cards, or any other historical indexes maintained by
   Cardiff County Court, Cardiff Civil Justice Centre, or their predecessors?
   3.

   Were any archived or off-site repositories under HMCTS control
   considered or searched?
   4.

   Please identify the records retention, disposal or archival policy,
   schedule or instruction relied upon in concluding that records of this age
   would no longer be held. I note that the current publicly available County
   Court Records Retention and Disposition Schedule specifies relatively short
   retention periods for possession action cases. I should therefore be
   grateful if you could identify the historical policy, schedule or
   instruction that governed the records relating to these proceedings and, if
   possible, indicate when they would have been scheduled for destruction or
   transfer.
   5.

   Does HMCTS hold any administrative record of the destruction or transfer
   of the relevant files, such as destruction registers, certificates,
   transfer schedules or equivalent records?
   6.

   Were any records relating to these proceedings transferred to another
   repository, including The National Archives, Glamorgan Archives, or any
   other archive? If so, I should be grateful if you would provide the
   destination, date and any available reference.
   7.

   Even if the original case files no longer survive, do any registers,
   indexes or other administrative records remain recording the existence,
   progress or disposal of proceedings involving the Williams family, the
   Buckler family, Western Ground Rents Ltd, BP Pension Trust Ltd, BP
   Properties Ltd, or Great House Farm (Ty Mawr), Llandough?

The proceedings in question include the High Court possession proceedings
of 1955 (including enforcement on or around 4 July 1955), the Cardiff
County Court proceedings culminating in the possession order of 11 December
1962 before Judge Temple Morris QC, and the Cardiff County Court
proceedings of 1974, including the part-heard hearing of 3 July 1974, the
application for leave to enforce in September 1974, and the subsequent
correspondence concerning the proposed unilateral licence arrangement.

I have already been making enquiries of HM Land Registry. However, my
present request concerns the court's own records, the searches undertaken
in relation to those records, and any surviving administrative record of
their retention, destruction or transfer.

If the information requested is not held within the Enforcement Section, I
should be grateful if this enquiry could be forwarded to the appropriate
court manager, records officer or other HMCTS team responsible for
historical court records, and if you could kindly confirm that this has
been done.

Thank you for your assistance. I look forward to your substantive reply
addressing each of the points above.
