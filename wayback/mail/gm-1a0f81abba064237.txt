Dear Dr Wigley,

Thank you for your acknowledgement of ATISN 27589 and confirmation that a
response is due by 26 October 2026.

For the avoidance of doubt, I should clarify the two historical dates
reproduced in your acknowledgement:

“Possession was taken on 29 November 1988” and “Demolition of the farmhouse
began on 6 December 1988” were included in my request as contextual
information identifying the period and events to which the requested
records relate. Their repetition in the acknowledgement should not be
treated as an acceptance by me that either date, or any legal
characterisation of possession or demolition, has been established by the
Welsh Government or by me. This whole situation maybe unprecedented. It was
already a landmark legal case in 1987, without this added layer  we may now
be understanding.

My request is for recorded information held by the Welsh Government/Cadw
concerning the matters specified in points 1–3. I am not asking Cadw to
determine title, possession, limitation or any other legal issue, and
nothing in this FOI correspondence is intended to constitute an admission,
waiver, settlement or abandonment of any rights or position concerning
those matters.

Please also expressly confirm that records falling within the scope of
ATISN 27589, including any relevant historic listing, inspection,
photographic, administrative and file-disposal records, will be preserved
pending completion of the request and any applicable internal review or
complaint process.

I look forward to the substantive response by 26 October. And sincerely
thank you for your time and attention in this matter. You are appreciated.

Regards,

Requester
