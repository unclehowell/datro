   of an ownership dispute and the subsequent sudden destruction of the
   property in 1988, the building was incompletely understood.

   Ltd, followed the 1988 demolition by approximately a year. The application

   indicative scheme of 18 dwellings. The subsequent reserved-matters scheme
   approved on 3 September 1992 is for 20 dwellings.

   the churchyard and identified a medium-to-high potential area. What it does
   not establish by itself is the full extent of the archaeological remains
   subsequently encountered.
