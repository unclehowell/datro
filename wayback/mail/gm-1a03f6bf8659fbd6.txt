**Request: physical examination of DBDT79/1**

Catalogue entry:

Lease (counterpart) of land and premises situate in Pembroke Terrace,
Cardiff, from the Marquess of Bute to Mr John Williams, May 1877 [1
parchment, plan in margin].

Please examine the original document (not the catalogue description alone)
and confirm:

1. Parties and date – Does the original name the Marquess of Bute and Mr
John Williams, and is it dated May 1877?

2. Property description – Exact wording of the land and premises. Does it
expressly locate the property in Pembroke Terrace, Cardiff?

3. Llandough / other references – Any mention (body text, plan, schedule,
endorsements or annotations) of Llandough, Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr,
Western Ground Rents, or any locality outside Pembroke Terrace?

4. Marginal plan – Does the plan identify the location sufficiently? If
procedures allow, please supply a digital image or scan of the plan.

5. Further identifying details – Any adjoining owners, plot numbers,
parish, street numbers, measurements, previous ownership, rent,
consideration, or cross-references to other instruments.

If scanning is permitted, images of the parties clause, property
description and marginal plan would be ideal. Otherwise a transcription or
detailed description of those sections will suffice.


**Catalogue wording discrepancy**

Most other 1877 entries in the series describe the grantor as the “Trustees
of the Will of the late Marquess of Bute”. DBDT79/1 is catalogued simply as
“from the Marquess of Bute”.

From the original, please confirm:

- who executed the lease;

- the capacity in which the grantor is described;

- whether the Marquess is shown acting personally or through trustees;

- any evident reason for the difference from the other 1877 entries.

(I note the 3rd Marquess attained majority around this period, but I seek
the Archives’ evidence-based confirmation rather than assumption.)


**Purpose**

I am reconstructing the historical title position for Great House Farm / Tŷ
Mawr, Llandough, and need to distinguish any Cardiff property records from
those relating to the Llandough estate. Please report the physical contents
of DBDT79/1 separately from the catalogue description, noting any
differences.
