Since July 2024, Companies House have transferred to The National Archives or destroyed paper records for England and Wales companies dissolved between 1992 and 2003 to comply with the government’s stated retention policy and UK GDPR.

Companies House transfer records to The National Archives that meet the selection criteria in the Operational Selection Policy<https://cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk/documents/osp25-regulation-of-companies-final.pdf>. Any records not selected for permanent preservation are then destroyed. All paper records for companies dissolved before 1992 were destroyed many years ago in line with this long-standing policy.

By early 2024, Companies House had also disposed of microfiche records for companies dissolved over 20 years and transferred any selected microfiche records to The National Archives.

CH maintains an online ‘dissolved records index<https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/dissolved-search>’ for companies dissolved between 1989 and 2009 which includes company name (and any changes of name), number, date of incorporation and dissolution, and registered office address.
