Dear FOI Unit,

Under the Freedom of Information Act 2000 and/or the Environmental
Information Regulations 2004 (whichever regime the Council considers
applicable to the material), I am requesting information held in relation
to:

   - Planning application *89/01396/OUT* (outline permission granted 13
   March 1990); and
   - The subsequent reserved matters application *92/00671/RES*,

both concerning land at Great House Farm, Leckwith Road, Llandough,
subsequently developed as Church View Close.

I am seeking to reconstruct the documentary planning and consultation
record between the outline permission and the subsequent
detailed/reserved-matters approval.
1. Consultation responses to application 89/01396/OUT

The Council's own case officer's report on application 89/01396/OUT
records, in its consultations section:

"Cardiff Bay Development Corporation: No objections – however, the
Corporation would hope that the trees on the slope of the site facing
north-west are retained and that the public right of way which runs through
the site will be retained."

The same report refers to further consultation responses from the *National
Rivers Authority* and the *County Engineer* by reference to attached typed
sheets ("please type attached sh②" and "please type attached sh③"), without
reproducing their substantive contents in the disclosed copy.

Please therefore provide:

   1.

   The *complete original written response from the Cardiff Bay Development
   Corporation* concerning application 89/01396/OUT, including any
   accompanying letter, report, plan, attachment or other document forming
   part of that consultation response.
   2.

   The *complete National Rivers Authority consultation response* referred
   to in the report as one of the attached sheets, including the original
   document and any accompanying material.
   3.

   The *complete County Engineer consultation response* referred to in the
   report as one of the attached sheets, including the original document and
   any accompanying material.

I am deliberately not relying upon the handwritten numbering of sheets ②
and ③ to identify which sheet belongs to which consultee, as the numerals
are difficult to distinguish in the surviving copy. Please identify the
corresponding sheet/document by reference to its contents or file
provenance.

   4. Any *covering correspondence, transmission record or internal record*
   showing when the NRA and County Engineer responses were received by the
   Council and how those responses were placed on, incorporated into or
   considered as part of the planning file.

The officer's report for the outline application describes the indicative
scheme as *18 dwellings*, whereas the subsequent reserved-matters scheme
appears to comprise *20 dwellings*.

Please therefore provide:

   12.

   Any document held by the Council recording or explaining the change from
   the indicative *18-dwelling* scheme at outline stage to the *20-dwelling*
   scheme submitted/approved at reserved-matters stage.
   13.

   Any planning assessment, officer report, correspondence,
   committee/delegated report or other record explaining the planning basis

   14.

above for applications *89/01396/OUT and 92/00671/RES*.

Please search the relevant planning application files and associated
