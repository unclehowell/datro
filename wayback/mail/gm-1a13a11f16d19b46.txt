On Wednesday, our village experienced another incident.

Both authorities have failed to fulfill their duties to protect our community.

I've written to both authorities to raise serious concerns.

You can read my letter below.
FAO: South Wales Police
Vale of Glamorgan Council

8th October 2026


Dear both

Illegal firework usage in Llandough

I’m saddened to report that another firework related incident took place in Llandough last night.

This is impacting some of the most vulnerable members of our community, causing distress to the elderly, small children and animals.

It is apparent that those responsible for setting off fireworks are not residents and instead come into the community from outside.

A lack of action to date is sending a message to those responsible that their behaviour is acceptable and will be tolerated.

There have been several instances in which fireworks have been set off past 11.00pm at night, in direct contravention of the law. There may also be other laws and regulations that have been broken.

I would therefore be grateful if you would:

- Outline full details of all firework-related incidents in the Llandough ward reported to your respective authorities within the past 24 months;

- Confirm all actions that were taken following those incidents to address the problems reported;

- Outline all actions proposed by your authorities to prevent inappropriate firework usage in the Llandough ward in future;

- Confirm all measures available to your respective authorities to detain and apprehend the individuals responsible for inappropriate firework usage in the Community of Llandough as well as any restrictions that may be imposed to prohibit those individuals from entering the Community boundary; and

- Confirm details of any powers conferred on your respective authorities which enable you to introduce additional regulations or byelaws to impose stricter restrictions prohibiting firework usage within the Community of Llandough.

I have not copied this message to our PSCOs because they are not responsible for operational decisions, and as such it would be unfair to attribute blame to them for the failure to address this problem.

However, I reiterate my request for your respective authorities to meet with me at the earliest convenience to discuss this ongoing problem, with an invitation also extended to our PCSOs.

Thank you for your consideration of this matter, and I look forward to your prompt response.

Kind regards,

Cllr George Carroll
Elected member

Llandough ward
Vale of Glamorgan Council
I'm pleased to report both authorities responded promptly.

The Police have confirmed a Problem Oriented Police Plan is being developed in conjunction with our PCSOs.

The Inspector has confirmed he'll update me in due course, with further escalation promised if this proves insufficient.

The Council too have agreed to take part in a multi-agency meeting to address the problem.

The time for discussion has long passed.

We need to see surveillance and enforcement, so that those responsible for these disturbances know their behaviour will not be tolerated.

As a community, it's vital we keep up the pressure.

NEXT WEEK: Penlan Road
temporary traffic lights

Temporary traffic lights will be in place on Penlan Road on Tuesday 13th October to allow for Welsh Water works to be carried out safely.

They will operate from 9.30am to 2.30pm.

The lights will be situated outside the Memorial Hall, with Spencer Drive and Cogan Pill Road also impacted.

The bus stops outside the Hall will be suspended due to proximity to the works.

Access will be maintained for residents at all times.

If anyone has further questions, please let me know.

Please see below for further information.
https://www.facebook.com/share/v/1Eqf5imB1i/

Llandough Hospital meadow

I've received many enquiries about the Llandough Hospital meadow, access to which has been restricted.

I've contacted the Health Board about this, as I know many residents enjoy using this route.

They've confirmed it has been fenced off as a temporary measure following misuse of the site.

They do not intend to close it permanently, but have restricted access while safety arrangements are reviewed.

A fortnight ago, I requested a likely timescale.

The person I spoke with said they were unable to provide one at present, but said they'd request an update.

I've chased them on this.

If anyone has further questions, please let me know.

The meadow has been a good benefit to our community and I’m pressing for it to be opened again as soon as possible.

Penarth Road water leak

Problems like the Penarth Road water leak require different agencies to work together.

The manhole from which the water is emanating is BT's responsibility, but the fault causing the leak is Welsh Water's responsibility.

The Council is in charge of highway safety, so they must make sure the algae forming in the discharge is cleared to prevent a slip hazard.

All responsible bodies must work together to fulfil their responsibilities, rather than blame each other.

I emphasised this to the Council this week.
