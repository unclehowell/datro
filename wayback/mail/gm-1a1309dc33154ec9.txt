Thank you for your email.

In relation to your request for the recorded basis on which records were treated as outside the scope of FOI 2026-024, Amgueddfa Cymru does not hold a separate search log, file note, internal note or other recorded document setting out the rationale for that assessment.

The original response was based on searches which identified no records relating to the reported discovery described in your request, namely the alleged discovery of a soldier, horse remains and associated armour at Great House Farm, Llandough. While searches identified records relating to other archaeological finds from Church Farm and archaeological investigations at Great House Farm, staff concluded that those records did not relate to the specific discovery described in your request and therefore fell outside its scope. No separate record of that assessment was created.  We have since provided you with the records referred to as being from Church Farm.

No records remain withheld.
