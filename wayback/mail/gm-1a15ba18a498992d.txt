> > > The Welsh Government's statutory guidance for community and town councils recognises the importance of accessible meetings, public participation and transparency. It also requires councils to have regard to the guidance when exercising their relevant functions. The Act's section 48, inserting paragraph 27A into Schedule 12 of the Local Government Act 1972, requires the person presiding over an open community council meeting to give members of the public in attendance a reasonable opportunity to make representations about the business, subject to the statutory exception.
