Further to my clarification sent today, for the avoidance of doubt: William
Beverly Buckler, my late father, died at Llandough Hospital on 15 May 1992.
Information about a deceased person is not personal data under the Data
Protection Act 2018, s.3(2), so recorded information about him falls within
the scope of this FOI request.

I add, as a specific search lead, that he was reportedly taken from his
hospital bed at Llandough Hospital on 3 December 1988 to be charged with
assaulting two bailiffs, criminal damage, and wanton or furious driving.

Sion Buckler (Ret'd Sergeant)

> 3. Chronology and specific search leads

> The following events provide context for identifying any corresponding

> 29–30 November 1988 — possession and eviction
>
> The historical chronology records possession on 29 November 1988. A
> contemporaneous South Wales Echo report dated 30 November, titled “Angry
> scenes as farmer evicted” and attributed to Peter Bibby, describes the
> eviction sequence and reports that bailiffs entered the property and that
> the roof of an outbuilding had already been partly removed. The chronology
> also associates demolition of outbuildings with 30 November.
>
> There is therefore a date distinction between the recorded possession date
> and the newspaper’s account of the eviction. I cannot presently reconcile
> the dates. Please search across both 29 and 30 November, rather than
> treating either as an exclusive date for the relevant incident.
>
> 2–5 December 1988 — court proceedings and proposed access to belongings
>
> Contemporary reporting describes further court proceedings in the days
> before the farmhouse was demolished, including an arrangement under which
> access to collect belongings was reportedly permitted subject to conditions
> and pending a further hearing.
>
> The precise terms and procedural details should be verified from the
> original records rather than inferred from press summaries. This chronology
> may nevertheless help identify any police liaison, requests for assistance,
> public-order concerns or communications concerning access to the premises.
>
> 6 December 1988 — demolition
>
> The heritage record identifies 6 December 1988 as the demolition date. The
> archived historical chronology reports that a demolition team arrived in
> the early hours and that the farmhouse was demolished that morning.
>
> I emphasise that the reported arrival time is not evidence that police
> were present at that time. I am asking whether responsive police records
> exist, not inviting an assumption that an attendance or deployment took
> place.
>
> 9–13 December 1988 — discrepancy requiring verification
>
> A South Wales Echo report dated 10 December 1988 appears to state that
> William Buckler was released on bail the previous day, which would be 9
> December.
>
> Separately, a notebook attributed to William Buckler and retained in the
> family archive contains an account referring to police having “locked me up
> for 2 weeks” and to a search involving family antiques and the taking of
> documents. The entry is a family-held historical account; it is not, by
> itself, independently verified proof of detention, a search, seizure or the
> precise dates involved.
>
> If the notebook’s reference to two weeks from 29 November is literal, it
> would point to a date around 13 December. That does not readily reconcile
> with the newspaper’s reported release date of 9 December. I do not ask the
> Force to accept either account in preference to its own records. The
> discrepancy is a reason to check the relevant dates and any associated
> records.
