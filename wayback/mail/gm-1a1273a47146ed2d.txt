I hold the stage-one decision of 19 April 2026 and the stage-two decision of 25 May 2026. I also hold the schedule of 43 documents on the first-registration files released on 30 July 2026.

4. Whether the inclusion of the woodland (or any land beyond the 1938 conveyance) is regarded as a possible mistake capable of alteration or indemnity under the Land Registration Act 2002 and Practice Guide 39.
