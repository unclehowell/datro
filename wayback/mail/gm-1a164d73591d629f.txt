Dear Information Commissioner's Office,

Complaint under section 50 of the Freedom of Information Act 2000 — Ministry of Justice / HM Courts & Tribunals Service, MoJ ref IR FOI 260813002 (internal review outcome letter of 9 October 2026)

I ask the Commissioner to decide whether the Ministry of Justice dealt with my request in accordance with Part I of the Act.

The request and the dates
- Request (MoJ ref 260813002): recorded information about court proceedings in 1988–1989 (Barry Magistrates' Court and Cardiff) connected with the eviction and demolition at Great House Farm, Llandough-juxta-Penarth, Vale of Glamorgan, including (item 7) any retention, destruction, disposal or archive-transfer records for those court files, and (item 8) any file references, case numbers, register entries or archive references, even where the files themselves no longer survive.
- Original response: 12 August 2026, information not held.
- My request for internal review: 13 August 2026. The MoJ extended the review on 9 September 2026.
- Internal review outcome: letter of 9 October 2026, upholding "not held" under section 1(1)(a).

The points I ask the Commissioner to decide
1. Whether the MoJ made adequate searches. The review letter gives no description of any search: no system, no search term, no date range and no record series. Its conclusion rests on what "may have been" done with the records: that they "may have been destroyed or transferred to an approved place of deposit", and that it is "likely" they were disposed of or transferred.
2. Whether items 7 and 8 were considered at all. They ask for administrative records about the files (retention and disposal schedules, destruction or disposal certificates, transfer and accession records, register or index entries), not for the case files. Records of that kind are created when files are destroyed or transferred, and may survive when the files do not. The review does not give a held / not held answer for either item. Without a search for, and an answer on, items 7 and 8, the conclusion that the information is "not held" under section 1(1)(a) has not been reached on an adequate basis.
3. Whether the MoJ met its duty under section 16. The review suggests Glamorgan Archives "may hold" records transferred from HMCTS, but does not say whether any transfer list or accession record shows that court records for these courts and years were in fact transferred, or give any reference.
4. Timeliness of the review: asked on 13 August 2026 and decided on 9 October 2026.

What I ask the Commissioner to require
a. A held / not held answer for each numbered item, in particular items 7 and 8.
b. A description of the searches made for each item (systems, terms, dates and record series, including any destruction registers, transfer lists or court record indexes).
c. If any record was transferred to an archive, the receiving body and the reference.

I can supply copies of the request, both responses and the extension letter on request.

This is a records complaint only. It makes no allegation against any individual.
