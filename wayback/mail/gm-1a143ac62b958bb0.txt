Dear RICS,

I am researching Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough-juxta-Penarth, South Wales. The public working evidence catalogue is at https://greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com, with the title and chronology index at https://dartboard.bucklerfamily.estate and the long-term archive at https://wayback.bucklerfamily.estate.

The site passed through Western Ground Rents Limited (1938), the BP Pension Trust / BP Properties Ltd (1969), and was subject to possession proceedings culminating in demolition in November–December 1988. Valuation Office field books, National Farm Survey / CWAEC records and other survey material are already the subject of enquiries with The National Archives.

Please advise whether RICS holds any central or member records, practice archives, or guidance relating to surveyors or valuers who may have worked on this site between 1969 and 1993, and whether any preservation notice is appropriate for member files.
