Dear SPAB Archive team,

I am researching the history of Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough-juxta-Penarth, Vale of Glamorgan (approx. ST 168 733), the house beside St Dochdwy's Church. The family's recorded transcription of the index to an 1824 Bute estate survey (Glamorgan Archives, DB/E/1, plan 19) gives the name "Cedfin or Ty Mawr Farm". The house was demolished in late 1988. The South Wales Echo of 3 December 1988 ("History fight to save farm") reported that Cadw was considering spot-listing the farmhouse and that an inspector was due to visit that weekend.

Please could you check the online catalogue and your paper indexes for any casework file, correspondence or photographs about:

1. Tŷ Mawr / Great House Farm, Llandough-juxta-Penarth (also "Llandough near Penarth", "Llandough-juxta-Penarth Farm"); and
2. any notification, appeal or enquiry about the house, its threatened demolition or its possible listing in 1988–89, or at any earlier date.

If a file exists, I would be grateful for its reference, its date range, a short description of the contents (for example survey notes, photographs, correspondence with Cadw, the Welsh Office or the local authority), and how it may be consulted. I note the Archive is closed to visitors from 9 November 2026 to 4 January 2027.
