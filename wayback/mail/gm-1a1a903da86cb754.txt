Dear Requester



Freedom of Information Request – FOI/2026/16



I am writing in response to your recent application for information held by the Library (Ref FOI/2026/16) received here at the Library on 16 October 2026 and stated the following:



“This is a new request under the Freedom of Information Act 2000. It is
outside the scope of FOI/2026/09 and its internal review, which
concerned Bute series including D153 and D205 and the 1877
transaction. It is also separate from my enquiry of 27 September and
follow-up of 10 October 2026 on D153/1–8.

PLEASE NOTE: DUPLICATE PLACE AND FARM NAMES IN GLAMORGAN
Glamorgan has two parishes called Llandough, and each had a farm
called Great House.
IN SCOPE: Llandough-juxta-Penarth (Llandough near Penarth and
Cardiff), hundred of Dinas Powys. Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr stood
beside St Dochdwy's church, approx. ST 168 733.
OUT OF SCOPE: Llandough-juxta-Cowbridge (Llandough near Cowbridge),
and the Great Houses at nearby Llandow and Aberthin. For example,
"Manors of Llandough and St Mary Church" (Penrice and Margam Estate
Records, 1619–1704) appears to be the Cowbridge Llandough; please
confirm if you know otherwise.
Please do not send or rely on records for the Cowbridge-area places.
If an entry says only "Llandough" or "Great House" and the parish
cannot be told, please say so rather than assume.

Part 1 — S7, Survey of the lands of William Herbert, kt, dec., in the
lordships or manors of Dinas Powys, Cogan, Llandough and others,
January 1610.
Please tell me:
(a) Whether S7 contains an entry for Great House or any holding in
Llandough-juxta-Penarth, and the folio.
(b) The name of the holder or tenant recorded, the tenure (freehold,
copyhold, leasehold) and any rent or acreage given.
(c) Whether S7 is digitised or filmed, and the cost of a copy of only
those folios.

Part 2 — Cardiff MSS on Microfilm (CARDMFS), filmed 1980–1984.
Glamorgan Archives (ref 2026/4097b) identified Bute X.6(12), abstracts
of payments and receipts by the Bute trustees, 1877 (copy), at Cardiff
Library.
Please tell me:
(a) Whether Bute X.6(12), or any Bute X.6 item, is on the CARDMFS
microfilm, and the reel and frame reference.
(b) If so, how I can obtain a copy of the entries relating to
Llandough-juxta-Penarth or Daniel Thomas, and the cost.

Part 3 — A lease of Great House said to date from 1667.
The family's account (not yet supported by a document) is that the
Williams family took Great House, Llandough-juxta-Penarth, in 1667 on
a lease for 999 years. Great House is described in published sources
as a freehold of the Vaughan family from the 16th to the late 18th
century, after which it came to the Bute estate. Please tell me
whether the Bute Estate Records, including the Herbert and Windsor
family papers within them, or any other NLW collection, hold:
(a) Any lease, counterpart lease, assignment or enrolment,
c.1660–1675, of Great House or any messuage in
Llandough-juxta-Penarth, to any person named Williams, or for a term
of 999 years (or any other long term).
(b) Any later deed, abstract of title, schedule or rental that recites
or refers to a lease of Great House for 999 years or for a long term.
(c) The conveyance or other instrument by which the Bute estate (or
the Windsor family before it) acquired Great House from the Vaughan
family or its heirs, with its date and reference, and whether it
recites any lease or tenancy then outstanding.
(d) Any Vaughan of Llandough family papers, and where they are held if
not at NLW.

If you consider any of this reasonably accessible by other means under
section 21, please say exactly where and how.

Public working evidence catalogue: https://greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com <https://greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com/>
Title and chronology index: https://dartboard.bucklerfamily.estate <https://dartboard.bucklerfamily.estate/> and
https://bpvsbuckler.bucklerfamily.estate <https://bpvsbuckler.bucklerfamily.estate/>
Long-term archive: https://wayback.bucklerfamily.estate <https://wayback.bucklerfamily.estate/>”
