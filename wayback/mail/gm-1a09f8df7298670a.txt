Dear Requester,

Thank you for your email received into our enquiries system 27th August relating to the deed in our collection with document reference DBDT/79/1.

The catalogue description for DBDT/79/1 had a few small errors which have now been corrected.  In answer to your questions:


  1.  Parties and date – Does the original name the Marquess of Bute and Mr John Williams, and is it dated May 1877?

The document is a counterpart lease dated 2 May 1877 between the Trustees of the Will of the late Marquess of Bute and Mr John Williams of Newport.

  1.  Property description – Exact wording of the land and premises. Does it expressly locate the property in Pembroke Terrace, Cardiff?

‘All that piece or parcel of land with the messuage or tenement and outbuildings thereto now being erected and built or part thereof by the said lesee, situate, lying and being in Pembroke Terrace in the parish of St John the Baptist, Cardiff containing by admeasurement sixty-nine feet or thereabouts on the north side thereof, thirty-one feet or thereabouts on the east and west sides thereof and sixty-nine feet six inches or thereaboutson the south side thereof bounded on the north by land of the Marquess of Bute, on the east by Pembroke Terrace aforesaid, on the south by land demised to David Price and on the west by land of Mr John William Vachell.’

  1.  Llandough / other references – Any mention (body text, plan, schedule, endorsements or annotations) of Llandough, Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, Western Ground Rents, or any locality outside Pembroke Terrace?

There is no mention of Llandough, Great House Farm or Ty Mawr or any other property outside of Pembroke Terrace.

  1.  Marginal plan – Does the plan identify the location sufficiently? If procedures allow, please supply a digital image or scan of the plan.

The plan clearly shows a property on Pembroke Terrace.  We can provide a digital copy if you require it at a charge of £15.60 (inclusive of VAT).  Please find attached a digital photography order form.

  1.  Further identifying details – Any adjoining owners, plot numbers, parish, street numbers, measurements, previous ownership, rent, consideration, or cross-references to other instruments.

See above for full description.  There is no further information included.
We hope that this answers your questions in relation to this document but please do let us know if you would like order a digital copy.
