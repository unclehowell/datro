Preservation notice — Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough — do not destroy, transfer or dispose
To each recipient, in your own capacity as holder or likely holder of
records. Please do not treat this as a joint reply. Answer only for
your own holdings.

Sirs/Madam,

This is a preservation notice. It is not a claim form and not service
of proceedings. It does not replace any FOI, review or subject access
request already with you.

Land: Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth, adjoining
St Dochdwy's. Titles WA231076, WA240304 (closed), WA513690, WA735527,
and any related title. Planning refs 89/01396/OUT and 92/00671/RES.

Please preserve, and do not destroy, transfer out of your custody
without a written receipt, or dispose of, any file, plan, photograph,
tape or electronic record in your possession, custody or control
relating to:

1. The 1877 split, the 1916 and 1939 tenancies, and the 1938 Bute to
Western Ground Rents conveyance.
2. The 1955 and 1962 possession orders, the 1974 proceedings, and BP
Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2.
3. First registration: WA231076 application 30 November 1982; WA240304
application 23 February 1983; later amalgamation.
4. The 1988 possession (29 November) and demolition (begun 6
December), including listing, planning, police and any liaison papers.
5. Mary Doreen Williams (died 26 March 1983) and William Buckler, so
far as your file concerns this land.

Also preserve, if you hold them, the following planning and
land-charge classes. Absence from a 2026 file disclosure is not proof
they never existed:

6. Any section 52 Town and Country Planning Act 1971 agreement, later
section 106 instrument, deed, undertaking or other agreement
regulating the development or use of this land, and any corresponding
local-land-charge entry.
7. The historic planning-register entries required by the Town and
Country Planning General Development Order 1988 for 89/01396/OUT and
92/00671/RES (applications, reserved matters, plans and drawings, and
decision information).
8. The officer's report annexes to 89/01396/OUT, including Appendix B
(further GGAT comments after discussions with the Trust), and
Appendices C–E.
9. The condition chain on 89/01396/OUT: conditions 16–18; the
Council's October 1990 confirmation of condition 17; GGAT's July 1992
archaeological warning; the September 1992 reserved-matters approval;
and the 1993–94 papers on whether condition 18 had been discharged.
10. Any preliminary environmental-assessment / screening determination
for the outline application (site described as about 3.4 acres / 1.38
ha).
11. Any record of the statutory consultation route used because the
land was, or was treated as, a site of archaeological interest / SMR
entry under the 1988 Order.
12. The complete 92/00671/RES file, and any sale, option,
conditional-contract or transfer paper on the planning files covering
BP Properties to Ideal Homes Wales, including the recorded transfer of
3 December 1993.
13. The application-receipt record for 89/01396/OUT (form dated
October 1989; reconstructed Council receipt 8 November 1989).

Please stop routine deletion, including archived mailboxes, for
anything in that scope.

The public evidence index is https://greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com/
. The investigation distinguishes the farmhouse parcel from the
eastern fields. The 1987 Court of Appeal decided possession,
limitation and the October 1974 letters. It did not decide the
documentary root of the farmhouse parcel. These records go to that
question. This notice is not a finding against any of you.

Please acknowledge, name the officer responsible for the hold, and say
what you hold in scope. If you hold nothing, say what you searched.

Nothing in this letter abandons any claim concerning title,
registration, concealment, limitation, reopening or alteration of the
register.
