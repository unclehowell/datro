Dear Requester

Thank you for your reply.The only specific reference that  appears in the Bute Estate Collection is for file D153/1-8.

Deeds relating to premises in p. Llandough, co. Glam., comprising a dwelling house adjoining the churchyard, 1811 (D153/1-2), land (19 a.) for a brick and tile works at Cogan Pill, 1875 (D153/3) and land (33 a.), formerly part of Great House Farm, for the Llandough Limeworks (33 a.), 1876-1912 (D153/4-8). Plan engrossed on D153/3 (1875) and 4 (1876).

Ty Mawr is mentioned in the Llandough documents from within the collection, this could be translated to Large House or Great House, but this is only speculation

D 219. Llandough manorial leases and agreements,
 

The deeds include leases of the farm of Cydfin Farm or Ty Mawr Farm, (107 a.), 1552-1824 (D219/1, 4-5, 8, et seq.), the tmt and lands called Upper Cogan Pill, with the fishery there, 1790 (D219/6), Llandough Farm (166 a.), 1824-32 (D219/9-13), Cogan Pill Farm (153 a.), 1824-8 (D219/14-17), Corners Well (107 a.), Lower Cogan Pill (24 a.) and Green Sayes (71 a.), 1824-6 (D219/18-20), and Cogan Farm (320 a.), 1824 (D219/21), all in p. llandough

 

Otherwise you would be required to visit the Library to  search through the rental books and other documents that may be of interest to  locate any documentation relating  to Great House Farm

 

If you wish to have copies of the documents I have listed, please do not hesitate to contact me.

Dear Requester

Thank you for your enquiry. At the Library we have the Bute Estate Records, to help you with your search I have included a link to the catalogue entry. You maybe able to identify what documents would be relevant to the property.

https://archives.library.wales/index.php/bute-estate-records

If you wish to locate maps relating to the Bute Estate, you can locate the details through our main catalogue. To carry out this search,use the link provided below

https://discover.library.wales/discovery/search?vid=44WHELF_NLW:44WHELF_NLW_NUI

Click on Advanced Search, under 'Search Filters' you will see the word Title with a downward pointing arrow, click on this, from the drop screen menu  choose Classmark.

Type in Bute as you search term and click on the search button. A result page should show 290 results for you. 

Another search you are able to carry out would be through the main catalogue again, but this time include 'Bute Estate' in the search box and then on the results page,you can choose map on the resource type filter which appears on the left hand side of the page.

We also produce a leaflet for 'Sources for the History of Houses' and have included a copy in the attachment.

I hope the above information will prove helpful. If you wish to visit the Library or require any further assistance, please do not hesitate to contact me.
