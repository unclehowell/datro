Dear Requester,

ATISN 26-0141 - Great House Farm, Llandough

Thank you for your request which was received on 29 October.  You asked for:


  1.  You ask that Welsh Government, including Cadw, provides any information it holds about the furniture, furnishings, antiques, fixtures, heirlooms and other movable property originating from Great House Farm. You ask that this should include inventories, acquisition or transfer records, provenance, photographs and correspondence.
  2.  You state that you have found evidence in a number of historical sources of alternative names by which Great House Farm may have been know in the past, which you have asked us to also search by.  You provide a list of these alternative names for the site in your email of 16:52 on 29 October titled as above, and also in your email of the same time and date titled ‘Nomenclature supplement — Great House Farm / Ty Mawr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth’.

I note that you refer to a previous request of 2 July asking for the information listed in point 1 above.  However, this email was only copied to Welsh Government’s Freedom of Information Officer and was not therefore treated as an information request to Welsh Government at that time.

You will receive a response to this latest request by 26 November 2026.  If you have any queries about this request, please do not hesitate to contact me at the address below.  Please remember to quote the reference number above in any future correspondence.

The request you sent me contains personal information about you - for example, your name and address. The Welsh Government will be the data processor for this information and, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation, it will be processed in order to fulfil our public task and meet our legal obligations under the Act to provide you with a response. We will only use this personal information to deal with your request and any matters which arise as a result of it. We will keep your personal information and all other information relating to your request for three years from the date on which your request is finally closed.  Your personal information will then be disposed of securely.
