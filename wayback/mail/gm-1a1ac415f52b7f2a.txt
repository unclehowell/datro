Thank you for your enquiry. BGS have made a significant amount of data/records searchable and accessible through the following routes, providing a free, self-service option, to avoid charges that may be incurred if BGS staff were to carry out the searching on your behalf. I hope the following information and links are useful:



  1.  Users can search the Geoindex GeoIndex (onshore) - British Geological Survey<https://www.bgs.ac.uk/map-viewers/geoindex-onshore/> for BGS datasets and navigate the map-based index which holds a wealth of information including boreholes and site investigations that we have collected or have obtained from other sources .I have attached user guidance notes.
  2.  You can search for available mine plans in your area using the Mine Plans Catalogue | OpenGeoscience | Our data | British Geological Survey (BGS)<https://mine-plans.bgs.ac.uk/?_ga=2.8199282.1770190543.1784532596-1462738668.1784532596> .  Many of the plans are scanned and can be freely viewed online. If you identify a BGS plan that cannot be viewed, or you require a pdf copy of a plan you have viewed, please contact [email redacted]<[email redacted]>, providing the BGS mine plan ID and name as we can then check availability and provide a quote to supply.  As a guide, the cost to supply a pdf copy of a plan is £12 + vat per plan or £18 + vat per plan if there isn’t a digital copy available and we have to scan a hard copy.   There is also a minerals data layer on the Geoindex.
  3.  I will direct you to the maps portal BGS maps portal | OpenGeoscience | Our data | British Geological Survey (BGS)<https://webapps.bgs.ac.uk/data/MapsPortal/?_ga=2.39298340.648850980.1793813983-1992634300.1793813983> for free online viewing of over 45,000 BGS geological approved maps and sections. Please let us know of any maps of interest as we can arrange supply (pdf/paper). Your local authority will hold records of land use, registered landfills/ tipping etc. Unapproved historical fieldslip and field notebook records (if available for your site) are held in the BGS strongroom and available to view on request in the BGS Library, by appointment.
