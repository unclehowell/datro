Dear Monitoring Officer,

I request an internal review of the Council's response to my Environmental Information Regulations 2004 request (reference 00210772).

Thank you for the response issued on 7 July 2026. I appreciate the time taken to consider my request and the searches undertaken. However, having carefully reviewed the response alongside the earlier correspondence, I do not believe my clarified request has been fully addressed, nor do I believe the response adequately explains the scope of the searches that were undertaken.

Clarification process
On 28 May 2026 the Council advised that the relevant service area required clarification regarding the nature and reason for my enquiry.

I provided that clarification on 7 June 2026.
On 9 June 2026 I was informed that my clarification had been forwarded to the Planning Department for consideration.
Given that sequence, I reasonably expected the final response to address the full scope of my clarified request. Instead, the response appears to have focused primarily upon identifying two planning applications and referring me to the Planning Register.
Those applications are relevant, but they represent only one aspect of the information requested.

Scope of my request
My request concerns environmental information relating to the historic Great House Farm (Ty Mawr) estate, including land now forming Church View Close and adjoining land on the north side of Leckwith Road.

The request extends from approximately 1875 onwards where records exist.
It includes, where held:

planning records;
officer reports;
committee reports and minutes;
planning consultation responses;
planning correspondence;
environmental reports;
archaeological reports;
heritage assessments;
demolition records;
redevelopment records;
engineering drawings;
highways information;
drainage and sewer records;
GIS and mapping datasets;
aerial photography;
environmental assessments;
land-related correspondence;
estate management records;
property records;
archived files;

and any other environmental information falling within Regulation 2(1) of the Environmental Information Regulations 2004.

Matters that remain unanswered
Although the response identifies two planning applications, it does not explain whether information was sought or considered in relation to several parts of my request.

In particular, it does not explain whether searches were undertaken for:

archived paper planning files;
committee reports and minutes;
officer reports;
consultation responses;
supporting technical documents;
demolition records relating to Great House Farm;
archaeological reports held by the Council;
heritage assessments;
environmental assessments;
engineering reports;
highways records;
drainage records;
GIS datasets;
correspondence relating to redevelopment;
records inherited from predecessor authorities;
records transferred to Glamorgan Archives or any other repository.

Adequacy of the searches
To assist my understanding of the Council's response, I would be grateful if the internal review could explain:

Which Council departments were asked to search.
Which electronic record systems were searched.
Which paper archives were searched.
Whether off-site archived records were searched.
Whether records inherited from predecessor authorities were searched.
Whether any GIS databases were searched.
Whether searches extended beyond the Planning Register itself.
Whether search terms or keywords were used and, if so, what they were.
Whether date restrictions were applied.
Whether any information was identified but withheld.

Information not held
Where the Council concludes that information is not held, I respectfully request that the review explains:
which reasonable searches were undertaken before reaching that conclusion;
whether the information never existed;
whether it was transferred elsewhere;
whether it was destroyed;
if destroyed, the applicable retention schedule and date of destruction;
if transferred, the destination repository.
Information withheld

If any information has been withheld under the Environmental Information Regulations, I request that the review identifies:
the precise Regulation relied upon;
the reasons why that exception applies;
where required, the public interest considerations;
whether partial disclosure or redaction was considered.

Planning Register
The response relies upon Regulation 6(1)(b) on the basis that planning information is publicly available.

I would therefore appreciate clarification as to whether the online Planning Register contains the complete planning files, including:

officer reports;
committee reports;
consultation responses;
internal memoranda;
correspondence;
amendments;
supporting plans;
technical reports;
conditions;
enforcement material (if any);
archived documents.
If it does not, I request disclosure of any remaining environmental information held by the Council.
GIS information

The response states that my request for GIS information lacked sufficient detail.
For the avoidance of doubt, I seek copies of any GIS datasets or historic mapping held by the Council relating to Great House Farm/Ty Mawr, including where available:

parcel boundaries;
planning polygons;
historic base mapping;
aerial imagery;
highway extents;
rights of way;
drainage assets;
land referencing;
scanned plans attached to GIS records.
Personal data
Separately, to the extent that any records contain my own personal data, I request that this element of my request also be treated as a Subject Access Request under Article 15 UK GDPR.

Closing
I have sought throughout this process to clarify and narrow my request wherever asked to do so. My intention is simply to understand the complete environmental and administrative record held by the Council concerning Great House Farm/Ty Mawr.

If the Council has conducted comprehensive searches, I would be grateful if the internal review could explain those searches in sufficient detail to demonstrate that all reasonable steps have been taken. If further information is held, I request that it be disclosed unless a specific statutory exception applies.
Thank you for undertaking the internal review. I look forward to receiving the outcome.

Dear Mr Buckler

Thank you for your request for information about Great House Farm, Williams/Buckler Family, Llandough, Vale of Glamorgan and the further clarification that was received on the 7th June. Your request has been processed under the Environmental Information Regulations 2004.

The Council's Planning records have been reviewed and the Planning department has provided the following response:

In line with your clarification that was received on the 7th June, searches have been undertaken by the Council's Planning department in relation to the historic Great House Farm / Ty Mawr estate in Llandough. This includes land adjacent to and including Church View Close and the north side of Leckwith Road, with particular reference to the area identified within Land Registry title WA231076 and the timeframe indicated from 1875 onwards, where records exist.

We have been advised that the links to the planning records relating to the redevelopment of the site during the 1980s-1990s have previously been provided by colleagues in the Planning department. These records, including associated documentation, are publicly available via the Council's Planning Register and can be accessed using the relevant application reference numbers at View or Comment on a Planning Application .
This information is therefore excepted under Regulation 6(1)(b) of the Environmental Information Regulations 2004 - information already publicly available and easily accessible by other means.

Whilst planning files may contain some archaeological information, it is also noted that archaeological records for the Vale of Glamorgan are maintained by Heneb (formerly Glamorgan-Gwent Archaeological Trust). We suggest that you contact Heneb directly to access relevant records. Details on how to contact them can be found at Heneb - The Trust for Welsh Archaeology . To the extent that the Council does not hold further archaeological reports beyond those contained within planning files, Regulation 12(4)(a) of the Environmental Information Regulations 2004 (information not held) applies.

Following searches of Planning records, no additional information has been identified within the scope of your request. You asked whether any records may have been transferred to Glamorgan Archives or otherwise. It is possible that historic material of this nature may be held externally, and you may therefore wish to contact Glamorgan Archives directly. Details on how to contact them can be found at https://glamarchives.gov.uk/contact/.

From the clarification provided, the Council has been unable to identify which specific GIS datasets are being sought. Please provide further clarification regarding this and once received, we will consider this element further.

As previously advised, the Council does not hold records relating to the legal case of BP Properties Ltd v Buckler. Neither the Vale of Glamorgan Council nor its predecessor authorities were parties to that case, which appears to have been a private legal matter. Accordingly, information relating to those proceedings is not held by the Council.
