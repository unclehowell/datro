Dear NRW Access to Information team,

This is a request under the Environmental Information Regulations 2004.

I am researching the history of Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth, Vale of Glamorgan, and of the woodland between Llandough and Leckwith. My aim is to identify and preserve every surviving record of who owned and occupied that woodland, how it changed hands, and who decided what, when and on what record. Please search the records NRW holds, including those inherited from the Countryside Council for Wales (and, before it, the Nature Conservancy Council) and from Forestry Commission Wales.

A. Cwm Cydfin SSSI (site 0524, "Cwm Cydfin, Leckwith", around ST 165 739)
Your published citation gives notification dates of 1972 and 1985 and says it is not the legal copy; your boundary data records the site as last notified on 9 December 1985.

1. The full legal citation and notification map, and the 1972 notification and 1985 re-notification documents.

2. The list of owners and occupiers served with each notification, and any later record of the site's owners or occupiers up to 2013, including any notice or record of a change of ownership (for example around 1988 and 1994 to 1996).

3. Any representations or objections received on either notification, the record of how each was decided (date, and the committee or officer role), and any record of how the 1985 boundary was fixed.

4. Any management agreement, notice of proposed operations or consent application under section 28 of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981, and site-file correspondence with an owner or occupier, 1972 to 2013.

B. Woodland at Leckwith Road, Llandough, now registered under title WA735527 (Forest of Cardiff)
HM Land Registry records a transfer of this woodland to Forest of Cardiff (registered charity 1002867) dated 10 November 1994, and I understand that part of it was transferred to Goldsworthy Homes in 2005.

5. Any woodland grant, felling licence, management plan or dedication agreement affecting this woodland, 1950 to 2010, with the owner or applicant recorded; and any record relating to the 1994 transfer to Forest of Cardiff or to the 2005 transfer of part of it.

C. Forestry Commission lease of Leckwith Woods
Cardiff's published park history records that Leckwith Woods (about 109 acres given to Cardiff council by Western Ground Rents Ltd, completed in April 1942) was leased to the Forestry Commission, and that the council agreed in June 1985 to accept the surrender of the lease.

6. The lease, its plan and dates; the compartment or stock maps for that woodland; and the 1985 surrender with any plan.

D. Records no longer held

7. If any of the above existed but is no longer held: when it was destroyed or transferred, under which retention schedule, where any transfer went (for example to The National Archives or a record office), and the record authorising it.

On personal data: where an owner or occupier was a private individual and you consider that regulation 13 applies, I would be grateful for the record with only that name redacted, and a note of whether each party was a private individual, a company or a public body. I ask for the names of companies and public bodies in full.

If any part is too wide, please tell me under regulation 9 how to narrow it rather than refusing it; Part A is my priority. A "not held" answer to any part is just as useful.

Please reply within 20 working days, by 17 November 2026.
