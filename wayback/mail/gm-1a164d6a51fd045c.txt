Dear FOI Team,

FOI 2026-024 / 032 / 036 — clarification of point 2 (your email of 20 October 2026)

Thank you for your email of 20 October. My answer is your first option: I am seeking records relating specifically to the c.1880 discovery at Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth (near Penarth and Cardiff, Vale of Glamorgan), the farm beside St Dochdwy's Church. I am not seeking all records of medieval ironwork that match the search terms regardless of any connection to that discovery.

To identify the discovery:

1. The find is the medieval ironwork discovered under the rear wing of the farmhouse c.1880, as described in the two published sources quoted in my email of 19 October: GGAT, "Land at Great House Farm, Llandough: An Archaeological Assessment" (August 1990, citing RCAHMW 1988), and Holbrook and Thomas, Medieval Archaeology 49 (2005), p. 70, n. 142. Both state that the ironwork is held by the National Museum.

2. Point 2 is therefore limited to collection, accession, register or object records for medieval ironwork (including a visor, lance head, helmet or horse equipment), acquired or recorded from 1880 to 1930, whose findspot, provenance or donor note links the object to this site. Because older entries may use a variant form, please include entries recorded as "Great House Farm", "Great House, Llandough", "Tŷ Mawr" or "Ty Mawr", "Cedfin" (the name used for Tŷ Mawr in the 1824 Bute estate survey), "Llandough-juxta-Penarth", or "Llandough, near Penarth / Cardiff", including entries carried over from the former Cardiff Museum's registers. An entry giving only "Llandough" or "Great House" is relevant only if the record ties it to Penarth or Cardiff, or to the c.1880 find. This excludes the Great House Farm at Llandough near Cowbridge.

3. If a search on that basis would still exceed the cost limit, please tell me under section 16 which part can be done within it, and I will narrow further.

4. A held / not held answer, with a short description of the systems and terms searched, would be enough.

Your email concerned point 2 only, so I understand points 1, 3 and 4 of my email of 19 October are proceeding. Please confirm the date by which you now expect to respond.

No fee is agreed. If any charge would apply, please tell me first and offer a free route (an electronic copy or inspection).
