Dear Bev,

Thank you for checking the system and for your prompt and helpful reply.

I am grateful for the clarification regarding the historical position of
Cardiff and Vale cases prior to 2002, and I will now pursue enquiries with
Glamorgan Archives.

Before I do so, I would be grateful if I could clarify one point concerning
the result of your search, particularly in relation to my grandmother, Mary
Doreen Williams.

My purpose is not simply genealogical. I am attempting to reconstruct and
forensically assess the historical circumstances surrounding her death in
1983, and there is information concerning the circumstances before and
around her death which I am seeking to reconcile against the surviving
official documentary record.

Could you therefore please confirm whether your statement that you are
"unable to locate any details" means only that neither individual appears
in the records or computerised/indexed system currently available to your
office, or whether your office is able positively to state that neither
death was ever referred to the coroner?

In particular, if you are able to clarify, I would be grateful to know
whether the absence of a record in your system excludes, or does not
exclude, the historical existence of:

   - a report of the death to a coroner;
   - a preliminary coroner's enquiry;
   - a post-mortem examination;
   - an inquest;
   - correspondence with the police, hospital or other authority;
   - a case file or register subsequently transferred to an archive or
   destroyed in accordance with an applicable retention policy.

I appreciate that you may not hold the historical records themselves. I am
not asking you to speculate about the circumstances of either death. I am
simply seeking to ensure that I correctly understand the evidential
significance of the negative search result before proceeding to the archive.

If possible, I would also be grateful if you could confirm the name of the
coroner jurisdiction or office responsible for Llandough/Llandough Hospital
in March 1983, when Mary Doreen Williams died, and whether you know which
body would have inherited or transferred that jurisdiction's surviving
records.

For completeness, if there are any records held off-system, legacy indexes,
paper registers, accession records or transfer information which are not
searchable through the system you checked, I would be grateful if you could
let me know whether those exist and who now holds them.

I appreciate your assistance. I am trying to establish the documentary
facts carefully and avoid drawing conclusions before the relevant records
have been located or ruled out.

Kind regards,

Sion Buckler (Ret'd Sergeant)
