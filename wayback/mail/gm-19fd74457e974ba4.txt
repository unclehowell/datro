For what you will find on the online catalogue: you will only find catalogued items. It includes the ones held at BT Archives. You can tell the difference on where the items are held by looking at the 'Held at' line. Here is an example (unrelated to your area of interest):

On my system of archival catalogue: we can search catalogued and uncatalogued items. I also searched the uncatalogued items for your keywords, but only unrelated records came up. In addition to that, most uncatalogued items are records which are less than 20 years old. Any file from c.late 1890s or 1900s is already catalogued so you can't miss them.

4. Restricted, closed or unprocessed material. Were any records identified which could potentially relate to the subject but which were unavailable because they were restricted, closed, uncatalogued, unprocessed or otherwise inaccessible?

The only material that would have restricted access would be records that hold individuals' private information. But considering all the people concerned were alive during the late 1800s and early 1900s, it's very unlikely anyone would still be alive and have any personal information that would still be up-to-date. The other type of records that are restricted are some philatelic items, which don't concern your research.

5. Records transferred to BT Archives. You explained that relevant telecommunications material appearing in the catalogue is held at BT Archives. We don't have any transfer records any longer, it's been too long ago since the records were transferred to BT Archives. But you can easily find out which items are held at BT Archives when searching our online catalogue. See above the screenshot. Using the Finding Number mentioned on one of our online catalogue entries, you should be able to trace the item by contacting BT Archives directly. They are the only ones who have the most up-to-date information about the archival items that were transferred to them.

I'm afraid that we don't hold any record that aren't about postal employees. So we wouldn't be able to find anything about this individual in our records. If there is any record of him in the records held at BT Archives, it's not mentioned in any of the file's descriptions.

Concerning all the terms you listed, the relevant records would be kept at BT Archives, so I can't know more about it.
