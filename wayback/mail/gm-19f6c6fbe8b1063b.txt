> Our remit covers complaints where someone believes they've been treated
> unfairly or received poor service from NHS organisations or from *UK
> government* departments and their associated public organisations.
>
> If the complaint involves public health services in *Wales*, this will
> usually fall under the remit of the *Public Services Ombudsman for Wales*:
> https://www.ombudsman.wales/
>
> Similarly, if the complaint is related to how a solicitor or legal
> professional has handled a case, it is likely to fall under the
> jurisdiction of the *Legal Ombudsman*: https://www.legalombudsman.org.uk/
>
> If your complaint is about HMCTS then you will need to complete the full
> complaints process and provide us with the formal response with an MP’s
> referral.  You can follow the link below for further information on the
> full complaint process:
>
>
> https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/hm-courts-and-tribunals-service/about/complaints-procedure
>
> If your concerns are primarily about judicial decisions and potential
> violations of his rights under the European Convention on Human Rights
> (ECHR). As the Parliamentary and Health Service Ombudsman (PHSO) *does
> not have the remit* to investigate judicial decisions, you could consider
> the following options:
>
>    1. Judicial Reviews and Appeals:
>    You will need to seek independent legal advice about the possibility
>    of appealing the decisions or applying for a judicial review. Legal
>    professionals can guide you on the feasibility of further legal action.
>    2. European Court of Human Rights (ECtHR):
>    If you believe your rights under the ECHR have been violated, you may
>    wish to bring his case to the ECtHR. You will need to review the criteria
>    for applying to the ECtHR at the court’s official website:
>    https://www.echr.coe.int/.
>    3. Equality and Human Rights Commission (EHRC):
>    The EHRC provides guidance and support concerning human rights and
>    discrimination issues in England, Scotland, and Wales. You can contact them
>    for information or assistance: https://www.equalityhumanrights.com/en.
>    4. Citizens Advice:
>    For free, impartial advice, you could approach Citizens Advice, which
>    may guide you on your next steps, including accessing pro bono legal
>    services: https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/.
>
> I hope you find this information useful.
