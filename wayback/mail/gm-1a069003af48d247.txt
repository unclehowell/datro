>
> I appreciate that your latest enquiry is intended to establish the
> circumstances surrounding the water supply to Great House Farm (Ty Mawr) in
> 1988, rather than seeking access to your personal data.
>
>
>
> As previously advised, we have been unable to locate any records relating
> to the property following the searches undertaken. Our enquiries indicate
> that the property was demolished during the 1980s and any operational,
> customer or property records that may once have existed would fall well
> outside our retention periods and are no longer held.
>
>
>
> As we do not hold any surviving records relating to the property, we are
> unable to confirm whether any request was made to disconnect the supply,
> who may have made such a request, whether supporting authority was
> provided, whether works were undertaken, or whether any associated
> correspondence or operational records were created.
>
>
>
> Equally, we have been unable to identify any surviving archived indexes,
> work orders, engineer reports or other records relating to the property. As
> no records have been located, we cannot provide further information
> regarding the circumstances of any destruction beyond confirming that
> records are retained in accordance with applicable retention schedules and
> legal requirements.
>
>
>
> Whilst I appreciate this may be disappointing; Welsh Water can only
> provide information that is currently held. We are unable to speculate on
> events or actions that may have occurred where no records remain available
> to verify them.
>
>
>
> In response to your request for clarification regarding our retention
> periods, these vary depending on the type of data or information held. For
> example, telephone recordings are retained for 12 months, whereas account
> notes may be retained for significantly longer periods depending on the
> nature of the information.
>
>
>
> We are generally required to retain customer correspondence for between
> six and seven years. During this period, correspondence may remain
> accessible through a customer's online account before it expires and is
> removed in line with the relevant retention schedule.
>
>
>
> That said, following the migration to our current billing systems in 2015,
> historical call notes, emails, webforms and other communications that may
> previously have been associated with a customer or property account, were
> not transferred and are therefore no longer available through our current
> operating systems.
>
>
>
