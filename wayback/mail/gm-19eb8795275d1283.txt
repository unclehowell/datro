Dear Monika,

Thank you for taking the time to search the Cardiff Central Library
collections and for your helpful reply.

I appreciate that "Williams" is far too common a surname to search
effectively without further information.

The family associated with Great House Farm (Ty Mawr), Llandough, included
Mary Doreen Williams (died 1983), her father John Williams, and later her
son, my father William Buckler and his wife, my mum; Branwen Buckler. The
property was historically known as Great House Farm or Ty Mawr and stood in
Llandough near Cardiff.

The reference to Daniel Thomas comes from historical accounts of the
quarrying and land transactions affecting the estate. My understanding is
that there was a conveyance or land exchange involving Daniel Thomas and
the Bute Estate which has been referenced in later histories of the area.
Unfortunately I do not yet have a deed reference number, which is one of
the reasons I am trying to trace the original records.

I am particularly interested in:
• Any references to Great House Farm / Ty Mawr, Llandough. • Estate records
relating to the Bute Estate and Daniel Thomas. • Local history files,
newspaper cuttings, photographs or manuscripts relating to the Williams
family of Great House Farm. • Material relating to the historic house,
farm, manor, coach house, estate, quarrying activities, or later demolition.

Thank you also for directing me to Glamorgan Archives. I have been making
enquiries there and will continue to do so.
If any estate maps, local history files, manuscript collections, newspaper
indexes, parish material, or catalogues come to mind which may contain
references to Great House Farm, I would be very grateful for any further
suggestions.

Thank you again for your assistance.

Kind regards,

Sion Buckler (Ret'd Sergeant)
