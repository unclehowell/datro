Dear Requester,

Thank you for your correspondence and for sharing the forensic review of Cadw's 1988 assessment of Great House Farm (Ty Mawr), Llandough. I acknowledge the strength of feeling expressed in your letter and recognise the longstanding concerns that you and your family have raised.

The Committee’s role is to scrutinise the work of the Welsh Government and associated public bodies, including holding ministers and public authorities to account for their policies, administration and use of public resources. However, the Committee does not have a remit to investigate, adjudicate upon, or oversee individual complaints or dispute. Nor does it have the powers to reopen historical decisions or direct public bodies to undertake investigations into specific matters.

The Committee is therefore unable to take on an oversight role in relation to the issues you raise or to intervene in these matters.

The matters you raise appear principally to concern the actions and decision-making processes of Cadw in relation to a specific historical assessment. Cadw are responsible for responding to requests for information about their records and decision-making processes, and for considering any representations made regarding the accuracy or completeness of the historical record.

Thank you again for writing to the Committee.
