Dear Sion Buckler,

Thank you for your email received into our enquiries system 13th April.

There is a gap in the cause registers we hold for Cardiff County Court so the only record that has been transferred to us that covers 1974 is a funds ledger (document reference ACA/8/2) .  This ledger records payments made in relation to County Court orders for periodical payments.  I am not sure whether this ledger would be of any relevance to your search but should make you aware that as some of the orders relate to infants, access to this ledger would be restricted in accordance with data protection legislation.  If you can provide us with a bit more information about the case that you are seeking information on we will be happy to advise further.

We hope that this information is of use to you.

Yours sincerely,
