Dear Requester,

ATISN 26-0075 - Great House Farm, Llandough

Thank you for your request which was received on 19 October.  You asked for:

A. ATISN 26-0010 – you request an internal review of the response provided on 19 October.

Please note that this is currently being conducted by the Welsh Government Departmental Records Officer under ref.  ATISN 26-0010 and will be responded to separately in due course.

B. ATISN 27589 (Cadw; your ref qA2496208)

You note the letter of 19 October 2026 refusing ATISN 27589 under section 14(2). You ask for a response on three points:

1. Section 14(2) applies only to a request that is substantially similar to an earlier one. You ask which points of ATISN 27589 the Welsh Government considers ATISN 27021 already covered. Point 3 of ATISN 27589 asked for "Any transfer, destruction or disposal of those files, with the schedule used and the date." You state that ATISN 27021 did not ask for that.

2. The letter stated that no new information "has been generated or become available". You ask for confirmation that this means that no information is held on any point of ATISN 27589 beyond what was released under ATISN 27021, or instead whether we declined to search again.

3. The letter sets out the right to complain to the Information Commissioner but not the internal review procedure, although it refers to the internal review of ATISN 27021. You ask how, and by when, you can ask for an internal review of ATISN 27589. You state that you will not treat the time for asking as running until Welsh Government have confirmed this.

You note that the question in point 3 of ATISN 27589 is also being dealt with under ATISN 26-0009 and state that you are note therefore making any new request under this submission.

C. New requests under the Freedom of Information Act 2000 and the Environmental Information Regulations 2004

You the following new requests for information concerning Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth, Vale of Glamorgan:

1. Registered files. The internal review of ATISN 27021 identified registered files (including A--CL172-06-017/1 Community of Llandough, CL172-10-001/1, A--CL172-10-002/1, A--CL172-10-001/1, and six files of potential interest). For each file you request the register entry or index record showing its title, opening and closing dates, and current status (retained, transferred, destroyed or not traced) and, where the entry says a file was destroyed or transferred, the date and the record of the destruction or transfer. You state you are not asking for the contents of these files.

2. Property and deeds records. The response to ATISN 26-0010 stated that records of Welsh Government property, deeds and leasing are retained until the property is sold or the lease ends. You ask us to confirm whether the Welsh Government or the Welsh Office has ever owned, leased, acquired or disposed of any interest in land at Great House Farm, Leckwith Road or village green VG41, Llandough, between 1950 and 1997, and provide any deed, lease or property file record of it. You ask us to state if no information is held.

3. Wartime farm records, 1936–1955. You ask to provide any recorded information held by the Welsh Government, including records inherited from the Welsh Office, about the farm in 1936–1955:
(a) any record of the Glamorgan War Agricultural Executive Committee, or its post-war successor, about the farm, including any possession, requisition or cultivation direction;
(b) any claim or payment under the Compensation (Defence) Act 1939;
(c) any record naming the occupier or owner of the farm in that period.
Your understanding is that the 1941–43 National Farm Survey is held at The National Archives (MAF 32), and are not therefore requesting this. You ask us to us to provide the references for any other record has been transferred to The National Archives.

4. Compulsory purchase and stopping-up orders, 1950–1995. You ask us to confirm whether the Secretary of State for Wales or the Welsh Office made or confirmed any compulsory purchase order, stopping-up order or diversion order affecting the land at Great House Farm between 1950 and 1995, and provide a copy of any such order and of the decision confirming it. ATISN 26-0010 searched under the farm name and two company names. You state that Orders of this kind are usually titled by road or place, and request that we  also search under "Leckwith Road, Llandough", "Llandough-juxta-Penarth" and "Church View Close". You ask us to provide the references if such records have been transferred to The National Archives.

D. Preservation of records

You request that the following are not destroyed or weeded while ATISN 26-0009, the review in Part A, the requests in Part C and any complaint to the Information Commissioner remain open and reference your emails of 28 September and 1 October 2026:

1. the index of physical registered files, and the registered files themselves, referred to in the internal review of ATISN 27021 (including A--CL172-06-017/1, CL172-10-001/1, A--CL172-10-002/1, A--CL172-10-001/1 and the six files of potential interest identified in that review);
2. any 1988 listing file for Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, or any part or remnant of it;
3. the originals of the two inspection photographs dated 29 July 1988 released under ATISN 27021, with any negatives, digital files, envelopes, captions or other information recorded with them;
4. any disposal or destruction logs, certificates or schedules of destruction covering Welsh Office or Cadw records for 1970–1997;
5. the retention schedules in force for those records in 1970–1997;
6. any transfer lists to The National Archives or a Place of Deposit for those records, including any selection records made under OSP 7.


You will receive a response to Parts B – D above by 16 November 2026.  If you have any queries about this request, please do not hesitate to contact me at the address below.  Please remember to quote the reference number above in any future correspondence.
