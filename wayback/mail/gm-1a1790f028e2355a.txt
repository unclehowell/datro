Thank you for your request which we received via CPS Wales on 21 October 2026. This is being actioned as a request under the FOIA for information held by a public authority.

Freedom of Information (FOI) is a public disclosure regime, not a private regime. This means that any information disclosed under the Act, by definition, may become available to the wider public.  Please be aware that FOI applies only to information that a public authority, in this case the Crown Prosecution Service (CPS), holds in recorded format at the time a request is received. The CPS is not required to create it or seek this from a third party.

There is a 20-working day limit in which we are required to respond to requests.
