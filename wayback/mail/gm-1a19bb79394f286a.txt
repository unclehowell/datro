Dear Historic Buildings & Places,

I am researching the history of Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough-juxta-Penarth, Vale of Glamorgan (approx. ST 168 733), the house beside St Dochdwy's Church. The family's recorded transcription of the index to an 1824 Bute estate survey (Glamorgan Archives, DB/E/1, plan 19) gives the name "Cedfin or Ty Mawr Farm". The house was demolished in late 1988. The South Wales Echo of 3 December 1988 ("History fight to save farm") reported that Cadw was considering spot-listing the farmhouse and that an inspector was due to visit that weekend.

As the Ancient Monuments Society was a national amenity society for England and Wales at that time, please could you tell me whether the Society's casework records include anything about the house, for example:

1. any notification, request for spot-listing, or correspondence about its threatened demolition in 1988–89; or
2. any earlier record of the building.

If such records exist, I would be grateful for a reference and a short description, and to know whether they are held by the Society or have been deposited with an archive.

I would welcome a short acknowledgement.

Kind regards,

Requester
