Further to my email of 28 October 2026 about the ITV/HTV Report Wales items CAN 2775 (11 April 1974, item 4) and CAN 2904 (1 November 1974, item 8), and to assist your searches:

I believe the cottage in both entries to be Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough-juxta-Penarth, near Penarth and Cardiff. It is not Llandow in the Vale of Glamorgan, and it is not Llandough Farm opposite the hospital, which is a separate farmstead.
