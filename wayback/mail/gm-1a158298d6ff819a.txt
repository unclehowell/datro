Before we can consider point 2 of your request, we would be grateful for clarification.

The searches undertaken in relation to FOI 2026-024, FOI 2026-032 and FOI 2026-036 were directed at specific records, locations and objects associated with the matters raised in those requests.

Point 2 of your current request seeks searches for "medieval ironwork (any object type)" using a number of place names, provenance references and donor details. As drafted, this would significantly broaden the scope of the searches previously undertaken and could encompass a substantial number of collection records.

As you will be aware, there are a number of properties named "Great House Farm" across Wales and "Llandough" is not a unique place name. In addition, Amgueddfa Cymru holds a substantial collection of medieval ironwork and it is not clear whether you are seeking records relating specifically to the alleged discovery at Great House Farm/Tŷ Mawr, Llandough (near Penarth/Cardiff), or a broader category of collection records matching the search terms listed in your email.

To assist us in identifying the information you are seeking, please clarify:

  *   whether you are seeking records relating specifically to the alleged c.1880 discovery at Great House Farm, Llandough (near Penarth/Cardiff); or
  *   whether you are seeking all records of medieval ironwork matching any of the search terms listed in your email, regardless of whether they have any recorded connection to the alleged discovery.

Once we have received your clarification, we will be able to consider the request further.

Please note that the request will be placed on hold pending receipt of your clarification.
