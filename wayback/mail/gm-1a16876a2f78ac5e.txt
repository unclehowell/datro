apologies for the delay in replying.

Yes, Bute X.6(12) is still held in Central Library - I had a look at it and, unfortunately, couldn't find any information about the property you are researching. We do have quite a vast collection of Bute Papers (please see attached documents for details).

If you wanted the document scanned to you, that would be done by Glamorgan Archives, as they have specialised equipment to do so. If you wish, we can transfer it to them and they will get in touch with you about the cost.

I had a look at the index of our Manuscript collection and couldn't find any matches with the places and people mentioned in your message. If you wanted us to conduct detailed research, we would have to discuss it with senior management and Glamorgan Archives, as it is not one of our core services. There would be a charge, but at the moment I can't provide you with details, until we assess what needs to be done.

Alternatively, you could book an appointment and visit us in person, if you wanted to see any documents from the Bute collection.

Please advise how you would like to take it forward.

Dear Cardiff Central Library Local Studies / Manuscripts Team,

Glamorgan Archives has advised me (email reference 2026/4097b,
received 30 September 2026) that your library holds a Bute collection
containing reference Bute X.6(12), described as “abstracts of payments
and receipts by the trustees of the Marquis of Bute’s properties, 1877
(copy)”. I am writing to establish access to that exact item and to
ask whether related Bute Estate records in the same collection may
assist a targeted historical search.

The property under investigation is Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr,
Llandough-juxta-Penarth, adjoining St Dochdwy’s Church (approx. ST 168
733), Cardiff. Please keep this distinct from the separate Llandough
Farm opposite the hospital, Llandough juxta-Cowbridge, and Llandow.
The historical question is whether surviving estate material in
1876–1878 records a transaction involving Daniel Thomas / D. Thomas
and the Bute Estate or its trustees concerning this particular
farm/defined parcel. That is a research question, not an assertion
that X.6(12) necessarily concerns this property.

Please confirm:
1. whether Bute X.6(12) is still held by Cardiff Central Library, its
full item title/date coverage and any catalogue or manuscript
identifier;
2. whether the item can be inspected or copied, what the
booking/form/fee conditions are, and whether a digital image of the
relevant 1877 entry can be supplied;
3. whether staff can check the entry/index for Daniel Thomas, D.
Thomas, Great House Farm/Tŷ Mawr, Llandough, Llandough Limeworks,
quarrying/mineral rights or a dwelling adjoining St Dochdwy’s
churchyard;
4. whether related Bute X.6 account/receipt volumes, estate ledgers,
correspondence, rent/lease records, conveyances or attached maps/plans
survive for 1876–1878 and can be searched; and
5. if no relevant entry is found, which volumes/indexes were checked
and whether there is a reliable route to other Cardiff Library
manuscript registers or a transfer/custody record.

For any possible match, please provide the exact reference, date,
parties as written, property description, amounts/transaction terms if
recorded, acreage/boundaries, and whether a map or plan accompanies
the entry. Please do not infer that a generic “Llandough” entry refers
to this farm without a supporting description.

Thank you for your help. Please direct me to the correct collection
custodian if Bute X.6(12) has moved or is managed elsewhere.

Kind regards,
Requester
