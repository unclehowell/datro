Dear Sion

Thank you for your enquiry. At the Library we have the Bute Estate Records, to help you with your search I have included a link to the catalogue entry. You maybe able to identify what documents would be relevant to the property.

https://archives.library.wales/index.php/bute-estate-records

If you wish to locate maps relating to the Bute Estate, you can locate the details through our main catalogue. To carry out this search,use the link provided below

https://discover.library.wales/discovery/search?vid=44WHELF_NLW:44WHELF_NLW_NUI

Click on Advanced Search, under 'Search Filters' you will see the word Title with a downward pointing arrow, click on this, from the drop screen menu  choose Classmark.

Type in Bute as you search term and click on the search button. A result page should show 290 results for you. 

Another search you are able to carry out would be through the main catalogue again, but this time include 'Bute Estate' in the search box and then on the results page,you can choose map on the resource type filter which appears on the left hand side of the page.

We also produce a leaflet for 'Sources for the History of Houses' and have included a copy in the attachment.

I hope the above information will prove helpful. If you wish to visit the Library or require any further assistance, please do not hesitate to contact me.
