Thank you for your detailed reply of 22 October, and for clarifying that your correspondence of 11 October reflected your assessment of the proposal as submitted, against the Council's Scrutiny work programming principles, rather than a formal decision of a Scrutiny Chair or Committee.

I understand that scrutiny of this topic would not review individual cases or revisit an individual historic planning decision, would not determine private title, and would not replace the statutory information-rights procedures. I also note that final agreement rests with the relevant Scrutiny Committee and Chair, that the Committee does not currently have capacity for another Task and Finish review while it completes its current piece of work, and that a new topic remains subject to the Council's normal work programming process.

On that basis, I wish to pursue a separate, county-wide topic as follows.

Proposed title: Management of, and public access to, historic planning, land-charges and heritage records.

Purpose: To consider whether the Council's arrangements for retaining, transferring, cataloguing, locating and providing access to historic records are clear, effective and appropriately documented. Reliable historic records matter to any resident, landowner or conveyancer tracing the planning and land-charges history of land anywhere in the Vale.

Proposed scope:

1. The policies, responsibilities and procedures for retaining, transferring, disposing of, indexing and retrieving historic records, including records inherited from predecessor authorities at the 1996 local government reorganisation and earlier reorganisations, how those handovers were documented, and records held on paper or in off-site archives.

2. How searches for historic records are carried out and documented, and how missing, incomplete or unlocated files, appendices and supporting material are identified and explained.

3. How requests for access to historic planning, land-charges and heritage information are received, routed, monitored and answered.

4. Whether published procedures, allocation of responsibilities, record-search practices and service standards could be improved.

The Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr) matter is the background to this suggestion, but it is not a request for Scrutiny to determine, review or revisit that case or any other individual case. I note your point that a topic of this kind would not reflect upon or review individual cases, and I am content for the work to proceed entirely at the level of the Council's systems and practices.

The proposed work would concern policy, governance, records management and service delivery. It would not ask Scrutiny to determine property ownership, make findings about individual liability, or decide the merits of a historic planning decision. My outstanding information requests, including the internal review of EIR request 00210772 and the requests concerning the missing planning appendices, will continue separately through the information-rights routes.

I will submit this as a separate topic suggestion through the Scrutiny Topic Suggestion Form linked from the Council's published Scrutiny webpage (https://www.valeofglamorgan.gov.uk/en/our_council/Council-Structure/scrutiny/scrutiny.aspx).

As the topic spans both historic planning records and corporate records management, I would welcome your advice, or that of Democratic and Scrutiny Services, on which committee or committees it falls under within their current remits.
