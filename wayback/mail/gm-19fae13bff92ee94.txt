I couldn't find any specific file about a Mr William from Great House Farm, but the main records we have that are useful for family history research are the Appointment Books, and the pension and gratuity records. We also have Establishment Books and staff magazines that can be helpful too. You can find more information about what we hold here:  https://www.postalmuseum.org/collections/the-archive/family-history/  Please bear in mind that most villages, small towns, hamlets, etc. such as Llandough had business that ran a postal service, and these people were not directly employed by the Post Office, therefore, we won't have any information. If Mr Williams had another business, he was likely not employed by the Post Office. A local archive might hold newspaper cuttings or other information about this person: https://glamarchives.gov.uk/
You are welcome to visit our archives, but I believe most of the records and information you are looking for will be held at BT Archives. You can contact them directly to organise an appointment.
