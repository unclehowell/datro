NEXT SUNDAY: ROAD CLOSURES
FOR CARDIFF HALF MARATHON


**
------------------------------------------------------------
Dear Requester

Road closures will affect our community on the morning of Sunday 4th October for the Cardiff Half Marathon.

Penarth Road, Barry Road and Cogan Spur will all be closed, as the marathon course runs along these roads.

Llandough Hill will also be closed, to prevent vehicles diverted via Penlan Road from inadvertently trying to access Penarth Road while it's shut.
Diversions will be in place, and you can see the full road closure map above.

The closures will operate between 9am and 12pm.

Further details can be found at the Cardiff Half Marathon website here.  (https://www.cardiffhalfmarathon.co.uk/event-info/road-closures/)

https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1BzkRccTvm/

If you have further questions, Requester, please let me know.

Next community litter pick

Our last community litter pick has had great feedback.

I've even had a couple of messages asking when the next one is taking place!

Llandough Community Council organise these events throughout the year, but they usually put them on hold during the winter, due to lack of interest.

But given the enthusiasm, I thought it best to harness it!

We discussed it at last week's Community Council meeting, and I'm exploring organising another in the coming weeks.

I'll need to liaise with the Vale Council to arrange equipment, but the Community Council are on board and have said they'll help.

If you're interested in taking part, Requester, drop me a message!
https://www.facebook.com/share/v/1KDWx1SUjX/

Licensing Service of
Revd Phil Maudsley

I had a wonderful evening on Tuesday at the Licensing Service of Reverend Phil Maudsley, the new Penarth Ministry Area lead.

It was great to see many members of our community there, including Llandough Community Council Chair Mo Misra, Reverend Susannah Griffin and lots of other residents.

Phil and his family have recently moved to South Wales from North Yorkshire, and I look forward to working with him.

I know everyone in Llandough will join me in welcoming them to our community.

Greenway Close verge

When I was out in our village recently, a Greenway Close resident raised the overgrowth on the nearby verge.

As you'll see from the first picture, it was in need of attention.

I raised with the Council. And I'm pleased to report it's now been cut back.

Thanks so much to the resident for coming over and raising this.

It helped ensure it was cleared.

As I always say, we're a team in Llandough.

By all chipping in, we make our village better for everyone.

If you ever spot anything that needs attention, Requester, do let me know!

Preventing car thefts

I spoke with two people who've recently purchased Faraday boxes while I was out meeting residents this week.

With attempted car thefts reported in our village in recent months, police have advised everyone with keyless fobs to keep them in a Faraday pouch or box as a security measure.

It's a sensible precaution and a small expense to protect your car.

It was good to see people following the advice.

If you require further information, please drop me a message.

Our Plan for Llandough!


As your councillor, I'm working with you all to secure a better future for Llandough. Together, we have a plan to deliver for the village.

Our plan is making Llandough better by:

1. Sorting out the parking problems
2. Stamping out dog fouling
3. Repairing our roads
4. Stopping littering and flytipping
5. Delivering better sports facilities
6. Keeping the whole community involved

If you have any suggestions of what you'd like to see in Our Plan, please get in touch!
