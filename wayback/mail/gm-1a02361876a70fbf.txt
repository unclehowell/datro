Sir/Madam,

I write to give formal notice of documentary evidence which, if correctly
understood, raises a potentially material question concerning the
historical identity, ownership and/or title to land formerly known as *Great
House Farm (Ty Mawr), Llandough*, subsequently developed for residential
purposes including *Church View Close*.

This correspondence is intended as a *governance and integrity notification*.
It is not an FOI or EIR request, nor am I asking the Council at this stage
to accept my allegations or conclusions as established fact.
1. The land and subsequent development

The land concerned is the former Great House Farm / Ty Mawr site at
Llandough.

The site was subsequently developed for residential use. Planning and
associated administrative decisions were therefore made in circumstances in
which ownership of the relevant land appears to have been treated as
sufficiently established for those purposes.

I am notifying the Council because documentary evidence now assembled
appears to raise a material question as to whether the historical title
position was in fact conclusively established, and whether that question
was adequately identified, examined and recorded within the Council's
administrative and planning records.
2. Potential title-chain discrepancy

The documentary record presently available to me indicates a historical
dispute concerning the land involving my grandmother and the
Williams/Buckler family on one side and BP-related entities on the other.

My understanding of the available records is that:

   - proceedings in 1974 did not result in a final judicial determination
   conclusively establishing the underlying title in the manner subsequently
   assumed;
   - a further hearing listed for *31 October 1974* did not proceed
   substantively;
   - the land was subsequently registered in the name of BP-related
   entities around *1982*;
   - my grandmother died in *1983*, without, on the evidence presently
   available to me, a final judicial determination of the underlying ownership
   dispute or compensation resolving the family's claim;
   - the subsequent *1987 Court of Appeal proceedings, BP Properties Ltd v
   Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2*, did not, in my assessment, constitute a
   comprehensive adjudication of every underlying historical title question
   now arising from the documentary record.

I emphasise that I am *not asking the Council to determine the civil title
itself*. Rather, I am asking whether the Council has properly established
what was actually determined by the courts, what remained disputed, what
documentary basis was relied upon for treating title as settled, and
whether that distinction was properly reflected in its own records and
decision-making.

That distinction is important.
3. Documentary evidence

I have assembled documentary material concerning the historical title
chain, court proceedings, registration history, conveyances and associated
records, together with a chronological record of notifications and
responses.

The core documentary evidence can be supplied to the Council immediately.

Some of the underlying material and references have also been raised in
previous correspondence with the Council and other public bodies. I
therefore request that this notification be considered alongside the
relevant material already held on Council files, rather than treated as an
isolated new allegation.
4. Authorities previously notified

The matter has previously been raised with, among others:

   - the Planning Department — from 31 January 2026 and subsequently;
   - the Council's FOI / Information Governance function, including case
   *00210772*;
   - Councillor George Carroll — through multiple contacts between February
   and July 2026;
   - Corporate Complaints / the Monitoring Officer — including
   correspondence dated 27 July 2026 and the determination dated 12 August
   2026;
   - Cadw and the Royal Commission on the Ancient and Historical Monuments
   of Wales (RCAHMW);
   - other public bodies involved in the continuing documentary and
   evidential trail.

I am concerned that the substantive governance question identified above
has not yet been independently addressed.
5. Matters requiring independent examination

In particular, I request that the Council establish:

   1.

   *Whether the historical title dispute was ever conclusively determined
   by a competent court*, distinguishing between orders concerning
   possession, proceedings concerning particular issues, and an actual final
   determination of the underlying title.
   2.

   *What documentary evidence the Council historically relied upon when
   treating ownership of the relevant land as established* for planning,
   development, conveyancing or other administrative purposes.
   3.

   *Whether the Council's planning and administrative records contain any
   recognition of the historical dispute*, including any consideration of
   competing title claims or unresolved matters affecting the identity or
   ownership of the land.
   4.

   *Whether any independent review of these issues has previously been
   undertaken*, and if so, by whom, on what terms, and with what
   conclusions.
   5.

   *Whether any current Council officer involved in reviewing this
   notification has previously participated materially in the decisions,
   complaints, correspondence or investigations now under examination*,
   and, if so, what arrangements will be made to ensure genuine independence.
   6.

   *Whether the Council considers that any governance, record-keeping,
   planning or public-confidence issue remains outstanding*, irrespective
   of the ultimate determination of civil title.

6. Why independent examination is warranted

The fact that the land has subsequently been developed does not, by itself,
resolve the historical documentary question.

If the evidence demonstrates that a material title issue was overlooked,
misunderstood or inadequately recorded, that may have implications
extending beyond the original ownership dispute, including the accuracy and
integrity of public records and the basis upon which subsequent
administrative decisions were made.

Conversely, if the Council considers that the historical record
conclusively resolves the matter, I ask that it identify *the specific
documentary and judicial basis for that conclusion*.

That is why I am requesting an independent examination rather than asking
individual officers who have previously dealt with the matter simply to
reaffirm earlier positions.
7. Formal request

I therefore formally request that the Council:

*a.* acknowledge this notification and identify the officer or function
responsible for independently reviewing it;

*b.* confirm how that review will be kept independent of officers who have
previously dealt substantively with the matter;

*c.* identify the principal Council records and documentary evidence that
will be considered;

*d.* determine whether the historical title issue described above has
previously been properly examined and recorded;

*e.* identify any material governance or administrative issue that remains
outstanding; and

*f.* provide a written response setting out the process to be followed and
the anticipated timescale.

For the avoidance of doubt, I am *not asking the Council to accept that
fraud, unlawful dispossession or defective title has been established*. I
am notifying the Council that documentary evidence appears to raise a
potentially material question concerning the historical identity and/or
title to land upon which subsequent development took place, and I am asking
the Council to establish whether that question has ever been properly
examined.

If the Council considers that determination of the underlying civil title
falls outside its functions, I would nevertheless ask it to address the
separate governance question: *whether its own historical records and
decision-making properly identified and dealt with the title issue on the
evidence available to it.*

This is a *formal governance and integrity notification*, not an FOI or EIR
request.

I would be grateful for written acknowledgement and confirmation of the
process and timescale for an independent determination.
