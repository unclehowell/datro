<p><strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">To each recipient separately, in your own capacity as a current or potential holder, custodian or controller of relevant records:</span></strong><o:p></o:p></p>
<p>This notice is addressed to each recipient <strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">individually</span></strong>. It is
<strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">not a joint request</span></strong>, and it does not ask any recipient to speak for, investigate on behalf of, or answer in place of any other person or organisation. Each recipient is asked to respond only
 in respect of records within that recipient's own possession, custody or control, including records inherited from a predecessor organisation or retained on its behalf.<o:p></o:p></p>
<h3>1. Nature and purpose of this notice<o:p></o:p></h3>
<p>This is a <strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">formal preservation notice</span></strong> concerning documentary material relating to Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth, adjoining St Dochdwy's, and the subsequent development
 of the site, including the area now associated with Church View Close.<o:p></o:p></p>
<p>It is <strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">not a claim form, is not service of proceedings, and is not intended to constitute or replace any FOI request, internal review, complaint, subject-access request or other statutory process already
 made to any recipient.</span></strong><o:p></o:p></p>
<p>The purpose of this notice is to require the preservation of potentially relevant material while the documentary history, title chain, registration history, planning history, possession proceedings, demolition and subsequent development are being reconstructed.<o:p></o:p></p>
<p>A separate first-stage preservation notice has been issued or is being issued to Cadw / Welsh Government FOI, HM Land Registry Wales Office and DPO, Heneb / GGAT HER, RCAHMW, Vale of Glamorgan FOI Unit and DPO, Glamorgan Archives, South Wales Police FOI,
 The National Archives, the Wales Office FOI team, and the National Library of Wales. Persimmon Homes (Wales) Ltd has received a separate preservation letter.<o:p></o:p></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">You are receiving this notice separately because the published documentary record places you, or a predecessor whose records may remain in your custody or control, within the documentary, legal, professional,
 administrative or development chain concerning this land.</span></strong><o:p></o:p></p>
<h3>2. Land and documentary scope<o:p></o:p></h3>
<p>Please preserve, and do not destroy, alter, overwrite, discard, transfer out of custody without a recorded handover, or otherwise dispose of, any record within your possession, custody or control relating to the land or matters identified below.<o:p></o:p></p>
<p>The scope includes, without limitation, <strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">originals, copies, working papers, drafts, annotations, plans, photographs, negatives, films, tapes, maps, registers, indexes, correspondence, emails and attachments,
 electronic files, archived mailboxes, databases, metadata, internal notes, meeting records, instructions, client or matter files, survey material and other associated records.</span></strong><o:p></o:p></p>
<p>The material requested includes:<o:p></o:p></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">1. Historical ownership, occupation and conveyancing</span></strong><o:p></o:p></p>
<p>The 1877 split; the 1916 and 1939 tenancies; the 1938 Bute to Western Ground Rents conveyance; the 1969 Western Ground Rents to BP Pension Trust conveyance; and any plans, schedules, abstracts, searches, correspondence or other documents relied upon in relation
 to those transactions.<o:p></o:p></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">2. Possession proceedings and subsequent litigation</span></strong><o:p></o:p></p>
<p>The 1955 High Court and 1962 Cardiff County Court possession orders; the 1974 proceedings;
<strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2</span></strong>; House of Lords leave papers, including
<strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">YHL/PO/JO/10/11/2536</span></strong>; and
<strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">Buckler v United Kingdom (1989)</span></strong>, including associated correspondence, instructions, evidence, plans and working papers.<o:p></o:p></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">3. Land registration</span></strong><o:p></o:p></p>
<p>The first registration of <strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">WA231076</span></strong>, including the application dated 30 November 1982; the first registration of
<strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">WA240304</span></strong>, including the application dated 23 February 1983; subsequent amalgamation, transfer or closure; and every plan, conveyance, abstract, search, application, correspondence trail or
 professional file relied upon in connection with those registrations or changes.<o:p></o:p></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">4. 1988 possession and demolition</span></strong><o:p></o:p></p>
<p>The taking of possession on or about <strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">29 November 1988</span></strong> and the demolition commenced on or about
<strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">6 December 1988</span></strong>, including any planning, listed-building, heritage, police, enforcement, media, contractor, survey, photographic or inter-agency liaison material.<o:p></o:p></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">5. Planning and subsequent development, 1989–1996</span></strong><o:p></o:p></p>
<p>Planning application <strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">89/01396/OUT</span></strong>; reserved matters application
<strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">92/00671/RES</span></strong>; conditions 16–18; GGAT / Cotswold Archaeology material; records concerning any
<strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">section 52, section 106, planning obligation, local land charge or equivalent instrument</span></strong>; and the transfer from BP to Ideal Homes dated
<strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">3 December 1993</span></strong>, together with all associated plans, correspondence, approvals, agreements, searches and completion material.<o:p></o:p></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">6. Family records</span></strong><o:p></o:p></p>
<p>Records concerning <strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">Mary Doreen Williams (died 1983)</span></strong> and
<strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">William Buckler</span></strong>, insofar as those records relate to Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, the land described above, its occupation, ownership, possession, disposal, registration, development or demolition.<o:p></o:p></p>
<h3>3. Predecessor and inherited files<o:p></o:p></h3>
<p>Where you hold, or may hold, a file originating with a predecessor, successor, merged practice, affiliated practice, client or other entity, that material is also within scope.<o:p></o:p></p>
<p>This includes, without limitation, records originating from:<o:p></o:p></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">Loosemores of Cardiff; Robbins Olivey &amp; Blake Lapthorn / Blake Morgan; Linklaters &amp; Paines; Debenham Tewson / DTZ / Cushman &amp; Wakefield; Wyn Thomas + Partners; Ideal Homes Wales; BP Properties; BP Pension
 Trust; Cotswold Archaeological Trust; Cardiff Bay Development Corporation; and South Glamorgan County Council.</span></strong><o:p></o:p></p>
<p>The reference to a predecessor file does not assert that you necessarily hold such a file. It identifies material for which a reasonable custody or succession inquiry should be made where applicable.<o:p></o:p></p>
<h3>4. Preservation steps<o:p></o:p></h3>
<p>Please take reasonable steps to prevent the routine destruction, deletion, alteration or overwriting of potentially relevant material, including material held in
<strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">archived mailboxes, legacy systems, off-site storage, document-management systems, backup repositories and other electronic or physical stores</span></strong>.<o:p></o:p></p>
<p>Where relevant material has been transferred to another repository, archive, successor organisation or third party, please preserve the information necessary to identify the transfer and its current location, including any accession, file, box, reference
 or custody details.<o:p></o:p></p>
<p>Nothing in this notice requires the creation of records that do not exist. The request concerns the preservation of records already held or controlled by you.<o:p></o:p></p>
<h3>5. Purpose and legal position<o:p></o:p></h3>
<p>This notice is <strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">not a finding against any recipient</span></strong>, and I am not asking any recipient to determine title or adjudicate the underlying dispute.<o:p></o:p></p>
<p>The <strong><span style="font-family:&quot;Aptos&quot;,sans-serif">1987 Court of Appeal judgment</span></strong> is relied upon here only for matters actually determined by that judgment, including the issues concerning possession, limitation and the October 1974
 letters. This preservation notice does not treat that judgment as having determined the complete documentary root of title to the farmhouse parcel.<o:p></o:p></p>
<p>The underlying documentary history, including the relationship between the farmhouse parcel and the agricultural land subsequently dealt with through Western Ground Rents and BP, remains a matter requiring reconstruction from the primary records.<o:p></o:p></p>
<p>Accordingly, preservation is requested precisely so that the documentary record can be established from the records themselves rather than through assumptions based on later registration, later occupation, later planning treatment or secondary descriptions.<o:p></o:p></p>
