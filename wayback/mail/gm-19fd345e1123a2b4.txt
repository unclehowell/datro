Diolch Rhian,

I'm currently at the Welsh Settlement in South Argentina, otherwise I'd be
there myself. So for our private study and research of our family land,
former home and history, we require photos be permitted to be taken, of
artifacts of interest, so they may be sent to me here.

I'm attempting to reconstruct a replica of our old 800 year old farm house
here as a museum/ open house. We're also trying to understand the
unresolved and ongoing ownership dispute between our family and BP, who
were awarded possession of our farm and home since 1667, which resulted in
us being dispossessed of our ancestral birthland as children in 1988. It's
all a very bizzar case.

The MP for Cardiff covering Llandough responded yesterday and is now aware
of the queries and concerns I'm raising with the case and we appreciate
your cooperation and service and thank you in advance. Subject to me
viewing the records, it's possible I may be warranted in proceeding with a
Senedd public enquiry request, as this could be a matter of public
interest.

No single centralised chronology with all supporting evidence appears to
exist for our land, family and former home and it's history and herritage.
And so this is the purposeful and meaningful work I have chosen to
undertake.

Fascinately world champion boxer Peerless Jim fought his first fight in our
barns. Marconi stayed with us while inventing radio. The house was a court
house and stood on a Roman villa and monestry. It's a trove of hidden
history.

Kind regards,

Sion Buckler (Ret'd Sergeant)
