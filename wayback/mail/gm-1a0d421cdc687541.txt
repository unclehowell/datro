Dear Requester,

Thank you for your email received into our enquiries system 8th September.  Please find below answers to your questions in turn.  Please note that prices quoted are without VAT based on the fact that you are in Patagonia.

**26 May 2026 (2026/2370)**
1. 1974 demolition petition, Llandough juxta Penarth — ref or not held.
There is not a petition held within our collection. You would need to know who the petition was submitted to in order to search for any further reference to it.  If it was submitted to the planning authority there may be mention in the Planning Committee minutes and reports – see point 6 below.

**29 May 2026 FOIA/EIR (2026/2372)**

2. Williams/Buckler as owners, tenants, or reporters of the 1880 floor discovery.
One source of information about ownership would be rate books. UC/73/1 is a rate book for 1882 – this is the nearest we have to 1880.  A search of this (and later volumes UC/73/1-5 covers 1882-1902 and then UDPE/T/30 and UDPE/T/32 cover dates after 1900) could be undertaken by staff on your behalf at a charge of £50 which would include copies of relevant entries.

3. Gaps / missing volumes / withdrawals 1950–1989.
We have never had a full set of records that would show the full tenancy/ownership history of Great House / Ty Mawr Farm. With all historical research of this nature, it is necessary to piece together information from surviving records that have been deposited with us.  There are not any records that have been withdrawn from our collections that would have provided additional information to this search for the period 1950-1989.

4. Why the fabric was not scheduled before 1988 (or “Cadw holds this; we hold nothing”).
Not held in our collections – suggest contacting Cadw and the Royal Commission on the Ancient and Historical Monuments of Wales.

5. Internal preservation assessments.
As above - suggest contacting Cadw and the Royal Commission on the Ancient and Historical Monuments of Wales.

6. Planning files for this acreage / demolition / Church View Close — file refs, not class names.
We do not hold planning files (it is possible some files may still be held by the Vale of Glamorgan Council Planning Department), but we do hold copies of the Planning Committee minutes and reports.  Mary Buckler started looking at the Planning Committee minutes and reports on her last visit to the searchroom on 13th August.  The document references to start with for these would be DCVG/C/4/36/76-77 and DCVG/C/4/36/94 these are for the planning applications 1989/01396/OUT and 1992/00671/RES.  It is possible that the minutes may make reference to any earlier applications or discussions.  A search of these records and anything else that they lead to could be carried out by staff at a charge of £50 to include copies of any relevant pages found.

7. When the known archaeology was raised in planning.
If any record of this is in our collections it would appear in the Planning Committee records described above in point 6.

8. EIAs / HER / developer briefs 1980–1990.
The HER for Wales is searchable via the Heneb website: Historic Environment Records (HER) - Heneb<https://heneb.org.uk/historic-environment-records-her/>.  You could also contact the Glamorgan Gwent Archaeological Trust to see if they have any relevant information.

**27 July / 5 August 2026 (2026/3309)**

This was consulted and photographed by Mary Buckler in our public searchroom.  If you would like to receive copies of the pages relating to Ty Mawr this would be two images (one from DB/E/1 the survey and one from DB/E/2 the terrier).  Total cost £17 (£13 for the first image in the order and £4 for the next image).

10. Lambert Williams–Bute agreements 1818–1835 (not the D160 maps already seen).
Not held. Possibly at the National Library of Wales?

Not held – this is a National Library of Wales reference: Llandough manorial leases and agreements, - National Library of Wales Archives and Manuscripts<https://archives.library.wales/index.php/llandough-manorial-leases-and-agreements> You should contact the NLW for access or copies.

12. D153/4–8 1876 limeworks severance — not seen.
Not held – this is a National Library of Wales reference: Deeds relating to premises in p. Llandough, co. Glam., comprising a dwelling house adjoining the churchyard, 1811 (D153/1-2), land (19 ..., - National Library of Wales Archives and Manuscripts<https://archives.library.wales/index.php/deeds-relating-to-premises-in-p-llandough-co-glam-comprising-dwelling-house-adjoining-churchyard-1811-d153-1-2-land-19>.  You should contact the NLW for access or copies.

13. DXJF/64/27–32 full sketch bundle — notes seen; confirm sketches and refs.
We believe that the specific references within the bundle 27-32 that would be of interest to you are items DXJF/64/30-31: sketch of former layout of part of village of Llandough-juxta-Penarth by Mrs. Nancy Buckler of great house farm, Llandough, with notes c1950 [1 card, 1 paper]. Is that what you have already seen?  Other items in the bundle do not relate to Llandough.

19. Penarth estate/tithe/OS/boundary plans of the house plot (not Cowbridge 1924/38).

You can view the tithe map online here: Tithe Maps of Wales<https://places.library.wales/viewer/4622411#?cv=&h=95&xywh=10530%2C8509%2C3549%2C1196> along with the accompanying apportionment: Tithe Maps of Wales<https://places.library.wales/viewer/4542756#?cv=1&h=95&xywh=-507%2C-68%2C2508%2C897>.
