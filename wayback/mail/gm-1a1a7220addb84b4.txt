Estate and surrounding-area forms: “Cedwyn Estate”; “Cydwin”; “Cydfin Farm or Ty Mawr Farm, (107 a.)”; “Cedfin or Ty Mawr Farm”; and “Cwm Cydfin Wood”. “Cedwyn Estate” is the family’s working name for the wider historic estate, not an established period name. The Bute catalogue range 1552 - 1824 is not a specific date for its Ty Mawr wording; the 1824 Stewart survey records “Cedfin or Ty Mawr Farm”; and “Cwm Cydfin Wood” appears in a 1902 Bute sale catalogue.
