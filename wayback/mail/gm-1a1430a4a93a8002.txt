Dear Owen and Nia,

Thank you, Owen, for speaking with me. I have now examined the catalogue
information for another item held by the National Library of Wales, and it
helps clarify why I need to distinguish the different recordings,
broadcasts and later publications.

I want to establish what happened to the reporting, what actually aired,
what survives, and when the material was disclosed or made publicly
accessible. I am raising concerns and asking questions, not asserting that
deliberate censorship or a special non-disclosure agreement has been proved.

*The omissions and editorial questions*

My principal concern is that the coverage I have seen does not convey the
full human circumstances of the events. In particular, three infant
children and the fact that my mother was pregnant at the time appear to
have been omitted.

I am also concerned about the apparently abrupt edit following the
reference to the property as prime development land overlooking Cardiff
Bay. I would like to establish whether that cut appeared in the original
broadcast or was introduced when a later viewing copy was edited.

There is a further personal dimension involving Nicola Heywood-Thomas, the
presenter identified in the material. For a period, she was like a second
mother to me. That connection is important to my understanding of the
story, although I recognise that the existence of a personal relationship
does not, in itself, establish what the broadcaster knew or was required to
include. I would like to know whether footage involving her was broadcast,
whether other versions survive, and whether any relevant editorial or
confidentiality record exists.

*The chronology I need to reconcile*

The following are separate events and records, and I would be grateful for
your help establishing their precise relationship.

   -

   *30 November 1988 — eviction and injunction coverage.* The National
   Library of Wales Watch and Listen catalogue has an item entitled *Eviction
   News Material, 30 November 1988*:
   https://gwylio-gwrando.llyfrgell.cymru/detail/507738. Its catalogue
   dates recording and broadcast to 30 November. It describes a one-minute HTV
   Wales Betacam item involving Miles Fletcher, bailiffs removing furniture,
   removal vans and [member of the public] leaving the farm. The abstract refers to a
   High Court injunction preventing demolition.

   Crucially, the cataloguer says “Not viewed”. The tape contains unrelated
   clips, the position of this particular clip is unknown, and the description
   may have been taken from the broadcaster’s log. The record does not supply
   a catalogue-creation date or online-publication date.
   -

   *5 December 1988 — separate demolition report.* I am investigating a
   distinct *Wales at Six* demolition headline/report and the associated
   off-transmission recording. I do not want the 30 November eviction and
   injunction item mistaken for the 5 December demolition coverage. I would
   like to establish whether the latter report aired, whether the surviving
   material represents the complete broadcast, and whether scripts, running
   orders, transmission logs or rushes survive.
   -

   *6 December 1988 — demolition.* This is the recorded date of the
   farmhouse demolition in the heritage record. The event date should be kept
   separate from filming, broadcast and archival dates.
   -

   *25 July 2025 — National Library of Wales YouTube publication.* The
   viewing copy I have identified as 4GXmR7mAas0 is associated with this
   publication date. The relevant link is https://youtu.be/4GXmR7mAas0. I
   understand the online publication was by the National Library of Wales, not
   an ITV upload. I would like the uploader and publication metadata retained
   and checked, and the exact relationship between this copy and the catalogue
   item above established.
   -

   *Archive donation and digitisation.* The National Library of Wales holds
   ITV Wales/HTV material following the archive donation announced in 2012. I
   would like to know whether the specific recordings under discussion were
   included in that transfer, and whether their individual accession,
   digitisation and catalogue histories are available.
   -

   *My disclosure requests and subsequent contacts.* I would like the
   requests, responses, referrals and any internal handling records reconciled
   against these individual items. A later public upload does not, by itself,
   answer whether the footage was previously disclosed to me or when the
   Library’s catalogue entry became accessible online.

I also note that Owen’s reported reference to a December 2025 YouTube
publication does not match the 25 July 2025 date associated with the
viewing copy identified above. These dates should not be assigned to the
same item until we establish precisely which video was meant.

*What I would appreciate from ITV Wales*

Please help establish:

   1. The transmission date, programme, duration and running order for the
   1988 eviction/injunction report and the separate 5 December demolition
   report.
   2. Whether ITV holds the complete broadcasts, off-transmission masters,
   scripts, rushes, outtakes, edit histories or other versions.
   3. Whether the abrupt cut around the property’s development value was
   present in the original broadcast or belongs to a later edit.
   4. Whether relevant footage involving Nicola Heywood-Thomas aired, and
   whether any alternative version survives.
   5. Whether any relevant editorial instruction, contractual restriction
   or case-specific non-disclosure agreement exists. I am asking whether such
   a record exists, not asserting that one does.
   6. What ITV records show about receiving, routing and handling my
   disclosure requests.
   7. Whether ITV can identify the specific items transferred to the
   National Library of Wales and provide any available provenance or accession
   information.

I would also welcome guidance on the best way to coordinate these enquiries
with Beryl Evans and the National Library of Wales Enquiries team,
particularly concerning the creation and first online publication of
catalogue record 507738 and the search for separate 5 December material.

The Library’s stated restrictions on viewing original media and reusing
footage may be entirely routine. They do not, by themselves, establish
concealment or unusual treatment. Equally, the existence of a public
catalogue entry does not settle the questions about broadcast completeness,
the history of my disclosure requests or the publication dates of
individual items.

I am seeking a reliable chronology and an accurate account of the surviving
evidence. If any part of my understanding is mistaken, I would welcome a
clear correction supported by the relevant record. If a record cannot be
found or no longer survives, please distinguish that from a question that
has not yet been searched.

Thank you for your help. Please let me know who will coordinate the
enquiries and which records ITV can realistically investigate.

Kind regards,
