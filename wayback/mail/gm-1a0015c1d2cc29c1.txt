FOI Team,

Thank you for your detailed response and for undertaking the additional
enquiries.

I appreciate the further information you have provided concerning the
searches undertaken. However, I would like to request an internal review
under the Freedom of Information Act 2000.

My concern is not simply that no objects have been identified. It is
whether the searches undertaken have fully addressed the scope of my
original request, particularly the documentary evidence concerning the
provenance, acquisition, transfer, ownership, identification and dispersal
of material originating from Great House Farm (Ty Mawr).

I would be grateful if the internal review could consider the following
points.

*1. Information identified concerning Great House Farm*

Your response states that the additional enquiries identified “some
information relating generally to Great House Farm and the surrounding
area”.

Please could you identify what categories of information this refers to
and, where it is not exempt, provide the relevant recorded information or
explain where it can be accessed?

This appears potentially relevant to the original request even if it does
not itself identify furniture or household objects.

*2. Scope of the archived electronic search*

You state that the Information Technology team reviewed “archived
electronic systems and records retained by the Museum”.

Please could the review clarify:

   - which archived systems were searched;
   - the approximate date ranges covered;
   - whether historical email or correspondence archives were searched;
   - whether legacy collections-management databases were searched;
   - whether databases or systems preceding the current CMS were searched;
   and
   - whether any archived systems were unavailable, incomplete or incapable
   of being searched.

*3. Correspondence and administrative records*

My original request expressly sought correspondence concerning the
identification, recovery, ownership or location of objects, as well as
correspondence with the Bute Estate, Cardiff Council, Cardiff Castle and
other public authorities.

Your response refers generally to “associated collection documentation” and
“administrative records”, but does not make clear whether historical
correspondence files were specifically searched.

Please could the internal review establish whether searches were made of:

   - curator correspondence;
   - departmental correspondence files;
   - donor correspondence;
   - acquisition correspondence;
   - disposal correspondence;
   - loan correspondence;
   - provenance correspondence;
   - correspondence with Cardiff Council;
   - correspondence with Cardiff Castle;
   - correspondence with the Bute Estate;
   - correspondence with other museums or public authorities; and
   - any relevant institutional or external correspondence indexes.

*4. Search terms*

Thank you for identifying many of the search terms used.

Please could the internal review confirm whether the search also included
combinations and variants involving:

   - “Bute Estate”
   - “Bute”
   - “Cardiff Castle”
   - “Cardiff Council”
   - “Great House Farm”
   - “Ty Mawr”
   - “Great House”
   - “Llandough”
   - “Buckler”
   - “Williams”
   - “Mary Williams”
   - “Mary Doreen Williams”
   - “household effects”
   - “contents”
   - “furniture”
   - “furnishings”
   - “heirlooms”
   - “fixtures”
   - “estate”
   - “dispersal”
   - “recovery”
   - “provenance”
   - “ownership”
   - “transfer”
   - “demolition”

I appreciate that historical records may not have been consistently
indexed, which is why I would be grateful for confirmation of the
methodology used.

*5. Transfer, disposal and destruction*

Your response states:

“We are also not aware of any relevant collection records relating to such
material having been transferred to another institution or destroyed.”

Please could the internal review clarify whether this conclusion is based
upon:

a. a search of transfer/accession-out records;

b. a review of retention and disposal records;

c. enquiries with relevant collections staff;

d. review of archival transfer documentation; or

e. institutional knowledge rather than a documentary search.

I am particularly interested in distinguishing between “no record exists”
and “no record was identified in the systems searched”.

*6. The original request concerning external institutions*

The original request was deliberately framed broadly because the question
concerns possible dispersal of the contents of a demolished historic
property.

It expressly included records concerning Cardiff Council, Cardiff Castle,
the Bute Estate, museums, Arts Council England, DCMS, RCEWA and other
public authorities.

I would therefore be grateful if the review could confirm whether records
of communications or transactions involving those bodies were searched
within Amgueddfa Cymru's records, rather than simply searching the Museum's
object catalogue for Great House Farm objects.

*7. Advice and assistance*

You have stated that Cardiff Council and Cardiff Castle “would appear to be
the organisations most likely to hold records relating to furniture,
furnishings or other movable property associated with Great House Farm”.

I appreciate that you cannot transfer another authority's records to me.
However, could you please provide any further information available to the
Museum as to *which particular department, collection, archive or record
series at Cardiff Council or Cardiff Castle is most likely to contain such
material*?

That would materially assist me in directing subsequent FOI and archival
enquiries.

I am grateful for the additional work already undertaken. I am requesting
the internal review because I would like the record to be clear as to
precisely what was searched, what information was identified, and what the
negative result does and does not establish.

I am not asking Amgueddfa Cymru to determine any allegation concerning the
historical ownership of Great House Farm. The purpose of the review is
simply to ensure that the documentary search corresponds fully with the
scope of the original FOI request.

Please treat this email as my formal request for an internal review of FOI
2026-020.

Regards,


Requester 



Williams / Buckler Family Estate
