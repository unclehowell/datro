Dear Requester,



Many thanks for contacting WTSWW with this interesting enquiry, although I am afraid we are not in a great position to help.



We don't currently have any data relating to this site in the office - we may have had some historically but some of the relevant paperwork was lost in a fire in about 2003.



If we did have any data remaining after that, I gather it would most likely have been in reports by Mary Gillham, in which case it would have been passed to SEWBReC, who digitised her archive.



We can't be sure that there is anything relevant in there, but it's definitely worth looking into - perhaps get in touch with them to enquire if you haven't already? https://www.sewbrec.org.uk/

Dear Wildlife Trust of South and West Wales,



I am researching the history of Great House Farm, Llandough-juxta-Penarth, and of the farmland and woodland between Llandough and Leckwith, Vale of Glamorgan.



The Trust's own history records that its Glamorgan predecessor began as the Glamorgan County Naturalists' Trust, founded in January 1961, later the Glamorgan Wildlife Trust, and that the present Trust was formed by merger in 2002. Please could you tell me:



(a) whether the Trust holds, or knows the present location of, any site files, survey records, maps or photographs for Llandough-juxta-Penarth or Leckwith (including the wooded slopes between the two) dating from about 1961 to 2002;

(b) whether any of those records mention Great House Farm, or record who was consulted as owner or occupier of the land; and

(c) if the older Glamorgan records have been deposited with an archive, which archive holds them and under what reference.
