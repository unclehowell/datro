Dear Glamorgan Federation of Women's Institutes,

I am researching the history of Llandough-juxta-Penarth, near Penarth, and in particular of Great House Farm in the village.

Glamorgan Archives holds, under reference DXNO16/2, a Llandough Women's Institute scrapbook of cuttings, photographs and memorabilia. People's Collection Wales gives the same reference for a Llandough Women's Institute scrapbook, 1973-1988, which it says was compiled in response to a project from the Federation's Environment and Agriculture sub-committee, "Fifty Years of Farming or Land Use in your Locality, 1937-1987".
