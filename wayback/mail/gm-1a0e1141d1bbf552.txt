Diolch yn fawr. Thank you for the records, the descriptors and the

One correction of place is required.

The request concerns Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough near Penarth /
Cardiff. Among the disclosed PAS material is at least one record for a
different property of the same name: Great House Farm, Llandough near
Cowbridge (including 2022.18.1, NGR SS9934672805, medieval lead Romanesque
mount). There is also a Great House farm entry at Llandow. Those are not
the site named in the request. Two Llandoughs, and more than one Great
House Farm, sit in the same county. The homonym is easy to miss and
material.

Please treat the Cowbridge / Llandow entries as out of scope for this
request unless you hold recorded information showing they were, at any
time, indexed, cited, or treated as if they related to Great House Farm,
Llandough (Penarth / Cardiff). If any such cross-attribution exists in
historic files, catalogues, correspondence, or search notes, that recorded
information is within scope and I ask that it be disclosed.

If labels or findspot fields are now amended to distinguish the two farms,
please do not overwrite the original wording without retaining the prior
form. I ask that any correction be recorded as a dated amendment, with the
earlier descriptor preserved, so the sequence of how the records stood when
searches were first run remains inspectable. Please do not destroy, weed,
or replace the versions as disclosed on 22 September 2026.

One small point from my email of 26 August also remains. As item 7 I asked
for any recorded information showing the basis on which the Museum
concluded that the identified records were unrelated to the reported
nineteenth-century discovery. If that conclusion was reached by staff
judgement and not written down, please simply confirm that no recorded
