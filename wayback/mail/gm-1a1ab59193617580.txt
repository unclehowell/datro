Dear Requester,

Environmental Information Regulations Reference No:  2026/224580

Thank you for your request for information about:

  *   Whether HSE or HM Factory Inspectorate holds any of the following for the period 1 October 1988 to 30 June 1989:

1. any notification received about demolition or building operations at this site under the legislation then in force, including the name of the contractor and of the person or company for whom the work was done;
2. any record of an inspector's visit, inspection, enforcement action or reported incident at the site in that period;
3. any index or register entry showing that such a notification or visit was recorded, even if the file itself no longer exists.

If no information is held, please tell me:
(a) which record series and systems were searched, including any historic paper indexes;
(b) the retention period that applied to such notifications and inspection records in 1988–89; and
(c) whether any transfer or disposal record exists for them.

Your request was received on November 3rd 2026 and I am dealing with it under the terms of the Environmental Information Regulations 2004.

I can confirm that your request is being dealt with under the Environmental Information Regulations 2004 (EIR).  However I must advise you that following a search of our paper and electronic records, I have established that the information you requested:

Is not held by the Health & Safety Executive
a) Record systems COIN, F10 and Content Manager were searched
(b) The current retention period is 7 years, thus any records from 1988-89 are no longer available
(c) There are no transfer or disposal records
General guidance on how the Health & Safety Executive deals with requests under the Freedom of Information Act and Environmental Information Regulations can be found on the HSE website at http://www.hse.gov.uk/foi/index.htm
