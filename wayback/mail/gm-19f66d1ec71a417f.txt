Ms Longford,

Before responding to my earlier email, please may I add the following to my
enquiry, even if you require addition time to respond due to this addition
to my enquiry;

In addition to my email of 2 July, there are a small number of further
points which I would be grateful if you could address as they arise
directly from comparing the original response with the internal review.

The original response of 11 June 2026 stated:

"Any other associated records would have been either transferred to the
Royal Commission on the Ancient & Historical Monuments of Wales for
permanent preservation, or destroyed under the Public Record Act 1958."

However, the internal review of 2 July 2026 states instead that:

"There is also no evidence that any relevant files have been transferred to
the RCAHMW."

and

"It is possible that the file was destroyed before it was ever updated on
our electronic filing system."

These appear to be materially different positions.

Accordingly, I would be grateful if you could clarify the following:

   1.

   What evidence supported the original statement that any associated
   records would have been transferred to the RCAHMW or destroyed under the
   Public Records Act 1958?
   2.

   Following the internal review, does Welsh Government now accept that
   there is no evidence that any relevant records were transferred to the
   RCAHMW?
   3.

   Does Welsh Government hold any transfer lists, accession records,
   archive schedules or other documentation recording whether records relating
   to Great House Farm were ever transferred to the RCAHMW?
   4.

   If no such documentation exists, on what evidential basis was the
   original statement regarding transfer to the RCAHMW made?
   5.

   Likewise, if there is no destruction register, disposal log or audit
   trail relating to the relevant file, on what evidential basis has
   destruction been suggested?

Finally, the original response stated as fact that Great House Farm was
visited by Cadw inspectors and did not meet the statutory listing criteria.
The internal review, however, confirms that no assessment, report or case
file has been located.

Could you therefore identify the documentary evidence upon which that
conclusion was based, or alternatively confirm whether the statement in the
original response was an inference drawn from the existence of the two
photographs rather than from any surviving assessment records.

These points are intended simply to clarify the historical record and to
reconcile what appear to be two materially different explanations contained
within the original response and the internal review.

Thank you again for your assistance, and I look forward to your response.

Yours sincerely,


Sion Buckler (Ret'd Sergenant)

Royal Signals Cyber & Electronic Warfare Professional & Life Member
