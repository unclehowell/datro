Dear Mr Buckler,

Thank you for your email of 16 October 2026 which has been passed to me. Many of the links you have embedded into your email are unfortunately inaccessible, so please make any documentation you wish to send to us available in a pdf or word document format.

I note your request that bp entities place a hold on records relating to Great House Farm/Ty Mawr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth for the period 1969 to date, pending consideration of a possible claim.

Having reviewed the position, we are not aware of any outstanding or live cause of action that bp or any related entity would need to answer in relation to this property. The dispute concerning possession of Great House Farm was the subject of litigation between BP Properties Ltd and Mrs Buckler which was determined by the Court of Appeal in BP Properties Ltd v Buckler (1987). That litigation concluded with the Court of Appeal's judgment, and BP Properties Ltd went on to obtain possession of the site, as your own email records.

In the absence of any explanation of what new claim you believe may now arise, or against which legal entity, we are not in a position to understand why a document preservation obligation would exist, and we are therefore not able to confirm that a preservation hold of the kind you describe will be put in place.

If you do believe you have a specific legal claim against any bp related entity, we would invite you to set out the nature of any interest you claim to hold, the basis of any potential claim, including the legal grounds relied upon and the relief sought so that it can be properly considered.

We look forward to hearing from you with the above information.

Yours sincerely,

Jack Sheehy
