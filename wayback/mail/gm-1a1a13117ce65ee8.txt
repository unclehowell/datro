Dear Network Rail FOI team,

I am researching the history of land at Llandough-juxta-Penarth, Vale of Glamorgan, including Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr) and the neighbouring woodland, which was the subject of BP Properties Ltd v Buckler (Court of Appeal, 1987).

Glamorgan Archives holds a Penarth Harbour, Dock and Railway drawing titled "Longitudinal section of open drain on Lord Bute's land near Llandough" (DABP/8/3/2/89, early 20th century). I am also trying to confirm whether a drainage agreement dated 15 May 1924 involving the Marquess of Bute is referred to in the relevant land records and, if so, where that drainage ran and on what terms.

Under the Freedom of Information Act 2000 and, so far as the information is environmental, the Environmental Information Regulations 2004, please could you tell me whether Network Rail holds, and if so provide copies of:

1. Any deed, agreement, licence or easement for a drain, culvert or watercourse between the Penarth Harbour, Dock and Railway Company (or the Taff Vale Railway or Great Western Railway as successors) and the Marquess of Bute, his trustees or agents, or the Corbett family, at or near Llandough or Cogan, 1880–1948.
2. The railway land plans or terrier plans for the line between Grangetown and Cogan/Penarth that show adjoining owners and drains near Llandough.
3. Any record, register entry or plan of the drain shown in DABP/8/3/2/89, including later maintenance or diversion.

If no information is held, please say so. If the records are held by another body (for example a records store, the Historic Railways Estate or an archive), I would be grateful for its name.
