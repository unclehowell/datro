Dear CUCAP Librarian,
I am researching the history, land use and occupation of Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough-juxta-Penarth, Vale of Glamorgan, a holding beside St Dochdwy's Church that extends towards Leckwith Woods. The farm was the subject of BP Properties Ltd v Buckler (Court of Appeal, 1987), and dated aerial images are among the few independent records of how the house, garden, green and woodland were used over time.
Your online catalogue lists 2,473 records for the Vale of Glamorgan, most of them not yet digitised. I understand the Collection is currently closed, so I would like to register an interest and ask whether a catalogue search could be made for any vertical or oblique photographs covering:
1. Llandough village and church, centred on National Grid reference ST
1685 7297; and
2. the adjoining land west towards Leckwith Woods and Leckwith (roughly ST 155–175, 725–745).
For each frame found, I would be grateful for the catalogue/frame reference, the date flown, whether it is vertical or oblique, and whether a digitised or reference copy exists. Images from 1947 to the 1990s are of most interest.
If the search cannot be made while the Collection is closed, please could you tell me when it is expected to reopen, or whether the catalogue can be consulted in another way.
