Bev,

Thank you for clarifying the position regarding the Freedom of Information
Act.

I understand that coroners and their records are not subject to the FOI
access regime. I am therefore not seeking to pursue the matter as an FOI
request if that is not the appropriate route.

My primary purpose is to establish whether either of these deaths was ever
referred to the coroner and, if so, whether any surviving record exists and
what lawful route is available for an interested family member to obtain
information from it.

I would therefore be grateful if you could clarify the following, insofar
as you are able:

   1.

   Whether your office has any record or index indicating that either
   William (Billy) Buckler, who died at Llandough Hospital on or about 29 May
   1990, or Mary Doreen Williams, whose death was registered in March 1983 in
   South Glamorgan (Vol. 28, p. 3029), was reported to or dealt with by the
   coroner;
   2.

   If either death was the subject of a coroner's investigation, whether an
   inquest, post-mortem examination or other coroner's investigation took
   place;
   3.

   Whether any historical record relating to either death remains in the
   custody of the coroner's service, or whether such records have been
   transferred to another archive, authority or successor body;
   4.

   If records exist but cannot be disclosed directly, what procedure is
   available for a descendant or other interested person to establish the
   basic factual information concerning the case;
   5.

   If your office has no record whatsoever of either death, whether that
   means that no coroner's record exists, or simply that the surviving records
   are no longer held by your office.

I am particularly interested in establishing the documentary chain rather
than necessarily seeking disclosure of confidential medical information. A
confirmation that a death was, or was not, referred to the coroner would
itself be useful.

If you are not the appropriate office to answer these historical-record
questions, I would be grateful if you could identify the relevant archive,
successor body or other custodian to whom I should direct the enquiry.

Thank you again for your assistance.

Regards,


Requester
