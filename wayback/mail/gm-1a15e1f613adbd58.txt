As previously explained, the Crown Prosecution Service (CPS) cannot accept or consider information, evidence or other material sent directly to us by members of the public. If you have information that you believe may be relevant or wish to raise concerns about how the matter is being dealt with, you should contact the police directly. They will be able to consider the information you provide and advise you further.

I appreciate that this may not be the response you were hoping for, but I hope this explains why the CPS is unable to assist you further.
