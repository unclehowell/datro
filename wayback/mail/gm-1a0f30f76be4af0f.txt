Freedom of Information Act 2000 Request

Sir/Madam,

I am making this request under the Freedom of Information Act 2000.

I seek all recorded information held concerning furniture, furnishings, antiques, fixtures, heirlooms and other movable property originating from Great House Farm (Ty Mawr), Llandough, including any and all items we received from Bute Estate, Cardiff Castle, Cardiff Council.  Weather it be museums, archives or any other public body. I know museums have artifacts from our land in Llandough. 

Specifically, I request copies of all:
-Inventories, catalogues and asset registers.
-Acquisition, disposal, loan and transfer records.
-Provenance files and ownership records.
-Photographs of items identified as originating from Great House Farm.
-Correspondence concerning the identification, recovery, ownership or location of such items.
-Internal reports, legal advice and memoranda relating to Great House Farm furniture or household effects.
-Correspondence mentioning the Buckler or Williams families in connection with these items.
-Records concerning any efforts by Cardiff Council or Cardiff Castle to trace, recover or secure items believed to have originated from Great House Farm.
-Correspondence with the Bute Estate, museums, the Department for Culture, Media and Sport, the Reviewing Committee on the Export of Works of Art and Objects of Cultural Interest (RCEWA), Arts Council England or any other public authority regarding objects from Great House Farm.

For context, I am allegeing that my family was unlawfully dispossessed of Great House Farm through fraud, and that furniture and other historic possessions belonging to the family were consequently dispersed. It's possible the bailiffs from BP were also SAS soldiers. We are still gathering actionable intelligence. 

I appreciate that the Freedom of Information Act does not require the authority to determine the merits of that allegation. However, it is directly relevant to understanding why I seek the recorded information.

I am currently developing a full-scale replica of Great House Farm in Patagonia, Argentina as a museum dedicated to preserving the history of the house, its occupants and its contents. Should records identify surviving items from the farm, whether held by Cardiff Council, Cardiff Castle, museums or other public institutions, I intend to pursue their lawful return or transfer so they may ultimately be reunited with the reconstructed Great House Farm museum. This statement is provided solely as context for my request and does not seek a determination under the Freedom of Information Act.

If any information is withheld, please identify each exemption relied upon and explain why it applies, including the outcome of any public interest test where required.

If another public authority is more likely to hold the requested information, I would be grateful if you would transfer the request or advise me accordingly.

I look forward to your response within the statutory time limit.

Yours faithfully,

Sion Buckler (Ret'd Sergeant)
