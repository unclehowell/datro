Dear Requester,

Thank you for your emails dated 29 October 2026 regarding Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth.

We have considered the information contained within your email and the additional search terms you have provided.

The Environmental Information Regulations 2004 (EIR) provide a right of access to recorded environmental information held by a public authority. Regulation 2(1) defines environmental information and includes information concerning matters such as the state of the environment, factors affecting the environment, and measures or activities affecting or likely to affect the environment.

Having considered your correspondence, we do not consider that the information you have requested regarding the disconnection, shut off, cap, termination or otherwise interference with the water supply to Great House Farm constitutes a request for environmental information falling within the definition at Regulation 2(1) of the EIR.

The information requested relates to the disconnection of a specific property. The fact that information concerns a water supply to a property does not, of itself, make it “environmental information” for the purposes of regulation 2(1) of the Environmental Information Regulations 2004. The information must fall within one or more of the categories in regulation 2(1). The requested information concerns the fact and circumstances of the property's disconnection. It does not describe the state of any element of the environment, such as water, air, soil or land, within regulation 2(1)(a), nor does it concern a factor such as a substance, energy, noise, radiation, waste, emission, discharge or other release which affects or is likely to affect those elements within regulation 2(1)(b). Further, the disconnection is not a measure or activity affecting or likely to affect environmental elements or factors, or as one designed to protect them, within regulation 2(1)(c). The remaining limbs of regulation 2(1), concerning reports on environmental legislation, relevant economic analyses, and human health and safety or built structures insofar as they are affected by environmental elements, factors, measures or activities, are likewise not engaged.

As the request is directed to the disconnection of this property rather than to any environmental state, effect, factor, measure or activity within regulation 2(1), the information does not fall within the definition of environmental information under the EIR.

Even if this information were considered to be environmental information for the purposes of the Regulations, DCWW would have to refuse the request under Regulation 12(4)(a) as we do not hold the information requested. We do not hold the historical information sought. In accordance with our Retention Policy, any customer or property records from the 1980s that may have existed from that period would fall outside of our data retention periods., and we would no longer hold records dating back to that time. We are also unaware of any other public authority that may hold this information.

For this reason, we are unable to carry out the additional historical searches requested or provide an audit trail identifying search terms and systems in respect of information that is no longer held.

Your email also requested confirmation as to whether any surviving historic records, indexes, work-order records or archived material relating to Great House Farm/Ty Mawr exist. Some such information could constitute environmental information, depending on the nature of the information held and whether it fell within the definition of environmental information in Regulation 2(1). However, for the reasons set out above, we do not hold any historic records, indexes, work-order records or archived material falling within the scope of your request. We are therefore unable to determine whether any such information, if it existed, would constitute environmental information. In any event, as we do not hold the requested information, the request would fall to be refused under Regulation 12(4)(a). We are also unaware of any other public authority that may hold this information.

We hope this clarifies our position.
