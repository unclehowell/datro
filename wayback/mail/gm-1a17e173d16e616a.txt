Thank you for your email of 21 October 2026. I note that you would like some advice on four points relating to submission of FOI complaints to the ICO. I will address each of your points in turn.

1. I note that you have a query about whether the ICO can consider complaints about a public authority’s compliance with section 46 of the FOI Code of Practice. Unfortunately, the ICO is unable to consider complaints about public authority’s compliance with the section 46 as part of a section 50 complaint. For further information on the way the Information Commission monitors public authorities adherence to the section 46 Code of Practice, please see the following guidance: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/foi/freedom-of-information-and-environmental-information-regulations/section-46-code-of-practice-records-management/#monitors

2. Where a public authority deletes, alters or conceals information within the scope of a request with the aim of preventing the disclosure of that information, the public authority may have committed a section 77 offence under FOIA or regulation 19 offence under the EIR.

If you consider that a public authority has committed a section 77 offence when responding to a request for information you have made, you can submit a complaint to us using our FOI complaint form: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/foi-and-eir-complaints/foi
