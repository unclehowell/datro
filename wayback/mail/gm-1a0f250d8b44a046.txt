2. Documentary title sequence and suspected criminality

The public evidence index is available at:

https://greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com/

The material assembled there identifies a documentary sequence which, in my
present assessment, raises questions as to whether the family's historic
proprietary interest in the farmhouse parcel was displaced through the use
of records, possession evidence and registration material relating to a
different or wider parcel, and whether subsequent registration and dealings
then relied upon that resulting title position.

For convenience, I refer on the website to this as the "synthetic title"
issue. That is a description of the documentary pattern I have identified,
rather than an assumption that criminal liability has already been
established.

The distinction is important because the 1987 Court of Appeal proceedings
concerned possession, limitation and the October 1974 correspondence. My
concern is that those proceedings did not determine the underlying
documentary root of title to the farmhouse parcel now represented by the
registered title history.

3. 1955 possession order and the neighbouring parcel

A further issue requiring examination is the relationship between the 1955
possession order and the land historically occupied and claimed by the
Williams/Buckler family.

My evidence indicates that the 1955 order related to a neighbouring parcel
rather than the family's core farmhouse holding. I am concerned that
subsequent possession and development activity may nevertheless have
treated that order, or the associated possession history, as extending to
land outside the parcel to which it actually related.

Recent excavation and works on the former family land make this a live
evidential issue. I ask that the underlying order, plans, descriptions and
subsequent land use be considered against the documentary title sequence
rather than assuming that the parcels were identical.

4. Possible loss or destruction of relevant records

I hold dated photographs concerning works which removed a commemorative
tree planted by my great-grandfather on or adjoining the former Great House
Farm / Church View Close land.

I have made a narrowed FOI request to the Vale of Glamorgan Council (case
00211484) seeking the instruction, works order, authorisation and any
record linking the tree to the Williams/Buckler family or to the 1988
demolition.

More generally, I am concerned that parts of the planning and land-history
record which could bear upon the ownership history are incomplete or
unavailable. I am not presently asking the CPS to draw an inference from
that absence. I ask that any potentially relevant records held by public
authorities or other custodians be identified and preserved while the
police and other investigations are ongoing.
