FOI Team,

I write in response to your disclosure dated 23 September 2026 concerning
my Freedom of Information request regarding Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr and
records relating to BP Properties Ltd v Buckler.

I appreciate the additional catalogue references and the release
information provided. However, your response also identifies a number of
matters concerning the scope, timing and methodology of the searches
undertaken which require clarification.

Please therefore treat this email as a *formal request for internal review*,
as well as a request for clarification of the matters set out below.
1. Physical accession/transfer of YHL/PO/JO/10/11/2536

Your response states that:

“The series that this record formed part of were released on our catalogue
on Discovery on 20th May 2026.”

You also state that TNA *holds transfer paperwork* for:

*YHL/PO/JO/10/11/2536 – Judicial Papers A-C 1-63*

Please therefore confirm, insofar as the information is recorded in TNA's
transfer/accession records:

   - the date on which the physical records comprising YHL/PO/JO/10/11/2536
   were transferred into the custody of The National Archives;
   - the accession/transfer reference associated with that transfer, if
   recorded;
   - whether the transfer paperwork identifies the transferring body or
   institution;
   - whether the physical records were already in TNA's custody during my
   earlier enquiries in August 2026, notwithstanding that their catalogue
   description was only released on Discovery on 20 May 2026; and
   - whether the *20 May 2026* date refers solely to publication/release of
   catalogue metadata on Discovery, rather than the date on which the physical
   records entered TNA custody.

I am particularly seeking to distinguish *physical custody/accession*
from *catalogue
publication*, as these are not necessarily the same event.
2. Scope and methodology of the initial search

Your response states:

“The initial response from our records enquiries team looked for the four
terms (‘Great House Farm’, ‘Ty Mawr’, ‘Llandough’ or ‘Cedfin’) within the
PRO and RW series and did not find any relevant information.”

My original request, however, expressly identified a number of alternative
search routes, including:

   - Western Ground Rents Ltd;
   - BP Pension Trust Ltd;
   - BP Properties Ltd;
   - Mountjoy Ltd;
   - BP Properties Ltd v Buckler;
   - the 1982–1983 registration;
   - the 1980–1988 government/public campaign;
   - the 1988 eviction and demolition;
   - Cadw and central-government communications;
   - ECHR Application 14464/88;
   - transfer, destruction or disposal records; and
   - HMC9/311 and related archival references.

Your present response confirms that a wider catalogue search was
subsequently undertaken and that searching *BP Properties Ltd* produced the
House of Lords judicial record.

Please therefore clarify:

   - whether the *initial search* was in fact limited to the four
   property/location terms and the PRO/RW series stated in your response;
   - why the named corporate litigant *BP Properties Ltd*, expressly
   identified in my request, was not included in that initial search;
   - whether any search log, enquiry record, search worksheet or other
   recorded audit trail exists showing the terms, series and dates searched at
   each stage; and
   - whether the later wider search was materially different from the
   initial search in a way that explains why the judicial record was not
   identified earlier.

I recognise that TNA cannot necessarily undertake an unlimited historical
research exercise. My question here is narrower: *what searches were
actually undertaken, when, and against which terms/series?*
3. Search undertaken after 20 May 2026

Your response states that the relevant parliamentary series had been
released onto Discovery on *20 May 2026*, yet the subsequent correspondence
records a negative result until the search parameters were expanded to
include the named legal/corporate party.

Please explain, insofar as this is recorded:

   - whether any searches of Discovery or relevant TNA catalogue data
   concerning this matter were conducted between *20 May 2026 and 13 August
   2026*;
   - if so, whether those searches included “BP Properties Ltd” or the case
   title *BP Properties Ltd v Buckler*; and
   - if no such search was undertaken before 13 August 2026, please confirm
   that fact.

This is important because the chronology appears to be:

*20 May 2026 – catalogue release of the relevant series*
*earlier enquiries – negative result*
*13 August 2026 – explicit instruction to search the named corporate/legal
party*
*subsequent wider search – judicial record identified*

I am seeking to establish the documentary search history accurately, rather
than infer the reason for the earlier negative result.
4. Transfer, retention, destruction or disposal records

Your response states that TNA holds transfer paperwork for
YHL/PO/JO/10/11/2536 and has disclosed three documents concerning its
release/manifest arrangements.

Please confirm whether those records contain, or whether TNA otherwise
holds, information showing:

   - the date of transfer or accession;
   - the previous custody/location of the records;
   - whether the records were transferred directly from the Parliamentary
   Archives or another repository;
   - whether any associated material was excluded, withheld, destroyed,
   disposed of or retained elsewhere; and
   - whether the transfer documentation identifies any earlier repository
   or custodial history.

I am not asking TNA to reconstruct the contents of uncatalogued material. I
am asking what *recorded provenance and custody information* TNA holds
concerning the records now identified.

I would also take this opportunity to ask again, if you hold any more
information I've asked for, not disclosed to me, please disclose in your
next reply. This case went before ECHR (Buckler vs United Kingdom 1989) and
their window to reopen that ruling based on such matters as concealment, is
as I understand, 6 months. It would be a grave injustice to deny or delay
or tortioustly interfere with our quest for justice, anymore than has
already been the case for my heavily dispositioned family by this whole
already very prolonged, very  traumatising matter.
5. Internal-review outcome

Please review the original response in light of the above and confirm
whether TNA considers that the searches undertaken adequately addressed the
scope of my original request.

Where TNA considers that information is not held, please distinguish where
possible between:

*(a)* information establishing that no such records were ever transferred
to TNA;
*(b)* information showing that records were transferred elsewhere;
*(c)* information showing that records were destroyed/disposed of; and
*(d)* the absence of a discoverable catalogue reference or search hit.

These are materially different conclusions for the purposes of my
documentary research.

I would be grateful for the internal-review response within 14 working days.

Regards,
