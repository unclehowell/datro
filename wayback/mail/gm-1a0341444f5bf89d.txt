Good afternoon

I have addressed each of the points raised in your correspondence based on the information available to this office.


  1.  The search was conducted using the court's current internal case management system. The proceedings referenced in your enquiry pre-date the introduction of modern electronic case management systems and would originally have been maintained as paper records. No records relating to the proceedings identified were located.
  2.  No surviving court file relating to the proceedings specified in your request has been identified. This office has not located any surviving historical finding aids, registers, order books, cause books, warrant books, index cards or similar records relating to those proceedings.
  3.  The conclusion that records of this age would not ordinarily be held is based on HMCTS records retention arrangements for possession proceedings. Under the records schedule available to this office,” paper records are retained for three years from the last meaningful transaction and then destroyed. Where applicable, records may be retained for longer if subject to litigation, audit, inquiry or other legal hold requirements. We do not hold information identifying the specific historical retention schedule applicable to the proceedings 1974.
  4.  Destroy/Delete: The record is securely destroyed (paper records shredded; electronic records permanently deleted). Many civil court records follow this route after their retention period expires.
  5.  No destruction register, destruction certificate, transfer schedule or equivalent administrative record relating to the proceedings specified has been identified by this office.

  4.  Please identify the records retention, disposal or archival policy, schedule or instruction relied upon in concluding that records of this age would no longer be held. I note that the current publicly available County Court Records Retention and Disposition Schedule specifies relatively short retention periods for possession action cases. I should therefore be grateful if you could identify the historical policy, schedule or instruction that governed the records relating to these proceedings and, if possible, indicate when they would have been scheduled for destruction or transfer.
  5.  Does HMCTS hold any administrative record of the destruction or transfer of the relevant files, such as destruction registers, certificates, transfer schedules or equivalent records?
  6.  Were any records relating to these proceedings transferred to another repository, including The National Archives, Glamorgan Archives, or any other archive? If so, I should be grateful if you would provide the destination, date and any available reference.
  7.  Even if the original case files no longer survive, do any registers, indexes or other administrative records remain recording the existence, progress or disposal of proceedings involving the Williams family, the Buckler family, Western Ground Rents Ltd, BP Pension Trust Ltd, BP Properties Ltd, or Great House Farm (Ty Mawr), Llandough?

Sir/Madam,

I wrote to you on 10 June 2026 requesting access to historical court records relating to Great House Farm (Ty Mawr), Llandough, Vale of Glamorgan. I have not received a response.

I am seeking confirmation of whether any surviving court records exist relating to possession, title, and related proceedings involving the Williams / Buckler family and the successive parties asserting title through Western Ground Rents Ltd and subsequently BP Pension Trust Ltd / BP Properties Ltd.

The proceedings and related matters I am seeking to identify include:

  *   High Court possession proceedings and the possession order of 1955, including any related enforcement records (including the enforcement action concerning the agricultural land on or around 4 July 1955);
  *   Cardiff County Court proceedings of 1962, resulting in the possession order dated 11 December 1962 (before Judge Temple Morris QC), including any related claim for mesne profits;
  *   Cardiff County Court proceedings commenced in 1974, including the part-heard hearing of 3 July 1974 at which Mary Williams asserted ownership and relied upon adverse possession / limitation arguments, the application for leave to enforce in September 1974, and subsequent correspondence concerning the proposed unilateral licence arrangement in October and November 1974;
  *   The subsequent Court of Appeal proceedings in BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] 55 P & CR 337.

I request confirmation of whether any surviving records remain, including (but not limited to):

  *   pleadings;
  *   statements of case;
  *   orders;
  *   warrants;
  *   correspondence;
  *   exhibits;
  *   judgments;
  *   case files;
  *   registers or indexes referring to these proceedings or the property.

If records remain, please advise the procedure for obtaining copies or arranging inspection, including any applicable fees.

If no records remain, please confirm what searches have been undertaken, which repositories or record systems have been checked, and whether any destruction, retention, or transfer records exist relating to these proceedings.
