## Why a Commercial Guardianship?
Rather than a traditional commercial takeover, the type of agreement would
have a responsible custodians on site. Guardianship tenancies are not new
to Councils in Wales.

Occupancy would provide:

* Active Security & Maintenance: A regular physical presence on-site to
deter anti-social behavior and immediately flag structural or environmental
issues.

* Preservation of Heritage: respect the Scheduled Ancient Monument status
of the structures. Any workspace configuration would be entirely
low-impact, fully reversible, and designed to protect the historical
integrity of the architecture.

* Sympathetic Integration: mindful of the surrounding Lavernock Point
Nature Reserve and local businesses. Small team to operate quietly,
ensuring zero disruption to local wildlife, public footpaths, or
neighboring holiday operations.

I believe that breathing new life into these historic structures through a
controlled, professional occupancy could be an excellent model for
sustainable heritage preservation.

I welcome the opportunity to discuss this concept with you and my US
business associates, to see if a mutual arrangement could be reached. Could
you please let me know if this is a proposal your organisations would be
open to exploring?
