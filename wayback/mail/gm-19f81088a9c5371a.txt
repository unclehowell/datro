Ms Daniel,

Further to my internal review request, I would like to provide a short
clarification regarding the scope of my original FOI request.

Having reviewed my original request submitted on 3 July 2026, I wish to
emphasise that the request was expressly for recorded information, not
merely for physical objects held within NLW collections.

The original request specifically sought:
inventories, catalogues and asset registers;
acquisition, disposal, loan and transfer records;
provenance files and ownership records;
photographs;
correspondence concerning identification, recovery, ownership or location;
internal reports, legal advice and memoranda;
correspondence involving the Buckler or Williams families;
correspondence with Bute Estate, Cardiff Castle, museums and other public
bodies.

Therefore, my concern is not that NLW does not hold furniture as part of a
collection. I understand that point. My concern is whether relevant
documentary records were searched across appropriate archival collections
where references to such items may exist.

For example, where NLW holds Bute Estate material relating to Ty
Mawr/Llandough, I would appreciate confirmation as to whether those
collections were searched for incidental references to:
household inventories;
fixtures;
valuations;
estate correspondence;
transfers or disposals;
provenance information;
references to occupants or contents of the farmhouse.

This clarification is provided to assist the internal review and ensure the
original request is considered in its full context.
I appreciate the catalogue references already provided and look forward to
confirmation of the searches undertaken.
Yours sincerely,

Sion Buckler (Ret'd Sgt)
