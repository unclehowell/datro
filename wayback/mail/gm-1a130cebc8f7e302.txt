Dear Requester,

Thank you for your email.

Based on the information you have provided, we believe that this is the Valuation Office map covering the property you have referred to: https://discovery.nationalarchives.gov.uk/details/r/C1667398 (IR 131/4/96).

Unfortunately, there is no way of identifying the relevant IR field book on our catalogue without additional information. We would need the income tax parish and the assessment number, which we would expect to be found via the map linked above (IR 131/4/96). We have checked the Genealogist website which has digitised some of these records, but its coverage appears not to include Glamorgan.

We have made a guess that the income tax parish is Penarth, but we would still need the assessment number of the individual farm in order to identify the right field book: https://discovery.nationalarchives.gov.uk/results/r/1?_q=penarth&_cr=IR%2058&_dss=range&_ro=any&_srt=1)

Your options for accessing information from IR 131/4/96 is to either travel to Kew to review it and ascertain the field book number yourself, or to request a paid search by TNA staff, or hire an independent researcher.

Details on the options available for paid research can be found via the following pages:

https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/paying-for-research/

A public authority is allowed to charge fees for access to information under section 9 of the FOI Act. The prices we charge are set out by the Fees Regulations under the Public Records Act (1958), and are based on recovering the costs of providing the services. Fees are typically charged for research and provision of information within open records in TNA's collection.

Information on booking a visit to The National Archives and how to register for a reader's ticket can be found here: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/visit/researching-here/booking-a-visit-to-view-documents/
