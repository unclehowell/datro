I have not previously asked HM Land Registry to record the following
construction of the title chain. I do so now as a factual statement of the
case I put on the instruments and their plans. I am not asking you to
determine ownership. I am asking you to confirm what the surviving
first-registration records show about which parcel was lodged.
The land I refer to as the House Plot is the former farmhouse, garden and
curtilage of Great House Farm, Llandough, immediately west / north-west of
St Dochdwy’s Church, later part of Church View Close, [postcode redacted].
My case on the chain is:
The holding was two parcels. The 1916 Bute agricultural tenancy (John
Williams) covered both. Farm rents and cottage rents were accounted
separately on the Bute rental listings.
After quarrying ceased in or about 1928, the House Plot was treated as
severed from the agricultural tenancy and held as a separate proprietary
parcel by those through whom I claim.
The 1938 conveyance from the Bute interest to Western Ground Rents Limited,
construed against its plan, conveyed the eastern agricultural parcel. It
did not convey the House Plot.
The May 1939 agricultural tenancy between WGR and John Williams concerned
that eastern parcel. It did not comprise the House Plot.
The 1955 enforcement papers and the 11 December 1962 Cardiff County Court
possession order against Frederick Buckler and Mary Williams (sued as Mrs
Buckler) were directed at the farmhouse and garden. Those proceedings
asserted a right to possession. They did not construe the 1877, 1938 or
later deeds and were not a conveyance of the House Plot into the WGR estate.
The 31 December 1969 conveyance (WGR to the BP Pension Trust parties), the
23 May 1975 conveyance (BP Pension Trust Limited to BP Properties Limited),
and the 1982 first registration of WA231076 could only pass such land as
then stood in that documentary estate. On the construction above, that
estate did not include the House Plot.
Title WA231076 (and WA240304, later amalgamated) nevertheless included the
House Plot. That is the alleged mistake as to extent.
Please therefore add these numbered questions to the live enquiry (also
customer-support refs 260812-6561894, 260812-6565153, 260917-6714794,

Confirm whether the 1938 Bute–WGR conveyance and plan, or the 1939 tenancy
