> With regards to which records mention things like ownership and the
> dispute, most of the newspaper article clippings naturally discuss it.
> Archive Number 6048942 provides a short history of the site and notes the
> family’s residence there and Archive Number 6048945 notes that ‘The house
> remained partially recorded because of problems created by an ownership
> dispute. The house and farm buildings were suddenly and completely
> destroyed by B. P. Properties Ltd. on 6th December 1988 amid considerable
> local controversy’. If you would like further details about the content of
> these records, they can be viewed in our Search Room, located in the
> National Library of Wales, which is open Tuesday through Thursday. Scans of
> records can also be requested, using the attached order form. We also do
> offer a limited document summary service. Please see our price list for
> details.
>

>
> In addition to these 14 records, we may hold some historical aerial
> photography which may include the site. This would likely include vertical
> aerial photography taken from a high altitude by the Royal Air Force and/or

> would like to request a search of our historic aerial photography
