Thank you for contacting the Housing Ombudsman Service.

Your email has been received and will be reviewed by a member of our casework team. If a response is required, we will contact you within 15 working days. If your query is in relation to a request under the Data Protection Act 2018 or the Freedom of Information Act 2000 please email DPO@housing-ombudsman.org.uk
