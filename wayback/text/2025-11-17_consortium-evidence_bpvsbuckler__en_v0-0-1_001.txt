Thank you for your enquiry.  We will endeavour to respond within 15 working days, unless you have selected our Priority Service option in which case we will be in contact with further details and information on paying for this service soon.  In the meantime, you may find the information below helpful:

For information on historic sites in Wales and details of archive holdings see our database and catalogue, https://coflein.gov.uk/en/

Where possible we recommend that you make an appointment to visit our archive search room and library<https://rcahmw.gov.uk/services/> to view archive material in person and take photos without charge.  This is generally much quicker and cheaper than ordering scans remotely.

Many of our digital images are available to download, purchase and licence directly from Coflein.  Guidance on how to do this can be seen in this short video: Downloading images from Coflein (youtube.com)<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gKpL61F1M2w&t=3s>  If the image which you want is not available via this system, this is often due to copyright restrictions and you may wish to identify an alternative image which is available, if possible.

If you are requesting a dataset, please be advised that this data is now freely available under the Open Government Licence (nationalarchives.gov.uk)<https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/> and you can carry out the searches yourself for terrestrial<https://datamap.gov.wales/layers/geonode:nmrw_jan2024_sites> and maritime<https://datamap.gov.wales/layers/geonode:nmrw_jan2024_maritimesites> data on Data Map Wales and on Historic Wales (arcgis.com)<https://historic-wales-rcahmw.hub.arcgis.com/#zoom=0&lat=300000&lon=258000&layers=BFFFFFTFFTTT>.

If you are contacting us to request physical access to a historic site please note that the Royal Commission does not own any of the sites listed on Coflein, nor do we hold contact details for site owners.  You will need to contact the site owners directly with your request.

If you are concerned about the condition of a historic site or ancient monument please contact the Welsh Government’s Historic Environment Service, Cadw or the relevant local authority.  Similarly, if you have a query regarding the statutory status of a historic site or the planning process relating to Listed Buildings or Scheduled Ancient Monuments there is a range of information available on Cadw’s website: Advice & Support | Cadw (gov.wales)<https://cadw.gov.wales/advice-support>
The Royal Commission’s primary role is to create, curate and communicate information on the historic environment, we also play a role as consultees in the planning process for the historic environment on the basis of this information.  However, whilst our staff are passionate about the historic environment in Wales, as a body we are not responsible for enforcing planning or heritage legislation, safeguarding historic sites, nor do we act as a lobbying group.
