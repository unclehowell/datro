Family records and longstanding local accounts indicate that a copy of the

Estate may at one time have been available through Cardiff's library or
local studies collections. The Williams family relied upon documentary
evidence relating to the farm during disputes concerning occupation and
ownership during the twentieth century, and family members consistently
referred to such records.

I therefore respectfully request that Cathays Branch and Heritage Library,
or the appropriate Cardiff Council department, undertake or arrange a
search of any relevant holdings, including:

• Local studies collections and vertical files. • Heritage and local
history indexes. • Newspaper cuttings collections. • Photographic
collections. • Map collections. • Catalogue entries and accession records.
• References to Great House Farm, Ty Mawr, Llandough. • References to
Daniel Thomas, the Bute Estate, quarry agreements, land exchanges, or
conveyances. • References to John Williams, Mary Williams, or other members
of the Williams family associated with the farm. • Records indicating
whether any such material was transferred to Glamorgan Archives or another
repository.

In particular, if any relevant material was formerly held by Cardiff
Libraries or Cardiff Council but subsequently transferred, withdrawn,
re-catalogued, or otherwise relocated, I would be grateful for any
information identifying where those records are now held and any catalogue
references that may assist my research.

If this request would be more appropriately handled under the Freedom of
Information Act 2000 or Environmental Information Regulations 2004, please
treat it accordingly or transfer it internally to the relevant department.

relating to Great House Farm and the Williams family's connection to the
