Thank you for sending us your Awards for All application.  We look forward to finding out more about your plans for supporting your local community and the difference your project will make.

We will now check that your application is complete and if so, start our assessment in due course.  We may need to contact you to find out more information but this is not always necessary.

Please note Awards for All England applications take around 8 weeks to assess. If a decision is made to offer a grant there is then a set up stage before payment is made. We would recommend applications are submitted 4 months in advance of anticipated project start date.
If this impacts on the delivery of your project please contact us<http://www.biglotteryfund.org.uk/help-and-support>.
