Thank you for contacting the Serious Fraud Office (SFO).

We aim to answer all correspondence that falls within the remit of the SFO within 20 days.
Please note, we will not reply to matters that do not fall within the remit and responsibility of our agency (details below).
We are also unable to accept reports, referrals or allegations at this email address or via telephone.
How to make a report to the SFO : If you wish to make a report to us, please refer to our website, which includes a secure online reporting form - Reporting serious fraud, bribery and corruption - Serious Fraud Office (sfo.gov.uk)<https://www.sfo.gov.uk/contact-us/reporting-serious-fraud-bribery-corruption/>  this is sent directly to the appropriate team in our Intelligence Division where all submissions are carefully considered.

If you have already made a report to us through our secure reporting form, and have not been contacted within 20 working days, please assume that your allegation(s) or complaint(s) do not meet the statutory remit of the SFO. We suggest that you consider taking up your concerns with another organisations / agencies who may be able to assist you (Please see below).

Our Remit: We investigate and prosecute the most serious or complex cases of fraud, bribery and corruption.
The SFO is a highly specialised government department and takes on a small number of large-scale economic crime cases at any one time. You can find out more about our remit on our website here: About us - Serious Fraud Office (sfo.gov.uk)<https://www.sfo.gov.uk/about-us/>
For information about how we operate as a non-ministerial government department, you may wish to refer to the Attorney General's framework agreement: SFO_Framework_Agreement.pdf (publishing.service.gov.uk)<https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/772685/SFO_Framework_Agreement.pdf>
If your query does not fall within our remit we advise you to direct your query to another suitable agency. Please see below for details of other organisations / agencies who may be able to assist you.

Details of other organisations/agencies:
*       Fraud or cybercrime outside the remit of the SFO - Consider reporting to Action Fraud<https://www.actionfraud.police.uk/> or call them on 0300 123 2040 or +44 207 386 3620 from overseas.
*       Scam Call/Email - The SFO does not deal with these enquiries. Please make a report to Action Fraud using the above contact details.
*       Immigration - Please contact the Home Office Visas and Immigration online at https://www.gov.uk/report-immigration-crime or you can report the matter by calling 0800 555 111.
*       Police related matters - Please contact the Home office on : Home Office - GOV.UK (www.gov.uk)<https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/home-office> or you can contact your local police force by calling the police non-emergency phone number 101.
*       Crown Prosecution Service (CPS) matters - If you are unsure whether your enquiry is a matter for us or the CPS, we advise you to refer to the CPS website: https://www.cps.gov.uk/.
*       Legal advice - If you require legal advice and need assistance finding a solicitor, the Law Society of England & Wales can provide details of relevant firms and lawyers. Further information can be found at: Find a Solicitor - The Law Society<https://solicitors.lawsociety.org.uk/> and Solicitor Directory | The Law Society of Northern Ireland (lawsoc-ni.org)<https://www.lawsoc-ni.org/solicitors>
*       Citizen's advice - If the above agencies are not applicable, you may wish to contact the Citizens Advice Bureau (CAB) which provides free, independent, confidential and impartial advice regarding your civil rights and responsibilities. Your local CAB office can be found at Citizens Advice<https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/>
Please also note that we will not respond to telephone enquires via the main Switchboard.

Thank you again for taking the time to contact the SFO, we hope the above information has been of assistance.
