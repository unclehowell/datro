A. ATISN 26-0010 – request for internal review

Thank you, and please pass my thanks to Emma Harvey-Woodason, for the response of 19 October 2026. I ask for an internal review of ATISN 26-0010 on the six points below. Each quotes the response and then asks the question it leaves open; I would be grateful for an answer to each point.

1. The response says: "any routine records would have been destroyed in line with the corporate retention schedule applied at the time". Which retention schedule is meant (its title, version and date), and which entry in it applied to the file series in item 1 of my request?

2. Item 2 of my request asked, if files were destroyed, for "the date and authority". Is there any record of an actual destruction of files in those series relating to the farm or the two companies, giving its date and authority? If no such record is held, please say so, so that it is clear whether the sentence quoted at point 1 describes a recorded event or the likely position.

3. The response says that "disposition would have been carried out by a predecessor body". Which predecessor body is meant?

4. The response says: "Before 1997, the Welsh Office or other agencies … would have had their own retention schedules." Does the Welsh Government hold those pre-1997 schedules for the file series in item 1? If it does, please provide them. If it does not, please say so and, if known, where they are held.

5. The response says: "Searches were carried out to find files mentioning the farm name and the companies listed in your request." Which systems, indexes and file series were searched, with what search terms and over what date range, and was the index of physical registered files included? (The internal review of ATISN 27021, dated 2 July 2026, describes searches of physical registered files, including A--CL172-06-017/1 Community of Llandough, and a wider search of files from around the 1980s–1990s.)

6. The response says that historical material "would then have been transferred by us to The National Archives (or a designated Place of Deposit)". Which Place or Places of Deposit were used for Welsh Office records of this kind, and what are the series references of any of the file series in item 1 that were transferred?

C. New requests under the Freedom of Information Act 2000 and the Environmental Information Regulations 2004

These are new requests and not part of the review above. They concern Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth, Vale of Glamorgan.

1. Wartime farm records, 1936–1955. Please provide any recorded information held by the Welsh Government, including records inherited from the Welsh Office, about the farm in 1936–1955:
(a) any record of the Glamorgan War Agricultural Executive Committee, or its post-war successor, about the farm, including any possession, requisition or cultivation direction;
(b) any claim or payment under the Compensation (Defence) Act 1939;
(c) any record naming the occupier or owner of the farm in that period.
I understand the 1941–43 National Farm Survey is held at The National Archives (MAF 32), so I am not asking for it. If any other record has been transferred to The National Archives, please give the reference rather than treating that part as not held.

2. Compulsory purchase and stopping-up orders, 1950–1995. Please confirm whether the Secretary of State for Wales or the Welsh Office made or confirmed any compulsory purchase order, stopping-up order or diversion order affecting the land at Great House Farm between 1950 and 1995, and provide a copy of any such order and of the decision confirming it. ATISN 26-0010 searched under the farm name and two company names. Orders of this kind are usually titled by road or place, so please also search under "Leckwith Road, Llandough", "Llandough-juxta-Penarth" and "Church View Close". If such records have been transferred to The National Archives, please give the reference.

If no information is held for any part, please say so. If anything is withheld, please give the exemption or exception relied on.
