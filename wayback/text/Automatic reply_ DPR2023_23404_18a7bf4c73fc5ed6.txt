Armed Forces Badge applications and submission details can be found at the following - Armed forces veterans badge application form - GOV.UK (www.gov.uk)<https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/armed-forces-veterans-badge-application-form>

If you have an enquiry relating to an ongoing Subject Access Request (SAR), please update your contact details with the following APC Disclosures email address: APC-Sp-ParlDiscl-Triage-Mailbox@mod.gov.uk<mailto:APC-Sp-ParlDiscl-Triage-Mailbox@mod.gov.uk>

All enquiries should be submitted to the email address provided with a note of your DPR reference number. We will forward the enquiry to this address on your behalf.

Your enquiry will automatically be placed in a queue. You will only be contacted by a Disclosures Officer in the circumstance that further documentation or clarification is required to progress your request. If you believe your request requires urgent priority and can provide supporting information to show the urgency (i.e. with a court date pending) we will investigate and ascertain, if, we are able to prioritize the request.

Please note that we do not have the resources, and therefore will not reply to any confirmation of arrival or basic hasteners of cases.  Under the General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018, this section has up to 30 days to complete your request, though this may be extended by a further two months if your request is complex.  If extended, you will receive notification of this.

Please note that if you have requested any of the following it will be automatically sent on to the relevant section for processing:

Disclosures 1 – Requests for a partial disclosure or Welfare-related requests (eg. a Certificate of Service, replacement red book, job references, confirmation of service number)  Obtain information about yourself held by MOD - GOV.UK (www.gov.uk)<https://www.gov.uk/guidance/obtain-information-about-yourself-held-by-mod>

Disclosures 3 – Requests for medical records  Obtain information about yourself held by MOD - GOV.UK (www.gov.uk)<https://www.gov.uk/guidance/obtain-information-about-yourself-held-by-mod>

Disclosures 4 – Requests for the records of deceased personnel (Freedom of Information)  (Request records of deceased service personnel - GOV.UK (www.gov.uk)<https://www.gov.uk/guidance/request-records-of-deceased-service-personnel>)

The release of personnel records is subject to the rules and regulations laid down in the Data Protection Act 2018 and we are required by the Act to verify the identity of an individual prior to releasing any information.
