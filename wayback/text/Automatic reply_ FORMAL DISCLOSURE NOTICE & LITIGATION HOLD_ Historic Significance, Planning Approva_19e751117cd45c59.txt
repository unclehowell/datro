Diolch am gysylltu â Heddlu De Cymru.
Nid ydym yn cymryd adroddiadau gan y cyhoedd, asiantaethau partner na heddluoedd eraill drwy e-bost mwyach.
Defnyddiwch un o'r dulliau canlynol:
1. Mewn argyfwng, ffoniwch 999 ar unwaith.
2. Ar gyfer achosion nad ydynt yn rhai brys, defnyddiwch yr adnoddau defnyddiol ar-lein ar ein gwefan: https://www.south-wales.police.uk/cy-GB/riportio/riportio/
3. Ar gyfer asiantaethau partner a heddluoedd eraill, defnyddiwch y ddolen ganlynol: https://www.south-wales.police.uk/cy-GB/gwasanaethau-partner/gwasanaethau-partner/
4. Ffoniwch ein llinell achosion nad ydynt yn rhai brys ar 101 i siarad gyda gweithredwr.
5. Gellir cael cyngor drwy'r Sgwrs Fyw ar ein gwefan: https://www.south-wales.police.uk/cy-GB/
6. Ewch i’r orsaf heddlu agosaf sydd ar agor i'r cyhoedd. I weld cyfeiriad ac oriau agor eich gorsaf heddlu agosaf, ewch i'n gwefan drwy ddilyn y ddolen hon: https://www.south-wales.police.uk/cy-GB/cysylltu-a-ni/dewch-o-hyd-i-orsaf-heddlu/
Os oes gennych amhariad ar y clyw neu leferydd ac rydych wedi cofrestru ymlaen llaw gyda'r gwasanaeth SMS brys, gallwch ddefnyddio ein gwasanaeth ffôn testun ar 18000 neu anfon neges destun ar 999.

Os ydych yn gofyn am ddiweddariad ar achos presennol, llenwch y ffurflen hon a rhowch eich rhif cyfeirnod digwyddiad/trosedd: https://www.south-wales.police.uk/cy-GB/cysylltu-a-ni/af/cysylltu-a-ni-beta/cysylltu-a-ni/
I weld yr holl wasanaethau riportio, ewch i'n gwefan: https://www.south-wales.police.uk/cy-GB/


Thank you for contacting South Wales Police.
We no longer take reports from the public, partner agencies and other police forces via email.
Please use one of the following methods:
1. In case of an emergency, please call 999 immediately.
2. For all non-emergency situations, use our handy online reporting tools on our website: https://www.south-wales.police.uk/ro/report/
3. For partner agencies and other forces please use the following link: https://www.south-wales.police.uk/partners/partner-services/
4. Call our non-emergency line on 101 to speak to an operator.
5. Advice can be provided via the Live Chat function on our website: https://www.south-wales.police.uk/
6. Attend your nearest police station that is open to the public. For locations and opening times please see our website via this link: https://www.south-wales.police.uk/contact/find-a-police-station/
If you have a hearing or speech impairment and you’ve pre-registered with the emergency SMS service, you can use our textphone service 18000 or text us on 999.

If you’re requesting an update on an existing case, please fill out this form and include your incident/crime reference number: https://www.south-wales.police.uk/contact/af/contact-us-beta/contact-us/
For our full online reporting services visit our website: https://www.south-wales.police.uk/
Ymateb nad yw'n Argyfwng\ Non-Emergency Response

Oes angen i chi siarad â'r heddlu ond nid oes angen ymateb brys arnoch? Gallwch roi gwybod am fater drwy ein gwefan https://www.heddlu-de-cymru.police.uk, neu ffonio 101. Gallwch ddefnyddio 101 i roi gwybod am achosion nad ydynt yn rhai brys i unrhyw heddlu yng Nghymru a Lloegr. Mewn argyfwng, ffoniwch 999 bob amser.

Do you need to speak to the police but don’t require an emergency response? You can make a report via our website https://www.south-wales.police.uk, or call 101. 101 can be used to report a non-emergency to any force in Wales and England. In an emergency always dial 999.

Cymorth Iaith Gymraeg\Welsh Language Support

Mae Heddlu De Cymru yn croesawu derbyn gohebiaeth yn Gymraeg a Saesneg. Byddwn yn ateb gohebiaeth a dderbynnir yn Gymraeg yn Gymraeg ac ni fydd gohebu yn Gymraeg yn arwain at oedi.

South Wales Police welcomes receiving correspondence in Welsh and English. Any correspondence received in Welsh will be answered in Welsh and corresponding in Welsh will not lead to a delay in responding.
