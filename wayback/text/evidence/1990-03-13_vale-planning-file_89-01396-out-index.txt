ARCHAEOLOGY CONDITIONS (decision notice, 13 March 1990, pp.2–3)
16. The developer shall afford access at all reasonable times to any archaeologist nominated by the Local Planning Authority, and shall allow him/her to observe the excavations and record items of interest and finds.
   Reason: To enable satisfactory archaeological investigation.
17. No development shall take place until a field evaluation survey and archaeological assessment report has been carried out by a qualified archaeologist to the satisfaction of the Local Planning Authority. This work shall be carried out within three months of the date of this consent.
   Reason: To enable satisfactory archaeological investigation.
18. The detail plans to be submitted shall pay full regard to the results and findings of the archaeological investigations referred to in condition 17 above.
   Reason: To ensure that the future development of the site has regard to the results of the archaeological investigations.

The officer's report recommended a single standard archaeology condition (Welsh Office Circular 1/85, Appendix B). The decision notice carries three.
