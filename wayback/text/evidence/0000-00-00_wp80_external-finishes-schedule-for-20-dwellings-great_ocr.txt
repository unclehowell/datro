IDEAL HOMES WALES LTD.
SPECIFICATION FOR EXTERNAL RENDERING 9/8/1993

MATERIALS

Cement shall be to either BS.12: BS.146: BS.4207 or BS.5224

Sand shall be to BS.1200. Sand must be properly graded.

Waterproofers must be used in accordance with manufacturers
reccomendations.

Metal quoin beeds/bell mouths etc. must be stainless steel

Nails must be stainless steel

Background - to be concrete blockwork NOT CLAY brickwork of any kind.
Concrete bricks in limitted quantities are permissible to achieve the coursing
of the concrete blockwork.

WEATHER

Be aware that very hot or very wet weather will require attention by the site
foreman of the most appropriate form of action.

Mortar mixes shall be used within 2 hours in summer and within 3 hours in
winter.

Mortar should be mixed dry and water then added.

MIX : TWO COAT RENDERING

UNDERCOAT Shall not exceed 16mm. thick and not be less than 8mm thick.
12mm thick is preferable. Provide scratch key for finishing coat.

USE 1:2 1/2 to 3 1/2 masonry cement to sand with a waterproofer (no
plasticiser)

FINISHING COAT

10mm thick; plain float finish with wood float, not a steel float. The
background coat should be fully dry and set before applying the finishing coat
(normally 3 days).

USE: 1:4 to 5 masonry cement to sand with waterproofer (no plasticiser).

FINAL FINISH 2 coats of masonry paint; COLOUR 'WHITE. Use Crown Stronghold Plioway.
See painting Specification for full details.

TILCON MORTAR MIX

COLOURED MORTAR SPECIFICATION

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION

GAUGING WATER:

Only clean water should be used. As uniformity of colour is important, care should bve taken
to ensure that the mortar is mixed regualr consistency.

CEMENT GAUGING:

It is strongly advised that the same brand, type and source of cement be used throughout the
Contract, and that the site gauging is carried out accurately by weight or volume to ensure
colour consistency.

SITE STORAGE:

Mortar should be tipped on to a clean banker board with a sealed base and sheeted when not
in use.

MASONRY PROTECTION:

Stacked bricks and blocks should be protected from the weather at all time. Never use wet
bricks/blocks and always protect the top course of new work during construction against the
weather. This is very important to guard against disfiguration of the brickwork from
efforescence and/or lime bloom, and also during winter conditions when there is a danger of
attack by frost.
