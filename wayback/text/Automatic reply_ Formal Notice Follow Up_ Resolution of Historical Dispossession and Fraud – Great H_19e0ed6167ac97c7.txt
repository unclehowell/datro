Thank you for contacting TI-UK. This account is monitored regularly but please note the following guidance:


Reporting Corruption
If you were contacting us to report corruption or receive advice on a specific case our website has a list of organisations and their complaints routes.  How to report corruption | Transparency International UK<https://www.transparency.org.uk/who-we-are/contact-us/how-report-corruption>. If you have exhausted the formal complaints mechanisms your alternative routes are through your Member of Parliament/Local Councillor. We are unable to assist with specific cases. If you require advice on Whistle-blowing the dedicated charity Protect Contact us - Protect - Speak up stop harm<https://protect-advice.org.uk/contact-us/> have an advice and helpline and we encourage you to speak to them directly.

Where individuals are at risk of harm please contact the emergency services. If you have submitted allegations of sexual abuse these emails will be deleted for data privacy reasons.

If you were contacting us to share views, information, or background in relation to bribery and corruption relevant colleagues will be provided with this as background where relevant. Our limited resources mean we are unable to respond directly.

Press/ Media
If you wish to speak to our press/ media colleagues your email will be passed on to them. Alternatively, please use press@transparency.org.uk<mailto:press@transparency.org.uk>

TI movement/ Berlin Secretariat
If your email related to the wider work of Transparency International and not our role as the UK Chapter then you should contact them directly via their website details Contact - Transparency.org<https://www.transparency.org/en/contact>

Access Data Requests
If you submitted an access data request we will respond accordingly. The email should have been appropriately headed to allow us to do this. Please re-send with the correct heading.

Jobs/ Internships
If you submitted a CV or enquired regarding intern or job vacancies, we are unable to respond to you. All our roles are advertised and open to your application at the time. Please follow these on our jobs page or via LinkedIn.
