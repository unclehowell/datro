REQUEST FOR INFORMATION: Wartime and post-war farm records for Great
House Farm / Tÿ Mawr, Llandough, 1936–1955

Thank you for your request for information of 20 October 2026 about wartime and post-
war farm records for Great House Farm / Tÿ Mawr, Llandough, 1936–1955. We have
handled your request under the Freedom of Information Act 2000 (FOIA).

Your information request and our response are set out below.

Western Ground Rents Ltd (company no. 00342092), to which the Bute Estate
conveyed its interest by a conveyance dated 25 December 1938, obtained a
possession order on 4 July 1955. I am trying to establish from the records how the
farm was dealt with between 1936 and 1955, who took each decision, and on what
record.

Please tell me whether Defra holds, and provide, the following for Great House Farm
for 1936–1955:
1. any National Farm Survey or other MAF farm record for the holding, including the
    occupier, the owner and the acreage recorded;
2. any record of the Glamorgan War Agricultural Executive Committee (or its
    successor, the Glamorgan Agricultural Executive Committee) about the farm:
    inspections, gradings, cultivation or other directions, supervision orders, or any
    possession, requisition or termination of tenancy, with the date and the committee
    or officer (by role) who made each decision;
3. any application for, or decision on, the Minister's consent to the operation of a
    notice to quit the holding (Agricultural Holdings Act 1948), with the date, the
    decision and who made it;
4. any compensation claim or payment for the land under the Compensation
    (Defence) Act 1939 or the Agriculture Acts;
5. any correspondence with Western Ground Rents Ltd, the Bute Estate or their
    agents about the farm, or about the possession order of 4 July 1955 and the land
    it covered.

If records in scope have been transferred to The National Archives (for example MAF
32, the National Farm Survey, or the MAF 80 series of committee minutes), please
give the reference rather than treating that part as not held. If any record in scope was
destroyed, please give the retention or disposal schedule that applied and, where
recorded, the date and the authority for its destruction. If any part is held by the Welsh
Government rather than by Defra, please say so.

Following a search of our paper and electronic records, we have established that the
information that you have requested is not held by Defra.

We can confirm that the information may be held by the Welsh Government, which
can be contacted at https://www.gov.wales/contact-welsh-government#52148

We attach an annex giving contact details should you be unhappy with the service you
have received.

If you have any queries about this letter please contact us.
