Individual cases
In line with the general practice of select committees, the Committee is unable to take up individual cases, including those relating to the Parliamentary and Health Service Ombudsman (PHSO). The Committee is responsible for scrutinising the general work and performance of the PHSO, and to examine any reports it lays before Parliament, but it does not have any power to compel or direct the Ombudsman to act in a particular way.
The Committee cannot review the PHSO's decisions about individual cases. This includes the PHSO's decisions on whether or not to accept a case. As the office of the PHSO is independent, decisions cannot be overruled by a government minister or any parliamentary committee.
However, if you are not happy with a PHSO decision, you can ask the PHSO to review that decision. Further information about how to do this can be found on the PHSO website<https://emea01.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ombudsman.org.uk%2Fabout-us%2Ffeedback-about-our-service&data=04%7C01%7C%7C4e5fe3cdd40749f2cd8a08d93c83ee22%7C84df9e7fe9f640afb435aaaaaaaaaaaa%7C1%7C0%7C637607359612036559%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C1000&sdata=erXQPIDEv3LG%2Ft4B4sJCMEvOAyyS0IRPyx%2BQrCbVs0Q%3D&reserved=0>.
