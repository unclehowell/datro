2. Specific events during the requested period

29–30 November 1988 – possession and forced entry
The published chronology records possession being obtained on 29 November; South Wales Echo reporting places the forced entry and eviction on Wednesday 30 November. Please search both dates. The reporting (front page, "Angry scenes as farmer evicted", Peter Bibby) describes bailiffs forcing entry to the farmhouse, William Buckler being injured and taken to Llandough Hospital, part of an outbuilding roof already removed, and eight other residents made homeless with the family.
Please search for incident or occurrence entries, control-room records, calls for assistance, deployment or attendance logs, communications with bailiffs, and any decision to attend or not attend.

1 December 1988 – the family's belongings
The family's account records that their belongings, including papers and photographs, were left in the house or put into the road. I cannot establish whether this was reported to police. Please search for any recorded complaint, request for assistance or operational entry concerning property at the site.

2 December 1988 – High Court order on the farmhouse
The chronology records a High Court order that the farmhouse and its contents were not to be touched pending a further hearing, and that Mr Justice Anthony Evans allowed Branwen Buckler to enter to collect belongings, subject to agreement by BP's solicitors. Please search for any police notification, liaison or request for assistance concerning that order, access to the premises or the handling of belongings.

3 December 1988 – arrest/charge from hospital
Contemporary reporting ("History fight to save farm" and "Billy's unhappy family", South Wales Echo) and the chronology record that police took William Buckler from his hospital bed at Llandough Hospital to be charged with assaulting two bailiffs, criminal damage, and wanton or furious driving. This is a direct police action within the requested period. Please search specifically for the associated crime/occurrence record, custody record, charge record and file references, and any record of their transfer, retention or destruction.

5 December 1988 – injunction refused; Cadw listing decision
Judge Norman Francis refused to continue the injunction pending consideration by the European Commission of Human Rights. The same day Cadw announced that the house would not be listed. Please search for any police notification, liaison or request for assistance concerning the anticipated demolition.

6 December 1988 – demolition of the farmhouse
The chronology describes a team of about twenty with bulldozers arriving just after 4 a.m., with the farmhouse demolished by breakfast (South Wales Echo, "Bulldozed Before Breakfast"). Branwen Buckler and the children are reported to have witnessed it. The regional Historic Environment Record states that the buildings were demolished by B.P. Properties Ltd on 6 December 1988 amid considerable local controversy. The 4 a.m. time is the demolition team's reported arrival, not a verified time of police attendance.
Please search for any record of officers attending, being deployed, being requested but not attending, or communicating with BP Properties Ltd, the demolition contractors, the local authority or others about the demolition, including any prior notification of a pre-dawn operation.

7–9 December 1988 – local reaction and bail
Reporting records local anger, including a public meeting between villagers and councillors on 8 December. On Friday 9 December magistrates bailed William Buckler on condition that he stay away from the site (South Wales Echo, Saturday 10 December 1988). Please search for any public-order or community-contact entries, and any record relating to the bail condition or its enforcement.

The search should continue to 31 December 1988, including any later complaints, correspondence or operational entries about the aftermath.

3. Indexing aids outside the requested period (no extension sought)
