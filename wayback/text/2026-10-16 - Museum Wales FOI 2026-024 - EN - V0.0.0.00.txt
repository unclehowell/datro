The information requested regarding the searches undertaken was provided in our response of 5 October 2026.

As explained in that response, Collections staff reviewed records identified through references to Great House Farm, Ty Mawr Farm and Llandough, together with records returned through associated place-name searches. Records relating to the wider Llandough area, to other locations sharing the name "Llandough", or to other properties bearing the name "Great House Farm" were also reviewed.

We also confirmed that searches of the earliest accession registers encompass the former Cardiff Museum accession records, which became part of the National Museum of Wales collection without re-accessioning.

Amgueddfa Cymru does not hold a separate search log, audit schedule or other recorded document providing further detail beyond that already supplied.

We therefore have no further recorded information to provide in relation to this matter.
