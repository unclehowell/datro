We aim to respond to all feedback within 1 working day. If you are making a complaint or making a comment that requires investigation, we will assign your feedback to the relevant area of the organisation and will aim to provide a full response within 15 working days. If we cannot provide a full response within 15 working days we will contact you to let you know the reason why and let you know when we will be able to reply in full.

​If your feedback does not concern services provided by the House of Commons, you may not receive a reply. Full details of the House of Commons feedback policy<https://www.parliament.uk/business/commons/governance-of-the-house-of-commons-/feedback/> explain that we are not able to respond to matters relating to Government policy or the behaviour and actions of Members of Parliament.

If, however, your feedback concerns services provided by the House of Commons, we will be in touch shortly.

Our feedback policy covers the following areas:

  *   the standard of service we provide
  *   the behaviour of our staff
  *   any action or lack of action by staff affecting an individual or group


The CCC policy covers all House of Commons and bicameral services but does not cover the following:

  *   Matters that have been fully investigated through the CCC procedure
  *   Anonymous complaints
  *   Issues that fall under existing HR policies such as grievances, whistleblowing, safeguarding concerns, or the Respect policy
  *   Complaints where procedures and remedies are set out in legislation, e.g. Freedom of Information Act, Data Protection Act
  *   The House of Lords - although feedback can still be recorded and the Customer Team will share the feedback with the House of Lords Communication Team
  *   Statements by and actions of Members of the House of Commons
  *   Proceedings by the House of Commons, including Select Committee proceedings
  *   The Government, including the work of Government departments, statements by Ministers, and Government policy
  *   Political parties, public bodies and other external organisations


If your feedback concerns a subject not covered by the CCC policy you may not receive a reply.
