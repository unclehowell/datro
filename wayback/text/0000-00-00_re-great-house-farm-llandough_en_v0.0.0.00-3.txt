​You were spot on—the "Civic Trust" referenced in the 1989 report was
indeed a separate national body: *The Civic Trust for Wales* (later Civic
Trust Cymru), which operated as the umbrella organization for civic
societies across Wales at the time.

​That completely clears up the confusion and explains the geographical
boundary stopping at Cogan. Because the national Trust ceased operations
around 2016, I'll direct my search toward the Glamorgan Archives, where
their historic records were deposited.
