Dear Requester

Great House Farm, Llandough (ATISN 27589)

Thank you for your request for information received on 28 September 2026.

We have considered your request under the Freedom of Information Act 2000.

The information you have requested was the subject of a previous request submitted by
you under reference (ATISN 27021), to which Welsh Government has already
responded, including undertaking an internal review. Following a review of your latest
request, we are satisfied that it is substantially similar to the earlier request and that no
new information has been generated or become available since our previous response.

Accordingly, Welsh Government is refusing your request under section 14(2) of the
Freedom of Information Act 2000, which provides that a public authority is not obliged to
comply with a request if it is a repeated request made by the same person, where it is
substantially similar to a previous request and a reasonable interval has not elapsed.

For ease of reference, the previous response, together with the outcome of the
associated internal review, can be found here:
