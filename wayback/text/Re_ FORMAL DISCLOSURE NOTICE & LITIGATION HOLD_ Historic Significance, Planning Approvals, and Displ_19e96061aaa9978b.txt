Thank you for your detailed reply and for identifying the records relating
to Great House Farm and the Buckler family.

Having reviewed the descriptions, I am not yet certain that the 1938 Great
House Farm sale catalogue relates to the same Great House Farm, Llandough
that later became the subject of the BP litigation involving the Buckler
family. I will investigate this further.

My current research is focused on the ownership, occupation and legal
history of Great House Farm, Llandough, particularly during the twentieth
century and in connection with BP Properties, BP Pension Trust, tenancy
arrangements, conveyancing, planning matters and any records that may help
establish the chain of title. 1800/1900's too.

Could you please advise whether Glamorgan Archives holds, or is aware of,
any records relating to:

   - BP Properties Ltd;
   - BP Pension Trust;
   - Great House Farm, Llandough (near Penarth);
   - conveyances, tenancy agreements or estate management records
   concerning the property;
   - planning applications, redevelopment proposals or demolition records
   relating to Great House Farm;
   - correspondence between public bodies and the owners or occupiers of
   the property;
   - any records that may assist in tracing ownership or occupation between
   the 1870s and the late 1980s.

I would also be grateful if you could advise whether any estate maps, tithe
maps, valuation records, rating records or other archival sources might
help establish the historical ownership and occupation of the property.

Thank you again for your assistance. Your reply was meaningful and
appreciated.
