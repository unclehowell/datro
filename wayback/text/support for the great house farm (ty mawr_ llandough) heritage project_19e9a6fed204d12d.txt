I am currently developing an independent heritage research project focused 
on the history of Great House Farm (Ty Mawr), Llandough, and its 
surrounding landscape.

The aim of this work is to carefully bring together historical, 
archaeological, and documentary evidence to better understand the full 
timeline of the site, and to make this information accessible to the public 
in a clear and engaging way. Hence website https://bpvsbuckler.datro.xyz

As part of the project, I am planning:

   - A detailed digital reconstruction and Augmented reality guided tour of 
   the Llandough site


   - A public-facing archive of gathered historical evidence and 
   interpretations


   - A physical scale model / replica of the farm, to be created in 
   Patagonia as part of a wider educational and cultural display


   - Open public access to the findings, continuing the tradition of shared 
   community heritage work

The project is intended as an open heritage initiative, with the goal of 
education, preservation of local history, and public engagement.
