Dear Sir or Madam,
I am seeking access to historic administrative records held by Cardiff
County Court (or its successor) in relation to civil proceedings heard in
1974. These proceedings took place at Cardiff County Court (or the relevant
South Glamorgan county court) with hearings in July 1974 and on or around
30–31 October 1974.
I understand from the Ministry of Justice that information relating to
court cases is held by the court for its own purposes and that the
appropriate route is to contact the court directly under the relevant
procedure rules, rather than via the Freedom of Information Act.
I am not requesting copies of pleadings, evidence, or any judicial reasons.
I am specifically seeking access to administrative and procedural metadata,
namely:
The case number(s) / cause number(s) allocated to the proceedings.
Any Daily Cause Lists / court lists for the July 1974 hearing and the
hearing listed on or around 30–31 October 1974, including any outcome
annotations (e.g. adjourned, withdrawn, vacated, stood out).
The recorded administrative outcome of the proceedings (for example,
whether they were adjourned, stayed, withdrawn, discontinued, or proceeded
to final determination).
Confirmation of whether any final judgment, possession order, or
declaration of title was sealed or entered in respect of the proceedings.
Any surviving case register / ledger / minute book / index entry showing
procedural steps taken and the closure status.
Please could you:
Confirm whether such administrative records are held locally or have been
transferred to another body (for example, Glamorgan Archives).
Indicate the correct procedure, rule or practice direction under which I
should make this type of request for historic administrative court
information.
Explain whether there is a specific form, fee, or application route I
should use, and what level of detail you require (e.g. full party names,
addresses) to identify the case.
I am happy to provide the parties’ names and any other details required
once you have confirmed the appropriate process.
Thank you for your assistance and guidance.

Regards,
