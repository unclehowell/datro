ATISN 27589 (Cadw; your ref qA2496208) – further to part B of my email of 19 October 2026

This adds three short points to part B of my email of 19 October 2026. It does not repeat the questions in that email or any question now with you under ATISN 26-0009.

1. Which regime applies. The letter of 19 October 2026 says: "We have considered your request under the Freedom of Information Act 2000." ATISN 27589 asked about the consideration of a building for listing, its inspection and its demolition in 1988. I believe that is likely to be environmental information within regulation 2(1)(c) and (f) of the Environmental Information Regulations 2004 (measures and activities affecting land and built structures). The EIR have no equivalent of section 14(2). A request can be refused only under an exception, such as regulation 12(4)(b), with the public interest test in regulation 12(1)(b) and the presumption in favour of disclosure in regulation 12(2). Please reconsider ATISN 27589 under the EIR and treat this email as representations under regulation 11. If the Welsh Government considers that the EIR do not apply, please tell me why.

2. Whether it is a repeat. The letter says that ATISN 27589 is "substantially similar to the earlier request and that no new information has been generated or become available since our previous response". As my email of 19 October noted, point 3 of ATISN 27589 (any transfer, destruction or disposal of the files, with the schedule used and the date) was not asked in ATISN 27021. ATISN 27589 was also made after ATISN 27021 was answered on 11 June 2026, and it expressly followed the Wales Office's response to 26FOI 103 of 22 September 2026. Please take both points into account in your reconsideration.

3. Advice and assistance. If the Welsh Government considers that any part of ATISN 27589 cannot be answered as framed, please give me advice and assistance (section 16 FOIA; regulation 9 EIR) on how it could be framed so that it can be answered, without repeating ATISN 27021. For example, it would help to know whether there is a register, index or file list for 1988 Cadw listing work in the Vale of Glamorgan that could be searched instead.

Please also confirm that the preservation hold I asked for in part D of my email of 19 October covers any records within the scope of ATISN 27589 while this is considered.

Thank you for your help.
