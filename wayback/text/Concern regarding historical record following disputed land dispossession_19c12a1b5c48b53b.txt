Sirs/Ma'am,

I'm writing to raise a concern regarding the integrity of the historical
record following disputed dispossession of land i.e. BP vs Buckler 1987

After forced removal and demolition of our home, archaeological excavation
and removal of items took place on land whose ownership had never been
conclusively adjudicated at the time of registration. BP asserted it, but
it was never determined in a ruling beforehand as the July 1974 case
between BP and my grandmother was adjourned and the October 31st 1974
appeal and audience with a judge she made just 24 hours before, was
squashed by letters sent by BP that same morning.

I am not making allegations here, simply stating facts about this landmark
legal case. And asking whether this type of circumstance is relevant to
archival or historical record considerations.

Yours faithfully,


Sion Buckler
