What you choose to post shapes what people come to you for. It’s easy to share ideas without stepping back to ask what they’re building toward. The people who stand out are clear on why they’re posting and how each idea connects to where they want to go. This month is about making more deliberate choices with what you share. Content with a clear direction helps others understand what you want to be known for. When each post has a purpose, your content starts to work in your favor. It shapes how people see your strengths and influences the opportunities that come your way. How to Create with Strategic Intent Strong content starts with clarity. When you know what you want your work to lead to, it becomes easier to decide what’s worth sharing and what isn’t. Here are some ways to bring more intention into your content: Choose topics that reflect the work you want to be known for, while still leaving room for what feels timely Pay attention to which ideas resonate and use that insight to refine how you show up Build familiar formats that people can recognize and return to over time Be selective with what you engage with so your voice stays focused and consistent Share perspectives that help others see your depth of thinking, not just your activity Remember, these are guidelines, not hard rules. There’s space to adapt them based on what feels right in the moment. But when your choices are intentional, your content becomes easier to follow and easier to trust. Over time, that clarity compounds into a stronger presence that reflects where you’re going.
