Thank you for getting in touch with us here at Doc Society!

Unfortunately, we are unable to respond personally to your email. Due
to the high number of enquiries we receive, we cannot accept proposals
or watch content that has not been submitted through the official
application process. We do not engage in partnerships with studios or
production companies for commissioned content creation. Please find
key information below on our programmes, funds and resources available
to documentary filmmakers.

We recommend that you register for our monthly newsletter for
up-to-date news and fund opening dates. Sign up at the bottom of this
page: http://www.docsociety.org/#mailout


Our Funds
Each fund is listed on our website and details how to apply, the
criteria, and fund opening dates. All up-to-date information can be
found on these linked below. If you have completed an application and
have a question, please use the messaging tool in the application
system. It’s easy and will likely get a faster response from us! Visit
https://app.docsociety.org. Login with your credentials (your user
name is your email). Once you are logged in, scroll down the page
until you find your application module. Click on the Send Message
button. Write to us! We will respond as soon as possible:
