# Master Plan: hbnb.datro.xyz

## Vision
Establish hbnb.datro.xyz as the premier landing page for the "Hotspotβnβ" platform — enabling users to host apps on Wi-Fi access points. Within 12 months, it should be a fully compliant, content-rich site with visible product information, community engagement, and polished SEO.

## Category
E-commerce

## Current Status
Most SEO-complete subdomain with OG tags, Twitter card, schema.org Organization markup, Google Analytics, and sitemap.xml. React SPA. Missing visible SSR content, privacy policy, cookie consent banner, social links, blog, contact information pages, and product screenshots in raw HTML.
