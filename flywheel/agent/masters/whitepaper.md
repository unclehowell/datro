# Master Plan: whitepaper.datro.xyz

## Vision
Establish whitepaper.datro.xyz as the central repository for DATRO Consortium's technical papers, research documents, and academic publications. Within 12 months, it should host a well-organized collection of whitepapers with preview, download, and citation capabilities.
