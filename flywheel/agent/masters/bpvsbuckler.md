## Vision
Establish a premier platform for land title case research and advocacy, empowering legal professionals and researchers with indexed document access.

## Current Status
Advocacy and research site requiring a transition to a more sophisticated document indexing and retrieval system.

## Strategic Roadmap
- **Phase 1: Foundation**: Index existing case files and legal documents for full-text search.
- **Phase 2: Professionalism**: Develop a structured research portal with advanced filtering and categorization.
- **Phase 3: Growth**: Launch a community advocacy portal for collaborative legal research and reporting.
