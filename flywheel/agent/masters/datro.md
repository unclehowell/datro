## Vision
Serve as the authoritative and unified portal for the Datro consortium, projecting a consistent and professional brand identity.

## Current Status
Main consortium portal requiring greater brand consistency and a more cohesive narrative across its various branches.

## Strategic Roadmap
- **Phase 1: Foundation**: Harmonize brand elements (logo, typography, color palette) across the entire portal.
- **Phase 2: Professionalism**: Develop a comprehensive brand guideline and apply it to all sub-pages and modules.
- **Phase 3: Growth**: Launch a centralized newsroom and media center to amplify consortium-wide achievements.
