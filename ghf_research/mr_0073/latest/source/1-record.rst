   South Wales Echo, Saturday 21 January 1989, p.2: court report on William (Billy) Buckler

A public-order charge against William (Billy) Buckler was withdrawn by the Crown Prosecution Service at Barry magistrates' court; a bail condition on the remaining charges kept him half a mile from the Great House Farm site. Free text from the archive preview: “Buckler who lived at Great House Farm Llandough until it was demolished by the owners BP Properties in November last year ... has been ordered by the court not to go within half a mile of the Great House Farm site”
