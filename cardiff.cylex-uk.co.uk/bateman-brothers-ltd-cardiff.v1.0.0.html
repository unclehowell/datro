Company description

Established in 1953 Bateman Brothers Ltd has offered an impressive range of quality catering equipment for sale and hire for over half a century. At Batemans we are proud to have a history of working closely with our suppliers to ensure excellent service both locally in Cardiff, South Wales and across the rest of UK As well as supplying commercial companies, clubs, pubs, restaurants and individual party planners with crockery, cutlery, glassware, tableware and table linen for all types of functions Bateman Brothers also provide a full range of cleaning and paper products, bar accessories, table linen, washing and janitorial equipment for general sale.
