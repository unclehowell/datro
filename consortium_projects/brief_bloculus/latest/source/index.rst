Bloculus - Project Brief: Cedwyn Estate Pilot
==============================================

Bloculus pairs each place on the ground with one stable `Uber H3 <https://h3geo.org>`__ hexagon reference, and gives that reference its own web address.
This edition (version 1.0.0) makes the Cedwyn estate, Llandough and Leckwith, south-east of the river Ely in south Wales, the first Bloculus pilot.
Each of the estate's 59 numbered land parcels is proposed to carry the H3 cell of its centre point, and that cell its own sub-domain under ``bucklerfamily.estate``.
