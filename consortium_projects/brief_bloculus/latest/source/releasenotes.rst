Release Notes and Notices

This section provides information about what is new or changed, including urgent issues, documentation updates, maintenance, and new releases.

This Release (Version 1.0.0)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
- **2026-Nov-01** - `Major release: the brief is retargeted to the Cedwyn estate (Llandough and Leckwith, south-east of the river Ely) as the first Bloculus pilot. The earlier coastal pilot area is retired from this document.`
- **2026-Nov-01** - `All addresses move to bucklerfamily.estate (sub-domains such as <h3 cell>.cedwyn.bucklerfamily.estate) until a dedicated top-level domain, for example .cedwyn, is available.`
- **2026-Nov-01** - `Kept the H3 thesis (one hexagon reference per place, addressable, routable). Added the Cedwyn specifics: 59 numbered land parcels, one H3 cell per parcel centre point, and a parcel sub-domain proposal.`
- **2026-Nov-01** - `Sale prices, investment figures and royalty examples from the earlier edition are removed; the pilot's model is a proposal with no prices set.`
- **2026-Nov-01** - `New annex: H3 layers over the pilot area (resolutions 8, 9 and 11). The earlier coastal annex maps are retired.`
- **2026-Nov-01** - `Built in English. German, Spanish and French editions of 1.0.0 are pending translation (see Known Issues).`
