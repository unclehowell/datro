Site procedures · Evidence and archive

Every disclosure request to every institution: what was asked, when, the statutory clock (FOI 20 working days, SAR 1 month, EIR 20 working days), the reply or silence, and what is still to obtain before court.

https://bucklerfamily.estate/ · SOP 1.0.0

1 · Purpose and scope

Every disclosure request to every institution: what was asked, when, the statutory clock (FOI 20 working days, SAR 1 month, EIR 20 working days), the reply or silence, and what is still to obtain before court.

In scope: the pages and data files served at bucklerfamily.estate. Out of scope: facts owned by another site (read them from their owner, below) and any other branch.

2 · Source of truth and data flow

This site owns: Every disclosure request and its status (data/request-status.json).

It reads: Correspondence from the Post Office; deadlines from the statutes.

Inputs to read live before any write:

  • datro@dartboard (Cloudflare Pages build)
  • Gmail replies (via the email site's sync)

Artifacts are linked from Wayback and open in the shared artifact viewer; no copy is kept on this site (single-source rule).

3 · Design system (frozen)

The current layout, navigation, colours, typography and the shared header are frozen. Any change to them is a class C change request approved by the executive. Pages start directly under the shared header; no repeated page titles.

Owner decisions in force on this site:

  • Apex Rule
4 · Content standard

As in the determinism standard and content rules: every factual sentence dated and sourced; record, the family's account and our case labelled; UNRESOLVED instead of a guess.

5 · Public-text rules

All rules in content and public text apply, checked against rules.json on the exact new bytes.

Family privacy and holdings (standing rule)

Standing rule for every maintainer (project owner, 24 Oct 2026):

  • Do not list or characterise what the family holds, or does not hold, in papers or heirlooms. The only family holdings a public site may name are Mary Williams' statement, family photographs, and William Buckler's diaries.
  • Write no sentence about the family's possession or lack of a document. State the public record and the archive that keeps it instead.
  • Never publish current family addresses, the whereabouts of living family members, or any heirloom and where it is kept.
  • Reason: to avoid inviting a repeat of the events of the 1950s.
  • Sweeps for existing text are run separately; a maintainer adds no such statement and raises any found in the highlight report.
6 · Change control

Classes, template and commit rules: change control.

7 · Deploy and verify

Branch: dartboard
Route to live: datro@dartboard (Cloudflare Pages)

Then verify live at 390 px and 1280 px as in deploy and verify.

8 · Routine checks and consistency
  • Recompute each open request's status against today's date: answered / partially / refused / ignored / overdue. A request that crossed its deadline since yesterday is the main daily change.
  • Check the question ledger for replies logged since yesterday and confirm the dartboard shows them.
  • Sampled links and the “artifacts still to obtain” board resolve.

Cross-site: settled values match the consistency ledger; artifact links point to Wayback; evidence citations resolve on Docs.

Every site also serves /llms.txt, /.well-known/mcp.json, /agent/pages.json, /agent/search?q= and /agent/page?slug=. Reference: API, WebMCP.

10 · Issue and risk log

Kept in the Project Office registers in the handbook format, filtered to this site. Open UNRESOLVED points that touch this site are shown on its pages in the ledger's words.

11 · Handover

At the end of each session: branch head commit, live deployment, what changed, anything half done, the next three actions, and any other agent working on this branch. Filed with the highlight report.
