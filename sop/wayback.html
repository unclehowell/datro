Site procedures · Evidence and archive

The central artifact store (datro branch wayback): originals of documents, media, email copies and transcripts that the wiki and every other site link to.

https://wayback.bucklerfamily.estate/ · SOP 1.0.0 verbatim site

1 · Purpose and scope

The central artifact store (datro branch wayback): originals of documents, media, email copies and transcripts that the wiki and every other site link to.

In scope: the pages and data files served at wayback.bucklerfamily.estate. Out of scope: facts owned by another site (read them from their owner, below) and any other branch.

2 · Source of truth and data flow

This site owns: Original artifacts: scans, PDFs, images, audio, video, email copies, transcripts; the chronology (chronology.json, CH ids).

It reads: Nothing upstream. Additions arrive through the evidence sweep with a manifest entry.

Inputs to read live before any write:

  • datro@wayback (manifest + per-item pages)
  • Legal Wiki evidence register (which EV ids should resolve to a wayback copy)

Artifacts are linked from Wayback and open in the shared artifact viewer; no copy is kept on this site (single-source rule).

3 · Design system (frozen)

The current layout, navigation, colours, typography and the shared header are frozen. Any change to them is a class C change request approved by the executive. Pages start directly under the shared header; no repeated page titles.

4 · Content standard

As in the determinism standard and content rules: every factual sentence dated and sourced; record, the family's account and our case labelled; UNRESOLVED instead of a guess. This is a verbatim site: originals are never paraphrased or corrected; only their captions and indexes are edited.

5 · Public-text rules

All rules in content and public text apply, checked against rules.json on the exact new bytes.

6 · Change control

Classes, template and commit rules: change control.

Must never change:

  • Never overwrite or delete an original; additions are new files with a manifest entry.
  • Keep the manifest schema stable (other sites read it); schema changes go through MANIFEST_SPEC.md first.
  • Leckwith visit photos stay unpublished (Glamorgan Archives undertaking).
  • Redact private individuals' phone numbers and home addresses.
7 · Deploy and verify

Branch: wayback
Route to live: datro@wayback (Cloudflare Pages)

Then verify live at 390 px and 1280 px as in deploy and verify.

8 · Routine checks and consistency
  • Manifest integrity: every manifest entry's file exists (HTTP 200) and its hash matches when recorded; no two entries share a hash without a duplicate mark.
  • Every EV id linked from the wiki that should have a wayback copy has one; list the gaps (do not create items — the evidence sweep owns additions).
  • Index page counts (badges) equal the manifest count.
  • Broken links on the landing page and the 25 sampled item pages.

Cross-site: settled values match the consistency ledger; artifact links point to Wayback; evidence citations resolve on Docs.

10 · Issue and risk log

Kept in the Project Office registers in the handbook format, filtered to this site. Open UNRESOLVED points that touch this site are shown on its pages in the ledger's words.

11 · Handover

At the end of each session: branch head commit, live deployment, what changed, anything half done, the next three actions, and any other agent working on this branch. Filed with the highlight report.
