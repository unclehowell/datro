Repo housekeeping · Brand guideline

The look and voice the Great House Farm sites already share, taken from the live CSS and tokens (BucklerFamilyFarm public/ghf-lang/ghf-tokens.css, ghf-theme.css and the colour-pair table in ghf-boot.js 2.1.4). Nothing here is new; it is the standard to keep.

Marks and names

  • The estate is Cedwyn Estate (Welsh Ystâd Cedwyn, Spanish Finca Cedwyn); the farm is Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough. Keep the circumflex in Tŷ.
  • Every site carries the same three bars at the top: a blank strip the height of the WordPress bar (45 px to 782 px wide, 49 px above), the WALES | PATAGONIA | AI bar with flags and the EN / CY / ES menu, and the ‹ SITE NAME › tour bar with full-screen at its left and the menu at its right.
  • Flags: https://bucklerfamily.estate/brand/flags/ (wales.svg, uk.svg and the others the header uses). No heraldry.
  • Site names are the ones in the shared chain, in all three languages; a page never repeats the bar’s name as its own title.

Typography

  • Font: Barlow, then Helvetica Neue, Arial, system sans-serif. Serif for quoted documents: Georgia. Code and references: the system monospace.
  • Scale: 12 · 14 · 16 · 17 · 20 · 22 · 26 px on phones; from 783 px wide 18 / 22 / 28 / 36 px for md, lg, xl and 2xl.
  • Running text (the Legal Wiki reading size): clamp(1rem, 1rem + (1vw − .2rem) × .545, 1.3rem), 16.4 px on a 390 px phone and 20.8 px on a laptop, line-height 1.55, one line between paragraphs, a 760 px reading column.
  • Headings line-height 1.2. Square corners (radius 0) on buttons, inputs and cards in the hybrid theme.

Spacing

4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 px (--ghf-space-1 to --ghf-space-7).

Day and night

A saved choice wins; else the device setting (prefers-color-scheme); else the local time (day 07:00–19:00). ghf-boot.js applies it before the first paint and basecamp.js keeps following the device while nothing is saved. A page drawn dark is inverted for day the same way; photographs, maps and the chessboard keep their own colours.

Layout pattern

  • Minimal landing: the first screen states what the site is and offers the main way in.
  • Detail in collapsible sections (<details>), closed by default except the one the visitor came for.
  • Pages start right under the header; no in-body titles that repeat the bar.
  • Mobile first: every page is checked at 390 px wide with no sideways scroll; tap targets at least 36 px.

Images, captions and credits

  • Every image and document is a Wayback artifact, opened in the shared viewer; a caption gives what it shows, its date and its archive reference.
  • Credits name the holding institution in its own form, e.g. “Llyfrgell Genedlaethol Cymru – The National Library of Wales”, with the rights status it publishes.
  • No private phone numbers, home addresses or photographs of young children.
  • Family holdings named only as Mary Williams' statement, family photographs and William Buckler's diaries.

Language

  • English is the working language; Welsh and Spanish come through the EN / CY / ES menu and the three names of every site in the chain.
  • Welsh place names keep their Welsh spelling (Tŷ Mawr, Llwyn-yr-Eryr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth as the record has it).
  • Patagonia pages carry Spanish as a first language beside Welsh and English.

Tone of voice

  • Plain, dated, sourced. Third person and past tense for events.
  • Institutions and roles, never living private people.
  • No disclaimers or hedging lines; the record, the family’s account and our case are labelled instead.
  • Our own prose never uses the words in rules.json; “occupier” for the family’s position on the land.
