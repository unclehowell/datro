1897 - Marconi is the Williams Families house guest, at Great House Farm

According to family tradition (not yet confirmed in Marconi’s technical papers or estate correspondence)…

Guglielmo Marconi slept at Great House Farm while working on wireless telegraphy experiments, including transmission tests between Lavernock Point and Flat Holm.

By the time Marconi visited in 1897, the house was firmly referred to as Great House Farm in press reports.
