~1850-1950 – Tree Planting
A ceremonial tree planting takes place at the farm, a public symbolic act of land continuity and identity.

Reference: People's Collection Wales item #871416
Description: "A black and white photograph of tree planting by John Williams (Grandfather) to prove land ownership at Great House Farm, Llandough."
Significance: This is CRITICAL primary evidence. John Williams performed a ceremonial tree planting as physical proof of possession — a common Welsh folk practice where planted trees served as permanent markers of tenure. The tree planting appears to have occurred circa 1916, around the time the Marquis formalised the yearly agricultural tenancy.
This photograph should have been entered as evidence in the 1987 case.
