Governance

How public text is written on every site. The word list is kept as data so checks can be automated: rules.json.

Content standard
  • Every factual sentence carries a date and a source link.
  • Record, the family's account and our case are labelled and never blended.
  • Quotations stay verbatim, with their source. Sites marked verbatim (Wayback, the Post Office, Watchtower, Primary Records, the Farm Album editions) carry originals and are never paraphrased.
  • UNRESOLVED is written where the record is silent or split.
  • Third person, past tense for events; present tense only for what a document shows.
Public-text rules
  • No living private person is named. Institutions and roles only. Dead family members may be named.
  • The reporting verbs and loaded words in rules.json (banned_words_in_own_prose) never appear in our own prose. They may appear inside a verbatim quotation.
  • Use "occupier" for the family's position on the land in our own prose.
  • No disclaimers, hedging lines or in-body titles that repeat the header.
  • Nothing about payment policy.
  • No private phone numbers, home addresses or photos of young children.
  • The WordPress Legal Wiki is read only for agents.
Family privacy and holdings (standing rule)

Standing rule for every maintainer (project owner, 24 Oct 2026):

  • Do not list or characterise what the family holds, or does not hold, in papers or heirlooms. The only family holdings a public site may name are Mary Williams' statement, family photographs, and William Buckler's diaries.
  • Write no sentence about the family's possession or lack of a document. State the public record and the archive that keeps it instead.
  • Never publish current family addresses, the whereabouts of living family members, or any heirloom and where it is kept.
  • Reason: to avoid inviting a repeat of the events of the 1950s.
  • Sweeps for existing text are run separately; a maintainer adds no such statement and raises any found in the highlight report.
Checks on the exact new bytes
  1. Search the changed text for every entry in rules.json.
  2. Search for any personal name; confirm it is an institution, a role, or a dead family member.
  3. Confirm every new date or figure has a source link that answers 200.
  4. Re-read the live page after deploy.
