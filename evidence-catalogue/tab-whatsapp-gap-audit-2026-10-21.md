## B. Legal and title checks

### 5. VG41 application deadline and the 31 December 1969 transfer

The chat records a statutory deadline of **2 January 1970**, two days after the reported WGR-to-BP Pension Trust transfer dated 31 December 1969. The proximity is a chronology fact only; it does **not** establish a deadline defect, manipulation or collusion. Tab's own counter-check says the registration authority could register a green without a private application. The Wiki already records the later VG41 objection period (1 May 1970–31 July 1972) in EV-0263.

**Required action:** cite the relevant statutory instrument/legislation and the actual transfer record separately. Do not infer causation from the two dates.

### 6. Land Registry application forms: WA231076 versus WA240304

The chat reports a comparison between **Form 1E** on WA231076 (1982) and **Form 1C** plus a statutory declaration on WA240304 (1983), with a lead to *Wontner's Guide* (1975), p.174. This is a research lead, not proof that BP made a false application or lacked title. A different applicant type or registration route may explain the form difference.

**Required action:** obtain the complete first-registration applications, declarations, title plans and supporting documents for both titles. Verify the form instructions and the cited passage in the actual 1975 guide before stating what each form was for.

### 7. *Smith v Molyneaux* [2016] UKPC 35 — do not overstate its effect

The legal-critique site already contains the necessary caveat: the Privy Council considered *Buckler* in a British Virgin Islands case; the parties accepted the relevant proposition and the Board held that permission need not be acknowledged. The Privy Council decision is persuasive, not binding, on an England-and-Wales court. It should not be described as a general appellate “approval” of every aspect of the 1987 *Buckler* judgment. Official case record: https://jcpc.uk/cases/jcpc-2013-0064

## D. 1940–42 woodland transfer — avoid conflating distinct parcels

The chat describes WGR offering approximately 109 acres of Leckwith Woods to Cardiff in 1940, with the transfer completed in April 1942. The available working land-transfer analysis treats that as a separate parcel question: the outline currently measured is not shown to reach the Great House Farm site, and no link to the 1916 agricultural tenancy has been established. The deed and plan, not the approximate press acreage, must decide whether any part overlaps A-North. Do not write that the 1942 gift included Great House Farm or the 1916 tenancy unless the plan and operative wording demonstrate it.

The reported post-1969 chain involving WGR, BP Pension Trust, BP Properties and the later woodland holder also needs to be kept distinct from the 1942 transfer to Cardiff; the instruments may concern different parcels/interests.
