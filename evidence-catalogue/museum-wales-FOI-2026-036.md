# Amgueddfa Cymru FOI 2026-036

Location in scope: Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, Llandough. Replies distinguish that holding from Llandough Farm (opposite hospital) and from other Llandoughs.

Reply of 5 October 2026 gave Ref 13.2 (waterwheel, Llandough Farm, collected 1 April 1979, deaccessioned), MS 3360/3 (1924 Llandough Estate sale particulars, already supplied under FOI 2026-032), and Church Farm accession 63.24 with three PDFs. No 1875–1915 register entry for farmhouse-floor ironwork.

Reply of 7 October 2026 (not-held clarification): no separate item-by-item exclusion schedule is held. MS 3360/3 page records Great House in the 1924 Llandough Estate particulars, occupied by W.G. Thomas & Sons, about 202 acres, Parish of Llanfair, homestead in the Village of Llandough. Ref 13.2 is recorded as a waterwheel from Llandough Farm (opposite hospital). No exemptions cited.

Reply of 8 October 2026 09:22 UTC (not-held close): the Museum has reviewed the material held under MS 3360/3 and already provided the relevant page. There is no catalogue record identifying Great House Farm within the document. Collections staff identified one page as relating to Great House Farm; that page was supplied and the information on it has already been disclosed and summarised. No further recorded information relating to Great House Farm has been identified within MS 3360/3. Nothing further to add. No new page, plan, or schedule attached. No exemptions cited.

Requester follow-up of 10 October 2026 19:14 UTC: notes the not-held on the exclusion schedule and asks which systems were searched (collections database, search-export, review log, email, paper file) and whether a physical file was retrieved, by whom and on what date (FOIA s.16). Asks confirmation whether the MS 3360/3 catalogue or page names which Llandough (Llandough-juxta-Penarth or Llandough-juxta-Cowbridge); if not, say so. Treats Ref 13.2 as the Penarth hospital site unless a further recorded field says otherwise. Not asking for a new search or a document said not to be held.
