# Thomas Williams and the Ynysawdre Coal Co., 15 June 1878 — not Great House Farm

## What it is
A copy Memorandum of Agreement "made the 15th day of June 1878 Between Thomas Williams of Ynysawdre Farm near Bridgend in the County of Glamorgan Farmer … and the Ynysawdre Coal Company", with an umpire's award of July 1878 (Edward David of Llandaff). The company had taken parts of the farm, "erected Works and deposited Tips", and the claim was for "losses and damages done to his Crops by smoke soot and other combustion". The award reads as £339 2s 6d ("thirty nine" is a reading of the handwriting, to be checked against the original).

## Why it is excluded
- The document names the farm and the tenant: Ynysawdre Farm, near Bridgend (Tondu), about 20 miles west of Llandough. Nothing in it mentions Llandough, Bute or Great House.
- The company was the Ynysawdre (Ynisawdre) Coal, Coke and Brick Co. of Tondu: https://nmrs.org.uk/mines-map/coal-mining-in-the-british-isles/swales/maesteg/ynisawdre-colliery/
- At Llandough in the 1870s the occupier was John Williams, not Thomas (poor-rate books, EV-0212, provisional reading): https://greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com/evidence/ev-0212-llandough-poor-rate-books-1870s/
- The wiki already lists it as not our farm: https://greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com/evidence/excluded-not-our-farm/

## What would change this
A separate agreement or award naming Llandough or Great House Farm. None has been found. As it stands, nothing in this document ties it to the 1878 payment mentioned in Sion Buckler's correspondence with Cadw.
