# FOI / EIR / SAR correspondence tracker — 19 October 2026

Mailbox read through **18 October 2026**. It covers 39 institutions and 253 conversations, 136 of which are FOI, EIR, SAR or internal-review requests. Sion sent 396 emails and received 353 replies; 199 of those replies go beyond an acknowledgement.

Full data: `public/data/foi-tracker.csv` (served at `/data/foi-tracker.csv`) and `src/data/foi-tracker.ts`.

## Method and limits

Built on 19 October 2026 from Sion Buckler’s Gmail (read-only), sent mail and inbox, through 18 October 2026, cross-checked against the wiki correspondence pages (greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com, pages 5356–5374). One row is one conversation with one institution: the email thread, plus any thread sharing its official reference number or subject. Deadlines are computed, not quoted: 20 working days for FOI and EIR (England and Wales bank holidays from 2019), one calendar month for subject access. An authority’s own stated date takes precedence where the row notes one. outcomeAuto and exemptionsAuto are read mechanically from the institution’s own words in each reply, with quoted earlier messages excluded. They can be wrong. Hand-checked rows say so in the note column. ‘Possibly answered in another thread — check’ means a later substantive reply from the same institution exists elsewhere in the mailbox. Requests made through WhatDoTheyKnow show only the notification emails here; the request and response text is on WhatDoTheyKnow. No email addresses, phone numbers or other contact details are included. Officials are not named; institutions and official reference numbers only. Contested history is the family’s account. Nothing here is a finding of fact or of wrongdoing.
