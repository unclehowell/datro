Repo housekeeping · Uniformity checklist

What every site, and every site manual, must have. A site that fails a line is raised in the highlight report; the manual records the gap.

Every manual

PartMust contain
OverviewWhat the site is, what it does, its purpose in the estate, who it is for, and its previous and next stop on the tour (or why it is off the tour).
How it worksBranch and head, build and deploy path, the source of truth it owns, what it reads, which sites read from it, and the shared layer (ghf-boot 2.1.4 last in <head>, tokens, Wayback viewer, agent layer).
Upkeep SOPContent standard, public-text rules, the family privacy and holdings rule word for word, change control with what must never change, routine checks, issue and risk log, handover, and deploy-verify at 390 px.
Agent interfacellms.txt, mcp.json, tools.json and webmcp.js status; every tool with its GET endpoint; curl -4 examples checked live; gaps listed, nothing invented.
ChangelogManual versions with dates, the branch head at build and a link to the site’s full history.

Every site

  • Loads https://bucklerfamily.estate/ghf-lang/ghf-boot.js?v=2.1.4 as the last element in <head> (not async, not defer), so the header, the ‹ › tour bar, the language menu and day/night are the same everywhere.
  • Appears once in campus/site_chain.json (BucklerFamilyFarm) with its English, Welsh and Spanish names; the tour order is changed only there.
  • Uses the shared tokens and its own colour pair from the brand guideline; no new fonts or colours without a class C change.
  • Starts directly under the header: no in-body title repeating the bar’s name; minimal landing, detail in collapsible sections.
  • Reads at 390 px with no sideways scroll, in day and night.
  • Links every artifact to Wayback and opens it in the shared viewer; keeps no copy of its own (information flows).
  • Answers /llms.txt, /.well-known/mcp.json and /agent/tools.json; any data file the site owns has a tool.
  • Public text: institutions and roles only, no living private person named, none of the words in rules.json in our own prose, no disclaimers, nothing about payment policy.
  • Family privacy and holdings: names no family holdings beyond Mary Williams' statement, family photographs and William Buckler's diaries; no family addresses or whereabouts; no heirloom or where it is kept.
  • Every factual sentence dated and sourced; record, the family’s account and our case labelled; UNRESOLVED where the record is silent.
  • Commits made by an agent carry the agent’s signature line; no force-push; another maintainer’s commit is never overwritten.
