Mandate and brief

Beyond FOI: preserve the source trail

Scope: FOI/EIR requests, internal reviews and complaints; archive catalogues and files; land registration, plans and conveyances; planning and land charges; archaeology and heritage records; court and administrative material; web/archive captures; email messages and attachments; witness accounts; chronology, parcel mapping and cross-record reconciliation.

Required result: a reproducible account of what was located, captured, requested, supplied, withheld, reported unavailable, still outstanding or confirmed destroyed by a supporting source. Preserve the original source or reliable copy and its provenance before interpreting it.

Distinguish states: “not held”, “not found”, “no reply”, “incomplete”, “withheld/refused” and “destroyed” are different. Non-response or a catalogue gap alone does not demonstrate that a record was destroyed.

Witness privacy: keep names, contact details, raw notes, recordings and personal correspondence in the restricted execution ledger. Note consent and provenance; separate first-hand observation from recollection and later interpretation. Public extracts require separate accuracy, privacy and lawful-release checks.

Relationship to P1/P3: provide traceable source updates and corrections by controlled version/change process. Do not make completion of every recovery route a prerequisite for preliminary museum or tour work.
