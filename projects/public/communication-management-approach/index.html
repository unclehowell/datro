Rules

  • One email per recipient body, covering everything for that body; no cc/bcc.
  • UK office hours; reply in the body’s latest thread, to the address it last replied from.
  • Before each send: inbox checked; draft read in full; refs, dates and quotes checked; no accusation as fact, threat or admission; no placeholders; no duplicate of a pending request.
  • Unverified items marked unverified; sources named.
  • Outside WhatsApp’s 24-hour window: approved template only.
  • Cadence changes only with Sion’s agreement (L-007).
  • No contact details or SAR replies on public pages.

Rules

  • Tab contacts no third party; every external message is sent by Sion.
  • Each update names its sources; unverified items are marked unverified.
  • Update cadence changes only with Sion’s agreement (L-007).
  • Public pages carry no private contact details or privileged advice.
