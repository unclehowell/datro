Stage Plan — Parcel-A Loss & Remedy Sprint (9 October–14 November 2026)

Stage objective: corroborate and legally characterise the point [SOURCE PENDING: execution record not yet found] that on 4 July 1955 WGR seized Parcel-A forest north of the house and ground south-east later registered as VG41 while enforcing possession of Parcel B; trace those exact polygons into present ownership/title; and obtain a solicitor’s route decision on remedies that can still be pursued. This is not a fresh inquiry into whether these areas were taken.

Stage boundary

In: corroboration and mapping of the established 1955 seizure account; the distinction between full Parcel A and the house/buildings/garden group Mary retained; 1962 house-group scope comparison; Mary’s statement and family evidence provenance; VG41 registration file and present title; north-forest 1955→1994→2005→current title chain; HMLR Schedule 4/8; protective notice/standstill decision; court/corporate disclosure; ICR.

Out: re-research of the 1939 agreement (already obtained; Parcel-B-only); routine D696 rent-book work unless a direct Parcel-A/woodland/VG41 lead appears; broad historical research unrelated to the actual lost land.

Stage controls

Zero tolerance: evidence-preservation and legal deadlines.

Progress: only an executed filing/request, acquired primary evidence, completed human review, written legal decision or formally recorded blocker counts.

Contrary evidence: retain results that weaken the family case. Primary records may alter the legal analysis of authority or title, and the VG41 file may identify an independent claimant/source. Such results must be recorded and evaluated without treating the family’s known account of what happened as a blank question.

Stage exit — 14 November 2026

The exit dossier must map the family’s established account of the 1955 seizure of the north forest and south-east VG41 ground during Parcel-B enforcement; state what the surviving legal process documents authorise or describe; assess WGR’s asserted underlying right; identify who claimed/owned the green by 1970; trace the same north-forest polygon into the current title chain and current owner(s); and record which remedies survive after limitation/purchaser/registration analysis.

Unresolved issues leave the stage only with a named evidential blocker and a next decision date. They do not reopen the general research loop. 🕒
