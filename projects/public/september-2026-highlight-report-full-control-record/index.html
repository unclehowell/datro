Issues, risks and concerns

  • No September response established that the two historic parcels were one parcel or that the 1982 registration was a mistake. Those remain evidence questions.
  • An acknowledgement is not evidential closure. A file remains open until its acceptance test is met or a documented decision closes it.
  • The woodland is for sale; absent a controlled decision or preservation action, there is a risk of open-market sale in early 2027.
  • The fraud/conduct line remains parked because no dishonest person has been identified in the current evidence set.
  • The judicial-review window associated with the 25 May HMLR decision has probably already closed, subject to legal verification.

Phase-1 evidence exhaustion principle

September establishes the programme as an evidence-gathering and exhaustion exercise before later material is destroyed, lost, dispersed or becomes harder to recover. Exhaustion means documenting each search and request, reconciling each response, preserving surviving material, identifying what was not found, recording search limitations, and closing only those avenues for which the project has a documented basis to stop. 🕒
