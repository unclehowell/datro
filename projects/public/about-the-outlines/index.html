How these pages follow PRINCE2 7, and where the outlines come from.

What changed on 21 October 2026

Official outlines; nothing invented
  • Every management product now uses the official PRINCE2 product outline, in the official order, with the official headings.
  • Where the record holds nothing for a heading, the page records that (“None this period”, “Not yet set”, “Not recorded”). Nothing is invented to fill a heading.
  • Tolerances are shown as not yet agreed by the Project Board; figures proposed in the Project Brief are shown as proposals.
  • Highlight reports: one per reporting period, consecutive, none overlapping. Reports for November 2025 to September 2026 are reconstructions made in October 2026 from the dated record.
  • Earlier pages with invented structures (dashboards, compliance manual, report templates, programme pages) are redirected to the product that now holds their real content.

Names used

Roles; 7th-edition names
  • Roles, not people: “the Executive”, “the Project Manager”.
  • PRINCE2 7 names: “approach” (not “strategy”), “issue management approach” (formerly change control approach).
