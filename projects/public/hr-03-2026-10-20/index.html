I-032 — Identity of ‘Mary Buckler’ in 1974–1983 records · Open · Owner: Sion's Tab agent.

  • Two daughters were registered Mary (births 1940 and 1954). The younger, Mary Buckler (later Godfrey), married in 1972.
  • The 31 October 1974 letters are addressed to “Mary Buckler” on the family-held copies (image SOURCE PENDING); the Court of Appeal describes them as “addressed to Mrs Buckler (described again as Mrs Williams)”. Both are recorded.
  • On that date “Mary Buckler” could denote the elder woman, Mary (Nancy) Williams, formerly Buckler, who never formally gave up the name (her 1983 death is indexed under both Buckler and Williams), or the daughter born 1940 if still unmarried. Not Mrs Godfrey.
  • The question: which woman the addressee denotes, and why it differs from the “Mrs Williams” used in BP’s 1974 action. The 1982–83 registrations stay open.
  • Open records: the letter image; the 1940 daughter’s marital status in 1974; the 11 December 1962 order; the 29 March 1961 deed on the WA231076 file (also a “two Fredericks” question: father and eldest son, both adults 1958–1967); item 11 of the HM Land Registry subject access request (the Form 1C declaration), due 28 October.
  • Full timeline: Legal Wiki page “Two Mary Bucklers? Name and identity timeline”.
