Issue management approach

Three issue types; Executive decides beyond tolerance

Issue types: request for change; off-specification; problem/concern. Record: Issue Register, with an issue report where needed. Authority: the Project Manager within tolerance; the Executive beyond tolerance or on any baseline change. Change budget: none.

Risk management approach

Identify, assess, respond, record, escalate

Identify: each research cycle, each reply received, each stage boundary. Assess: probability and impact (High / Medium / Low; programme risks on 1–5 scales), and proximity. Respond: avoid, reduce, fallback, transfer, share, accept. Record: Risk Register. Escalate: any risk outside tolerance, or scoring 15 or more, or needing spending or an outward step, goes to the Executive; a forecast tolerance breach brings an Exception Report.
