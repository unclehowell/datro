The need

I want all surviving evidence relating to Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr recovered.

What I want

  • Every available route for obtaining records and artefacts exhausted.
  • Anything that cannot be obtained positively ruled out as destroyed, missing, classified, or lost forever.
  • If evidence of fraud, concealment, or government involvement is discovered that meets the threshold to reopen a case or report it, that triggers a separate project running in parallel.

What I do not want in this document

How the work will be done. That belongs to the Project Brief. The detailed schedule belongs to the Project Plan.
