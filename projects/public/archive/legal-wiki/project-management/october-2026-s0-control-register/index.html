5. October completion states

  • Completed: acceptance test passed and evidence/result link recorded.
  • Carried forward: still required, with a new justified target.
  • Blocked: dependency prevents completion; blocker and owner recorded.
  • Awaiting external response: the project has completed its current action and is waiting on the external party.
  • Re-opened: new evidence or contradiction requires the task to return to active work.
  • Closed: a documented decision demonstrates that no further project action is required. 🕒

6. Evidence-exhaustion objective

The current phase is not simply “FOI chasing”. It is controlled exhaustion of reasonable evidence avenues while surviving records can still be preserved or recovered. Each avenue should end in one of four documented states: recovered and verified; not found after defined search; externally blocked / awaiting response; or closed with a recorded reason. This prevents silence, acknowledgement or administrative closure from being mistaken for evidential completeness. 🕒

7. Gate and reporting

October feeds the 18 December 2026 G0 decision. The monthly Highlight Report records what happened; the Delivery Breakdown defines what “done” means; this register records the October execution baseline; Analytics measures performance; the Evidence Library holds the underlying documentary record.
