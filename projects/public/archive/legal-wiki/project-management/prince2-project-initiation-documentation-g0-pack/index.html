Projects in the programme

Constraints

  • Research support only, not legal advice.
  • the Executive makes every external contact and request; Tab contacts nobody and orders nothing.
  • the Executive is outside the UK (2922): searchroom work needs paid copying or a proxy.
  • Privileged legal material is not published.
  • Free WordPress plan: no plugins; editing connection under a 30-day grace period (R-010).

Tolerances

Quality management approach

  • Standard: Editing Protocol, Claim–Evidence Linking.
  • Methods: line-by-line check against the opened source; claim audit; Executive review.
  • Records: Quality Register.

Risk management approach

  • Identify: each flywheel cycle, each reply received, each stage boundary.
  • Assess: probability and impact H/M/L (PM assessment); proximity.
  • Respond: avoid, reduce, fallback, transfer, share, accept.
  • Record: Risk Register.
  • Escalate: any risk outside tolerance → Exception Report.

Issue management approach

  • Types: request for change; off-specification; problem/concern.
  • Record: Issue Register; change requests in Change Control.
  • Authority: PM within tolerance; Executive beyond tolerance or on any baseline change.

G0 approval test

  • Each PID complete enough to authorise the next stage.
  • Business Case still justified within tolerance.
  • Roles accepted.
  • Product descriptions and quality criteria adequate.
  • Risk, issue, change and communication controls in place.
  • Stage Plan, Work Packages and acceptance tests defined.
  • No open exception above PM authority without a decision.

Operational assurance baseline — 19 October 2026

This is a dated control addendum to the working PID; it does not authorise external correspondence, paid orders, legal steps, or a new project baseline. The 18 December 2026 G0 gate remains unchanged. The HubSpot audit found 305 tasks (201 NOT_STARTED, 48 WAITING, 21 IN_PROGRESS, 35 COMPLETED); 111 open tasks are flagged overdue, 72 open tasks have priority NONE, and 55 HIGH-priority open tasks are overdue.

  • Association gap: 70 tickets are currently in open pipeline stages; 27 tasks are directly associated with tickets. The documented TASK → WP ticket → PROJECT ticket → PROGRAMME hierarchy must be reconciled against live associations; the older P1 deal has task associations that do not substitute for work-package links.
  • Held communications: 22 HubSpot tasks mirror Gmail drafts whose subjects explicitly say DON’T SEND. Ten were quarantined on 19 October as WAITING / LOW with a review date of 26 October; 12 remain to classify. Original bodies, recipients, message IDs and draft links must be preserved. Nothing is sent by this control update.
  • Organisation hygiene: 125 company records include eight duplicate-domain pairs. Among 180 contacts, 172 have a blank textual company field; that alone does not prove the CRM association is absent. Inspect associations and preserve distinct people, cases and correspondence before any merge.

Deterministic artifact-recovery cycle

  1. Trigger: every new reply, archive discovery, notice, deadline change or scheduled review starts a recorded cycle. Do not claim a six-hour cycle ran unless a run log or dated record proves it.
  2. Capture: preserve the original artifact or message, source/reference, sender/custodian, received/sent date, exact attachments and metadata. Keep sensitive or privileged material out of public pages.
  3. Catalogue: assign or confirm a stable Evidence Library identifier; record provenance, hash where available, preservation location, access restrictions and what the artifact does and does not establish.
  4. Control: update the authoritative case ticket; associate the task to its work-package and project ticket where those records exist; set one accountable owner, a justified priority, the next action and a verified due date.
  5. Accept: distinguish acknowledgement, substantive response, artifact received, search completed, no-records response, withheld/partial response and unconfirmed lead. An acknowledgement alone never closes the work.
  6. Escalate and learn: act the same day on a statutory/preservation threat; issue an exception when tolerance is forecast to be exceeded; record decision/lesson, next control date and evidence of completion.

Control rules: no automatic merge, deletion, sending, or completion changes to improve dashboard counts. Duplicate resolution requires review of associations and source context. The no-send quarantine is a safety state, not closure.
