Justice Programme — Brief (v4)

Brief date: 28 September 2026. Version 4: 16 October 2026. Derived from: Justice Mandate v4 (M1–M4). Stage: S0 Start-up & Initiation. Next gate: G0, 18 December 2026. Document status: Foundation for the Project Plan v4.

1. Project definition

Convert the four mandate objectives into parallel, evidence-led legal, administrative, political and public-interest workstreams, each subject to jurisdiction, standing, limitation, evidence and remedy tests. The project does not assume fraud, concealment or government involvement; it builds the documentary record that allows those questions to be answered with court-ready precision. The brief is the single source of truth for what the project will deliver, how it will be governed, and what success looks like.

2. Project approach

  • Evidence first: preserve, locate, catalogue and provenance every relevant record before any legal conclusion.
  • Title-first: reconstruct the 1877–1987 chain for each physical land object before choosing remedy routes.
  • Separate tracks: title/extent (P2-T); registration mistake (P2-T); indemnity (P2-T); fraud-based reopening (P2-F); current institutional remedies (P2-C). Evidence of a title defect does not establish fraud; missing records do not prove concealment.
  • Institution-first: give each body a properly particularised opportunity to investigate, disclose and remedy before litigation, without sacrificing limitation.
  • Counsel-gated: no irreversible step without G1 opinion (25 June 2027).
  • Communications-gated: public and political statements require Sponsor approval and legal review for defamation and prejudice risk.

3. Business case

  • Reason: potential loss of family title, possession and records; possible fraud/concealment/government involvement; heritage and narrative-justice value; public interest in governance accountability and land-registration integrity in Wales.
  • Options: (a) do nothing — records degrade and limitation clocks run; (b) evidence-only — preserves records but secures no remedy; (c) full programme — evidence, title, fraud, possession, institutional and narrative tracks in parallel.
  • Preferred option: (c), sequenced so evidence products feed legal decisions.
  • Benefits: verified evidence corpus; title/possession determination; viable reopening decision; quantified claim; public record correction; Argentina museum foundation; potential institutional reform.
  • Costs: Phase-1 £0–£800; professional costs TBC; adverse-costs exposure to be quantified at G1.
  • Risks: evidence destruction before retrieval (RED/AMBER); limitation expiry; no identified dishonest person; innocent proprietors in possession; public-rights overlay on the green; political and media misrepresentation.

6. Roles and responsibilities

  • ROLE-PGM (Programme/Project Governance): owns the mandate, approves G0/G1 decisions, authorises litigation, owns budget envelope, approves public communications.
  • ROLE-PM (Project Manager): day-to-day execution, evidence acquisition, FOI/preservation requests, control registers, highlight reports, G0 pack preparation, stakeholder communications within approved parameters.
  • Counsel/solicitors: instructed by 26 March 2027; G1 opinion 25 June 2027.
  • External experts: independent valuation, land survey, archive specialists as required.
  • Communications advisor (as required): media and public-interest strategy, subject to legal review.

7. Quality management approach

A task/product is complete only when its defined acceptance test is satisfied. External acknowledgement or administrative closure does not establish evidential completeness. Every evidence record retains provenance and distinguishes documented fact, family evidence and inference. Counter-evidence is recorded for every proposition. Public-interest claims are sourced and caveated; no inference of wrongdoing is published without qualifying evidence.

8. Risk management approach

Risks, issues and concerns are logged with ID, type, description, status and owner, reviewed weekly in the highlight report, and addressed through the following week’s plan. The RED/AMBER evidence-preservation risk is the top-priority exposure. Political and reputational risks are logged alongside legal risks.

9. Change control approach

Scope changes require Sponsor approval and are recorded in the issue/change register. The 9 October 2026 scope correction (full Parcel A including forest and green) is the controlling example. Version control: mandate v4, brief v4, plan v4 issued together on 16 October 2026.

10. Communications and public-interest approach

  • The wiki is the public documentary archive; HubSpot is the private execution ledger.
  • Public statements are evidence-led, neutral in tone, and legally reviewed before release.
  • Political engagement (e.g., Llandough Community Council, Welsh Government) is documented and follows the institutional remedy ladder.
  • No public allegation of fraud, concealment or government involvement without qualifying evidence on the fraud/concealment register.

11. Limitation and deadline matrix (summary)

  • Fraud/concealment: Limitation Act 1980 s.32 — discovery-based accrual.
  • Registered-land rectification: LRA 2002 Sch.4.
  • HRA: one year; JR: three months; ECHR fresh: four months; PSOW: one year.
  • Control: for every newly discovered fact, record exact discovery date, source and prior discoverability.
