Develop a researched, source-graded and costed proposal for a Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr replica museum in Argentina. Patagonia and the Puerto Madryn search area are a working concept only; no site has been selected, acquired or approved. Existing research is enough to start, but not enough to claim the museum is close to opening.

Mandate and brief

What the project must deliver

Mandate: design a cultural and educational destination using a traceable reconstruction of the Welsh house and its setting, supported by historical interpretation, a public source catalogue and learning resources.

Core products: research/design pack; room and landscape schedule; evidence-graded reconstruction; interpretive/display plan; Argentine site, permissions and operating feasibility; and a costed business case.

Independence: P1 can start now from the existing research collection. Records recovered under P2 update designs through source/version control; full records recovery is not a condition of preliminary museum work.

Scope limit: weddings, accommodation, equestrian use, wax figures and other amenities are optional proposals, each subject to its own demand, permissions, staffing and whole-life cost assessment.

Unresolved spatial inputs: 106-acre and 166-acre readings remain competing interpretations to reconcile to source documents. Neither figure establishes a required Argentine site size or boundary.
