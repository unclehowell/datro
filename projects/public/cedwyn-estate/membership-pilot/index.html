Cedwyn Estate algocracy: membership pilot

Project level (Phase 2) · version 1.0 · 21 October 2026 · Sion's Tab agent

A free, voluntary membership for residents and businesses of Llandough, Leckwith and Michaelston-le-Pit, run by published rules that members can read and vote on. It sits under Phase 2 (Cedwyn Estate).

“be sure prince 2 (projects.) has cedwyn estate as a new algocratic/ algocracy which residents and businesses can become members in.”

Programme owner, 21 October 2026 (verbatim)

Business case

  • Reason: give the estate a working community layer now: one place to find every local business and organisation, and a way for people to take part in how it is run.
  • Benefits: local businesses are found more easily; members see and shape the rules; the estate learns what people need. Measured by members joined, profiles updated by their owners, votes cast and enquiries passed between members.
  • Costs: hosting within existing accounts (directory site and join form); agent time; no spending without the owner's approval.
  • Options considered: do nothing; a static list; the directory plus membership pilot (chosen: lowest cost, reversible, opt-in).
  • Not part of this project: any statutory function, any land or title question, any paid product.

Membership tiers

ResidentPeople who live in the areaAn optional profile (only what they choose to share), one vote on published rules, notice of proposalsFree
BusinessBusinesses and organisations trading or based in the areaControl of their directory profile, one vote per business, member-to-member enquiriesFree

Coins: if used at all, coins are closed-loop store credit, redeemable only with participating members. They have no cash value and cannot be bought for money, sold, transferred outside the scheme or exchanged.

Governance principles

  1. Published rules. Every rule that ranks, shows or hides a profile, or counts a vote, is published in plain English and as the code that runs it.
  2. Members can see and vote. One member, one vote. Any member can propose a rule change; proposals are open for 14 days; a change passes with a simple majority of votes cast and a quorum of 10% of members.
  3. Open log. Every rule change, approval and removal is written to a public change log (no personal data).
  4. People over rules. Any member can ask for a human review of an automatic decision about them.
  5. Consent first. Residents appear only by their own choice; anyone can edit or delete their profile at any time.
  6. No statutory power. The pilot is a voluntary membership scheme. It does not replace or speak for any council.

Risks

RiskResponse

PECR: unsolicited emailOne introduction email per business, to its published business address only, with an opt-out line; no marketing email to private individuals without consent; all emails are drafts until the owner reviews them.
Consent recordsEach join request stores the consent tick and time; unapproved requests are deleted after 90 days.
Wrong or stale informationEvery profile shows its sources and the date last checked; a daily refresh; owners can correct their own profile.
Coins treated as moneyClosed-loop store credit only, no cash value, no purchase, no exchange.
Impersonation of a business or residentChanges are confirmed by email to the person or the business's published address before anything is shown.
