The programme keeps Welsh parcel and archive research distinct from the proposed Great House Farm II museum in Argentina and the Wales virtual reconstruction. The workstreams share research standards and reporting, but have separate land, legal, funding and delivery decisions. The physical museum has no selected or acquired Argentine site.

Project definition

Background, objectives, scope, constraints, tolerances, users, interfaces

Background

The Cedwyn Estate programme runs in two phases. Phase 1 is Parcel A (Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr). Phase 2 is the rest of the Cedwyn Estate, about 40–45 parcels (CH-004).

Project objectives

Develop a repeatable, source-linked method for assessing the remaining Cedwyn Estate parcels after the Phase 1 method and outcome are understood; record why each further parcel is researched, deferred or closed.

Desired outcomes

A prioritised parcel register, clear source trail and list of material unknowns; decisions tied to the evidence standard; a controlled membership pilot with separate consent and data-protection controls. The Argentina museum and Wales virtual-tour projects remain related but separate workstreams.

Project scope and exclusions

In: the remaining Cedwyn Estate parcels; the membership pilot. Out: Parcel A (Phase 1).

Constraints and assumptions

  • Phase 2 parcel work depends on Phase 1: the method, the evidence standard and the outcome on Parcel A decide whether and how each further parcel is opened.
  • The evidence programme depends on archive access (National Library of Wales, The National Archives, Glamorgan Archives, Land Registry) and on new tools as they arrive.
  • The membership pilot depends only on consent and data-protection controls (see its risks). It makes no statement about land ownership.
