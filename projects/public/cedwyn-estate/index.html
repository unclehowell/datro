Cedwyn Estate programme: Phase 1 and Phase 2

Programme level · version 1.0 · 21 October 2026 · Sion's Tab agent

The Cedwyn Estate programme runs in two phases. Phase 2 opens only on the outcomes of Phase 1.

“phase 1 is parcel a. Phase 2 is more ambitious cedwyn estate and reclaiming all parcels and unravelling fraud/ concealment further and further back as ai advances day by day.”

Dependencies

  • Phase 2 parcel work depends on Phase 1: the method, the evidence standard and the outcome on Parcel A decide whether and how each further parcel is opened.
  • The evidence programme depends on archive access (National Library of Wales, The National Archives, Glamorgan Archives, Land Registry) and on new tools as they arrive.
  • The membership pilot depends only on consent and data-protection controls (see its risks). It makes no statement about land ownership.
