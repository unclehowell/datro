# Changelog

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-04

### Added
- Added the Vision of Britain unit 10484613 alternate-name association “LLANDOUGH JUXTA BARRY” to the Llandough page, explicitly identified as a secondary database attribution to the 1831 Population Abstract. The original printed wording, page and row remain unverified.
- Added a separate searchable page for the two other historical occurrences of Llandough-juxta-Barry: the 1440 E-CIPM transcription (Barry or Cowbridge) and the 1922 Bute / Great Western Railway catalogue entry DB/17. Neither is linked to Great House Farm or the later Penarth parish.
- Expanded Llandough and related concept search forms to include supplied administrative, postcode, grid-reference, property and landmark locators. Contextual locators are searchable but are not promoted to independent name attestations.

### Method
- Kept original-source wording, catalogue descriptions and later database associations distinct. No conclusion is drawn about property identity from a shared place-name string.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-24

### Improved
- Provide consistent visible keyboard focus for term links, alphabetical navigation, search and interactive controls.
- Refresh the stylesheet cache key and align the lexicon data version / generated date with the rerelease.

### Validated
- 25 concepts / 147 recorded variants: every variant has a terminology status and source-description; 146 have a URL. The `BP plc` entry is explicitly a Wiki label without a source URL, so it has not been promoted to a sourced record.
- No concepts, terms or underlying claims were changed.
