Implementation status
=====================

This guide defines the intended shared explanation and editorial standard. It does not mean every existing page has already been audited or stripped of duplicate explanatory material. Site-wide footer links should direct readers here; page-by-page cleanup must preserve substantive case content and is only complete when verified.

`Return to the Great House Farm Wiki homepage <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/>`__.

**High-level theory cross-reference:** Editorial rule for this Wiki: link materially relevant pages to the `Cedwyn Estate Working Theory <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/top-level-understanding-williams-parcel-a-title-sabotaged-severed-and-buried-while-a-synthetic-mimic-estate-was-forged/>`__; state which proposition a source supports; distinguish direct evidence from inference and speculation; record counterevidence; and do not claim that the family currently owns all Llandough/Leckwith or that a Wales-wide strategy is proved without original parcel-specific title and independent corroboration.

Change history is maintained on the `Master Change Log <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/master-change-log-october-2026/>`__; no separate page-local version footer is maintained.

Agents and editors: follow the `Editing Protocol <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/editing-protocol/>`__ and the :ref:`no-page-purpose-prose rule <editorial-standards>`.
