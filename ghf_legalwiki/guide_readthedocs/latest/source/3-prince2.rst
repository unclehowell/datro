.. _prince2:

PRINCE2 page types
==================

.. _prince2-index:

PRINCE2 index
-------------

Lists the project’s management products and their state.

.. _prince2-mandate:

Project Mandate
---------------

The client’s instruction that starts the project: the need and the outcome wanted. Not how or when.

.. _prince2-brief:

Project Brief
-------------

Defines the project from the Mandate: objectives, scope, constraints, tolerances, users, outline business case, project product and approach.

.. _prince2-pid:

Project Initiation Documentation (PID)
--------------------------------------

The baseline for running the project: links the definition, Business Case, roles, approaches (quality, risk, issue, communication), plan, controls and tailoring.

.. _prince2-business-case:

Business Case
-------------

Why the project is worth doing: reasons, options, benefits, dis-benefits, timescale, costs and major risks. Reviewed at each gate.

.. _prince2-roles:

Organisation and roles
----------------------

Who holds each PRINCE2 role and whether it is confirmed.

.. _prince2-communication:

Communication Management Approach
---------------------------------

Who receives what, through which channel and how often.

.. _prince2-plan:

Project Plan
------------

Stages, dated schedule with dependencies, critical path, assumptions and the cost table.

.. _prince2-stage-plan:

Stage Plan
----------

The current stage in detail and the next stage in outline.

.. _prince2-pbs:

Product Breakdown Structure and Product Descriptions
----------------------------------------------------

The products the project must deliver, and for each: purpose, composition, derivation, format, quality criteria, quality method and responsibilities.

.. _prince2-work-package:

Work Packages
-------------

Agreed parcels of work: products, owner, dates, tolerances, dependencies and acceptance.

.. _prince2-registers:

Registers
---------

Risk, issue, quality, daily log, lessons and decisions, each with a unique ID and status. Records are closed, never deleted.

.. _prince2-change-control:

Change Control
--------------

Log of change requests with impact and decision, and the blank request form.

.. _prince2-ci-record:

Configuration Item Records
--------------------------

The controlled items (management products and EV pages), their status, location and owner.

.. _prince2-highlight-report:

Highlight Report
----------------

Periodic report to the Executive: status, work done, work next, tolerances, changes, issues, risks, lessons.

.. _prince2-report-templates:

Report templates
----------------

Blank Checkpoint, Exception and End Stage Reports in PRINCE2 7 composition.
