# SOP and Actions-On: Discovery of Fraud, Concealment or Government Involvement

## Purpose
Transparent fact-finding and evidence gathering continues. This SOP activates if primary records or disclosures establish fraud, concealment, or government involvement beyond the already-suspected patterns in the Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr case (BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] and related).

## Trigger
Discovery of:
- False statements in official records or court filings that were known to be false at the time.
- Systematic destruction, suppression or non-disclosure of records that should have been preserved or produced.
- Direct government or public-authority direction or collusion in the dispossession or subsequent cover.

## Immediate Actions
1. Preserve the source record (hash, screenshot, certified copy request).
2. Catalogue to greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com (Evidence Library, Anomalies Register, Missing Records Register, FOI Reply Register).
3. Cross-reference to the museum narrative at bpvsbuckler.bucklerfamily.estate (add or update scene in slides.json / timeline).
4. Draft (do not auto-send) targeted FOI/EIR/SAR/internal-review or preservation notice to the custodian.
5. Log in HubSpot as a note associated to the case contact/deal, stamped ROLE-RES / ROLE-ADM.
6. Prepare highlight report entry for the next PRINCE2 monthly report.

## Escalation Path
- Procedural chasers on statutory clocks remain auto-eligible.
- New claims, ICO complaints, or Ombudsman referrals stay in draft for sign-off unless explicit authorisation.
- Public-interest statement for the wiki and museum player remains factual and evidence-linked.

## Ongoing
Fact-finding remains the primary activity. No assumption of outcome; every stone is turned via records access.
