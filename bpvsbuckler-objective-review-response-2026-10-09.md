# Objective Review Response — Parcel-A Woodland and VG41 (9 October 2026)

## Purpose and controlling scope

This note integrates the independent *Great House Farm — Objective Review of Chronology, FOI Record and Routes to Remedy* (8 October 2026) into the active investigation. The priority remains the alleged 4 July 1955 taking of Parcel-A woodland and the land later registered as VG41, and the subsequent route of those exact physical areas into present title.

**Closed:** the 1939 WGR–John Williams agreement/plan is already held and its plan depicts Parcel B only. Do not create another acquisition task for it. D696 rent books are background only unless a specific entry directly concerns Parcel A, the woodland, VG41, or the 1955 event.

## What the independent review usefully corrects

1. **The 1969 deed / 1971 abstract gap is not Parcel-A-specific.** The missing items are recited in both BP conveyance chains. Their absence from HMLR's 30 July 2026 schedule is a discrepancy worth resolving, but does not by itself prove that Parcel A had no root or that Parcel B had a better chain.
2. **Do not lead with fraud.** First determine the physical extent, the source of WGR's right to possess each area, the scope of the 1955 order and execution, and whether the present register includes land by mistake. Fraud, deliberate concealment, and knowing misdescription remain contingent on proof of a specific actor, act, mental element where required, materiality, and causation.
3. **The 1955 order's plan matters as much as the words “whole farm”.** Reconstruct separately (a) the land adjudicated by the claim/order, (b) the land the writ/warrant authorised the officer to enter, and (c) the land actually taken on 4 July 1955. The physical polygon is the bridge to current remedies.
4. **The 1962 farmhouse/garden proceedings are a boundary-control record.** Compare their pleading, property description, plan, and mesne-profits basis with the 1955 execution record. Do not use them to reopen the closed 1939 plan finding.
5. **The 1905 conveyance, 15 May 1924 agreement, and 29 March 1961 deed are low-cost root-of-title tests.** Read them because they may establish or defeat a paper route into the registered title. They are a parallel title-chain test, not a substitute for reconstructing the physical 1955 taking.
6. **Provenance of family-held papers matters.** Record when and how the 1938 conveyance and 1939 tenancy came into family custody, and whether they were available to the family or its advisers during the 1986–87 litigation. Do not infer concealment if relevant documents were held but not produced; establish why.
7. **Do not assume WA240304 is Parcel A without its plan.** A title number is not a substitute for a polygon. Every historic instrument and current title must be plotted against the same identified ground.
8. **Do not generalise “land return is unrealistic” to the remaining woodland.** Built-over plots may raise strong purchaser-protection issues, but the remaining woodland is a distinct asset with a reported possible early-2027 sale. That creates a preservation and counsel-decision issue, not proof of wrongdoing by Forest of Cardiff.

1. **1955 execution pack:** collect/inspect the claim, pleadings, Order 14/order, plan or premises description, writ/warrant, officer's return/certificate, instructions to the enforcement officer, and any contemporaneous record of the land entered or left with the family. Compare with the 1962 farmhouse/garden proceedings.
2. **VG41 file:** obtain the complete 1967–70 statutory register and map, application/claim, claimant identity, supporting documents, notices, objections, provisional/final decision and any later corrections. Ask the Council to confirm the lawful inspection/copying route and the complete file's custodian.
3. **Polygon-to-title chain:** map the alleged 1955 woodland and VG41 ground, then compare the same geometry against the 1975 conveyance plan, WA231076/WA240304 source plans and applications, the 1993/94 Ideal Homes/Forest of Cardiff instruments, the 2 December 2005 excision, and current WA735527/CYM title plans.
4. **Counsel's limitation and remedies opinion:** separately decide historical causes of action, limitation/accrual, effect of subsequent possession and registration, present Schedule 4 eligibility, Schedule 8 indemnity, standing, proper defendants, and whether any protective step is justified.
5. **Sale preservation:** seek a prompt written decision from specialist counsel on whether the alleged interest is presently protectable and whether a narrowly framed standstill/preservation request to Forest of Cardiff is justified before any proposed 2027 sale.

### Parallel root-of-title test

6. Read the HMLR-file copies of the 1905 conveyance, 15 May 1924 agreement and 29 March 1961 deed. Record the exact parties, operative words, property schedule, plan, recitals and chain of title. Compare with the family-held 1938 conveyance. Do not reopen the closed conclusion that the 1939 plan is Parcel-B-only.

### Separate green-register route

7. Ask Welsh Government / Planning and Environment Decisions Wales to confirm in writing the precise correction provision and deadline applicable to VG41's historic registration and the evidence threshold for that route. The existence of a correction procedure does not establish that VG41 was wrongly registered; title ownership and the statutory grounds for correcting a commons/green register are separate questions.

## Legal guardrails

- The Limitation Act 1939 treated a right of action to recover land as accruing on dispossession/discontinuance in specified circumstances; the historical text and facts must be assessed by counsel. A possible wrong in 1955 does **not** automatically mean that title is recoverable in 2026.
- The current Land Registration Act 2002 Schedule 4 register-mistake route and Schedule 8 indemnity route are separate from a historical claim for the taking. Schedule 8 paragraph 8 has its own knowledge-based time rule, which counsel must apply to the precise facts.
- A unilateral notice is not a substitute for proving an interest; it must not be lodged speculatively. Identify the exact land and get advice first.
- Missing records are not proof of concealment. Weight each gap against the custodian's retention duty, search scope and transfer/destruction evidence.
- Public statements should say “alleged”, “family evidence”, “issue for counsel” or “documented” accurately. Do not state fraud, deliberate misdescription or government involvement as fact.
