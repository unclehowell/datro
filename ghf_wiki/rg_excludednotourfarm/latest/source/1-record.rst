Scope note and cross-reference
------------------------------

These items remain preserved as examples of similar names and properties that were presented or encountered but do not identify Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth. Exclusion means “not this property,” not that the source is false and not evidence of wrongdoing by itself.

The wider screening register records the disposition, limits and follow-up for the 1877 Cardiff leases, this Cowbridge-area name trap, the separate 107-acre Cydfin / Tŷ Mawr and 166-acre Llandough Farm catalogue entries, and other leads: `Kissed Frogs: source-screening and scope register <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/anomalies-register/bpvsbuckler-archive-research-leads/#kissed-frogs-register>`__.
