William Buckler’s 1983 Diary — “Daniel Thomas Receipts”
-------------------------------------------------------

**Source:** William Buckler, 1983 diary, note written on the inside cover.

   | “Daniel Thomas Receipts.
   | 31st March 1910
   | 31st Dec 1906
   | 30 June 1903”

Description
~~~~~~~~~~~

The note is a later documentary reference by William Buckler to three receipts associated with Daniel Thomas, dated 31 March 1910, 31 December 1906 and 30 June 1903. The underlying receipts are not presently represented by this image; the image records their citation in the 1983 diary.

Evidential significance and interpretation
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The three entries are a useful documentary clue to repeated financial dealings between the Williams/Buckler family and Daniel Thomas during 1903–1910. In the context of the wider evidence concerning Daniel Thomas & Son and the historical Williams Parcel A, the note may point to payments or other financial transactions connected with that relationship.

The sequence is potentially significant because it records dealings on three separate dates rather than a single isolated transaction. If the underlying receipts can be located, they may identify the payer, amount, purpose and property or transaction to which each payment related. That could establish whether the Daniel Thomas dealings were specifically connected with Parcel A.

**Evidence status:** The diary note is documentary evidence that William Buckler recorded references to three Daniel Thomas receipts. It is not, by itself, proof that all three payments concerned Parcel A, nor does it establish title, consideration or the legal effect of any underlying transaction. The original receipts should therefore be treated as a priority line of enquiry.
