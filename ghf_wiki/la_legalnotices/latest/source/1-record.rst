Legal Notices, Copyright and Disclaimer
---------------------------------------

This page sets out the legal framework for the Great House Farm Wiki (greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com) and associated public materials.

Purpose
~~~~~~~

The site is a public documentary archive and research platform maintained for the transparent gathering, cataloguing and presentation of surviving evidence relating to Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough-juxta-Penarth. It supports fact-finding, heritage preservation and public interest in the historical record. It is not legal advice.
