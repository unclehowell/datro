**What did H. J. Thomas record in 1974?**

**Why is the 1974 wording important?**

The current research identifies a discrepancy between descriptions suggesting access issues / an anomaly / a partial survey and the surviving record of a measured ground-floor survey. The question is what the original 1974 survey actually covered and what later summaries meant by those phrases.

**What happened in the 1988 listing assessment?**

Cadw’s 1988 listing records, as described in the site’s records chronology, concluded that the farm and barn were rejected for listing. Two photographs dated 29 July 1988 survive and were released in 2026. Cadw said the remainder of the paper file was most likely destroyed and that no destruction record exists.
