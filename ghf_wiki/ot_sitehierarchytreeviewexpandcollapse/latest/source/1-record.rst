Great House Farm and the disputed title
---------------------------------------

`Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr) <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/great-house-farm/>`__ stood beside `St Dochdwy’s Church <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/archaeology/>`__ at Llandough-juxta-Penarth. The `Williams / Buckler family <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/williams-buckler-family/>`__ traces its connection to the home to at least 1667; `Mary (Nancy) Williams’s statement <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/mary-williams-statement/>`__ (c.1974) records this (`Mary (Nancy) Williams testimony <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/testimony/mary-williams/>`__).

The central dispute is whether `Parcel A <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/the-two-parcels/#parcel-a>`__—the farmhouse, buildings, garden and yard—had a distinct title history from `Parcel B <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/the-two-parcels/#parcel-b>`__, the eastern fields. The family’s claim to Parcel A is that it did not pass into the `Western Ground Rents <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/western-ground-rents/>`__ and `BP <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/bp-properties/>`__ title chain. The question must be tested against the original agreements, conveyances, plans and `Land Registry files <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/land-registry-titles/>`__; the family’s wider argument is set out in the `overall working theory <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/top-level-understanding-williams-parcel-a-title-sabotaged-severed-and-buried-while-a-synthetic-mimic-estate-was-forged/>`__.

Occupation, possession proceedings and BP v Buckler
---------------------------------------------------

The 1955 `High Court possession papers <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e125-1955-high-court-order-14-possession-order-and-papers/>`__ and `writ and execution record <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e126-4-july-1955-writ-of-possession-execution-and-return/>`__, the `1959 and 1965 WGR offers <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e128-1959-and-1965-wgr-tenancy-offers-to-the-house-and-garden/>`__, the `1962 possession order <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e130-11-december-1962-cardiff-county-court-possession-order/>`__, and `Mary (Nancy) Williams’s 1974 plea and pleadings <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e135-3-july-1974-mary-williams-plea-of-ownership-and-pleadings/>`__ concern possession and the claimed status of her occupation [PENDING: `EV-0032 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0032-1955-order-14-possession-order/>`__; PENDING: `EV-0034 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0034-writ-possession-execution-return-1955/>`__; PENDING: `EV-0041 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0041-cardiff-county-court-possession-order-1962/>`__]. The `1974 licence letters <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e136-31-october-1974-licence-letters-bp-pension-trust-and-bp-properties/>`__ are material to the later dispute [PENDING: `EV-0049 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0049-mary-williams-plea-ownership-pleadings/>`__; PENDING: `EV-0050 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0050-1974-bp-licence-letters/>`__].

`BP Properties Ltd v Buckler <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/bp-properties-v-buckler/>`__ and the `1987 Court of Appeal judgment <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e141-31-july-1987-court-of-appeal-judgment-bp-properties-v-buckler/>`__ are the central judicial records [`EV-0080 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0080-bp-properties-v-buckler-judgment/>`__]. The judgment decided possession and licence; whether it finally determined the root-title claim on its merits is an open issue; the judgment’s scope and the issues left undecided are addressed in `BP v Buckler: Scope, Decided and Undecided <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/scrutiny-and-accountability/court-judicial-issues/bp-v-buckler-scope-decided-undecided/>`__.

Evidence of the family’s occupation and acts of ownership, including the tree-planting account, is recorded in `GHF-E151 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e151-family-tree-planting-and-other-acts-of-ownership-on-parcel-a/>`__ and `Mary (Nancy) Williams’s statement <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/mary-williams-statement/>`__.

1988 removal and demolition
---------------------------

The `ITV Cymru Wales report <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e113-itv-wales-report-great-house-farm-1988/>`__, the `3 December 1988 South Wales Echo article <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e090-n08-history-fight-to-save-farm-1988/>`__ and `the demolition chronology <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/demolition-1988/>`__ record the public dispute and the farmhouse’s removal. The newspaper article’s image and transcription are available in `GHF-E090-N08 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e090-n08-history-fight-to-save-farm-1988/>`__. The suggestion that persons in the footage may have been SAS or undercover personnel has no named source yet (SOURCE PENDING); Branwen’s September 2026 recollection is that the men who detained her appeared to be soldiers (GHF-E114; `Branwen testimony <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/testimony/branwen-buckler/>`__); this remains unconfirmed and is addressed in `the identity allegation <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/allegation-sas-undercover-personnel-bp-bailiffs-1988-unverified/>`__ and `GHF-E181 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e181-identity-role-persons-1988-eviction-footage-pending/>`__.
