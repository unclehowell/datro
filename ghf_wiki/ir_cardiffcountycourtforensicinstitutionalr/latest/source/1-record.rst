Forensic Assessment — Cardiff County Court
------------------------------------------

**Scope:** the historic Cardiff County Court record as an institutional custodian/administrator. Judicial merits are not recast as misconduct.

Critical role
~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The 1962 proceedings are a key control point because the action concerned the farmhouse and garden specifically and resulted in an order dated 11 December 1962. The court record may establish the exact land claimed, parties, pleaded basis, plan, mesne-profit claim and enforcement authority.

Adjudicative role and earlier title / limitation case
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The Court of Appeal later treated the 11 December 1962 possession order as an enforceable judgment which prevented the adverse-possession defence, calculated from 1953/1955, from succeeding before the 1974 enforcement steps. The separate family contention is that the relevant title or twelve-year limitation period for Parcel A may have matured much earlier—potentially by 1940 if adverse possession began in 1928. That alternative was not expressly analysed in the published 1987 reasons as a standalone date calculation.

The court’s role must therefore be examined on its own record. Mary and Frederick were defendants in the 1962 proceedings for the farmhouse and garden. Obtain the claim, defence, particulars, plan, order and any record of Mary’s reported “previous statement”; establish whether she raised her independent ownership case, what the judge decided about title and parcel extent, and what binding or preclusive effect the order could have on an earlier-accrued claim. Do not assume either that the order cured a defective root title or that it was legally irrelevant.

The 1954 tenant-making attempt is a separate family-chronology lead at `GHF-E182 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e182-1954-frederick-buckler-tenancy-attempt/>`__. The accessible published appeal report does not mention the year 1954; the source for that event remains to be located.

Forensic control test
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The present electronic-system search is not the same thing as recovery of the historic paper file. The critical evidence is therefore the retention, transfer, destruction or archive history of the 1962 case materials. The court’s adjudicative role must be scrutinised as well as its record-custody role: the 1962 claim and order later became a decisive part of the Court of Appeal’s limitation reasoning because the order was still enforceable in 1974.

Obtain the claim, particulars, defence, plan, order, mesne-profit schedule, any previous statement by Mary, hearing note/transcript, warrant and enforcement history. Determine exactly what land was claimed, whether Mary’s ownership assertion was pleaded or otherwise before the court, whether the order’s scope followed the same parcel boundary as the 1939 tenancy or the later “whole farm” terminology, and what the judge actually decided. The family’s 1954 tenancy-attempt lead (GHF-E182) is separate from the recognized 1949–1953 tenancy and should be considered only if a record shows it was relevant to the 1962 proceedings or later argument.

Current finding
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish judicial wrongdoing in the proceedings reviewed. This does not conclude that every procedural or evidential issue is resolved.** The issue is evidential reconstruction: what the 1962 court actually received and what land its order legally covered.

Human action
~~~~~~~~~~~~

Obtain the full 1962 file, including claim, particulars, order, plan, warrant/execution record, correspondence and any transcript/judge’s note. Compare it with the 1939 tenancy plan and 1949 surrender/re-grant.

**Acceptance:** the 1962 proposition-to-evidence matrix is complete or a defensible retention-search escalation is recorded.
