**Collapsed thread description — correspondence scope, chronology and source limitations**

This catalogue covers the available public correspondence concerning Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, Llandough Community Council, and the role of Paul Egan as Clerk in the June–October 2026 record. It links to the separate evidence page GHF-E183, which provides message-by-message citation anchors, full public transcriptions where available, two redacted copies of the family’s 13 October messages, and source-status notes where the original email body or headers were not recovered.

**Provenance rule:** a published transcription or later description is not represented as an original .eml file. The 20 September wording is quoted from the 28 September correspondence. The 21 September request and 28 September response are publicly transcribed, but the original messages with full headers were not separately recovered in the reviewed source. The 11 October refusal is also a transcription, not an attached original email file. The 13 October 01:07 UTC family email is superseded by the 03:25 UTC revision. The original text files do not carry their original mail headers; the timestamp association for these two versions comes from the published correspondence chronology.

**Privacy:** personal postal and personal email details are withheld from the public copies. The meeting’s remote-access passcode is not reproduced. A source-status note describes a message known from the chronology but is not the message verbatim.
