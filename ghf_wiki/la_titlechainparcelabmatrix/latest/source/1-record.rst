**Identity control:** Parcel A is the full 1877 house/quarrying estate, not a synonym for the farmhouse and garden alone. It includes the farmhouse/buildings; barns, stable and yard west of the house; garden; forest north of the house; and ground south-east of the house/garden later registered as VG41. Parcel B is the separate eastern agricultural fields. The title case, resting on Mary (Nancy) Williams’s c.1974 witness statement (`Mary (Nancy) Williams testimony <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/testimony/mary-williams/>`__), is that the 1877 arrangement took effect in 1928 and WGR’s later possession of Parcel B did not confer rights over Parcel-A forest or green. Later “whole farm” language blurred the two parcels; it did not make them one title by itself.
