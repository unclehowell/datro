D696 — Western Ground Rents records, c.1938–1944
------------------------------------------------

**Status: BACKGROUND / PARKED.** D696 is not a current priority work package.

The family already has the May 1939 WGR–John Williams agreement and plan, and that plan has been conclusively analysed as **Parcel B only**. The family also states that rent on Parcel A had ceased in 1928. Consequently the D696 rental books do not need to be inspected merely to re-prove the tenancy boundary.

**Retrospective archive lead (R52, 11 June 2026):** the supplied summary of the email reports a three-volume D696 collection, c. 1938–1944, and an archivist’s suggestion to cross-search “Western Ground Rents” with “Thomas” for possible Daniel Thomas agreements. This is a specific search suggestion, but it does not identify a particular item or establish a Parcel A connection. See `GHF-E106-R52 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/records-access-chronology/foi-reply-register/ghf-e106-r52-glamorgan-archives-email-11-june-2026/>`__. The status remains **BACKGROUND / PARKED** unless a precise record lead meets the reopening test below.

When D696 becomes worth pursuing
--------------------------------

Only reopen this item if a concrete catalogue or archive lead shows that a D696 record directly concerns **Parcel A, the woodland, the land now comprising VG41, or the 1955 taking**.

A D696 record that merely repeats the Parcel-B tenancy/rent position adds little. A record showing a separate Parcel-A treatment, a 1955 instruction, a boundary change or a WGR ownership statement could be material.

Project control
---------------

D696 is therefore a **conditional corroboration source**, not a stage-gate deliverable. Family time and archive costs should be directed first to the 1955 execution record, VG41 registration file, current VG41 title, and the woodland title chain.
