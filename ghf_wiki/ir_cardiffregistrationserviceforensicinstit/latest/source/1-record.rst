Forensic Assessment — Cardiff Registration Service
--------------------------------------------------

**Scope:** civil-registration function only.

Role
~~~~

The Service is relevant to identity and death-record chronology, including the date and identity of Mary Doreen (Nancy) Williams, formerly Buckler. It did not decide title, registration or possession.

Forensic control test
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Confirm the exact civil-registration entry, registration district, date and any lawful correction/transfer history. Where historic records have moved between custodians, record the chain.

Current finding
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**No adverse finding is established.** A death registration is corroborative identity evidence; it does not itself determine succession to land.

Human action
~~~~~~~~~~~~

Complete the outstanding transferred-record request and preserve a certified/official copy where obtainable. Cross-check the date against the 1983 HMLR correspondence only after the primary record is fixed.

**Acceptance:** identity/death evidence is certified and entered into the standing/succession chronology.
