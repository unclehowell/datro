Forensic Assessment — National Library of Wales
-----------------------------------------------

**Scope:** NLW as archive/custodian, especially the Bute Estate material relevant to the historic title and tenancy chain.

Why NLW matters
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The Bute Estate collection is one of the principal remaining routes to the pre-WGR history, including the 1877/early transaction material, Llandough manorial leases and agreements and related estate papers. The project has identified D219 as a priority series.

Forensic control test
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Search the catalogue and related uncatalogued material for Great House/Tŷ Mawr/Cedfin, Daniel Thomas, Williams, Llandough and the relevant Bute estate solicitors. Distinguish original deeds, abstracts, rentals, drafts and later copies.

Current finding
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish wrongdoing by the National Library of Wales. This is limited to the archive/search trail identified here and does not rule out further relevant records.** The institution is an evidence source and custodian. Missing material must be accompanied by a documented search/retention explanation before any inference is drawn. :sup:`🕒`

Human action
~~~~~~~~~~~~

Arrange a targeted research visit/request for D219 and any Corbett family papers relevant to the 1877 transaction and later land administration. Capture exact classmarks and plan references.

**Acceptance:** targeted search completed and all relevant classmarks, images or negative-search explanations recorded.

16 October 2026 — catalogue record 507738: “Eviction News Material”, 30 November 1988
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Source link:** `Watch and Listen item 507738 <https://gwylio-gwrando.llyfrgell.cymru/detail/507738>`__. The catalogue data supplied for this review describes a one-minute HTV Wales videocassette on ITV Betacam stock, with recording and broadcast dates both stated as 30 November 1988. The abstract describes Miles Fletcher pieces to camera, bailiffs removing furniture, removal vans and Branwen leaving the farm, and says the High Court granted an injunction stopping BP demolishing the farm.

**Limits:** the cataloguer’s note says “Not viewed”; the tape contains several unrelated clips and this clip’s position is unknown, so the description may come from the broadcaster’s log. The record as supplied has neither a catalogue-created date nor an online-publication date. Do not infer when the catalogue page went online from the event, recording or broadcast date.

**Distinction and publication chronology:** this 30 November eviction/injunction record is not the separate 5 December 1988 “Wales at Six” demolition headline/report. The project chronology records the YouTube viewing copy linked at `GHF-E113 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e113-itv-wales-report-great-house-farm-1988/>`__ as published on 25 July 2025. That is not a date supplied by item 507738; the catalogue record has no catalogue-created or online-publication date in the information supplied. The relation between the cassette and YouTube copy and the uploader/source history remain unconfirmed. Search separately for 5 December HTV Wales items and log exact catalogue identifiers and search scope. Ask the Enquiries team/Beryl Evans when item 507738 was created and first published online, and whether a distinct 5 December demolition package is catalogued. A negative result should be recorded as a dated search result, not proof that no material exists.

**Access and rights:** the supplied record states that original materials are not circulated, digital viewing is restricted to Reader’s Ticket holders on Library premises, and reuse requires written permission from ITV Wales. Those ordinary stated conditions do not establish a case-specific NDA or concealment. Physical/digital inspection of the tape and any actual collection of media remain pending.
