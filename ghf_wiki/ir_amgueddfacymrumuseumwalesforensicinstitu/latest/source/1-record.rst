Forensic Assessment — Amgueddfa Cymru
-------------------------------------

**Scope:** This page assesses only Amgueddfa Cymru — Museum Wales. It is not a finding against the Museum and does not merge its role with Cadw, GGAT, the Council, police, courts or private parties. :sup:`🕒10-10-2026`

Role actually supported by the record
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The Museum is relevant principally to the **artefact / provenance / present-remedy** workstream. The project’s 2026 FOI search (2026-020) is recorded as a negative search. The Museum was not the body that made the 1988 listing, possession or demolition decisions.

Forensic control test
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The relevant questions are narrower than title: what Great House Farm objects or records are held; how each item entered the Museum; whether accession/provenance records survive; whether an object can be linked to the farmhouse; and what current trustee/Charter powers are available if family provenance is established.

Current finding
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish institution-specific wrongdoing. This does not mean the institution had no involvement; the finding is limited to the particular acts and records assessed here.** A negative FOI response establishes only the scope/result of that search, not that material never existed elsewhere or was never transferred.

Human action
~~~~~~~~~~~~

Run object-by-object provenance requests only where a specific artefact is identified. Preserve accession numbers, acquisition documents, donor correspondence and catalogue history. Do not ask the Museum to adjudicate private land title.

**Acceptance:** each object has a provenance status, custodian, evidence source and proposed remedy.
