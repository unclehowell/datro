**ReOrg: the case on one line of sight.** Every key milestone of the Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr) story from 1877 to the live 2026 deadlines, including **every creation of a record, whether or not the original has been obtained**. Each milestone links to its dedicated evidence catalogue page and carries an amber box showing **what was known by that date but is not recorded in the account of the milestone**. This page does not recite the evidence; the catalogue pages do.

**How to read the amber boxes.** “Known by then” means known to the family, on a document, or on the public record at that date, with the source and status shown in the register below. “Not recorded” means it does not appear in the surviving account of that milestone that the Wiki holds (a recital, a judgment, a press report, an agency reply); for the 1955 and 1962 orders the primary papers themselves are still to be obtained (`E125 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e125-1955-high-court-order-14-possession-order-and-papers/>`__, `E130 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e130-11-december-1962-cardiff-county-court-possession-order/>`__), so the label will be checked against them. An omission is an observed fact; **whether it was careless, innocent, or concealed is not established by this page**, and each point is a ground for requesting the document, not a finding about any person. This is a family reconstruction for counsel, not legal advice. Truth statuses follow the Wiki’s `operating rules <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/ai-agent-index/site-agent-operating-rules-evidence-links-citations-change-control/>`__: DOCUMENTED FACT · FAMILY EVIDENCE · RECONSTRUCTION · LEGAL INFERENCE · UNRESOLVED.

What cuts against, and what the omissions would change
------------------------------------------------------

Stated in the same place as the accusation, under the Wiki’s rule that interpretation stays open to challenge.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Milestone
     - If the omitted facts had been weighed
     - Cuts against
   * - 1939 and 1940
     - Adverse possession against WGR would have matured about 1940 (HIGH)
     - The 1916 tenancy is recited as “the whole farm” and the family accepts it covered both areas; if the family held the 1939 plan in 1984–87 and did not use it, the fraud route weakens
   * - 1955
     - Mary joined as a party; the forest and green stay in Parcel A; no 1955 starting point for the later chain (HIGH)
     - The order, warrant and return are unseen, so its own wording and plan may cover the forest and green; F5 and F10 are not yet sourced; the 1955 taking is also the start of the historic limitation clock
   * - 1962
     - A boundary statement showing what WGR still sought (MODERATE)
     - The narrower claim may simply reflect that the house was the part still occupied
   * - 1974 to 1983
     - Ownership tried while Mary was alive; first registration with a qualified class of title or delayed (HIGH if the file shows a misstatement)
     - A solicitor may honestly have treated the 1962 order and 1974 licence as disposing of doubt; the Court of Appeal upheld the licence route; deeds were normally returned after registration
   * - 1984 to 1987
     - Title tried as well as licence (HIGH)
     - Absence of a deed from a register file is not proof of concealment; an amalgamation is not inherently improper :sup:`🕒`
   * - 1988
     - A listing or preservation notice would have required consent before demolition (HERITAGE)
     - Cadw coding was marginally below the bar on one of its explanations; no statutory duty then existed to consult occupiers; Cadw engaged in time in 2026
   * - 1989
     - A property-rights analysis might have differed (UNCERTAIN)
     - A second application is barred by Article 35(2)(b) and the Human Rights Act does not reach pre-2000 acts
   * - 1990 to 1994
     - Stronger archaeological safeguards and a more intact assemblage (HERITAGE)
     - GGAT did not demolish or decide listing, and the Council relied on counsel’s opinion
   * - 1994 to 2005
     - If the transfer was a gift, purchaser protections may not apply (UNCERTAIN)
     - The charity says it bought after due diligence; a bona fide purchase would change the route to indemnity
   * - 2026
     - A register-mistake and indemnity analysis tested against the 1983 file (MODERATE)
     - The judicial-review window from 25 May 2026 has passed; indemnity is time-limited
