Forensic Assessment — Ministry of Defence
-----------------------------------------

**Scope:** MOD records only where a concrete Great House Farm/Llandough connection is demonstrated.

Current evidence position
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The reviewed material does not establish that MOD determined the private title, possession or demolition of Great House Farm. The Department should not be inserted into the accountability chain without a specific record or statutory function. :sup:`🕒`

Forensic control test
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Any asserted MOD role must identify the record, date, responsible function, information received and action taken. Historic government involvement elsewhere is not a substitute for such evidence.

Current finding
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish Ministry of Defence wrongdoing or an operative role in the historic events. This is a source-limited assessment, not proof that no relevant record exists. 🕒10-10-2026**
