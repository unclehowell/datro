Editorial warning — 1938 “Cowbridge and Llandough Great House Farm” name trap
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**[PRESS REPORT; source references reported in the research file]** Do not identify the 1938 auction of the 185-acre “Cowbridge and Llandough Great House Farm”, reported in the Western Mail on 12 and 16 November 1938, as the Llandough-juxta-Penarth Tŷ Mawr property. The supplied research identifies it as a different farm, sold to L. D. Thomas. Until the sale particulars and plan are inspected, this is a strong identity warning, not a substitute for the primary plan.

   | should have prompted evaluation under the Ancient Monuments and
   | Archaeological Areas Act 1979.

   | a potential gap in statutory process that may affect the validity of the
   | permissions granted.

   | BP registered title in 1982 based on the disputed licence. The Council
   | should establish:
   | – Whether any dispute notice or heritage caution was lodged;
   | – Whether the Council disclosed relevant heritage information to the Land
   | Registry;
   | – What planning records exist from 1978–1988 regarding the site's known
   | archaeological potential.

   \**4. Corporate Identity Inconsistency*\*

   | During my father's appeal, the court treated BP Properties Ltd and BP
   | Pension Trust Ltd inconsistently—as separate entities when convenient, then
   | as one when dismissing his claim. This raises questions about how
   | thoroughly the litigation was scrutinised and whether the Council's records
   | reflect this uncertainty.

   WHY THIS MATTERS TO THE COUNCIL

   These issues go beyond a single historical dispute. They touch on:

   | – \**Planning record integrity*\*: Whether heritage information was
   | adequately documented and acted upon;
   | – \**Procedural compliance*\*: Whether statutory protections (Ancient
   | Monuments Act) were properly applied;
   | – \**Institutional liability*\*: Whether decisions were made on incomplete or
   | misleading information;
   | – \**Precedent risk*\*: Whether similar vulnerabilities may exist in other
   | historical planning decisions.

   | 4. \**Clarify the Council's current protocol*\* for handling historical title

   | material non-disclosure in past planning processes—to prevent recurrence.
