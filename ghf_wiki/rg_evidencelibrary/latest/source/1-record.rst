**Email-as-evidence rule — 17 October 2026:** A material email or conversation is an evidence object, not just a chronology entry. Its dedicated record must have a unique GHF-E catalogue ID; name the source mailbox or repository and exact thread/reference; state whether the original messages and attachments have actually been captured; give a neutral description of the exchange; and put interpretation, evidential significance and limitations in a separate section below. Until the original is captured and checked, label the page a placeholder and the description provisional; do not present a summary as a verbatim transcript or treat an unanswered email as proof of non-existence or misconduct. GHF-E183–GHF-E188 are the first six Heneb / HER thread records migrated to this pattern; other email catalogue pages elsewhere on the Wiki still require review and migration.

Vetting rule
~~~~~~~~~~~~

**GitHub copies are not automatically primary… … read more**

GitHub copies are not automatically primary evidence. Each migrated item should retain its original date, source/holder, catalogue or reference number where known, and an evidence-status label. Where the archive contains an assertion rather than a source record, the assertion remains a research lead and is not promoted to fact.
