What happened?
--------------

**[PRIMARY DOCUMENT HELD]** Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough, was the subject of possession litigation after the Williams/Buckler family’s long occupation. The reported history includes a 1916 yearly tenancy of the farm, WGR’s acquisition of the reversion in 1938, possession proceedings affecting most of the farm in 1955, and separate proceedings concerning the farmhouse and garden in 1962.

**[PRIMARY DOCUMENT HELD: plan and page 2]** 1939 WGR–John Williams agreement (DBDT123/7): plan shows Parcel B only, highlighted (Media item 88). Typed terms visible: monthly let, eight shillings a month, “as meadow or pasture land”; tenant John Williams; landlord Western Ground Rents Ltd (Glamorgan Archives catalogue) [`EV-0023 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0023-1939-wgr-john-williams-tenancy-plan/>`__]. Page 1 (parcels, acreage): not held — PENDING. `DBDT123/7 page <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/dbdt123-7-1939-wgr-john-williams-tenancy-agreement-plan/>`__.

**[PRIMARY DOCUMENT HELD; registration file incomplete]** The HMLR application dates are 30 November 1982 (WA231076) and 23 February 1983 (WA240304). Mary (Nancy) Williams, formerly Buckler, died on 26 March 1983. The 1988 possession and demolition sequence is recorded in contemporary press and official heritage/planning records; each type of record must be assessed separately.

**Key dates**

- **To 1938.** The Williams family farmed Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough, as Bute estate tenants (tenancy 1916) [PENDING: `EV-0014 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0014-bute-tenancy-john-williams-1916/>`__]. In 1938 the estate sold to Western Ground Rents Ltd (WGR) [PENDING: `EV-0019 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0019-bute-wgr-conveyance-plan-1938/>`__]. [PRIMARY DOCUMENT HELD]
- **1955–62.** WGR obtained possession orders against Frederick Buckler, Mary (Nancy) Williams’s husband; neither was executed [PENDING: `EV-0032 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0032-1955-order-14-possession-order/>`__; PENDING: `EV-0041 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0041-cardiff-county-court-possession-order-1962/>`__]. [PRIMARY DOCUMENT HELD] Mary (Nancy) Williams’s c.1974 statement says the farmhouse, buildings and about 10 acres (“Parcel A”) were sold by the Bute Estate to Daniel Thomas, with the freehold to pass to her grandfather when quarrying ceased, separately from the Bute tenancy (“Parcel B”). [TESTIMONY: `Mary (Nancy) Williams <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/testimony/mary-williams/>`__]
- **1958.** As “Mrs. M D. Buckler”, Mary told the county planning committee that three councils and WGR had “walked in and taken” the family’s land. [PRESS REPORT: Western Mail, 20 Nov 1958, p.7]
- **1969–75.** WGR conveyed Parcel B, plus an interest in the 1962 order on Parcel A, to BP Pension Trust (31 Dec 1969); our case: the land as a whole did not pass. On 31 Oct 1974 BP’s agents wrote to “Mrs Buckler (Mrs Williams)” granting a licence; on 23 May 1975 the farmhouse passed to BP Properties Ltd [PENDING: `EV-0050 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0050-1974-bp-licence-letters/>`__]. [PRIMARY DOCUMENT HELD]
- **1982–83.** BP applied to register the land (WA231076, 30 Nov 1982) and the farmhouse and garden (WA240304, 23 Feb 1983) [PENDING: `EV-0066 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0066-hmlr-wa240304-item-10-register-1983/>`__]. Mary died on 26 Mar 1983. [PRIMARY DOCUMENT HELD: HMLR schedule]
- **1984–88.** Writ 22 May 1984. Hollis J heard the case on 10 Jul 1986 and gave judgment for BP on 24 Jul 1986 [PENDING: `EV-0074 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0074-high-court-trial-judgment-1986/>`__]. The Court of Appeal dismissed the appeal on 31 Jul 1987; the House of Lords refused leave on 3 Mar 1988 [PENDING: `EV-0076 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0076-house-of-lords-petition-leave-papers/>`__]. BP took possession on 29 Nov 1988 and demolished the farmhouse from 6 Dec 1988. [PRIMARY DOCUMENT HELD; PRESS REPORT: South Wales Echo, 25 Jul 1986 p.3 and Nov–Dec 1988]

What did the 1987 Court of Appeal decide?
-----------------------------------------

**[PRIMARY DOCUMENT HELD — BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2]** On 31 July 1987 the Court of Appeal dismissed the appeal about possession of the farmhouse and garden [`EV-0080 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0080-bp-properties-v-buckler-judgment/>`__]. The judgment considered limitation and the legal effect of the 1974 licence letters. The High Court hearing took place on 10 July 1986, but judgment was given on 24 July 1986. Do not treat the appeal as deciding every later question about root title or the incomplete 1982–83 registration files.

Open questions and honest status
--------------------------------

**[HYPOTHESIS]** Open question: whether the title deeds and plans supporting the registrations covered the land now called Parcel A, and whether the registration files properly record notice and the competing family interest. No lead yet proves dishonesty. The 1983 WA240304 certificate is the strongest current lead because its supporting file can be tested; the certificate alone is not proof of wrongdoing. Whether notice was sent, to whom and when remains open.
