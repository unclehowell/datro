On 11 December 1962 Judge Temple Morris QC, in Cardiff County Court, made an order for possession of the farmhouse and garden of `Great House Farm <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/great-house-farm/>`__, with mesne profits since 1955, against `Frederick <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/frederick-buckler/>`__ and `Mary (Nancy) Buckler née Williams <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/mary-williams/>`__, on the claim of `Western Ground Rents <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/western-ground-rents/>`__ (WGR) [PENDING: `EV-0041 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0041-cardiff-county-court-possession-order-1962/>`__]. The possession order was never enforced before 1974.

**Parcel-scope correction — 9 October 2026.… … read more**

**Parcel-scope correction — 9 October 2026.** This 1962 proceeding concerned the farmhouse and garden Mary and the family were still physically occupying. That occupied centre was part of the full Parcel A, but it was not the whole of Parcel A. The family’s established case is that Parcel A also included the northern forest and the ground south-east of the house/garden later registered as VG41, both seized on 4 July 1955 while WGR enforced possession of Parcel B. The 1962 order must not be used to recast the family’s full Parcel-A title claim as limited to the house and garden.

**Questions the family raises about the order**

**Research note.**

- It depended on the farm still being one unit as in `1916 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1916-tenancy/#grant>`__; the 1924/1928 `Parcel A freehold <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1877-agreement/#takes-effect>`__ split, the `1938 conveyance <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1939-revised-tenancy/#y1938>`__ and the `1939 tenancy <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1939-revised-tenancy/#y1939>`__ were not, so far as the record shows, put before the court.
- WGR had to prove its own paper title to Parcel A against Mary as an owner-claimant, not rely on Frederick’s ended fields tenancy.
- County court jurisdiction in December 1962 was limited to property of £100 net annual rateable value; the limit rose to £400 only in 1963. If the farmhouse exceeded £100, the order may have been made without jurisdiction. The 1956 and 1963 valuation lists would test this.
- Service, the land description and the long non-enforcement are also under examination.
- On the family’s case the limitation clock for Parcel A started in 1928, when rent to the Thomases stopped, not in 1955 as the `1987 Court of Appeal <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/bp-properties-v-buckler/#court-of-appeal>`__ counted it [PENDING: `EV-0016 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0016-1928-last-rent-receipt-quarrying/>`__].

The Glamorgan Archives plaint file for the 1962 action is the key document still to be retrieved. The relationship between the 1962 order and the later 1987 litigation is recorded as a separate research question. See `Constructive Criticism & Counterarguments <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/constructive-criticism-counterarguments/>`__ for counterarguments concerning finality and procedural effect.

**What happened next:** on 19 September 1974 leave was given to enforce the order, and on 31 October 1974 the warrant was withdrawn, the day BP sent its licence letters (`1974 warrant <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1974-licence-letters/#warrant>`__; leave-to-enforce papers also catalogued here as GHF-E023-07). The `Court of Appeal in 1987 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/bp-properties-v-buckler/#court-of-appeal>`__ held that because the 1962 action began within 12 years of 1955, the landlord’s title had not been extinguished (judgment catalogued here as GHF-E023-08).
