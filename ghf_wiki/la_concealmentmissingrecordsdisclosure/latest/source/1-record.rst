This page links suspected concealment questions to the actual records-access trail. Missing material is evidence of a gap; it is not, by itself, proof of deliberate concealment. :sup:`🕒`

Record categories to track
--------------------------

- HM Land Registry historic title and application files;
- conveyances, plans and abstracts referred to in the title chronology;
- court and judicial administration records for BP Properties Ltd v Buckler;
- planning and heritage files;
- archival searches, FOI/EIR requests, internal reviews and responses.

What the Wiki should record
---------------------------

.. list-table::

   * - **Requested**
     - Exact record sought and date requested.
   * - **Found**
     - Record actually supplied, with catalogue reference.
   * - **Missing** :sup:`🕒`
     - Record identified by another source but not produced.
   * - **Destroyed / disposed**
     - Who says so, when, and on what documented basis.
   * - **Challenge**
     - Any defective-search, review or disclosure correspondence.

Use the `Missing Records Register <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/missing-records-register/>`__ as the live inventory and the `Records Access Chronology <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/records-access-chronology/>`__ as the dated disclosure trail. :sup:`🕒`
