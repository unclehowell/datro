Purpose
-------

This is the controlling legal-case layer for the Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr project. It connects the published evidence archive to the private execution record and prevents research activity from being mistaken for a legal claim.

**Control rule:** no issue is treated as established merely because a record is missing, a public reply conflicts with another source, or a named family member’s testimony alleges wrongdoing. Each proposition must be tested against primary evidence, counter-evidence, standing, limitation, causation and remedy. :sup:`🕒`

Separate legal workstreams
--------------------------

- **A — Title:** ownership and extent of the relevant land.
- **B — Registration:** first registration, amalgamation, later dealings, alteration/rectification and indemnity.
- **C — 1987 judgment:** pleadings, evidence, findings, appeal and later-discovered material.
- **D — Fraud / deceit:** parked as an allegation until evidence identifies an act, actor, knowledge/dishonesty where legally required, materiality and causation.
- **E — Public authority:** statutory function, decision, record trail, fairness, disclosure and correction.
- **F — Heritage / planning:** planning, archaeology, listing and knowledge chronology, kept distinct from title.
- **G — Loss / remedy:** possession, land, money, documents, artefacts, correction, acknowledgement and other legally available remedies.
- **H — Family / narrative justice:** the historical and public record, without allowing narrative language to substitute for proof.

Decision gates
--------------

- **Evidence gate:** source identified, acquired or a defensible negative-search record exists.
- **Identity gate:** the document, person, place, parcel and event are correctly identified.
- **Contradiction gate:** the strongest contrary interpretation has been recorded and tested.
- **Legal gate:** standing, jurisdiction, limitation and procedural route have been checked.
- **Action gate:** complaint, disclosure request, correction, referral, ADR, pre-action or proceedings is selected for a defined reason.
- **Remedy gate:** the requested outcome is legally available, proportionate and causally connected to the established facts.

**Legal-status convention:** DOCUMENTED FACT = supported by the cited record; FAMILY EVIDENCE / CONTENTION = evidence requiring assessment; LEGAL INFERENCE / ISSUE FOR COUNSEL = a legal proposition requiring qualified legal review. “Fraud”, “concealment”, “unlawful” and similar conclusions are not upgraded to fact without the necessary evidential and legal threshold.

New control model — cascade of accepted premises
------------------------------------------------

The case-control spine now treats the central forensic question as a chain-of-premises problem: identify the first materially wrong, incomplete or unconfirmed proposition; identify who introduced it; identify every later actor who relied upon it; identify the independent safeguard that should have caught it; and identify the document that proves whether the safeguard operated. This does not presume a conspiracy and does not excuse a later actor where the evidence establishes an independent duty or opportunity to correct the problem.

The new `Forensic Control-Point Chain <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/family-legal-case-control-room/forensic-control-point-chain-land-%e2%86%92-title-%e2%86%92-registration-%e2%86%92-court-%e2%86%92-enforcement-%e2%86%92-remedy/>`__ is now part of the case-control spine.

New control requirements
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- **Property-description mutation:** record the exact land description and plan at each material date; do not assume “Great House Farm” is one invariant legal parcel.
- **Authority chain:** record who requested, recommended, authorised, certified and executed each material step, and the source of that authority.
- **Evidence-before-court:** compare the propositions actually placed before the 1984–87 court with what BP and the family possessed and disclosed at the time.
- **Remedy firewall:** keep title, registration mistake/alteration, indemnity, fraud-based reopening and current administrative remedies as separate routes.
- **Institution isolation:** each institution/company/individual is assessed on its own dedicated page; cross-links do not merge responsibility.
