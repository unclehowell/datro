**Controlling Parcel-A chronology — 9 October… … read more**

**Controlling Parcel-A chronology — 9 October 2026.** The family’s established title case is that the 1877 arrangement took effect in 1928 when quarrying and rent to Thomas ended, passing the full Parcel A to the Williams family. Parcel A includes the farmhouse/buildings, barn, stable and yard west of the house, garden, north forest and south-east ground later registered as VG41. Parcel B is the separate eastern agricultural fields. The 1939 WGR–John Williams agreement/plan is already obtained and depicts Parcel B only. The title papers went missing in 1950–52 (Mary (Nancy) Williams, witness statement c.1974 (`Mary (Nancy) Williams’s statement <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/testimony/mary-williams/>`__)). [SOURCE PENDING] The 1949 attempt to make Mary’s Parcel-A occupancy permissive failed, and on 4 July 1955 WGR seized the north forest and south-east green while enforcing possession of Parcel B under “whole farm” wording. Mary’s defence of the occupied house/buildings/garden group did not narrow her full Parcel-A title claim.
