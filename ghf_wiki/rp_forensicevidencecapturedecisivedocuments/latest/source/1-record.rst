Evidence-page rule
------------------

For every item above the Wiki should maintain a dedicated page containing: **document identity; unique catalogue ID; exact date; repository/custodian; provenance; original or copy status; complete scan/image where lawful; transcription; physical description; chain of custody; what it proves; what it does not prove; contradictions; related evidence; legal significance; search status; and next action.** :sup:`🕒`

**Negative-search rule.** Where an institution says it does not hold an item, create a dedicated evidence page recording the request, search wording, repository scope, response, date, transfer/destruction explanation, and any next repository. “Not found” must never become “never existed”. :sup:`🕒`
