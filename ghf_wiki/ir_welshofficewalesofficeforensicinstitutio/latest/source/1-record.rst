Forensic Assessment — Welsh Office / Wales Office
-------------------------------------------------

**Scope:** historic/current Wales Office functions relevant to central-government oversight and records.

Role
~~~~

The Wales Office is not treated as the body that determined private title. Its relevance arises only where its records show ministerial/departmental awareness, correspondence, policy oversight or transfer/custody of records relevant to the case.

Forensic control test
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

For IR/26/16 and any related request, identify the exact record category, search scope, responsible policy area, exemptions, retention/transfer status and substantive answer. Distinguish a departmental records problem from the merits of the underlying land claim.

Current finding
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish wrongdoing by the Wales Office. This is not a finding that the office had no involvement; the assessment is limited to the particular correspondence, search and transfer issues identified. 🕒10-10-2026** The current objective is to obtain a reasoned answer and preserve any central-government record that can establish chronology or knowledge.

Human action
~~~~~~~~~~~~

Chase IR/26/16 to outcome, require a record-by-record explanation for any refusal/absence, and identify any transfer to The National Archives or successor department.

**Acceptance:** substantive result logged with the exact documents held/not held and escalation route. :sup:`🕒10-10-2026`
