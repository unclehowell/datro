Forensic Assessment — Home Office
---------------------------------

**Scope:** Home Office involvement only where a concrete historical policing/public-order/security record is identified.

Current evidence position
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The materials reviewed so far do not establish a Home Office decision that determined the Great House Farm title, possession or demolition. The project therefore does not attribute a substantive historic role to the Department merely because central government appears elsewhere in the wider record.

Forensic control test
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Only a specific identified file, correspondence trail or delegated function should bring the Home Office into the substantive accountability chain. Any claimed involvement must identify date, officer/role, legal function, information received and action taken.

Current finding
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish Home Office wrongdoing or an operative role in the historic events. This is a source-limited assessment, not proof that no relevant record exists.**

Human action
~~~~~~~~~~~~

Do not spend further project effort here unless a court, police, Welsh Office or records response identifies a concrete Home Office record. If such a lead appears, create a targeted request rather than a broad enquiry.

**Acceptance:** route remains parked unless a specific documentary lead emerges.
