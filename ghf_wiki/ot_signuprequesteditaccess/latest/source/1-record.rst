**Agent roles — Great House Farm programme:** `standing instruction for agents reading or editing this wiki, or working the matching HubSpot portal <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/agent-roles/>`__.

How the Two Edit Roles Work
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

- **Family (Administrator):** full edit rights. Publishes directly, no approval step, and can approve other people’s pending edits.
- **Friends of the Farm / public contributors:** can draft and submit pages, but cannot publish directly and cannot approve anyone else’s requests. An admin reviews and approves each edit before it goes live.
- **Everyone else:** a free account is enough to comment on any page — no request or approval needed for that.
