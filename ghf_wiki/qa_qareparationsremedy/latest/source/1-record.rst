Q&A — Reparations & Remedy
--------------------------

**What does ‘reparations’ mean in this project?**

Here it is used broadly to include possible forms of redress such as correction of the public record, recognition of historical loss, restoration where physically possible, recovery or preservation of records, and any legal or financial remedy that a competent authority or court might recognise.
