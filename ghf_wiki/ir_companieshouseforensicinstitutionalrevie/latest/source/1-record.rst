Forensic Assessment — Companies House
-------------------------------------

**Scope:** corporate registry function and surviving company-file evidence relevant to WGR, BP and later development entities.

Newly verified context
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Hansard recorded in May 1980 that Western Ground Rents had been bought by the BP pension fund. The Companies House evidence must now be used to identify the exact historical entity, transaction/ownership dates, directors and continuity of the legal entities, rather than relying on corporate shorthand.

Forensic control test
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Identify the precise company number/name at each relevant date for Western Ground Rents, BP Pension Trust/BP Properties and Ideal Homes. Determine what filings survive and who controlled the entities when the relevant conveyances, registration applications and litigation occurred.

Current finding
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish wrongdoing by Companies House. This is limited to the registry’s documentary role and does not rule out other actors’ involvement.** The registry is evidence about corporate identity and filings, not an adjudicator of the underlying land dispute.

Human action
~~~~~~~~~~~~

Obtain historic filings, name changes, annual returns/accounts, officer appointments and any surviving dissolved-company material. Reconcile these with the 1969 conveyance and 1982–87 registration/litigation chain.

**Acceptance:** a corporate-identity chronology exists for every company relied upon in the litigation theory.
