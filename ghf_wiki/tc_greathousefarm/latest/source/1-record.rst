Location and historical names
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**The place is associated in the records with… … read more**

The place is associated in the records with **Llandough-juxta-Penarth**, **Llandough near Penarth** and other geographical formulations. These are nomenclature variants to be tested against the underlying source, not interchangeable legal descriptions.

**IDENTITY CONTROL — TWO LLANDOUGH FARMS. The… … read more**

**IDENTITY CONTROL — TWO LLANDOUGH FARMS.** The Great House Farm documented on this page is **Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr at Llandough-juxta-Penarth**, beside St Dochdwy’s Church. **Llandough Farm (opposite hospital) is a separate farmstead in the same Llandough near Penarth/Cardiff locality.** It is not a former name, alias, subdivision, parcel, building, tenancy or nomenclature variant of Great House Farm. Likewise, the Museum’s wording **“Llandough Church Farm”** is retained as a source-specific term from the 1858 provenance and is **not** treated here as an established synonym for Great House Farm. No source may be reassigned from one farm to the other merely because both use “Llandough”.

**Location control — 1924 Llandough sale particulars**

The `Museum Wales catalogue entry MS 3360/3 <https://museum.wales/collections/online/object/5c76ee66-41ed-3800-86cf-8c1938ebf00e/Particulars-of-sale/>`__ describes a 1924 sale of nine farms and records the place as “Llandough, Llanfair (Glamorgan)”. Under this wiki’s scope, the Llandough/Cowbridge material is excluded and must not be conflated with Llandough-juxta-Penarth or used as evidence about Great House Farm. The exclusion and its effect on the 1924 drain-agreement research are recorded at `GHF-E119 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e119-15-may-1924-bute-corbett-drain-agreement-and-plan/>`__.

Evidence-first rule
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**A nomenclature variant is not itself… … read more**

A nomenclature variant is not itself evidence of ownership, fraud, concealment, amalgamation or legal identity. Each material proposition must be tied to the relevant primary evidence and tested against counter-evidence.
