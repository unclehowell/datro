Executive finding
-----------------

**The strongest case is not the 1877 “reverter” standing alone.** The more coherent forensic case is the chain from the 1939 fields-only plan, the 1949 surrender/re-grant, the absence of an identified independent Parcel A root in the WGR/BP chain, the 1982–84 registration papers, the 1987 amalgamation and the documentary gap in the possession litigation.

**Working probability — for research prioritisation only:** the independent review estimated roughly **45–55%** that BP never held a valid paper title to Parcel A on the evidence then seen. This is **not** a legal finding or probability of success; it is a reason to obtain the decisive documents before incurring major litigation cost.

8 October 2026 — New forensic paradigm and control-point model
--------------------------------------------------------------

**New analytical model:** do not begin by assuming that all institutions were coordinated or that every later actor was wrong. Instead identify the **first materially wrong, incomplete or unconfirmed premise**, the actor that introduced it, every later actor that relied on it, and the independent safeguard that should have detected it. A single foundational error can be amplified by later reliance without establishing dishonesty by every participant.

The immediate forensic question is therefore: **what exact physical land object did each legal instrument, title, register, court order and development record refer to?** The investigation should trace **historic land identity → ownership/tenancy → acquisition chain → first registration → 13 January 1984 registration activity → 2 February 1987 amalgamation → court evidence → enforcement → heritage/planning → record custody → present remedy.**

Reopening / indemnity firewall
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Keep four questions separate: **(1) title/extent;** **(2) registration mistake and alteration/rectification;** **(3) statutory indemnity for qualifying loss;** and **(4) fraud-based reopening of the 1987 judgment.** Proving a title or registration defect does not by itself prove dishonesty. A fraud-based reopening route requires an identified person and evidence that deliberate dishonesty was an operative cause of the judgment. The present evidence therefore supports investigation, not a finding of fraud. :sup:`🕒`
