Forensic Assessment — HM Land Registry Independent Complaints Reviewer
----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Scope:** the ICR’s review function concerning HM Land Registry customer-service/complaint handling. It does not determine title and is not an appeal court.

Current live issue
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The ICR reference **ICR/090/26** is now a live execution item. The family must verify the precise six-month referral clock from the ICR acknowledgement; the project has provisionally treated late November 2026 as the outer deadline.

Forensic control test
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The referral should identify whether HMLR’s final complaint response adequately engaged with the newly documented registration-file chronology: WA240304, A13/DL, 12 May 1983 correspondence, 13 January 1984 A3/register, and 2 February 1987 amalgamation. It should distinguish complaint-handling failure from the underlying title merits.

Current finding
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**As at 10-10-2026, no wrongdoing by the Independent Complaints Reviewer is suggested or established in the material reviewed. This does not mean every current complaint issue has been resolved.** The ICR is the current independent complaint-review route, not the forum to prove private title.
