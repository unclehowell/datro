Every material legal proposition must be traceable from the proposition to primary evidence and then to the proposed legal use.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Proposition
     - Evidence ID / source
     - Primary or secondary
     - Counter-evidence
     - Materiality
     - Legal use
     - Status
   * - Ownership / title
     - Record IDs
     - Primary preferred
     - Strongest contrary record
     - High / medium / low
     - Title / defence / remedy
     - OPEN
   * - Registration event
     - HMLR file
     - Primary
     - Register / plan / opposing case
     - High
     - Alteration / rectification / JR
     - OPEN
   * - 1987 finding
     - Court papers
     - Primary
     - Contrary evidence
     - High
     - Review / argument
     - OPEN
   * - Knowledge
     - Correspondence / dated records
     - Primary
     - Alternative custodian account
     - Variable
     - Disclosure / deceit / public-law
     - OPEN
   * - Loss / remedy
     - Title, valuation and consequential records
     - Primary + expert
     - Alternative causation
     - High
     - Damages / restoration / correction
     - OPEN

**Evidence threshold:** missing records remain “not found” until the Negative Search Log and alternative-custodian route have been exhausted; “missing” is not itself proof of concealment or destruction. :sup:`🕒`
