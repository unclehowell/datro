**Page ID:** 1482 · **Title:** Limitation & Procedural Deadline Firewall · **Evidence status:** Not yet labelled — see the Editing Protocol · **Version:** 1.0.0 · `Editing Protocol <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/editing-protocol/>`__

**Purpose:** protect every potentially live legal route before research, complaints, FOI/SAR, archive requests or public campaigning create a false sense that a limitation or court deadline has been preserved.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Field
     - Control
   * - Potential claim / route
     - Identify the specific cause of action, statutory route, appeal, review or complaint.
   * - Potential claimant
     - Name the actual person/estate/company with standing; never use “family” as a legal substitute.
   * - Potential defendant / public body
     - Identify the person, company, authority or office-holder legally answerable.
   * - Accrual
     - Record the earliest legally arguable accrual date and the competing accrual dates.
   * - Knowledge / concealment issue
     - Record only if supported by evidence and legally relevant to the applicable limitation regime.
   * - Limitation period
     - Do not assume. Confirm the governing statute/rule and any extension/discretion.
   * - Procedural deadline
     - Record appeal, review, complaint, tribunal or court deadlines separately from limitation.
   * - Evidence-preservation deadline
     - Record urgent preservation actions independently of the legal deadline.
   * - Last safe date
     - Use the earliest defensible date until qualified counsel confirms otherwise.
   * - Protective action
     - State what must be done before that date: advice, notice, application, claim or other protective step.
   * - Status
     - OPEN → CHECKING → COUNSEL REQUIRED → PROTECTED → CLOSED.

**Firewall rule:** a complaint, FOI/EIR request, SAR, archive enquiry or referral may preserve evidence or establish a record, but must not be treated as stopping limitation unless the law actually provides that effect.
