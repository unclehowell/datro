**Assessed 14 October 2026.** Scope: Western Ground Rents Ltd (WGR) as landlord and litigant in the Great House Farm chain. WGR is a historic company; this page assesses documented acts only. Inclusion is not a finding of fault.

Role
----

WGR took the Bute Estate reversion on the 1916 tenancy in 1938, was landlord in the 1939–1965 tenancy and possession sequence, and sold its interest to BP Pension Trust on 31 December 1969 (our case: Parcel B, plus an interest in the 1962 order on Parcel A, not the land as a whole). The main narrative is on the `Western Ground Rents Ltd <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/western-ground-rents/>`__ actor page; this review does not repeat it.

.. _finding:

Current finding
---------------

**As at 14 October 2026, the material reviewed does not establish dishonesty or misconduct by WGR.** The contention is that WGR’s “whole farm” wording was extended over Parcel A land; that is a contention to be tested against the order, claim, plans and execution record, not a finding. The company is a historic entity and no individual officer has been identified from the records.

.. _control:

Forensic control test and next step
-----------------------------------

Identify who instructed the 1955 and 1962 proceedings, what plan accompanied each, and what WGR’s own files recorded. Seek the Companies House file (see the `Companies House review <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/scrutiny-and-accountability/government-public-authority-involvement/companies-house-forensic-institutional-review/>`__) and the WGR rental books lead at `D696 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/d696-western-ground-rents-rental-books-plan-book-litigation-evidence-lead/>`__. **Acceptance:** the 1955 and 1962 instructions and plans are located, or a documented search result says they do not survive.
