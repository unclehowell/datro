   * - Museum MS 3360/3 (1924 “Llandough Estate” sale particulars, page headed Great House Farm)
     - Supplied with FOI 2026-032 on 22 September; the Wiki has not yet examined the page. The title (“Nine Well-Known Farms … forming part of the Llandough Estate”) points to the Llandough Castle estate at Llandough-juxta-Cowbridge, which has its own Great House Farm. In 1924 the Penarth farm was split between Thomas (Parcel A) and Bute (Parcel B), neither a “Llandough Estate”. Not the 1877 split in Mary (Nancy) Williams’s statement, and not the 15 May 1924 agreement on WA231076.
     - `R33 location check <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/records-access-chronology/foi-reply-register/ghf-e106-r33-amgueddfa-cymru-reply-further-collections-search-following-8-september-2026-foi/#location-check>`__ · `The Two Parcels <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/the-two-parcels/#same-name>`__
