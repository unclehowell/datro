1955

The surviving chronology records a High Court possession order in favour of Western Ground Rents and execution on 4 July 1955. The farmhouse and garden were left occupied, but the family position is that Parcel-A woodland and the green were taken at this event.

The new control point is therefore physical and juridical: **what exact ground did the warrant/possession process authorise the enforcement officer or WGR to take, and what exact ground was actually taken?** That question must be answered by the order, warrant, return, plan and contemporaneous evidence, not by the later use of the phrase “whole farm”.

New legal route to test
-----------------------

**Welsh Government guidance provides… … read more**

Welsh Government guidance provides procedures for correcting historical errors in commons and greens registers by adding or removing land where the statutory criteria are satisfied. That does **not** mean VG41 is wrongly registered; it means the registration record itself is capable of being independently tested if the primary file reveals a mistake. citeturn982810view0turn343265search6

Do not infer Council ownership merely because the Council maintains or administers the register. The project must identify the actual legal estate holder from the VG41 record and Land Registry/current title evidence. A private ownership claim and the public rights associated with a village green are separate questions.
