`About this page type → Read the Docs <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/read-the-docs/#testimony-pages>`__

One page per family member who has said or written anything on record. Each page lists only real sources, with date and reference: affidavits, witness statements, court records, letters, emails and newspaper quotes. Words in quotation marks are verbatim from the record held. Where nothing is on file, the page says so.

Elsewhere on this wiki, a statement that rests on a document cites the document. A statement made by a family member is attributed to that person and links here. A statement with neither is marked SOURCE PENDING.
