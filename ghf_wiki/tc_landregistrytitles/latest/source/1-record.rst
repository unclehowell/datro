**The land of Great House Farm was first… … read more**

The land of `Great House Farm <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/great-house-farm/>`__ was first registered at HM Land Registry by `BP Properties Ltd <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/bp-properties/>`__ in 1982–83. All documents cited here are in the `Evidence Library <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/>`__.

**Central title-chain test — 15 October 2026.** For WA240304, the live question is what primary evidence established WGR’s title to the farmhouse-and-garden parcel before the 1969 transfer and the 1975 conveyance to BP Properties. The 1939 tenancy plan is evidence about tenancy extent, not a substitute for the 1938 conveyance. The 1969 deed is absent from the disclosed schedule, not proven never to have been examined. Read the `1983 First Registration: Parcel A Title Basis and Alleged HMLR Mistake <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/scrutiny-and-accountability/procedural-fairness-notice/1983-first-registration-parcel-a-title-basis-alleged-hmlr-mistake/>`__ alongside the title chronology and evidence catalogue.

**1900 will / 1905 conveyance lead — unresolved.** A research note reports that 1900 newspaper coverage said Lord Bute’s will left Llandough land to his son Lord Colum. The particular article and the clause identifying the land have not been inspected. Inheritance to Lord Colum would be an intermediate succession event, not by itself a break in the title chain. The priority test is the 23 February 1905 conveyance and any schedule/plan recorded on WA231076 (GHF-E026-03), compared with the will/probate and the 1938 Bute–WGR conveyance. The deed and plan remain outstanding; no parcel conclusion follows until they are read. See `New Archival Leads, section 8.2 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/new-archival-leads-bute-archive-1793-manor-ty-mawr-plans-1983-registration/>`__.

The registration merged both parcels on the pre-1928 description (`1928 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1877-agreement/#takes-effect>`__) and did not disclose her claim.
