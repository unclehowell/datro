Q&A — Evidence, Records & Method
--------------------------------

**What counts as primary evidence?**

Original deeds, plans, court documents, title records, contemporaneous correspondence, official registers, photographs and archival copies are treated as stronger evidence for documentary questions than later summaries. Where only a secondary source survives, the site labels it accordingly.

**What is the role of family members’ testimony?**

Each family member’s testimony has its own page under `Testimony <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/testimony/>`__. Testimony preserves first-hand knowledge and provides research leads, including dates, names and descriptions of events. They can be corroborated by deeds, registers, newspapers, photographs and official records. Each statement is cited to the named person and the record it is in, alongside the deeds, registers and court records that bear on it.

**Why is machine-readable evidence important?**

The site includes an `AI Agent & Machine-Readable Index <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/ai-agent-index/>`__ so that people and research agents can find canonical pages, identifiers and public endpoints. Machine readability is intended to improve retrieval, not to replace human assessment of the source documents.
