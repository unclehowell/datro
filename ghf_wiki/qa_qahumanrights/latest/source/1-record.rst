Q&A — Human Rights & Public Law
-------------------------------

**Was there a European human-rights case?**

The research record identifies **ECHR application 14464/88**, introduced on 3 May 1988, registered on 12 December 1988 and declared inadmissible on 14 April 1989. The fact of the application and its outcome are historical records; the legal reasons and implications need to be read from the actual Strasbourg materials.

**What happened to the original Commission case file?**

**ARCHIVE CORRESPONDENCE.** The European Court of Human Rights Archives has confirmed to the family that the case file for application **14464/88** was destroyed in **2000**. The 14 April 1989 Commission decision remains available through HUDOC. The Wiki therefore does not state that the destroyed 1989 case file can still be ordered.

The dispute included litigation and possession proceedings. Article 6 concerns fair hearing and related procedural protections. Whether it has any application to a particular historical event would depend on the proceedings, dates, standing and admissibility rules.

**Why could Article 8 be relevant?**

Article 8 protects private and family life and the home, subject to its legal framework. The family describes `Great House Farm <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/great-house-farm/>`__ as the family home and records the 1988 eviction.
