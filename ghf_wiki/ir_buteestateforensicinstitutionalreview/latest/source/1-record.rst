.. _finding:

Current finding
---------------

**As at 14 October 2026, the material reviewed does not establish wrongdoing by the Bute Estate.** The wiki records the family evidence that the house parcel was conveyed to Daniel Thomas in 1877, so it does not treat Bute as holding an unbroken title to both parcels through 1938. How the estate acquired the freehold in the early nineteenth century is unresolved.

.. _control:

Forensic control test and next step
-----------------------------------

Examine the individual National Library of Wales D 219 deeds and plans and the Glamorgan Archives Bute catalogue items before any parcel conclusion is drawn. See also the `Glamorgan Archives <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/scrutiny-and-accountability/government-public-authority-involvement/glamorgan-archives-forensic-institutional-review/>`__ and `National Library of Wales <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/scrutiny-and-accountability/government-public-authority-involvement/national-library-of-wales-forensic-institutional-review/>`__ reviews. **Acceptance:** the 1877 and 1938 instruments and plans are read against each other and the result recorded.
