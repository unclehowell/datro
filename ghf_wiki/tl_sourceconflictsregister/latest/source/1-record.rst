.. _frame:

How the two parcels frame the conflicts
---------------------------------------

**FAMILY CASE — contention, not a finding.** Parcel A means the full 1877 house/quarrying estate: farmhouse/buildings, barn, stable and yard west of the house, garden, forest north of the house, and the ground south-east of the house/garden later registered as VG41. Parcel B is the separate eastern agricultural fields. The family’s established title case is that the 1877 arrangement took effect in 1928 and Parcel A passed to the Williams family; Mary’s later practical defence of the occupied house/buildings/garden group did not limit the full estate she claimed. The 1939 WGR–John Williams agreement/plan is already obtained and depicts Parcel B only. [SOURCE PENDING] The 1949 effort to characterise Mary’s Parcel-A occupation as permissive failed (no written record of the 2 February 1949 re-grant; GHF-E122). Mary (Nancy) Williams’s c.1974 witness statement (`Mary (Nancy) Williams testimony <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/testimony/mary-williams/>`__) records that the title papers disappeared in 1950–52. [SOURCE PENDING] WGR’s 4 July 1955 enforcement of Parcel B also seized Parcel-A north forest and south-east green under “whole farm” wording (writ and execution record sought: GHF-E126). Whether later actors knowingly promoted that conflation is a separate evidential question; it must not be inferred from the physical event or missing title papers alone. (`A-058 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/anomalies-register/#a-058>`__; `1877 Agreement — papers taken <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1877-agreement/#papers-taken>`__). :sup:`🕒`

Most conflicts below come from one pattern: a body describes “the farm” as a single holding, or dates an event from that single-holding description, where the documents show two parcels and two registered titles until the amalgamation of 2 February 1987. The entries record what each body said; they do not, by themselves, decide what the documents show.

SC-20 addendum — do not conflate the two D 219 farm entries
-----------------------------------------------------------

The NLW series D 219 catalogue explicitly distinguishes “Cydfin Farm or Ty Mawr Farm” (107 acres; D219/1, 4–5, 8 et seq., 1552–1824) from “Llandough Farm” (166 acres; D219/9–13, 1824–1832). The 166-acre entry is not the Tŷ Mawr entry and must not be used to supply Great House Farm’s acreage. Source: `official NLW D 219 description <https://archives.library.wales/index.php/llandough-manorial-leases-and-agreements>`__.

Keep the 1824 DB/E/1 plan 19 survey figure for “Cedfin or Ty Mawr Farm” separate as well: its current transcription gives 139a 3r 8p including woodland, with readings still provisional. These figures cannot be harmonised without comparing the deed boundaries, the survey schedule and the individual plans.

For the full dispositions of false matches, source duplication and contextual-only evidence, see the `Kissed Frogs screening register <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/anomalies-register/bpvsbuckler-archive-research-leads/#kissed-frogs-register>`__. The 6 November 1877 Clyde Street deed is a Cardiff building lease; the reported Llandough instrument remains unlocated.
