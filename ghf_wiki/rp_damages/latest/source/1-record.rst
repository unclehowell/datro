3. Rectification and indemnity
------------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Rule
     - Effect
   * - `LRA 2002 Sch 4 <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/9/schedule/4>`__ paras 2–3, 5–6
     - Register may be rectified to correct a mistake; not against a proprietor in possession without consent unless fraud/lack of care or unjust not to.
   * - `LRA 2002 Sch 8 <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/9/schedule/8>`__ para 1(1)(a)
     - Indemnity for loss caused by rectification (runs to whoever loses on rectification).
   * - Sch 8 para 1(1)(b), para 6(b)
     - Mistake not rectified: indemnity value capped at value when the mistake was made.
   * - Sch 8 para 5
     - No indemnity for own fraud; reduced for own lack of proper care.
   * - Sch 8 para 3
     - Costs only if incurred with the registrar’s consent.
   * - Sch 8 para 8
     - Simple contract debt: 6 years from when the claimant knew or ought to have known.
   * - `LRR 2003 r.195 <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/1417/rule/195>`__
     - Simple interest. Before 10 Nov 2008: judgment-debt rate. From 10 Nov 2008: Bank Rate + 1%. None for periods without due diligence (r.195(3)).
