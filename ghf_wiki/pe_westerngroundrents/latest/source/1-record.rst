**Page ID:** 17 · **Title:** Western Ground Rents Ltd · **Evidence status:** Not yet labelled — see the Editing Protocol · **Version:** 1.0.0 · `Editing Protocol <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/editing-protocol/>`__

**Western Ground Rents Ltd** (WGR) held the documentary reversionary interest conveyed by the `Bute Estate <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/bute-estate/>`__ in 1938 and was the landlord in the later possession and tenancy sequence at `Great House Farm <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/great-house-farm/>`__ until it sold to `BP Pension Trust <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/bp-properties/>`__ in 1969. The family’s established title case is that the 1877 arrangement took effect in 1928, passing the full Parcel A to the Williams family. Parcel A includes the farmhouse/buildings, barn, stable and yard west of the house, garden, north forest and south-east ground later registered as VG41. Parcel B is the separate eastern agricultural fields. The already-obtained 1939 WGR–John Williams agreement/plan depicts Parcel B only. The disputed question is how WGR’s later descriptions and enforcement of Parcel B were extended over Parcel-A forest and green on 4 July 1955 [SOURCE PENDING: no execution record yet], and how those areas entered later title records.

Parcel BWGR’s documentary chain principally concerns the eastern agricultural fields. Its later dealings with the occupied farmhouse/buildings/garden group must not be treated as defining or establishing title to the full Parcel A, which, on Mary (Nancy) Williams’s c.1974 witness statement (`Mary (Nancy) Williams testimony <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/testimony/mary-williams/>`__), passed to the Williams family when quarrying ceased (last rent to Thomas 1928) and [SOURCE PENDING] included north forest and south-east green.

Events
------

- 1938 — takes Bute’s reversion on the 1916 tenancy (`details <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1939-revised-tenancy/#y1938>`__) [PENDING: `EV-0019 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0019-bute-wgr-conveyance-plan-1938/>`__]
- 1939 — grants John Williams a revised tenancy in May 1939; its plan shows the fields east of the house (`details <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1939-revised-tenancy/#y1939>`__; `plan <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1939-revised-tenancy/#plan>`__) [`EV-0023 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0023-1939-wgr-john-williams-tenancy-plan/>`__]
- 1949 — grants Frederick an unwritten yearly agricultural tenancy of Parcel B from 2 February [PENDING: `EV-0025 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0025-frederick-buckler-unwritten-tenancy-1949/>`__]. [SOURCE PENDING] The same step was the first failed attempt to make Mary’s separate Parcel-A occupation appear permissive (`details <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/frederick-buckler/#tenancy-1949>`__)
- 1955 — ends Frederick’s Parcel-B tenancy on 2 February and obtains a High Court order worded for the “whole farm” (`details <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1955-possession-order/#order>`__) [PENDING: `EV-0032 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0032-1955-order-14-possession-order/>`__]; on 4 July, while enforcing possession of Parcel B, WGR also seized, on the family’s established account, the Parcel-A north forest and south-east green, while Mary stayed in the occupied farmhouse/buildings/garden group (`details <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1955-possession-order/#enforcement>`__) [PENDING: `EV-0034 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0034-writ-possession-execution-return-1955/>`__]
- 1959 — offers Mary a tenancy of the occupied farmhouse/buildings/garden group; she refuses and asserts ownership [PENDING: `EV-0039 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0039-wgr-tenancy-offers-1959-1965/>`__]. This concerned the remaining occupied centre, not the full Parcel A, whose north forest and south-east green had already been seized [SOURCE PENDING] (`details <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1962-possession-order/#refusal-1959>`__)
- 1962 — obtains a possession and mesne profits order at Cardiff County Court, 11 December 1962; not enforced against the house (`details <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1962-possession-order/#order>`__) [PENDING: `EV-0041 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0041-cardiff-county-court-possession-order-1962/>`__]
- 1965 — agents’ letter of 2 March 1965 offers “Mrs Williams” a weekly tenancy of the farmhouse and garden at £2; not signed; the estate treated her occupation as adverse from then (`details <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1962-possession-order/#offer-1965>`__) [PENDING: `EV-0039 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0039-wgr-tenancy-offers-1959-1965/>`__]
- 1969 — conveys to BP Pension Trust on 31 December 1969; the deed is not on the Land Registry’s first-registration files (`details <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/land-registry-titles/#conveyances>`__) [PENDING: `EV-0043 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0043-wgr-bp-pension-trust-conveyance/>`__]

**Research note.** The 1949–1965 steps read as repeated attempts to make Mary’s occupation of Parcel A permissive, and the 1969 sale as the point at which a Parcel A claim enters the BP chain without an identified documentary conveyance into WGR (`title test <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/the-two-parcels/#title-test>`__).
