Parcel A nomenclature
---------------------

- **Parcel A** — the primary analytical label for the full 1877 house/quarrying estate: farmhouse/buildings; barn, stable and yard west of the house; garden; forest north of the house; and ground south-east of the house/garden later registered as VG41.
- **house parcel** — shorthand for the full house/quarrying parcel, including its forest and green ground; it does not mean only the house/buildings footprint.
- **house-side parcel** — descriptive variant distinguishing the full Parcel-A estate from Parcel B, the separate eastern agricultural fields.
- **farmhouse / buildings parcel** — only the occupied building group within Parcel A, not an adequate label for the full Parcel-A extent.

Identity boundary
-----------------

Parcel A is not the same thing as the farmhouse or the “house and garden” area Mary remained able to occupy. The building and garden group sat within the full Parcel-A estate. On the family’s established title case, the 1877 arrangement took effect in 1928 and Parcel A included the forest north of the house and the south-east green ground as well. Parcel B remained the separate eastern agricultural parcel. Later “whole farm” wording blurred these distinct interests; it did not itself merge them into one title.
