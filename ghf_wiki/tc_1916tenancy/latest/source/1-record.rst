**In February 1916 the Marquess of Bute… … read more**

In February 1916 the `Marquess of Bute <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/bute-estate/>`__ granted `John Williams <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/john-williams/>`__ a yearly agricultural tenancy of `Great House Farm <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/great-house-farm/>`__. Its description of the “whole farm” was relied on by every later landlord and by the courts. This page is the home of the grant and of how later records reused it.
