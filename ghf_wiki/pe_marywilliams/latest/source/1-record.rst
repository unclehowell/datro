**Mary (Nancy) Williams (Mrs Buckler; 10 August 1913 … … read more**

**Mary (Nancy) Williams** (Mrs Buckler; **10 August 1913 – 26 March 1983**) was born at `Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr) <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/great-house-farm/>`__, Llandough, and lived there all her life. The family’s established title case is that the 1877 arrangement took effect in 1928 and Parcel A covered the full house/quarrying estate: farmhouse/buildings, barn, stable and yard west of the house, garden, forest north of the house, and the south-east ground later registered as VG41. Parcel B was the separate eastern agricultural fields. She was the daughter of `John Williams <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/john-williams/>`__ and married `Frederick Buckler <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/frederick-buckler/#marriage>`__ in 1936; they had seven children, among them William, the defendant in `BP Properties Ltd v Buckler <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/bp-properties-v-buckler/>`__. She claimed the full Parcel A through the `1877 agreement <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1877-agreement/#sale-1877>`__, including the north forest and south-east green ground as well as the farmhouse/buildings and garden. After the 1955 seizure, she resisted removal from the occupied house/buildings/garden group and refused the later tenancy offers. That practical defence of the remaining occupied centre does not narrow her underlying title claim to it. After Frederick’s death in 1967 she reverted to her maiden name, Williams. Her own account is `Mary (Nancy) Williams’s statement <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/mary-williams-statement/>`__ (c. 1974).
