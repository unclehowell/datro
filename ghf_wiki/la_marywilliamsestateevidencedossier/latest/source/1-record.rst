This dossier controls the evidence needed to determine Mary (Nancy) Williams’s proprietary position, occupation and the identity of the person or estate entitled to pursue any surviving legal route.

- Identity and death: civil registration, date/place of death and probate.
- Occupation: evidence that Mary occupied the farmhouse and the extent of land occupied.
- Ownership / title: deeds, conveyances, tenancy documents, plans, statements and registration material.
- 1982–83 registration: complete WA240304 application and supporting evidence; identify what was disclosed about Mary’s interest and occupation.
- Correspondence: Linklaters and other professional correspondence, especially post-death material and certification.
- Inheritance: will, intestacy/probate, beneficiaries and any transmission of rights.
- Post-death events: 1983 registration, 1984–88 possession litigation and any representations about Mary’s interest.
- Standing result: record the legal claimant identified by the evidence, not the family label.

**Acceptance test:** no conclusion about Mary’s legal interest is treated as established until the primary title/estate evidence and the complete 1982–83 registration record have been tested together.
