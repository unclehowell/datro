How to Connect ChatGPT to Great House Farm Wiki
-----------------------------------------------

**This page explains how contributors can… … read more**

This page explains how contributors can connect a free ChatGPT account directly to greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com for Wiki collaboration.

--------------

Step 1: Accept the WordPress Invitation
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

#. Check the contributor’s email for an invitation to join **greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com**.
#. The contributor should open the link in the email to accept the invitation and create or sign in to the associated WordPress account.

Step 2: Obtain ChatGPT
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

#. The **ChatGPT** app can be installed on an iOS/Android phone, or **chatgpt.com** can be accessed through a computer browser.
#. A free OpenAI account is required for access.

Step 3: Connect ChatGPT to the Wiki
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

- **Method 1 (Custom GPT Link):** The contributor should open the team’s Custom GPT link ``[INSERT_CUSTOM_GPT_LINK_HERE]``. When an action is run, ChatGPT will prompt the contributor to log into ``greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com`` to authorize access.
- **Method 2 (Application Password):**

  #. The contributor should log into ``greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com``.
  #. The contributor should go to **Users** > **Profile** and scroll down to **Application Passwords**.
  #. The contributor should enter ``ChatGPT`` as the name and select **Add New Application Password**.
  #. The generated key can then be entered in the ChatGPT action settings.

--------------

Collaboration Rules & Tips
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

- **Avoid Simultaneous Editing:** Contributors should work on separate drafts or distinct sections. WordPress saves the last received input, so working on the exact same draft at the same time could overwrite changes.
- **Author Tracking:** All contributions generated via the ChatGPT connection will be tagged automatically with the contributor’s WordPress username.
- **Free Tier Limits:** WordPress supports unlimited API requests, but free ChatGPT accounts remain subject to standard peak-hour usage caps.
