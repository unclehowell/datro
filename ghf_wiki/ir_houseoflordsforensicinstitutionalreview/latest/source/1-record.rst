Forensic Assessment — House of Lords
------------------------------------

**Scope:** appellate leave/application stage following the 1987 Court of Appeal decision.

Role
~~~~

The 1987 Court of Appeal report records: “Leave to appeal to the House of Lords was refused but a stay pending a possible application to the House was granted on terms.” [EV-0080]. [SOURCE PENDING] Further appellate leave was then sought from the House of Lords. The House of Lords stage must be distinguished from the Court of Appeal merits decision and from the later ECHR application.

Forensic control test
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Identify exactly what application was made, what grounds were advanced, what documents were provided, and what the procedural disposition was. The project should not infer that a refusal of leave endorsed every proposition in the Court of Appeal judgment.

Current finding
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish institutional wrongdoing. This does not conclude that every act, omission or record question is resolved.** The value of the record is to determine the final procedural history and whether any particular issue was or was not presented for further appeal.

Human action
~~~~~~~~~~~~

Obtain the House of Lords leave/application papers and incorporate them into the 1987 judgment reconstruction and preclusion matrix.

**Acceptance:** final appellate procedural history is documented with exact grounds and disposition.
