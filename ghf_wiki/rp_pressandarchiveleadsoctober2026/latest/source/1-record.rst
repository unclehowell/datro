1989 HER and demolition records
-------------------------------

**[PRESS REPORT; source lead: HER file GGAT02038s on Archwilio]** South Wales Echo, 11 February 1989, p. 2, is reported as stating that the Vale authorised legal action against BP Properties; that a 13 December 1988 letter sent through Debenham Tewson had gone unanswered; and that an enforcement notice was being prepared. Obtain and compare the full newspaper page with the underlying HER file before treating every detail in the report as established.

**[CATALOGUE ENTRY ONLY]** The March 1989 SMR record card in the same HER file is described as stating “building destroyed by owners”. The original card and its authorship/context should be inspected. This is a record of what the card says, not by itself a determination of legal responsibility.

**[PRESS REPORT]** Western Mail, 7 May 1958, reporting the Leckwith Common hearing. **[PRIMARY PUBLIC RECORD — verify original Hansard text]** Hansard, 25 November 1958, is cited for the proposition that no petition was lodged. **[CATALOGUE ENTRY ONLY]** TNA BD 11/3775 is a further archival reference to examine. Bute will and 1905 conveyance — unresolved identity/title test
--------------------------------------------------------------

**[PRESS REPORT / original will not yet inspected]** The 3rd Marquess of Bute’s will, dated 30 July 1894 and reported in October 1900, is said to leave Llandough and Leckwith to Lord Colum Crichton-Stuart. Obtain the will or a reliable probate copy before relying on that description.

**[CATALOGUE ENTRY ONLY until the deed is obtained]** The decisive cross-check is the conveyance dated 23 February 1905 filed or referred to on HMLR title WA231076. Obtain its full text, parties, schedule and plan; compare them to the will and mapped parcels. Whether the will’s disposition and the 1905 conveyance affect the land claimed as Parcel A remains an open question.
