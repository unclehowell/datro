**Purpose:** identify the legally entitled claimant for each proposed remedy before correspondence or proceedings are framed.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Issue
     - Potential claimant
     - Standing evidence
     - Missing evidence
     - Counsel status
   * - Historic title / ownership
     - Individual, estate or successor interest
     - Deeds, probate, title records
     - Complete chain and estate documents
     - Required
   * - 1982–83 registration
     - Person whose proprietary interest was affected
     - Occupation, title and registration evidence
     - Full HMLR application file
     - Required
   * - 1987 judgment
     - Original litigant / estate or legally recognised successor route
     - Pleadings, judgment, appeal record
     - Complete court file
     - Required
   * - Fraud / deceit
     - Person suffering legally recognised loss
     - Cause, act, knowledge/dishonesty and loss evidence
     - Forensic evidence and causation
     - Required
   * - Register alteration / indemnity
     - Registered proprietor / person with relevant interest
     - Official register and transaction history
     - Application/decision material
     - Required
   * - Public-law challenge
     - Person with sufficient interest
     - Decision and standing facts
     - Current decision/legal route
     - Urgent

**Rule:** standing is a hard gate. The Wiki may investigate on behalf of the family, but each legal claim must identify the actual claimant.
