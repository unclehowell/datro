Short answers to the questions a reader is most likely to have about `Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr) <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/great-house-farm/>`__ and `the Williams / Buckler family <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/williams-buckler-family/>`__. Start here
~~~~~~~~~~

**What is the central question about the land?**

Whether Parcel A, the house plot, ever passed into the Western Ground Rents → BP title chain, notwithstanding BP’s possession and the 1982–83 registration (`title test <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/the-two-parcels/#title-test>`__; `first registration <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/land-registry-titles/#first-registration>`__).

**What is the working theory?**

The working theory is that the farm was two parcels and that `Parcel A <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/the-two-parcels/#parcel-a>`__ never passed into BP’s title. It is a working theory under documentary testing, not a finding (`Where things stand <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/where-things-stand/>`__).

**Did the family have possession of the farmhouse?**

Yes, until BP obtained possession on 29 November 1988, despite the `1955 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1955-possession-order/#enforcement>`__ and `1962 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1962-possession-order/#order>`__ orders and the `1974 licence letters <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1974-licence-letters/#letters>`__ (`1988 possession <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/demolition-1988/#eviction>`__). What that possession meant in law is covered under `Legal & Ownership <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/qa/qa-legal-ownership/>`__.

**What happened to the farmhouse in 1988?**

BP Properties demolished it on 5–6 December 1988, a week after taking possession and the day after Cadw decided not to list it (`demolition <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/demolition-1988/#demolition>`__; `Cadw 1988 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/archaeology/#cadw-1988>`__). Details: `Demolition & Redevelopment <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/qa/qa-demolition-redevelopment/>`__.

**What is already documented, and what is still open?**

What the records establish, what is contended and what has not been shown are set out on `Where things stand <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/where-things-stand/>`__. Open research questions are kept in one register: `Open Questions & Research Priorities <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/qa/qa-open-questions/>`__.
