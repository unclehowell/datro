One research subject — with nearby same-name farms kept separate
----------------------------------------------------------------

This page is the Wiki’s visual **identity-control map**. The research subject is **Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr at Llandough-juxta-Penarth**, beside St Dochdwy’s Church. **Do not merge it with the separate Llandough Farm opposite the hospital.** Both are in the same Llandough near Penarth/Cardiff locality, but they are different farmsteads. “Church Farm / Llandough Church Farm” is also shown as a **source-specific, unresolved term**, not as an alias of Great House Farm. :sup:`🕒`

**Controlling parcel definition — 9 October 2026.** Parcel A is the full 1877 house/quarrying estate: farmhouse/buildings, barn, stable and yard west of the house, garden, forest north of the house, and ground south-east of the house/garden later registered as VG41. Parcel B is the separate eastern agricultural fields. The family’s established title case is that the 1877 arrangement took effect in 1928. [SOURCE PENDING] WGR’s 4 July 1955 enforcement of Parcel B also seized the Parcel-A north forest and south-east green under “whole farm” wording. Mary’s later defence of the occupied house group does not narrow the full Parcel-A boundary.

What the colours are doing
--------------------------

- **Blue = house/building layer.** These are names and descriptions attached to the farmhouse or its property/building identity.
- **Purple = combined farm layer.** “The farm”, “whole farm” and “the whole farm” describe the combined holding where the evidence supports that reading.
- **Green = the full Parcel A.** House/buildings, barn, stable and west-side yard, garden, north forest, and south-east green ground later registered as VG41. **Gold = Parcel B.** The separate eastern agricultural fields.
- **Terracotta = locality layer.** These place the site inside different historical or modern geographical frames.
- **Grey = institutional controls.** These are identifiers rather than alternative names.
- **Dashed boxes = qualification.** A dashed box means the connection is qualified, unresolved, or should not be treated as a clean synonym. :sup:`🕒`
- **Red perimeter = false friends.** They are shown precisely so that a researcher can see what looks similar but must stay separate.

The ambiguity in one sentence
-----------------------------

**One historical site can be called by a building name, a property/farm name, a parcel description, or a locality name — and a single document can move between those levels without announcing that it has changed level.**

That is why the Wiki keeps separate controls for the `Farmhouse <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/great-house-farm/farmhouse-nomenclature/>`__, `Building <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/great-house-farm/building-name-nomenclature/>`__, `Location <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/great-house-farm/location-name-nomenclature/>`__, `Parcel A <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/the-two-parcels/parcel-a-nomenclature/>`__, `Parcel B <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/the-two-parcels/parcel-b-nomenclature/>`__ and `Whole Farm <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/the-two-parcels/whole-farm-nomenclature/>`__ — while this page shows how easily the six layers collide in practice.

**Research rule:** follow the wording of the source first; resolve the identity second. Never allow a familiar name to do the work of a title, plan, grid reference, deed, HER record or other independent identifier.

**Cross-reference — Cedwyn / Cydfin place-name evidence:** References on this page to variants such as Cedwyn, Cydfin, Cedfin, Cydwin or Tŷ Mawr should be read alongside the `GHF-E173 complete reference index <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/ghf-e173-cedwyn-cwm-cedwyn-place-name-notes/>`__ and, where relevant, the `Llandough (Penarth) pre-1877 manorial evidence page <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/anomalies-register/llandough-penarth-cydfin-ty-mawr-manorial-evidence/>`__. Name similarity is a research lead, not proof of identical location, boundaries, ownership or title.
