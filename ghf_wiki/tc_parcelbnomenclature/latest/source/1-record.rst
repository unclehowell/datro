Parcel B nomenclature
---------------------

- **Parcel B** — the Wiki’s primary analytical label for the eastern agricultural parcel.
- **eastern fields** — descriptive shorthand for Parcel B.
- **fields to the east** — descriptive locator used in the Wiki’s reconstruction.
- **eastern agricultural parcel** — descriptive category, not a proven historical title.

Identity boundary
-----------------

Parcel B is not the same thing as the farmhouse or Parcel A. It is the eastern agricultural component of the two-parcel reconstruction. Later use of “whole farm” must not erase this distinction where the evidence shows separate parcels, tenures or titles.
