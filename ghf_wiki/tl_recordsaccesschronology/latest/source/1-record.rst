This page records the family’s requests for records about `Great House Farm <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/great-house-farm/>`__ from November 2025 to September 2026, and how each public body responded. It is drawn from the family’s own email correspondence. Officials are identified by role, not by name. Dates are when each email was sent or received.

It shows what it has taken to recover the evidence now in the `Evidence Library <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/>`__: requests refused as “not held”, records found but withheld as “out of scope”, requests renumbered or proposed for closure, official email addresses that bounce, and files said to be “most likely destroyed” with no record of destruction. :sup:`🕒`

-----------

   :header-rows: 1

     - What was asked for
     - Result so far
   * - Amgueddfa Cymru – Museum Wales
     - Records of the 19th-century find in the farmhouse; farm contents; everything indexed to the farm
     - Two refusals (“not held”), both upheld on review. Records it had already found were released only after a new request number, 57 days after the first request. The third request (FOI 2026-036) was answered on 5 October 2026 with the Church Farm records, accession 63.24 (`GHF-E106-R33 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/records-access-chronology/foi-reply-register/ghf-e106-r33-amgueddfa-cymru-reply-further-collections-search-following-8-september-2026-foi/>`__; evidence `A1 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e106-r33-a1-1931-letter-museum-wales-file-63-24/>`__, `A2 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e106-r33-a2-museum-wales-63-24-1-rowel-spur/>`__, `A3 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e106-r33-a3-museum-wales-63-24-2-spearhead/>`__). :sup:`🕒`
   * - Cadw / Welsh Government
     - 1988 listing file and correspondence
     - Two 1988 photographs released. The rest “most likely destroyed”, but no destruction record exists. Emails to Cadw’s general addresses failed to deliver at least eight times. Formal complaint in progress.
   * - HM Land Registry
     - First-registration files; correction of the register
     - Both complaint stages refused. Schedule of 43 file documents released after six months. The 1969 root deed is not on the file.
   * - Vale of Glamorgan Council
     - Planning, heritage and council records
     - Partial disclosure. Three planning appendices missing. Several requests and a complaint still open. :sup:`🕒`
   * - Royal Commission (RCAHMW)
     - Archive and accession records
     - Holds archive items but no accession data. A record for the site filed under the wrong place. Searches delayed by staff shortages.
   * - Other archives and courts
     - Deeds, court files, minutes, case files
     - Mixed. Some bodies were helpful. Others redirected the request elsewhere, or treated a court-records request as a personal data request.
