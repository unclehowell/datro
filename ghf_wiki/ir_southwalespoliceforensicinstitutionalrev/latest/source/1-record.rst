Forensic Assessment — South Wales Police
----------------------------------------

**Scope:** policing records connected with the eviction/demolition episode and later reporting; not civil title adjudication.

Current evidence
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The project has a confirmed negative FOI outcome under reference 719/26. That is a search result, not proof that no historic operational record ever existed. :sup:`🕒10-10-2026`

Forensic control test
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Identify the specific incident, operational record class, command/force area, retention rule and any transferred or destroyed records. Separate police operational decisions from the civil court authority under which possession was enforced.

Current finding
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish policing misconduct. This is not a finding that the historic police role or every operational act has been established or excluded.** The current work concerns historical-record recovery and the evidential status of reports/footage about the 1988 episode.

Human action
~~~~~~~~~~~~

Seek the search methodology and retention/disposal explanation behind FOI 719/26, and cross-check any surviving court warrant, press footage or contemporaneous account before alleging operational excess.

**Acceptance:** a defensible police-record search/retention trail and any relevant incident references are logged.

**Cross-institution records route:** the NPCC’s September–October 2026 correspondence searched accessible national/ACPO holdings and referred the requester toward individual police forces; it does not substitute for this force’s local operational records search. Compare the `NPCC forensic assessment <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/scrutiny-and-accountability/government-public-authority-involvement/npcc-forensic-institutional-review/>`__, especially `GHF-E106-R49 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e106-r49-npcc-foi-3156-2026-response-16-september-2026/>`__ and `GHF-E106-R51 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence-library/ghf-e106-r51-npcc-foi-3065-2026-search-disclosure-response-9-october-2026/>`__, with South Wales Police’s own `FOI 719/26 record (R38) <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/records-access-chronology/foi-reply-register/#R38>`__ and the linked press evidence below. A national nil return and a local nil return are separate searches.
