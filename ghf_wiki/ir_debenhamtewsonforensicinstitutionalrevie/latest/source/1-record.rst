Forensic Assessment — Debenham Tewson
-------------------------------------

**Scope:** the historical professional firm as surveyor/estate agent/valuer/auctioneer where the case record places it in a relevant transaction. Legal responsibility is not inferred from the firm’s professional category.

New contextual finding
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Contemporary records establish that Debenham Tewson firms operated as surveyors, estate agents, valuers and auctioneers. That makes the firm’s exact role in any Great House Farm valuation, sale, plan or auction material a concrete identity question rather than a generic assumption.

Forensic control test
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Identify the precise entity, date, client and document. For any Great House Farm material, determine whether the firm prepared sale particulars, valuation, plan, auction instructions or other representation about the property extent.

Current finding
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish wrongdoing by the actor under review. This is a source-limited assessment, not a timeless finding of non-involvement.** The evidential value is potentially high if a surviving professional plan or valuation records the farmhouse/fields distinction or the party from whom title was said to derive.

Human action
~~~~~~~~~~~~

Search Glamorgan Archives, The National Archives and relevant professional records for Great House Farm/Tŷ Mawr/Llandough, the 1938 sale, 1924 sale and associated plans. Capture the exact entity name and professional capacity.
