Pure section-12 refusals without a paid alternative are excluded.

2. Shopping list (collapsed)
----------------------------

Expand each item for the short description, fee type, amount where known, and status. This is the working “shopping list” of paywalls encountered while recovering the documentary record.

**HM Land Registry — official copies of title register and title plan — 18–19 September 2026**

**Institution:** HM Land Registry (GOV.UK “Search for land and property information” service)

**What was obtained:** Title register and title plan downloads for titles relevant to Great House Farm / Church View Close / woodland (including WA series titles).

**Paywall:** Standard statutory fee of **£14.00 per official copy** (title register + title plan). Confirmed by GOV.UK Pay receipts of 18 and 19 September 2026 (references 1000010749920, 1000010752498, 1000010752707).

**Status:** Paid. Official copies obtained and held by the family.

**Glamorgan Archives — digital copying service quote for ACA/8/2 — 7 September 2026 (ref 2026–3941)**

**Institution:** Glamorgan Archives (Cardiff Council)

**What was requested:** Digital copy of Funds ledger ACA/8/2 (1966–1978).

**Paywall:** Standard copying tariff quoted: single image £15.60; subsequent images £4.80; multiple images from multiple documents £60 per hour. For this item: 106 pages / 53 images = **£60** (one hour).

**Status:** Quote issued; payment form attached. Not yet commissioned as at 12 Oct 2026 (family reply noted the quote and deferred).

**Glamorgan Archives — multiple research/copy quotes in detailed reply — 24 September 2026 (ref 2026/4097)**

**Institution:** Glamorgan Archives

**What was offered (paid routes):**

- Rate-book search (UC/73/1–5 etc.) for ownership/tenancy evidence around 1880: **£50** including copies of relevant entries.
- Planning Committee minutes/reports search (DCVG/C/4/36/76-77, /94 etc.) for 89/01396/OUT and 92/00671/RES: **£50** including copies.
- DB/E/1–2 Stewart survey / terrier pages for Ty Mawr: two images = **£17**.
- UDPE/S/1/68 plans: five images = **£20**.
- D696 Western Ground Rents plans/rentals search: **£50**.

Prices stated without VAT because of the enquirer’s location in Patagonia. Digital photography order form supplied.

**Status:** Quotes issued. None of these specific paid searches commissioned as at 12 Oct 2026.

**RCAHMW — chargeable aerial-photograph searches & further queries — 29 September 2026 (R01 / RC26-0375)**

**Institution:** Royal Commission on the Ancient and Historical Monuments of Wales (RCAHMW)

**What was stated:** Aerial-photograph searches would be chargeable; further queries would be handled as public enquiries and may be charged. Price list (LES16 Rhestr Brisiau 2026) and order form attached to the reply.

**Paywall type:** Standard commercial search/reproduction fees for non-core enquiries.

**Status:** Noted; no paid search placed as at 12 Oct 2026. Supporting price list published on the R01 evidence page.

**TNA — Valuation Office Survey map IR 131/4/96 (OS Glamorgan XLVII.6) — 12 October 2026**

**Institution:** The National Archives (FOI Centre)

**What was requested:** Location-only FOI for the Valuation Office Survey 1910 map covering Great House Farm / Llandough-juxta-Penarth (sheet Glamorgan XLVII.6).

**Paywall:** TNA identified the piece (IR 131/4/96) but stated that access options are: (1) travel to Kew and examine the map in person, (2) request a **paid search by TNA staff**, or (3) hire an independent researcher. Explicit reference to FOIA section 9 fees and the Public Records Act fees regulations.

**Note:** The ordinary (non-annotated) OS 25-inch sheets for the same area are free on the National Library of Scotland. This paywall applies only to the Valuation Office annotated version.

**Status:** Open — unpaid. No paid search commissioned as at 12 Oct 2026.
