Forensic Assessment — Welsh Government
--------------------------------------

**Scope:** Welsh Government/Cadw current oversight, records and remedial handling. Historic decisions are attributed only to the body and date actually responsible at the time.

Live matters
~~~~~~~~~~~~

The current programme includes Welsh Government records/complaints concerning the Great House Farm heritage and public-authority history, including the live Cadw/Welsh Government information requests already logged by the family. The Government should be assessed separately from Cadw’s historic professional decisions.

Forensic control test
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

For every live matter identify the exact department/service, decision date, statutory or administrative function, evidence considered, records searched, response reason and available review/escalation route.

Current finding
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish Welsh Government-wide wrongdoing. This does not conclude that every specific decision, record or referral issue is resolved.** The active issue is whether current officials adequately respond to newly recovered evidence and identify competent remedies within their remit.

Human action
~~~~~~~~~~~~

Track Wales Office/Welsh Government correspondence to substantive outcome, including IR/26/16 where applicable. For Cadw, obtain and analyse the outstanding ATISN material and present a tightly framed remedial request based on the evidence already established.

**Acceptance:** each live Welsh Government/Cadw matter has a response, statutory basis, next route and deadline.
