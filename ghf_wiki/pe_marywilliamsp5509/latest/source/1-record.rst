Mary (Nancy) Williams (married name Mrs Mary Doreen Buckler) was born at Great House Farm in 1913 and lived there until her death in March 1983. This page lists only her own recorded statements: her witness statement, her court pleading and the newspaper reports that quote her. See also her biography page `Mary (Nancy) Williams <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/mary-williams/>`__.
