Recorded counter-findings (16 October 2026)
-------------------------------------------

**1. 1939 agreement (DBDT123/7)**

The photo on the wiki shows its typed terms: a monthly let at 8s, kept “as meadow or pasture”, signed by John Williams with WGR as landlord [`EV-0023 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0023-1939-wgr-john-williams-tenancy-plan/>`__]. TNA’s DBDT catalogue files it among small Llandough plot lets, not farm tenancies. It therefore reads as a separate grazing let, not a revised farm tenancy. This undercuts the wiki’s previous “closed” finding and the 1928 last-rent limitation point that treated it as the farm tenancy. Page 1 (parcels and acreage) is not online [`EV-0023 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0023-1939-wgr-john-williams-tenancy-plan/>`__]. If the family holds it, the reading could change.

**2. 1987 third argument**

In 1987 William’s own side argued that the farmhouse came under its own May 1975 conveyance [PENDING: `EV-0052 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0052-bp-pension-trust-bp-properties-conveyance/>`__]. That was his “third argument”. The family’s lawyers therefore knew of the separate conveyance at the time of the 1987 litigation. This weakens any later argument that the 1983 registration could not have been discovered.
