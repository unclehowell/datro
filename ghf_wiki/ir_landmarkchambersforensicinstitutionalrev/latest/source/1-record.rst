Forensic Assessment — Landmark Chambers
---------------------------------------

**Scope:** legal professional role only.

Role
~~~~

Landmark Chambers is relevant to the present project as a potential source of specialist public/land-law expertise, not as a historic actor in the Great House Farm events unless a concrete record establishes otherwise.

Forensic control test
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Do not treat present legal research or advice as evidence of historic conduct. A chambers’ role begins only when counsel is formally instructed and a defined opinion is commissioned.

Current finding
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish historic involvement or wrongdoing by this actor. This is not a universal finding of non-involvement; it reflects the sources identified on this page.**

Human action
~~~~~~~~~~~~

Approach specialist counsel only with the controlled solicitor brief and route-by-route questions. Require written scope, conflicts check, estimated costs and a defined deliverable before instruction.

**Acceptance:** counsel instruction either completed with scope/cost or declined for a recorded reason.
