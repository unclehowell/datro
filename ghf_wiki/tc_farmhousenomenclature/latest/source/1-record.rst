Farmhouse nomenclature
----------------------

- **farmhouse** — normal modern functional term for the principal dwelling.
- **farm house** — spaced form appearing in historical/public material.
- **the house** — contextual shorthand where the surrounding citation makes clear that the Great House farmhouse is meant.
- **principal dwelling** — descriptive category used here for clarity; not presented as a historical property name.

Scope
-----

This page does **not** make “farmhouse” a synonym for the whole farm or for Parcel A. The farmhouse is the building. Parcel A is the full 1877 house/quarrying estate: house/buildings, barn, stable and yard west of the house, garden, north forest, and south-east ground later registered as VG41. Parcel B is the separate eastern agricultural fields. Mary’s later defence of the occupied house/buildings/garden group was not the full geographical extent of Parcel A. On the title case, resting on Mary (Nancy) Williams’s c.1974 witness statement (`Mary (Nancy) Williams testimony <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/testimony/mary-williams/>`__), the 1877 arrangement took effect in 1928.
