**Daniel Thomas** and **Alfred Thomas** (Daniel Thomas & Son) are associated with limestone quarrying at Llandough. `People’s Collection Wales <https://www.peoplescollection.wales/items/1888101>`__ records that Daniel began the Llandough limeworks in 1851 and that Alfred continued them. Separately, `Mary (Nancy) Williams’s statement <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/mary-williams-statement/#:~:text=1877%3A%20The%20terms%20of%20the%20Thomas%20tenancy>`__ identifies Daniel as the buyer of the Great House Farm house parcel from the `Bute Estate <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/bute-estate/>`__ in 1877 and describes a tenancy under which the freehold was to pass to her grandfather when quarrying ceased. The underlying 1877 instrument/plan has not been located, so the legal character and extent of that transaction remain unresolved. Mary’s account names Alfred as the recipient of the last rent in 1928; the original receipt has not been identified.

Parcel AThe Thomas land is the root of the family’s claim to `Parcel A <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/the-two-parcels/#parcel-a>`__.

**Independent context, not title proof:** `People’s Collection Wales’ Alfred Thomas entry <https://www.peoplescollection.wales/items/1888101>`__ says Daniel Thomas started the limeworks at Llandough in 1851 and that Alfred continued them. This supports the Thomas family’s connection to the Llandough works; it does not establish ownership or boundaries for the separate, uninspected “33 acres” D153 lead. It is also a separate line from William Evans Williams of Pwll-y-Pant, Caerphilly (`identity distinction <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/llandough-limeworks/#identity-distinction>`__).

Events
------

- 1876 — limeworks on part of the farm (`Llandough Limeworks <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/llandough-limeworks/>`__)
- 1877 — Mary (Nancy) Williams’s statement reports that Daniel Thomas bought the farmhouse/buildings and about 10 acres from Bute and let them to her grandfather; the original instrument and plan have not been located, and sale versus quarrying arrangement remains to be tested (`details <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1877-agreement/#sale-1877>`__)
- 1924 — HM Land Registry holds a record of 15 May 1924 (a Bute–Corbett agreement about a drain). Whether it is connected to the Parcel A freehold is one of the questions the family is pressing HM Land Registry to answer (`details <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/land-registry-titles/#deeds-before-registration>`__ · `Legal Review, section 2 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/scrutiny-and-accountability/legal-review-fraud-land-human-rights/#land>`__)
- 1928 — Mary (Nancy) Williams’s account reports the removal of quarry machinery and a final rent of about £4 paid to Alfred Thomas; the original receipt and precise land description have not been located (`details <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1877-agreement/#takes-effect>`__)
- 1950 – 1952 — the family’s copy of the Thomas agreement taken from the farmhouse (`details <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1877-agreement/#papers-taken>`__)
- 1984 — a copy of the Bute–Thomas deed and its index card reported missing from Cardiff Library, the year BP served its writ (`details <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/1877-agreement/#library-1984>`__)
