**ASSESSMENT ISOLATION CONTROL — Cadw**

**Scope:** This page assesses **only Cadw**. Other institutions, companies, courts, professional firms or individuals may appear solely as counterparties, sources, witnesses, recipients, predecessors/successors or matters of chronology. Their own conduct, duties, decisions and potential responsibility are not assessed here; they belong on their separate actor-specific review.

**Accountability test:** identify the precise legal/entity identity; the named individual(s) or responsible role(s) where the evidence permits; the specific act, omission, order, instruction or representation; the source of legal or administrative authority and any delegation; who requested or authorised the action; what checks, objections, notice, appeal or review safeguards existed; what record shows the reasons; what happened afterwards; and what evidence links the conduct to the asserted consequence.

**Evidence discipline:** documented involvement is not itself proof of wrongdoing. Potential responsibility must be separated from established fact, inference and allegation. A missing record is not proof of its contents. Where the responsible individual, authorising person or source of authority is presently unknown, that uncertainty is recorded as an explicit identification/recovery task rather than guessed. :sup:`🕒10-10-2026`

**Cross-institution heritage/archaeology route:** Cadw’s listing records and GGAT’s archaeological advice are separate institutional streams. Compare the `GGAT source-linked chronology <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/scrutiny-and-accountability/government-public-authority-involvement/ggat-forensic-review-and-evidence-schedule-v4-0-3/>`__ and the `Welsh Government institutional assessment <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/scrutiny-and-accountability/government-public-authority-involvement/welsh-government-forensic-institutional-review/>`__; do not infer that one body authored or held another body’s records without a source identifying the transfer or joint action.
