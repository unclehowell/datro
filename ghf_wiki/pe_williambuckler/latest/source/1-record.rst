William “Billy” Buckler, son of Mary (Nancy) Williams, formerly Buckler, and Frederick Buckler, was the defendant in BP Properties Ltd v Buckler and lived at Great House Farm until the eviction of 1988. This page lists only his own recorded words and the court and Commission records of his case.
