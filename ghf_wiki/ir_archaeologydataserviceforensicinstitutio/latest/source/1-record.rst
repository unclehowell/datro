Forensic Assessment — Archaeology Data Service
----------------------------------------------

**Scope:** ADS is assessed only as an archival/data-access actor.

Role
~~~~

ADS may provide discoverability and access to archaeological reports or datasets relating to Great House Farm / Llandough. It is not a title adjudicator, planning authority or heritage decision-maker.

Forensic control test
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Determine which Great House Farm datasets/reports are deposited, their depositor, deposit date, metadata, accession identifiers, file versions and stated provenance. The key question is whether ADS preserves an independent trail showing what archaeological work was commissioned, reported and deposited.

Current finding
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish wrongdoing by the Archaeology Data Service. Repository results are limited to the date, scope and holdings searched.** Any absence from ADS is only a finding about that repository’s present searchable holdings unless the deposit/retention trail proves more.

Human action
~~~~~~~~~~~~

Request a targeted search for Great House Farm, Tŷ Mawr, Llandough and relevant project identifiers, asking for deposit metadata and file lineage rather than a general historical narrative.

**Acceptance:** a documented ADS search result with accession/deposit metadata and copies/links for every relevant item.
