**Quotation (verbatim):** 6 May 1897: “Proceeded to Lavernock to see mast … Fixed a wire atop the 107 ft pole”. 7 May: Flat Holm “110 ft pole”; “Slept at a small house owned by the person in charge of the Cremation House”. 15 May: apparatus “leaving it at Penarth”.

**Fits with:** A day-by-day record of the Lavernock work by Marconi’s assistant; it fixes where the apparatus and the team were on given days.

**Cuts against:** The extracts give the pole as 107 ft, not 120 ft, and mention Penarth and Flat Holm but not Great House Farm or a Mr Williams. They do not support the farm’s link to the 1897 work; nor do they rule it out (extracts only, c.1930 expanded version).
