**Quotation:** “Bail change for evicted farmer. EVICTED South Wales farmer Billy Buckler, whose Llandough house was demolished late last year, has had bail conditions changed on charges of assault, criminal damage and wanton or furious driving. Buckler, aged 40 […]”

**Proves:** South Wales Echo, 6 January 1989, reported Billy Buckler, aged 40, whose Llandough house was demolished, had bail changed on assault, criminal damage and wanton or furious driving charges.

**Does not prove:** Does not show byline, court, new bail conditions, or text before/after snippet; Does not establish outcome of charges; Is a press report, not a court record.
