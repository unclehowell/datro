**Quotation:** But after a hearing in chambers a judge at Cardiff Crown Court dismissed an application by Mr and Mrs Buckler for an injunction to restrain BP Properties Ltd, the owners of the land, from destroying or damaging the fabric of the building.

**Proves:** Western Mail reported, 6 Dec 1988, a judge dismissed the Bucklers’ injunction application against BP Properties Ltd; Cadw said the house lacked sufficient architectural merit, land lacked archaeological interest.

**Does not prove:** Journalist’s summary, not the judgment; Court identity disputed (Crown Court vs High Court per N11); Reported age (800 years) and four generations of occupation unconfirmed.
