**Catalogue entry (verbatim):** “DBDT129/3 Agreement (counterpart) for tenancy of Llandough Farm in the parish of Penarth, between Western Ground Rents Limited and Messrs David and Thomas Thomas [1 file, including plan] Aug 1940”.

**Same catalogue, related entry (verbatim):** “DBDT123/7 Agreement (duplicate) for tenancy of piece or parcel of land situate at Llandough between Western Ground Rents Limited and John Williams [1 file, including plan] May 1939”. This is already catalogued as `EV-0023 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0023-1939-wgr-john-williams-tenancy-plan/>`__.

**Why it matters:** In 1940 Llandough Farm was let to tenants named Thomas, not Williams. It is the separate farm, and the plan may show its boundary against Great House Farm (not yet seen).

**Proves:** The Glamorgan Archives catalogue records a 1940 WGR tenancy of Llandough Farm to David and Thomas Thomas.

**Does not prove:** The extent of either farm, until the document and plan are seen.
