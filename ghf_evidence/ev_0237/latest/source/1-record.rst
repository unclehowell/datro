**Catalogue description (verbatim):** “Papers relating to Welsh anti-fluoridation campaigners … Newspaper cuttings, 1981-1982; Correspondence with various anti-fluoridation supporters and campaigners, 1972-1982; Transcript from an Isle of Anglesey Borough Council meeting …”.

**Why it is listed:** per Sion Buckler (voice note, 19 Oct 2026): “Mary Williams was doing something to do with a guy with fluoride and they were doing political stuff about government overreach.” If she wrote to the national campaign in 1972–82, this is where a letter would be (inference).

**Cuts against:** Cardiff and the Vale were never fluoridated; in 1987 the only fluoridated areas in Wales were in Gwynedd and Powys (`Hansard, 23 Mar 1987 <https://api.parliament.uk/historic-hansard/commons/1987/mar/23/fluoridation>`__). No document found on the wiki, the family sites or in correspondence mentions fluoride.

**Does not prove:** Any link between Mary (Nancy) Williams and a fluoride campaign.
