**Quotation:** “who lived at Great House Farm, Llan-dough, near Cardiff, said: ‘All I’ve got left are the clothes [I] stand up in.’ Mr Buckler, evicted from the farm house by BP Properties, who demolished the building a week later, said”

**Proves:** South Wales Echo search result (13 Dec 1988) reports Mr Buckler saying he had only the clothes he stood up in, evicted and house demolished a week later by BP Properties.

**Does not prove:** Does not show headline, byline or surrounding text; No exact date is given beyond 13 Dec 1988; It reports his statement only, not an inventory or valuation of losses.
