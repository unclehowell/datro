**Related (19 Oct 2026):** the Commons Registration (Glamorgan) Act 1983 covers the register of common land only and does not mention village greens, so it did not validate or redraw VG41 (`EV-0232 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0232-commons-registration-glamorgan-act-1983/>`__).

**Proves:** Not held, so nothing yet. Reported: village green VG41 was registered on 16 June 1970; The Vale’s registers, compiled 1967–70, record claims to ownership.

**Does not prove:** Does not establish who claimed ownership, evidence or objections, exact area/map, any Commissioner decision, or vesting; Register entry alone doesn’t prove fee-simple ownership.
