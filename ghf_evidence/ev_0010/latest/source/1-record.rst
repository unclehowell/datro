**Quotation:** “Robert Williams, a mason’s labourer, was on Tuesday charged … with stealing three geese, the property of Mr. John Williams, Great House Farm, Llandough, on November 11, 1891.”

**Proves:** Newspaper reported Robert Williams charged with stealing three geese from Mr John Williams, Great House Farm, Llandough, on 11 November 1891; Remanded to Penarth.

**Does not prove:** Does not identify which John Williams, establish land ownership or tenancy terms, or specify the newspaper’s title, issue date, or page.
