**Quotation:** “ALL THAT piece or parcel of land situate at Llandough in the Urban District of Penarth in the County of Glamorgan and containing in area Three Point Three Five Two acres or thereabouts and forming part of Enclosure 106 on the Ordnance Survey Map Glamorgan Sheet XLVII.6 (1941 Edition)”.

**Proves:** Western Ground Rents Ltd conveyed this land, “with other property”, to Lakeside and Llandough Development Co Ltd on 5 Mar 1962 and took a £75,000 legal charge over it the same day; Glamorgan County Council bought 3.352 acres (part OS 106) for £16,800 on 11 Jun 1965, WGR releasing it as mortgagee; WGR then held other land within 100 yards of the site (First Schedule covenant).

**Does not prove:** Anything about the Great House farmhouse, the north woods or the south-east green — none is described; what the 1962 “other property” was (the 1962 conveyance plan is needed); WGR’s title before 1962 (see EV-0216).

**Cuts against the family’s case:** The site adjoins OS 57 “Llandough Farm” (1.203a), the separate farmstead south of St Dochdwy’s Church, about 320 m SSE of the Great House farmhouse (approximate, measured from the plan). The deed shows WGR dealing openly as owner of Llandough land (1948 Barry wayleave; 1962–65 sale and charge) with a public body at arm’s length.

Recitals and terms (transcript)
-------------------------------

- (1) Conveyance 5 Mar 1962, WGR (“the Company”) → Lakeside (“the Vendor”), of this land “with other property”; WGR reserved a right of way A–B (coloured green); Lakeside’s restrictive covenants at para 3.
- (2) Legal Charge 5 Mar 1962, Lakeside → WGR, securing £75,000 and interest.
- (3) Deed of Modification of covenants, 3 May 1965, WGR–Lakeside.
- (4) Wayleave Agreement, 21 Oct 1948, WGR and the Mayor, Aldermen and Burgesses of the Borough of Barry, £1 a year (Third Schedule).
- (5) Price £16,800. (6) WGR satisfied the rest of the charged land is sufficient security.
- Reservations: drainage near point D; Council to pay half the road cost E–D at £24 4s 1d per lineal yard; fencing.
- First Schedule: covenant “FOR the benefit of the property of the Company situate within a radius of 100 yards …”, with an educational / local-government use proviso.
- Plan L53: “GLAMORGAN COUNTY COUNCIL / LLANDOUGH / PROPOSED P.S. SITE”, 1/2500, 2-5-62, County Architect; signed for WGR by a director and the secretary. Neighbours: 57 “Llandough Farm” (1.203); wooded OS 54 (2.594) east and OS 38 (5.057) north-east.
