**Quotation:** All the materials from the excavations at Cae Castell, Rumney, Cardiff, have been destroyed and so have those from the Roman building at The Knap, Barry, from the Roman Villa site at Llandough and from Barry Village.

**Proves:** South Wales Echo (18 March 1983) reports a fire at the Trust’s Swansea warehouse destroyed excavation finds, including Llandough Roman villa material; About 90% unrecoverable.

**Does not prove:** Does not mention Great House Farm or show any Great House Farm record was in the warehouse; Trust reported most excavation records were salvaged.
