**Quotation:** […] farmer freed. ‘I will fight on to the death.’ FARMER BILLY Buckler vowed to continue his fight over the loss of his historic family home and more than 70 acres of land […]

**Proves:** The South Wales Echo (24 March 1989), under a headline fragment ‘farmer freed,’ reported Buckler vowed to continue his fight over loss of his family home and land.

**Does not prove:** Only a snippet is shown; The full headline, byline, and surrounding text are not visible, and the ’70 acres’ figure is not confirmed by this record.
