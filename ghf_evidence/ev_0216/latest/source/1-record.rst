**Quotation:** “F. Title shall be deduced, and shall commence, as follows:- with a Conveyance on sale dated 25th December 1938”.

**Proves:** A conveyance on sale dated 25 December 1938 was the root of title offered for this Llandough land in 1965.

**Does not prove:** Its parties, its parcels, or whether it included or excluded the Great House farmhouse and land (Parcel A); any irregularity. 25 December is the Christmas quarter day, and press reports put completion of the Bute/Mountjoy–WGR sale on 23 Dec 1938: an ordinary conveyancing date.
