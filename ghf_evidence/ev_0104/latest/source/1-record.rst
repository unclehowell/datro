**Quotation:** “Her husband’s family have been battling for more than 30 years to prove they owned the historic farmhouse at Llandough. That fight came to an end yesterday afternoon in the High Court in Cardiff and within hours owners BP Properties”

**Proves:** Shows the South Wales Echo of 6 December 1988 reported a 30-year family fight over the Llandough farmhouse ending in the High Court, Cardiff, with BP Properties acting within hours.

**Does not prove:** Snippet only; Headline and byline absent; Does not establish ownership or BP Properties’ actions; Names Cardiff High Court, differing from N10’s Cardiff Crown Court.
