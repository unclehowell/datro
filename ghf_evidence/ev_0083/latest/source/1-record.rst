**Quotation:** “The Buckler family have lived here at Great House Farm for more than four centuries.”

**Proves:** A TV news report stated the High Court in Cardiff granted an injunction preventing BP demolishing Great House Farm after eviction, reported BP’s 1969 freehold claim, and showed Branwen.

**Does not prove:** It does not identify or authorise the men shown (‘SAS style’ is not identification), confirm editorial intent, give exact broadcast date, or verify link to NLW item 507738; Captions machine-generated.
