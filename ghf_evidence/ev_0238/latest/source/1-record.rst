**Quotations:** Weekly Mail, 28 Nov 1903: “John Williams … son of Mr. William Williams, of Llandough Farm”. Barry Dock News, 3 Apr 1914: “Mr. Jenkin Morgan Williams, Llandough Farm, Llandough”.

**Note:** the father’s name in 1903 does not fit the Great House Williams line. Llandough Farm was the Rees family’s in 1887–1910. These may be a different Williams family (inference).

**Proves:** Two press mentions of Williams men “of Llandough Farm”.

**Does not prove:** A connection to Great House Farm or to the Williams family of Great House.
