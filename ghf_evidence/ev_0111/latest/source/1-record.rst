**Quotation:** I WAS concerned to read the report headlined Bulldozed Before Breakfast (Echo, December 6). How awful for the unfortunate Buckler family

**Proves:** Shows South Wales Echo, 21 December 1988, carried a reader’s letter responding to an Echo report “Bulldozed Before Breakfast” (6 December), with a photograph captioned Branwen Buckler near the remains of the farm house with children Amy, Hywel and Rhys.

**Does not prove:** Does not show the writer’s name or full letter text; Does not establish whether the 6 December report matches N15-07 or N11; Does not resolve whether buildings remained standing.
