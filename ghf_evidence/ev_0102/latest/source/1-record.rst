**Quotation:** “WHEN the bailiffs refused to let South Wales farmer Billy Buckler back into his family home they found eight other people sharing the rambling old farmhouse.”

**Proves:** South Wales Echo (3 Dec 1988) reported eight non-family residents found at Great House Farm when bailiffs refused to readmit Billy Buckler; Some had rent paid by the council.

**Does not prove:** Evidence of what was reported, not proof of truth; Does not show living terms, nature of allegations, title details, or confirm Chris Thompson’s identity with N07’s Christopher Thompson.
