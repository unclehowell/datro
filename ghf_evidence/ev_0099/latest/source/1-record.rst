**Proves:** A snippet of the South Wales Echo of 2 December 1988 reported a summons against BP to be heard Monday, a condition allowed by Mr Justice Anthony Evans letting Mrs Buckler collect belongings with BP solicitors’ agreement, and that the Buckler family had petitioned.

**Does not prove:** Does not show headline, byline, object of the petition, or surrounding text; Does not state the judge’s full order beyond the belongings condition; Does not resolve the Western Mail’s naming of Judge Norman Francis at the 5 December hearing.
