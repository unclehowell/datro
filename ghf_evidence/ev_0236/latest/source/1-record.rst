**Quotation (OCR):** “Marquess of Bute. — Supplies Penarth U.D. (part). Source of Supply (Nature and Sufficiency). — Spring from Lias limestone, Llandough. Yield not known. Works. — No filtration. No reservoir. Quantity of Water supplied. — Generally adequate. Quality of Water. — Good, but hard.”

**Context:** the same Return lists Cardiff Town Council as supplying “Penarth U.D. (part)”. The farm was a Bute estate farm until the estate land passed to Western Ground Rents. If the farm drew on an estate spring rather than the public main, a landlord could cut it off (inference, unconfirmed).

**Proves:** In 1914 the Bute estate ran a private spring supply at Llandough serving part of Penarth Urban District.

**Does not prove:** That Great House Farm was on that supply, in 1914 or in 1948–58.
