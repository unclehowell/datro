**Proves:** Newspapers reported that the sale of the Bute Cardiff estate interests to Western Ground Rents was completed on 23 December 1938.

**Does not prove:** The reports are general. They do not mention Llandough or Tŷ Mawr, and do not show which parcels were included or on what terms.
