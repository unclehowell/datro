**Quotation:** “all his estates in the parishes of Leckwith, with Llandough, Michaelston-le-Pit, Cogan, Sully, Lavernock, Penarth and St Andrews”

**Proves:** Several newspapers reported in October 1900 that the 3rd Marquess’s will left his estates in the parish of Leckwith-with-Llandough to Lord Colum Crichton-Stuart.

**Does not prove:** A press summary, not the will or any conveyance; It does not show Tŷ Mawr passed under the gift or its later history; See WA231076 conveyance.
