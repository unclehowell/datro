**Proves:** Not held, so nothing yet. Reported: WA735527 register describes an agreement dated 15 May 1924 between John, Marquis of Bute and James Pelham Stuart Corbett relating to construction and maintenance of a drain, referenced with a plan as item 2 in title WA231076.

**Does not prove:** Does not prove a freehold transfer or boundary of Parcel A. Unresolved: which land it burdens, whether it connects to 1928, whether it shows Bute dealing with Parcel A. Unrelated to the separate 1924 Llandough (Cowbridge) estate sale.
