**Why this page exists:** Two Bute farms stood in Llandough: Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr) and Llandough Farm. Records for Llandough Farm are listed here so they are not mistaken for Great House Farm.

Records (verbatim where quoted)
-------------------------------

- 1841 tithe apportionment (National Library of Wales tithe records): Thomas Rees, tenant of Bute, about 141.5 acres; John Williams about 119.7 acres, yard beside the churchyard (`EV-0215 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0215-tithe-apportionment-john-williams-c1841/>`__). That Rees’s holding was Llandough Farm is an inference, supported by the press below.
- Cardiff Times, 17 Mar 1865: “Mr. Jenkin Rees, second son of Mr. Thomas Rees, Llandough Farm” (`article <https://newspapers.library.wales/view/3383485/3383491/31/>`__)
- Weekly Mail, 6 Nov 1886: Thomas Rees, aged 89, “removed … to Llandough Farm, which he rented from the Marquess of Bute, and which he held until his death” (`article <https://newspapers.library.wales/view/3365383/3365390/150/>`__)
- Evening Express, 22 Jun 1898: rick fire “at Llandough Farm, the property of Mr. Evan Rees” (`article <https://newspapers.library.wales/view/3277036/3277038/26/>`__)
- Evening Express, 1 Feb 1910: death “at Llandough Farm, Cardiff, Ann Rees” (`notice <https://newspapers.library.wales/view/4214190/4214193/141/>`__)

The OS 25-inch map (National Library of Scotland georeferenced layer, c.1890s–1900s; edition not confirmed) names “Ty-mawr” north-west of the church and “Llandough Farm” to the south-east (`map <https://maps.nls.uk/geo/explore/#zoom=17&lat=51.4545&lon=-3.1995>`__).

**Cuts against / caution:** Some newspapers call a John Williams “of Llandough Farm” (1895 and 1904: see `EV-0224 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0224-1881-census-llandough-rg11-5288/>`__ and `EV-0227 <https://research.bucklerfamily.estate/evidence/ev-0227-cardiff-times-1904-john-williams-tenancy-since-1855/>`__), so the press mixed the two names. Weekly Mail, 28 Nov 1903, “son of Mr. William Williams, of Llandough Farm”, and Barry Dock News, 3 Apr 1914, “Mr. Jenkin Morgan Williams, Llandough Farm”, are unexplained.

**Proves:** The Rees family held a farm called Llandough Farm, rented from the Bute estate, from at least 1865 to 1910, separately named from John Williams’s Great House farm in the directories.

**Does not prove:** Exact boundaries of either farm, or anything about title.
