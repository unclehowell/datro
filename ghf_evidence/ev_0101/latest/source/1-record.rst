**Quotation:** EVICTED farmer Bill Buckler, caught up in a 33-year wrangle over his South Wales home, was taken from his hospital bed to face charges of assault on two bailiffs.

**Proves:** Search extract shows South Wales Echo, 3 December 1988, reported Bill Buckler, evicted farmer in a 33-year dispute, taken from hospital bed to face assault charges on two bailiffs; Gives age 40.

**Does not prove:** Snippet only; Headline, byline, court, plea and outcome not visible; Charge not finding; Age 40 conflicts with same-date Echo report giving 39; Hospital stay length cut off.
