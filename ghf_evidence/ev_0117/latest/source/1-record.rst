**Quotation:** Councillors authorised legal action against BP Properties; the council’s letter of 13 Dec 1988 via Debenham Tewson had gone unanswered; an enforcement notice was being prepared (legal director Alan Oliver) — summary of the report.

**Proves:** By February 1989 Vale of Glamorgan Council was reported taking legal and enforcement action against BP Properties over the demolition, after an unanswered 13 Dec 1988 letter.

**Does not prove:** It is a press report. It does not show the outcome, and it cuts against any suggestion that the council and BP acted together.
