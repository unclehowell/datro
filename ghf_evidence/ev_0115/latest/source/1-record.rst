`Image: EV-0115 — Press cutting: Charge against evicted farmer dropped (Barry magistrates court report) <https://wayback.bucklerfamily.estate/images/evidence/1989-01-00_wp1206_press-cutting-charge-against-evicted-farmer-droppe.png>`__

**Quotation:** “AN ALLEGATION that South Wales farmer Billy Buckler used threatening words and behaviour at his former home last year was withdrawn by the Crown Prosecution Service at Barry magistrates court yesterday.”

**Proves:** Reports that CPS withdrew, as out of time, an allegation Buckler used threatening words in Llandough on 29 April; Notes pending Great House Farm charges on 3 February.

**Does not prove:** Does not state findings of fact, describe the behaviour charged, give outcome of 3 February charges, or identify newspaper/date; November demolition date conflicts with other reports.
