**Quotation:** SOUTH Wales farmer Billy Buckler, whose home was demolished last year after a 40-year-old row over ownership, now plans to live in an old bus which he is converting into a mobile home.

**Proves:** Shows the 1989 South Wales Echo reported Buckler’s family living with his sister after his home’s demolition, his plan to live in a bus, and his £30,000 possessions claim.

**Does not prove:** Does not verify the £30,000 claim or whether records were among items taken; ’40-year-old row’ is a journalist’s estimate; Exact date and rest of article unseen.
