**Quotation:** …the excavations at Llandough near Cardiff, where the substantial remains of a Roman Villa and part of a later medieval house were discovered.

**Proves:** By April 1980, GGAT’s assistant director Henry Owen-John lectured publicly on Llandough excavations, reporting substantial Roman villa remains and part of a later medieval house discovered there.

**Does not prove:** Does not mention Great House Farm; Identifies excavation as the separate Roman villa site near St Dochdwy’s church (HER GGAT00768s); Only an extract transcribed.
