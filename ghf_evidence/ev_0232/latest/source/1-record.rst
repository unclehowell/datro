**Quotation:** Long title: “An Act to make provision for the validation of the registers of common land …”. Section 2(1)(b) defines “the Glamorgan register” as the register of common land kept for the former Glamorgan County Council area.

**Finding:** The Act covers the register of common land only. It does not mention the register of town or village greens, so it did not validate or redraw village green VG41. Validation under the Act was unit by unit (s.7), after newspaper notices and a period for representations (ss.4–6).

**Negative search:** no London Gazette notice by South Glamorgan County Council under the Act was found for 1983–87. Sections 4–5 required local newspaper notices, not Gazette notices, so any notices will be in the local press (from Sep–Oct 1983).

**Proves:** The 1983 Act did not apply to the village green register.

**Does not prove:** Anything about VG41’s own registration; its finality still rests on the Commons Registration Act 1965 procedure.
