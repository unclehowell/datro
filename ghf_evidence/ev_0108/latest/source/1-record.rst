**Quotation:** […] a bid to stop BP Properties profiting from the demolition earlier this week [of] an 800-year-old farmhouse on the outskirts of Cardiff. A small group of Llan-dough villagers met local councillors last night to express their anger at the way BP had demolished […]

**Does not prove:** Does not show headline, byline, names, proposed steps, or outcome; Gives only relative dating (‘earlier this week’) for the demolition.
