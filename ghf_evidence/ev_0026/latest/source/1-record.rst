**Proves:** Not held, so nothing yet. Reported: Mary (Nancy) Williams states papers, including the farm agreement, were taken from the farmhouse blanket box by a lodger placed by Knapp in June 1950 and passed to Knapp; Title deed reported missing 1950–52; 1987 Court of Appeal recorded her title documents never produced.

**Does not prove:** Not established who removed the papers, where they went, or exact contents, including whether E120 was present. No link shown between Knapp and WGR or the Bute estate; Missing papers do not prove who took them.
