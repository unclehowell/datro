**Quotation:** the farmstead of Great House, or Ty Mawr, which still embodies some of its earlier features, serving as the manorhouse of Llandough.

**Proves:** A letter signed “William Rees, Penarth” published in the Western Mail opposes demolition of Great House Farm and calls it “the manorhouse of Llandough”.

**Does not prove:** Not a title record; Each step of the manorial chain needs separate primary sources. Says nothing of family tenure or the 1877 arrangement. Writer’s identity and exact year (1974 or 1977) unconfirmed.

File stored in the wayback archive: `EV-0051 in the artifact index <https://wayback.bucklerfamily.estate/artifacts/#EV-0051>`__.
