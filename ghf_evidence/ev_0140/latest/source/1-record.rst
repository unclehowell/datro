**Quotation:** found, along with six male skeletons, during the erection of new buildings “buried with a lot of shells, limpet or cockle”

**Proves:** Museum catalogues object 63.24/1, an incomplete 14th–15th century iron rowel spur, as a 1858 surface find at Llandough Church Farm (ST168733), found with six male skeletons.

**Does not prove:** Does not identify Llandough Church Farm as Great House Farm/Tŷ Mawr; Provenance appears to derive from the 1931 letter, adding no independent findspot evidence.
