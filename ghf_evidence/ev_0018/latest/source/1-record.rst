**Quotation:** This spear head was found a[t] Llandough Church Farm when digging foundations for some new buildings about 1858. The spur was also found. About 6 skeletons of big men were also found in same place, all buried with a lot of shells, limpet or cockel.

**Proves:** In 1931 an unnamed writer at Cogan Pill recorded a spearhead and spur found at Llandough Church Farm c. 1858, with about six skeletons found in the same place.

**Does not prove:** Written c. 73 years after the find by someone not claiming to be a witness; Does not name Great House Farm or Tŷ Mawr, nor link the objects to the burials.
