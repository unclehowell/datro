**Quotation (verbatim):** “Included as a little altered early C19 farmhouse having group value with the adjoining cottage”; “Interior not available at resurvey”.

**Fits with:** Shows the bar Cadw applied to a Vale farmhouse in the same year, and that resurvey records state whether the interior was seen. Branwen’s statement says the officers came inside (EV-0255); the 1988 resurvey record for Great House would show whether the interior was seen and recorded.

**Cuts against:** A different building in a different village; listing decisions turn on each building. Resurveys were often done from outside only, which is consistent with routine practice rather than anything specific to Great House.

**Notes:** Comparator only. Not evidence about Great House Farm by itself.
