**Quotation:** “For the purposes of the experiments, Mr. Williams (of the engineering department, Cardiff) fixed upon Lavernock Point a pole 120 feet high, with a zinc cylinder at the summit, 5ft. 6in. by 4ft., insulated from the Flat Holm and Brean Down.”

**Proves:** Contemporary report of Marconi wireless telegraphy trials between Lavernock Point and Flat Holm, witnessed by Post Office officials Preece, Gavey and Fardo, with Mr Williams erecting the 120-foot pole.
