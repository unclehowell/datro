**Establishes:** The 11 June 2026 Welsh Government response says Cadw inspectors visited and assessed the house for listing, that it did not meet the listing criteria, and that the only inspection record then held by Welsh Government was the two photographs. The official PDF includes a farmhouse photograph and a barn photograph, both dated 29 July 1988. `Read the official response and photographs (PDF) <https://www.gov.wales/sites/default/files/publications/2026-06/atisn27021.pdf>`__. **Does not establish:** that no related file survives elsewhere, the identity of every decision-maker, a full rationale beyond the brief response, or the specific transfer or destruction history of any associated record.

**Does not prove:** The disclosure does not establish that no file survives outside Welsh Government. It says other associated records may have been transferred to RCAHMW for permanent preservation or destroyed under the Public Records Act 1958, but does not identify specific records or confirm which route applied to each item.
