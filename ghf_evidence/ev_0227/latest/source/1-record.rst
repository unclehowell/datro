**Quotation:** “Amongst the Marquis of Bute’s guests at the rent audit dinner at Cardiff on Saturday was Mr John Williams, of Llandough Farm … He succeeded his father in the tenancy of the farm in 1855 … Saturday was his 102nd rent audit.”

**Note on the farm (inference):** The report says “Llandough Farm”, not Great House Farm. The Rees family held the separate Llandough Farm from 1841 to 1910 (EV-0228), while directories place John Williams at Great House farm from 1868 to 1901 (EV-0225). So this John Williams is probably the Great House John Williams, named loosely by the paper. That match is an inference, not stated in the report.

**Cuts against:** The paper names “Llandough Farm”. Weekly Mail, 28 Nov 1903, has “son of Mr. William Williams, of Llandough Farm” (`article <https://newspapers.library.wales/view/3376526/3376538/247/>`__), which is unexplained and may be a different Williams family.

**Proves:** A Bute estate tenant named John Williams, called “of Llandough Farm”, was reported in 1904 as having succeeded his father in the tenancy of his farm in 1855.

**Does not prove:** On its own, which farm; nor any title or rights beyond a tenancy.
