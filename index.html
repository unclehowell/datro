Concerns, documentary anomalies and unanswered requests are not findings of fraud, concealment, discrimination or official misconduct. Each issue requires a defined proposition, primary evidence and consideration of material contrary evidence.

Referral gate

GateRequired record
1. Evidence preservationOriginal file or certified copy, source, custodian, date obtained, file hash where appropriate and an unaltered preservation copy.
2. Issue definitionOne factual proposition per issue; primary source; contrary evidence; evidential gap; and explanation of why the matter is material.
3. Independent legal assessmentWritten view on jurisdiction, legal elements, limitation, available remedy and whether the evidence justifies referral.
4. ReferralSpecific act complained of and relevant dates; indexed evidence; previous disclosures and responses; any documented conflict; requested assessment, not a demanded outcome.
5. Decision and remedyAcknowledgement/reference, allocation decision, request for further evidence, findings or reasons provided, and review route.

Record each finding as substantiated, refuted, unresolved, insufficient evidence or not assessed, with reasons and source references. Non-response, resistance, association between organisations or knowledge of another party's position is not, by itself, proof of collusion.
