Source: Published on the wiki 12 Oct 2026 (page 2919). Original address: https://greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com/records-access-chronology/foi-reply-register/national-counter-terrorism-security-office-nactso-email-correspondence-catalogue/

Evidence type: Email correspondence (primary record). Institution: National Counter Terrorism Security Office (NaCTSO). Forensic assessment: NCTSO — Forensic Institutional Review. Register: FOI & Public-Authority Reply Register.

This page catalogues, word for word, every email exchanged between the Williams/Buckler family and this institution about Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough, grouped by request thread and in date order. Each entry shows the email first, labelled by type. Repeated quoted history and email logos are trimmed so each email appears once; nothing is redacted and nothing else is altered, including spelling.
