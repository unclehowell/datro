Williams / Buckler family
23 October 1908 ·

Barry Dock News, p.3, “Court Leet at Llandough”: the court leet and court baron of the lordship of Bute passed “a vote of sympathy … with the family of the late Mr. John Williams, Great House, Llandough, for many years a juror of the court”. Fits Mary (Nancy) Williams’s statement that her grandfather died in 1908; jury service says nothing about ownership (EV-0243)
