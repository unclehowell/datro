Williams / Buckler family
22 May 1980 ·

Ted Rowlands MP criticised in the House of Commons how the BP pension fund, as buyer of Western Ground Rents, was treating householders’ requests. The debate does not mention Great House Farm; it concerns the WGR–BP ownership chain.
