Friends 58

Everyone in the story, from landlords to judges. A “friend” here means a person or body that appears in the record; it says nothing about the relationship. Avatars are generic.

John Williams (d. 1908)

Tenant farmer at Great House; juror of the Bute court leet. Succeeded his father in the tenancy in 1855 (1904 press report; identification is an inference).

family

John Williams

Mary (Nancy) Williams’s father; took over both tenancies in 1908; yearly tenant of the whole farm from 1916; tenancy surrendered 1949.

Mary (Nancy) Williams

Lived at the farmhouse; married Frederick Buckler in 1936; pleaded ownership in 1974; witness statement c. 1974; died at the farmhouse 26 March 1983.

Frederick Buckler

Joined Mary (Nancy) Williams at the farmhouse on their marriage in 1936; ran the farm from 1944; died 1967.
