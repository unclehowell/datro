Frederick Buckler

Mary (Nancy) Williams announces the passing of Frederick Buckler, aged 58.

His death is registered in the March quarter of 1967 (GRO death index, East Glamorgan 8b 364). The Court of Appeal gives “some time in the period 1965-67”; an estate paper gives December 1965. All three are shown; none is chosen.

Source

GRO death index, March quarter 1967, East Glamorgan 8b 364 (FreeBMD transcription); BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2; Legal Wiki, “Two Mary Bucklers?” timeline.
