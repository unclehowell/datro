Intro

The family page of the Williams and Buckler family of Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough-juxta-Penarth. The family held the farmhouse until it was demolished in December 1988, after BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2.

  • 🏠 Lived at Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough
  • 📜 Earliest date on the record: 1667 (Mary (Nancy) Williams’s statement; written source awaited)
  • ⚖️ Court of Appeal: 31 July 1987
  • 🚜 Farmhouse demolished: 6 December 1988
  • 🔗 greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com

Williams / Buckler family
20 November 1958 ·

Western Mail, p.7 (snippet only, EV-0037): “Mrs. Buckler claimed that the water was cut off from her house and grounds for nearly 10 years by order of Western Ground Rents.” She told the planning committee: “This is a Welsh case of Crichel Down.” The water date conflicts with a c.1988 cut-off (SC-48). The Press Archive also lists, unverified, a Western Mail report, “It’s a Crichel Down, says woman farmer”, said to record Mrs Buckler alleging that councils and Western Ground Rents had “walked in and taken” the family’s land. The original article and exact wording have not been recovered; if authenticated, this would evidence a contemporary allegation, not prove ownership or unlawful taking. The reported reference to “Cardiff Rural Council” must be reconciled with the administrative correction in SC-22. Press Archive — unverified references
