About The Welsh archaeological trust that holds the Historic Environment Record for the area.

The Welsh archaeological trust that holds the Historic Environment Record for the area. Great House Farm is HER GGAT02038s (NPRN 20182 at the Royal Commission; NGR ST16707335). Amgueddfa Cymru holds accession 63.24, finds from “Church Farm”, 1858 (letter from Cogan Pill, 22 November 1931: finds “at Llandough Church Farm c. 1858”); the identity of that farm with Great House Farm is unresolved.
