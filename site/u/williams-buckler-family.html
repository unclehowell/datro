Amy Williams

Williams / Buckler family
29 April 1990 (probate calendar) or the week to 2 June 1990 (South Wales Echo) · Announcement ·

William (Billy) Buckler

The Williams / Buckler family announces the passing of William (Billy) Buckler, formerly of Great House Farm, Llandough.

The probate calendar gives his death as 29 April 1990; the South Wales Echo of 2 June 1990 reported that he “died suddenly this week”. The two do not agree, and both are shown.

Source

Probate calendar 1991: “BUCKLER, William Beverley … of [Penarth] S Glam died 29 April 1990”, administration Llandaff 11 July 1991 (a probable match; EV-0274). South Wales Echo, 2 June 1990, p.2 (EV-0290).
