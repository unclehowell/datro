The BP pension companies that bought from Western Ground Rents in December 1969 and brought the 1974 and 1984–87 possession actions (BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2). In October 1988 the press reported the sale of about 1,300 acres of Welsh land to Intercontinental Land & Development (Cardiff) Ltd for £29,375,400; the farmhouse was demolished in December 1988; the site was sold to Ideal Homes Wales on 3 December 1993.

Land Authority for Wales with BP Pension Trust Ltd / BP Properties Ltd with The woodland north of the house
November 1988 · Record entry

Plots at Llandough bought, from Lavernock to Leckwith

Board minutes and papers of the Land Authority for Wales, 1988–1990 (The National Archives WA 9/49 and related files), cover the November 1988 purchase of sites from Lavernock to Leckwith including plots at Llandough. The South Wales Echo of 26 June 1990: “The woodland was sold to the LAW by BP Pension Fund which owned it until 1988”.

Source

TNA WA 9/49 (catalogue); South Wales Echo, 26 June 1990, p.3 (snippet). Legal Wiki EV-0269, EV-0270.
