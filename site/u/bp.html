In October 1988 the press reported the sale of about 1,300 acres of Welsh land to Intercontinental Land & Development (Cardiff) Ltd for £29,375,400; the farmhouse was demolished in December 1988; the site was sold to Ideal Homes Wales on 3 December 1993.

Plots at Llandough bought, from Lavernock to Leckwith
