About The Marquess of Bute’s estate, landlord of Great House Farm and much of Llandough.

The Marquess of Bute’s estate, landlord of Great House Farm and much of Llandough. Its 1824 survey maps the farm as “Cedfin or Ty Mawr Farm”, 139a 3r 8p; it leased to John Williams in 1877; it conveyed land at Leckwith to Cardiff Corporation in 1905; and in December 1938 the Bute interest at Llandough passed to Western Ground Rents Ltd. The plan of that 1938 conveyance has not been found.

The Bute interest at Llandough passes to Western Ground Rents Ltd

Conveyance of the Bute interest at Llandough to Western Ground Rents Ltd, dated 25 December 1938 in the 1965 contract for sale (condition F, root of title); the press reported completion on 23 December 1938. The deed’s plan, which would show exactly what land went, has not been found.

The Bute rent audit

Amongst the Marquis of Bute’s guests at the rent audit dinner at Cardiff on Saturday was Mr John Williams, of Llandough Farm … He succeeded his father in the tenancy of the farm in 1855 … Saturday was his 102nd rent audit.
