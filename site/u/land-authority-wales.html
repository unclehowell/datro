About The Welsh Office land body.

The Welsh Office land body. The press reported its purchase of sites from Lavernock to Leckwith, including plots at Llandough, in November 1988 (board papers at The National Archives, WA 9/49), and that “the woodland was sold to the LAW by BP Pension Fund which owned it until 1988” (South Wales Echo, 26 June 1990). Seven woods in Llandough and Leckwith passed from it to South Glamorgan County Council.
