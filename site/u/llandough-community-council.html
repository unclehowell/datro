About The community council for Llandough.

The community council for Llandough. In the record from 1990 (the South Wales Echo of 23 Feb 1990: the council “wanted the land registered” as a village green) and the developer’s 1994 planting plan (“to be maintained by LLANDOUGH COMMUNITY COUNCIL”). Its FOI reply of 14 June 2026 states that its minutes from 1982 were deposited at Glamorgan Archives in 2002. Its meeting of 22 October 2026 is remote.

Ideal Homes Wales with Church View Close and the green (VG41) with Llandough Community Council
12 January 1994 · Record entry

“Llandough Farm”: village green planting plan

Planning drawing received 12 January 1994: the site titled “Llandough Farm”; the “LLANDOUGH VILLAGE GREEN … to be maintained by LLANDOUGH COMMUNITY COUNCIL”. Ideal Homes Wales had bought the site from BP on 3 December 1993; the 20 houses of Church View Close followed.
