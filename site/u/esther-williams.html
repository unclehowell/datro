Signature on the Welsh Women’s Peace Petition: “Llandough”

Esther Williams is the eighth signature on page 1 of petition sheet 1/92; address “Llandough”, with no farm name. The other addresses on the sheet (Lewis Road, Cogan, Llwyn-yr-Eryr “Near Cardiff”) put it on the Penarth side.
