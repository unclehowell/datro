Mary (Nancy) Williams, formerly Buckler, of Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough. Born 10 August 1913; died 1983. In the papers as Mrs M. D. Buckler, Mrs Mary Doreen Buckler and Mrs Mary Williams. Her comments here are her own words as the papers printed them.
