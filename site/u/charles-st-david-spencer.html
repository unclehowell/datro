About Solicitor, of Llwyn-yr-eryr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth, and 6 Working Street, Cardiff; formerly clerk to the Whitchurch Justices.

Solicitor, of Llwyn-yr-eryr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth, and 6 Working Street, Cardiff; formerly clerk to the Whitchurch Justices. He put Llwyn-yr-eryr up for auction on 14 May 1936 (withdrawn) and died there in 1936. No link between him and the Williams family is found in the record.

Charles St David Spencer with Llwyn-yr-eryr, Llandough
22 September 1936 · Record entry

Trustee Act notice

“CHARLES SAINT DAVID SPENCER late of Llwyn-yr-eryr Llandough Juxta Penarth in the County of Glamorgan and of 6, Working Street in the city of Cardiff Deceased.” Two days later the paper reported his will: “solicitor, and formerly clerk to the Whitchurch Justices, of Llwyn-yr-Eryr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth, and of 6, Working-street, Cardiff, left everything to his widow”.

Source

Western Mail, 22 September 1936, p.2, and 24 September 1936, p.13 (British Newspaper Archive search text; page images not yet seen).

Llwyn-yr-eryr, Llandough with Charles St David Spencer with St Dochdwy’s Church, Llandough
14 May 1936 · Record entry

Offered at auction and withdrawn

“Messrs. Stephenson and Alexander (F.A.I) offered for sale at Cardiff on Thursday the leasehold residential property, Llwyn-yr-Eryr, Llandough near Cardiff, which was withdrawn, the reserve not having been reached. The solicitors were Messrs. Spencers.”

Source

Western Mail, 15 May 1936, p.9; adverts, Western Mail 25 Apr 1936 p.1 and 17 Oct 1936 p.3 (British Newspaper Archive search text). Glamorgan Archives DSA/14/91.
