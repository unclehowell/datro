Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr) · Llandough-juxta-Penarth

About

Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr) stood beside St Dochdwy’s church at Llandough-juxta-Penarth in the Vale of Glamorgan. The Williams family farmed there under the Bute estate. Mary (Nancy) Williams married Frederick Buckler in 1936 (as Mary (Nancy) Buckler née Williams; later known again as Mary (Nancy) Williams, formerly Buckler), and the family stayed in the farmhouse through a series of possession actions brought by later owners.

In BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2 the Court of Appeal dismissed William Buckler’s appeal. The House of Lords refused leave in March 1988. The farmhouse was demolished in December 1988 and houses were later built on the site.

The family says the farmhouse land was theirs by long occupation. That is our case; it is not a finding of any court. Where this page says what a document shows, it quotes or summarises that document and links to it.

  • 3 March 1988 House of Lords refuses leave to appeal
  • 29 April 1988 First eviction attempt fails
