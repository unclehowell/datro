Manual · Court Drills · Upkeep SOP

The standing operating procedure for Court Drills: what to check, how to change it, and how to prove a change is live.

Content standard
  • Every point has a primary source; a point without one is not published.
  • Quotes from the judgment are verbatim and checked against the BAILII text; ¶ numbers are its printed paragraph numbers.
  • basis “family’s case” where the reading is argument; “record” only where the facts stated are on the face of the cited record.
  • Judges and officials by office or court; institutions and roles only.
Public-text rules

All rules in content and public text apply, checked against rules.json on the exact new bytes.

Family privacy and holdings (standing rule)

Standing rule for every maintainer (project owner, 24 Oct 2026):

  • Do not list or characterise what the family holds, or does not hold, in papers or heirlooms. The only family holdings a public site may name are Mary Williams' statement, family photographs, and William Buckler's diaries.
  • Write no sentence about the family's possession or lack of a document. State the public record and the archive that keeps it instead.
  • Never publish current family addresses, the whereabouts of living family members, or any heirloom and where it is kept.
  • Reason: to avoid inviting a repeat of the events of the 1950s.
  • Sweeps for existing text are run separately; a maintainer adds no such statement and raises any found in the highlight report.
Change control

Edit work/courtdrills/points.json, run build.py, commit to datro@courtdrills (commit ends with the agent signature), verify live. Classes and template: change control.

Must never change:

  • Verbatim quotes are never reworded.
  • The plain one-line style and the red + popup pattern shared with the Grammar House.
Routine checks
  • Every src link answers 200.
  • Counts in the toggles match data/register.json.
  • 390 px and 1280 px: no sideways scroll; the ‹ › bar shows Court Drills between the Grammar House and FCG.
