Manual · Court Drills · Overview

An evidenced register of the Great House Farm proceedings, 1955–1988, in the plain monospace style of the Grammar House: one line per point, with procedural unfairness highlighted in red by default and two toggles for court mistakes and other miscarriages of justice. Tapping a line opens what happened, the quoted record, why it matters and the primary source.

https://courtdrills.bucklerfamily.estate/ · Manual 1.0.0

What it is

Court Drills · Procedural Unfairness: 23 points across the eight stages of the case: the 1955 High Court order under Order 14, the 11 December 1962 Cardiff County Court order, the 1974 action and the September 1974 leave to enforce, the 1983 first registration, the 1984 writ, the 1986 trial, BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2, and the 1988 possession and demolition.

What it does and its purpose

It drills the record for three kinds of point: procedural unfairness (10), court mistakes on the face of the record (8), and other miscarriages of justice (5). Each point carries its date, forum, what happened, why it matters and its source (judgment paragraph, register entry, archive or FOI reference). A point is marked “family’s case” where the reading is argument, not a finding.

In scope: the pages and data files served at courtdrills.bucklerfamily.estate. Out of scope: the line-by-line chronology (owned by the Grammar House) and any other branch.

What it owns in the estate: the court-drills register (data/register.json).

Who it is for

Visitors following the case, reviewers testing the proceedings for unfairness and error, and staff who keep the register consistent with the record.
