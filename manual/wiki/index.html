What it does and its purpose

The primary public record of the case: evidence register (EV-NNNN), Master Timeline (page 33), Mary (Nancy) Williams (page 9), John Williams (page 12), homepage chronology (template part “title-lines”), PRINCE2 highlight reports, Master Change Log (page 3923). Every other site cites it. Evidence files live in wayback; the wiki displays and links them.

In scope: the pages and data files served at greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com. Out of scope: facts owned by another site (read them from their owner, below) and any other branch.

What it owns in the estate: The Evidence Register (EV ids) and the Legal Wiki pages: the editorial record of the case.

Who it is for

Researchers, institutions, the courts and maintainers who need the record itself, with its source.
