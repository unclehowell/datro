## 2. Claim-status vocabulary (attach to every claim, visible and machine-readable)

- RECORDED FACT — supported by a primary document with evidence ID.
- FAMILY'S ACCOUNT — the family's position; unproven until primary proof.
- DISPUTED INTERPRETATION — parties disagree; both sides cited.
- OPEN RESEARCH QUESTION — not yet resolved.
- PROPOSED PROJECT — future objective, not operational.
- SUPERSEDED STATEMENT — replaced; prior wording retained with reason.
- PENDING / HYPOTHESIS / SOURCE PENDING — as used in the PID quality standard.

A disclaimer elsewhere is not a substitute for the label on the claim itself.

## 3. Parcel nomenclature control

Stable IDs govern. Never treat "the farm", "the house", "the estate", "Parcel A", and a title number as implicit synonyms.

- Parcel A (Phase 1): the 1877 house/quarrying parcel. Titles WA231076 and WA240304. Control document: research.bucklerfamily.estate/the-two-parcels/parcel-a-nomenclature/.
- Great House Farm II (Patagonia): a planned museum and interpreted landscape in Puerto Madryn. Not the original property, not a title, not an operational site.

## 4. Source authority

Every copied record displays: stable record ID, canonical source, source revision, mirror generation time, and whether current, superseded, or archival. The wiki is the dependable central record; backups before edits; delete nothing.

## 5. Editorial separation

Campaign framing (e.g. "enemy camp") is labelled advocacy. Institutional directory pages use neutral names. Original correspondence is preserved verbatim; annotation sits beside it, never silently rewritten.

## 6. Root homepage wording

Ownership and recovery statements must identify the specific parcel and interest, separate current recorded status from historical evidence and family position, and not describe an alleged wrong as conclusively established unless the record warrants it. Spelling: reparations, dispossession. Any "T&C apply" needs an explicit terms link.

## 7. AI agent authority

What agents currently do, what requires human review, what is proposed, and who is authorised to speak or act are stated separately. No visitor should infer autonomous legal authority from an archival or research platform.
