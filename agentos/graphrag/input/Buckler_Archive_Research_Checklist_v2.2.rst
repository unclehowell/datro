Forensic Approach
-----------------

This checklist moves beyond "find documents about Great House Farm" to a chain-of-causation reconstruction: Land → Ownership → Occupation → Acquisition → Development → Court Evidence → Outcome. Each phase builds evidential weight for solicitors, investigators, or a public inquiry examining whether there was a miscarriage of justice, procedural unfairness, concealment, or a defective factual basis behind the 1987 judgment.

**Forensic Note:** Do not only search "Great House Farm". **NLW confirmed that unscoped searches for "inventory", "valuation", "fixtures", "furnishings" etc. return hundreds of thousands to millions of hits and are not reliable for this property. Stick to location + family/company names.**
