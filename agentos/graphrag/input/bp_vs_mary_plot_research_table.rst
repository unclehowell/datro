Research Table: BP Plot vs Mary's Plot — Great House Farm / Ty Mawr, Llandough
================================================================================

**Theory:** BP's plot and Mary's plot were TWO SEPARATE plots of land. BP treated
Mary's plot as if it was theirs, subject to the 1962 possession order. But in the
theory, Mary's plot was NOT subject to that order — requiring fraud, concealment,
and deception to achieve.

Critical Observations for the Theory
================================================================================

1. **Mary's plot has no independent documentary references.**
   No conveyances in her name. No registered title numbers. No abstract of title.
   Her only "reference" is the court case ``[1987] EWCA Civ 2`` where she lost.

2. **BP's plot has a full chain of documentary title.**
   ``Conv. 31/12/1969`` (root) → ``Conv. 23/05/1975`` → ``WA240304`` →
   ``Conv. 19/11/1982`` → ``WA231076``. Both conveyances reference the SAME
   1969 root of title.

3. **The 1962 possession order is the key fraud vector.**
   The order was obtained against the Bucklers but NEVER ENFORCED for 12 years.
   If the order was for BP's plot, but BP used it to claim Mary's SEPARATE plot,
   that would be the fraud/concealment/deception the theory explores. The 1974
   "licence offer" came AFTER the failed enforcement attempt — suspicious timing.

4. **The court case treated both plots as one.**
   The judgment only dealt with "the farmhouse and garden." The trial judge
   "rejected the Defendant's claim to adverse possession EXCEPT as to the
   farmhouse and garden." This implies there was OTHER land (farm buildings/
   fields) that was NOT part of the possession dispute — possibly because it was
   on a separate plot.

5. **The two title numbers (WA231076 + WA240304) are significant.**
   They were registered separately (November 1982 and February 1983) and later
   amalgamated under WA231076. This suggests BP acquired the land in TWO
   SEPARATE TRANSACTIONS. Could one transaction be for BP's plot and the other
   for Mary's plot?

6. **HMLR admits they did not investigate Buckler interests.**
   "At the time of registration we did not receive or were aware of any interest
   that you or your family may or may not have had in the land." This means HMLR
   registered BP's title WITHOUT checking if the Bucklers had a claim to a
   SEPARATE parcel.
