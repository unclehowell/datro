2 Contract Details
2.1
Title
Invitation to Tender for Grounds Maintenance Contract 2026-2029
2.2
Description of the goods or services required
To maintain allotment site and a number of green spaces within the community of Llandough for the period 2026-2029.
NOTE: To register your interest in this notice and obtain any additional information please visit the Sell2Wales Web Site at https://www.sell2wales.gov.wales/Search/Search_Switch.aspx?ID=160039.

2.4
Total quantity or scope of tender
The areas to be maintained based on a contract specification include an allotments site, 6 green spaces and a number of smaller green spaces within the community of Llandough. Maintenance will be required for the growing season (March to October) each year in the period 2026-29.
3 Conditions for Participation
3.1
Minimum standards and qualification required
Tenderers will need to have public liability insurance of £5million, a spraying certificate with evidence of LANTRA training in the use of strimmers and brushcutters and risk assessments and method statements must be provided.
