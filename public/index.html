How the switch works

Orders against the family that rest on the title question are quashed and their points re-issued to the party that obtained them. The original ticket stays: it was admitted. Contingent costs post across to Team Regime in the shares proposed on the Reparations Schedule. Lines with no £ on the record yet stay where they are until a figure is found.
