Source archive

Dated snapshots of third-party pages cited as sources about Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough-juxta-Penarth. Each snapshot keeps the page as served, with its own styles and images, plus a text copy and a SHA-256. Pages are re-checked every 5 days; a new capture is stored only when the text changes.

How this archive works

Address format: /YYYYMMDDhhmmss/<address> opens a capture; /latest/<address> opens the newest one. The banner above each snapshot sits outside the archived page, which is shown unaltered in a frame.

Sources: sources.csv (every cited third-party address and its status) · check log · run history.

Pages that could not be reached are retried on the next run; where possible they are seeded from an existing Internet Archive copy, labelled with that copy's original date. Sites whose robots.txt forbids archiving are listed as not captured. Files over 25 MB link to an Internet Archive copy.

Snapshots are copies of third-party pages for reference; each belongs to its publisher.
