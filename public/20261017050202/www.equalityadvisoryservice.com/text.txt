What We Do
The EASS helpline
advises and assists individuals on issues
relating to equality and human rights, across
England, Scotland and Wales.
We are able to support
individuals to resolve their issues using
informal dispute resolution methods as an
alternative to county court or employment
tribunal. In some instances we may write to a
service provider on behalf of a client in
order to initiate a resolution. However, we
recognise that there are some situations where
issuing a claim may be the only option.
The EASS helpline does
not provide legal advice – for more
information on this please refer to our Legal
Advice policy.
