Holy Nativity, Penarth
Information
Sunday services are on the formal end, with a small but very faithful congregation, some of whom have been worshipping in Holy Nativity for many decades. Sunday services include hymns, readings, a sermon, and Communion most Sundays.
The churchyard has recently undergone a project to encourage more variety of plants and wildlife to celebrate God's creation.
