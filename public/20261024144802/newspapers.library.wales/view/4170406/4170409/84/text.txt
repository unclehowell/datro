Slept in Lime Kiln UNKNOWN MAN KILLED BY FUMES Mr. E. B. Reece conducted an inquiry at the Town-haU to-day on the body of the unknown, who was found in an unconscious state near the Llandough Limekilns, and died at the Cardiff Workhouse yesterday. George Boulton, a lime burner, said he dis- covered deceased at six o'clock last Saturday morning lying on the footplate of one of the kilns. James Snell said he usually went round the kilns between seven and eight o'clock in the evening to keep loiterers away. There were sometimes .i^s many as 30 or 40 people sleeping there. He went round on Saturday evening, but saw no one there on that occasion. Dr. Edwards, medical officer at the work- house, said the man was in a bad state when brought there. He was burned a/bout the right side of the body and was unconscious from the effects of the fumes. In treatment he rallied a little, and during that period was able to say "James Phillips" in answer to witness's query as to his name. He then lapsed into unconsciousness, and died on the Sunday. Death was due to deceased inhaling the fumes of the burning lime. A verdict was returned in accordance with the medical testimony.
