We protect Wales’s historic environment and promote its sustainable management for present and future generations.

How can we help you today?
In this section
Cof Cymru
Search our records on scheduled monuments, listed buildings, registered landscapes of historic interest, protected wrecks and World Heritage Sites in Wales to find sites, determine their extent and download descriptions.
Historic Assets
Find out how we protect and manage change to national historic assets in Wales. And find out how to look after your historic asset and what action to take if you need to make any changes to it.
Placemaking
Learn about our role in planning and the legislation that affects Wales's historic environment, how to prepare a heritage impact assessment, analyse setting and look after the historic character that makes places in Wales special.
Climate Change
Find out what we're doing to address both the causes and impacts of global warming and climate change and how this is affecting our irreplaceable historic assets across Wales.
Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023
The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 and its suite of supporting secondary legislation came into full effect on 4 November 2024.
