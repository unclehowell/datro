Description
1. Demolished December 1988.
2. A farmhouse of limestone construction of uncertain date. Internal details suggest that the house was C17, however certain features suggest that the core of the house may have been medieval. The house was altered and reconstructed by the Bute estate in the C19, and much of the exterior would seem to have dated from this period.
(Source: Site File notes by Richard Suggett)
J Hill 26/04/2004
