SWINE FEVER ATLLANDOUGEL AND RHOOSE. Swine fever has broken out at Llandough and Rhoose this week. Five pigs belonging to Mr Williams, Great House, have been slaughtered. On Tuesday morning, one of a herd of 23 was re- ported as being affected, and it was feared that the whole would have to be slaughtered.
