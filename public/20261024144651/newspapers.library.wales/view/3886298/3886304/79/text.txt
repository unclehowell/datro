I LLANDOUGH COURT LEET. ——— I AND LATE LORD NINIAN STUART. The several Court Leet and View of Frank Pledge of the Marquess of Bute, as lord of the manor, are now being held. At the Llan- dough Court Leet the Deputy-steward (Mr. Henry Morgan) made feeling reference to the death of Lord Ninian Crichton-Stuart, who, he said, was beloved by his constituents, and was one of the most popular members of the House of Commons. The Foreman of the Grand Jury (the Rev. F. F. Williams, rector of Cogan) also made pathetic allusion to the great loss sustained by the death of his lordship.
