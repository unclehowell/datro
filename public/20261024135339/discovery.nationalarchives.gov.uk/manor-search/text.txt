Manorial Documents Register
The
Manorial Documents Register (MDR)
is the official index to English and Welsh manorial records. It provides brief descriptions of documents and their locations in public and private hands.
Manorial documents recorded in the MDR are defined by the
Manorial Documents Rules
as ‘court rolls, surveys, maps, terriers, documents and books of every description relating to the boundaries, franchises, wastes, customs or courts of a manor’. Title deeds are not included in the MDR.
Read more about
manorial documents
and the legislation that explains why we maintain the MDR on behalf of the Master of the Rolls.
We have now
completed our programme
to update the MDR and make it available online. You can search the register using the form below, or browse our
alphabetical list of manors
.

update the MDR
or
contact us
with your enquiry.
