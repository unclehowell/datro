Administrative/biographical history
The Vale of Glamorgan Borough Council was formed in 1973 under the Local
Government Act 1972 and became formally operative in 1974. The council
possessed powers of a District Council and succeeded the older authorities
Barry Urban District Council, Penarth Urban District Council, Barry Municipal
Borough Council, Cowbridge Rural District Council and the greater part of
Cardiff Rural District Council. The Council was replaced in 1996 by the Vale of
Glamorgan County Borough Council. The new authority covered the same
area with minor additions including part of the former Ogwr/Bridgend District
Council.

DCVG/T/1 Rate valuation books
The following records relate to rates valuation and
amendments created by Barry Urban District Council,
Penarth Urban District Council, Cowbridge Borough
Council, Cardiff Rural District Council, Cowbridge
Rural District Council (1894-1974) and the Vale of
Glamorgan Borough Council (1974-1996). The
records of all the former district councils (1894-1974)
were used by the Vale of Glamorgan Borough
Council in its rates valuation as part of its
administration and no attempt has been made to
transfer records for the district councils to other
records held for these authorities. All volumes bore
the reference 'VOGR [and number]'.
Most of the valuation lists and direction sheets were
in use in routine administration in the treasurer's
department of the Vale of Glamorgan Borough
Council over a number of years and are heavily
annotated and amended. All dates assigned to
individual volumes should be interpreted as in
administrative use up to the abolition of the council in
1996. Most valuation lists contain amendments.
Direction sheets consist of amendments to the
valuations.
