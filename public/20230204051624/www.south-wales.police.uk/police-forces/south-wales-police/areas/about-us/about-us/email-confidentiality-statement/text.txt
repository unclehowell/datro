Email Confidentiality Statement
Emails and any files transmitted with them are not necessarily the view of South Wales Police.
They are intended only for the person or entity named above. If you have received an e-mail in error please notify the originator and erase the e-mail from your system. If you are not the intended recipient or the employer or agent responsible for delivering it to the intended recipient, you are hereby notified that any use, review, dissemination, distribution or copying of the e-mail is strictly prohibited.
E-mails and any files transmitted within them have been checked for all known viruses. The recipient should still check the e-mail and any attachments for the presence of viruses, as South Wales Police accepts no liability for any damage caused by any virus transmitted by the e-mail.
Unauthorised use or disclosure of emails may be unlawful.
