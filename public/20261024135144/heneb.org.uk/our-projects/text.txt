Protecting Wales’ Heritage Through Expert Guidance. Balancing development and preservation, our Historic Environment Advisory Services provide trusted expertise to safeguard archaeology and cultural heritage while supporting sustainable growth and community well-being.

Field Services
Heneb’s Field Services team offers a full range of archaeological services—from building recording to excavations. We work closely with developers, landowners, and communities to ensure compliance with regulations while safeguarding and documenting Wales’ rich historic environment.
