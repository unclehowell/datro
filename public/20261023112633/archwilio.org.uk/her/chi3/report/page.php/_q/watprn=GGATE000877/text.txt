Summary
A trial excavation was undertaken at Great House Farm by GGAT in 1990.
Description
A trial excavation was undertaken at Great House Farm by GGAT in 1990 in advance of redevelopment.
A series of machine cut trenches were put across the site to test its archaeological potential. A large part of the site was determined to be of non-low archaeological importance whilst some of the site was considered of higher importance and recommendations for watching briefs and recording were made (GGAT1990).
