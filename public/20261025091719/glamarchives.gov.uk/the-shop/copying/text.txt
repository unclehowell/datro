Digital photography
Digital copies can be provided as hard-copy printouts of photographs or as digital images by e-mail.
Please note:
Documents will not be copied if the original item is too large or if it is in a fragile condition.
In all cases, copying of any document is at the discretion of the Glamorgan Archivist.
Ordering copies by email / post
Orders will only be accepted with a fully completed
Digital Photography Order Form
(pdf) or
Digital Photography Order Form
(Doc). Please read the copyright declaration carefully before returning the form.
Prior to submitting an order for copies by post you may find it useful to discuss your requirements with our staff, either by e-mail at
glamro@cardiff.gov.uk
, or by post at:
Glamorgan Archives
Clos Parc Morgannwg
Leckwith
Cardiff
CF11 8AW
We aim to fulfil your order within 15 working days.
