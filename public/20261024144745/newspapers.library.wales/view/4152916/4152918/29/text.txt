TRAMPS AT LLANDOUGH The lime-kilna at Llandough is evidently a. favourite night haunt for tramps, for the police are continually engaged in clearing them away. About 1.30 a.m. to-day eight men of the seafaring class, having- no visible means of subsistence, were discovered doss- ing all this spot by Police-constable Morgan and other constables. As a result, the men, Alexander James, Thomas Lord, Richard Abrahams, Joseph Thomas, Timothy Been, Charles M'Carthy, John Morris, and Frederick Cross, apptaroo in the dock at Penarfch Police-court this morning (before Mr. J. H HaUett and Mr. J. G. Thomas) charged with vagrancy. The two former had been pre- viously charged with a, similar offence, and James was sent to prison for 21 days, and Lord for a week, the others being cautioned and discharged.
