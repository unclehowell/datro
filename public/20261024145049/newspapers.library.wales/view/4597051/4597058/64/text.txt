PENARTH POLICE COURT,
News
Copy
PENARTH POLICE COURT, MONDAY,—Before Mr 8. Batchelor (chairman), Mr W. B. Gibbs, and Mr W. L. Morris. SHBBP WORRYING AT LLANDOUGH. John Williams, Llandough Farm, charged John Johnson with allowing a dog belonging to him to worry two sheep in a field at Llandough on the 3rd instant, ibausing the death of one and breaking the leg of another, the value of which was M. Ordered to pay damages and costs. DRIVING WITHOUT LIGHTS. Edwin Walker for driving his trap without proper lights one hour after sunset at Penartb, was ordered to pay 5s and 4s costs, OBSTRUCTING THE PAVEMENT. Edward Ellis and Percy Watts were charged by P.O. Keovil with obstructing the pavement in Glebe-street, Penarth, on the 2nd instant. Watts was dismissed, and Ellis was fined 11 including costs. SLIDING ON THE HICHWAY AT PBNARTH. Michael Kidney and Bertie Evans, youths, were dismissed with a caution for sliding on the high- way at Penarth. DRUNK AND DISORDERLY AND ASSAULTING TBOt POLICE. John Sullivan pleaded guilty to being drank and disorderly at Penarth on the 29th ultimo, and was fined 5s.—George Webber was charged with being drunk and assaulting P.C. Keovil on the 4th instant. Fined 10s, or five days. CASE OR FALSE PRETENCES. John Kennedy, seaman, waa charged with obtaining clothing from the shop of Mr Phillips, 16, Glebe-street, Penartb, by false pretences. Evidence was given by John Morgan, manager of the shop, who said prisoner came there and wanted a lot of clothing, stating he was the second mate of the ship Elwy, lying at Cardiff Docks. He gave him the loan of £1 and supplied him with several articles of clothing, defendant stating he was waiting his wages, which amounted to £ 72.—Owing to insufficient evidence prisoner was discharged. PATERNITY CASE. Thomas Henry Hutchings, coal trimmer, was summoned by Louisa Ann Munro, 57, Maughan- street, both of Penarth affiliation case. Mr T. H. Belcher, solicitor, Cardiff, was for the defence, and Mr A. W. Morris, solicitor, for the prosecu- tion.-The Bench ordered the defendant to contribute 5s a week for 16 years, and to pay costs. ALLEGED THEFT AT BARRY DOCK. Patrick Higgins and Thomas Spencer were charged with stealing a suit of oilskins, the pro- perty of Daniel Bassett, on board the steamship Hazledene, lying at Barry Docks.—Prosecutor said he missed the clothing from the forecastle of the ship on Friday evening. He valued the suit at 10s.—Dock-constable Weeks and Police- constable Williams gave evidence, the latter stating that he arrested prisoners at the Seamen's Mission, Barry Docks, and that the clothes were found at the Priory, Barry.—In answer to the charge, prisoners said they knew nothing about it.—Remanded till Thursday, at Barry Docks Police Court.
