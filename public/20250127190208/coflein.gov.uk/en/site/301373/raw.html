    Description
    1. Composite pillar cross standing in the churchyard of St Dochdwy's Church. ex RCAHMW Glamorgan I, iii, p 61
    2. Composite pillar cross, 2.97m high, consisting of a two part squared shaft, having a central collar and base, but lacking a cross-head.
    Thought to be l.10th-e.11th c.
