Paying for research
We encourage you to visit The National Archives, where you can
do your research
and view documents free of charge, whether they are
online documents
or
not online
. If you want to pay someone to carry out the research on your behalf instead, you can use an independent researcher or our own paid search service.
Independent researchers
Independent researchers are not employees of The National Archives and any arrangements made are a private matter between you and the researcher. The fees they charge vary greatly but are often less than our own charges which are fixed at rates set out by the Fees Regulations under the Public Records Act (1958).
Our paid search service
This service may be useful for those who are unable to conduct the search themselves or visit us in person.
Under the Freedom of Information (FOI) Act 2000, you have the right to see information held by us unless one of the exemptions set out in the FOI Act applies.
